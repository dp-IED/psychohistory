# Forecasting Policy

## Role

Machine-readable limits and human heuristics. YAML is authoritative for runtime; body text guides reasoning.

## Synthesis heuristics

- **Minimum evidence gate**: Do not treat a forecast as “settled” when `evidence_items < 3` *or* when no source gives a quantitative base rate (implied odds, market, model, or historical frequency). Escalate uncertainty instead of converging.
- **Family routing**: If the question is a scheduled contest (sports, debates, awards with shortlists), classify as **sports** or **event-outcome**, not **general**. Apply sport-specific checks even when the upstream label says `general`.
- **Sports match outcomes**: Anchor on (1) market-implied win probability when liquid, (2) Elo/rating gap + home field, (3) recent form and rest, (4) known absences. Explicitly state why probability is *not* 0.5. Prefer wide intervals when lineups or motivation are unclear.
- **Check hygiene**: Only reference checks that exist in the registry. A skipped `nonexistent_check_*` is a defect—fix the checklist, do not silently skip.
- **Shrinkage**: Posterior mean pulls toward 0.5 by `shrinkage`. After poor calibration in a family, temporarily increase shrinkage or widen explicit verbal uncertainty until base rates improve.

## Blind-spot prompts (names in YAML)

- **sports_match_calendar_check**: Confirm date, competition, home/away, and that the fixture is not postponed or a friendly unless the question specifies.
- **base_rate_league_prior_check**: State typical home-win / draw / away baselines for the league or format; justify deviation.
- **injury_suspension_lineup_check**: List material availability constraints that move probability meaningfully.
