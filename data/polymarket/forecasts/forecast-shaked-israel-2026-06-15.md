**Prediction:** NO

**Reasoning:**

The 0.2% market price is generous. Ayelet Shaked has no plausible path to the Israeli premiership:

1. **No Knesset seat**: Shaked's Yamina party failed to cross the 3.25% electoral threshold in 2022. She has not held elected office since. You cannot become PM without being elected to the Knesset first.

2. **Political base collapsed**: Yamina's voters migrated to Likud and Religious Zionism. There is no secular right-wing constituency separate from Netanyahu's coalition. The Bennett-Shaked experiment of a secular right alternative to Netanyahu failed decisively.

3. **Coalition math blocks her**: Even if Shaked returned to Knesset with 2-3 seats, Israeli coalition formation requires 61+ seats. No coalition would put a 2-seat party leader as PM. The PM must command the largest bloc or be the compromise candidate acceptable to multiple blocs — Shaked is neither.

4. **Bennett government stigma**: Serving in the Bennett-Lapid government (2021-2022), which included Ra'am (an Arab Islamist party), permanently damaged Shaked's credibility with the right-wing base. She is seen as having "betrayed" the right by joining a government dependent on Arab party support.

5. **Netanyahu's longevity**: Netanyahu shows no signs of retiring. Even if he did, the succession would go to Yariv Levin, Israel Katz, or another Likud heavyweight — not an external figure with no parliamentary base.

**Bottom line**: This market is a lottery ticket trading on name recognition. The 0.2% price implies 1:500 odds — still generous given the structural impossibility. The high $989K volume suggests traders putting small amounts on long-shot scenarios for asymmetric payoff, not genuine probability assessment.
