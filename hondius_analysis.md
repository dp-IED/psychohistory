# MV Hondius Hantavirus Outbreak — Analysis (cutoff: 2026-05-21)

## Situation summary
- Andes virus (ANDV) identified — the ONLY hantavirus with documented human-to-human transmission
- 11 total confirmed/suspected cases (all passengers or crew), 3 deaths
- 147 persons aboard (88 passengers, 59 crew), 23 nationalities
- Ship departed Ushuaia 1 April, visited South Georgia, Tristan da Cunha, Saint Helena, Ascension, Cape Verde, Tenerife, Rotterdam
- 30 passengers disembarked at Saint Helena (24 April) before outbreak detected
- Index case (Dutch, 70M) died 11 April, never tested; his wife died 26 April in Johannesburg (confirmed)

## Non-passenger cases confirmed: ZERO as of May 21
All confirmed cases are passengers or crew who were aboard the MV Hondius.

## Exposed non-passengers under monitoring (could become cases)
1. 12 Radboud hospital workers — exposed to patient blood/urine without proper PPE (11 May), 6-week quarantine
2. ~55 KLM flight KL592 contacts (5 high-risk who helped the dying woman, 50 lower-risk within 2 rows)
3. ~20 French contacts hospitalized as of 16 May, none symptomatic
4. 41 people in 16 US states under monitoring
5. 16 individuals in Canada under monitoring
6. ~20-30 household/family contacts of 30 Saint Helena disembarkers
7. Two Singapore residents (tested NEGATIVE 8 May)
8. Healthcare workers in South Africa, Switzerland, UK, Netherlands

## Key epidemiological factors
- H2H transmission of ANDV requires prolonged close contact; ship outbreak only produced ~7 cases from index despite weeks in confined quarters
- Incubation period: 1-7 weeks, median ~2-4 weeks
- Past ANDV outbreaks: Epuyen 2018 (~30 cases, secondary attack rate ~4.5%), El Bolson 1996 (~16 cases)
- Those with April exposure could still develop symptoms through mid-June
- 30 Saint Helena disembarkers had ~10 days in community pre-detection (highest risk period)
- After outbreak detection (4 May), all passengers placed in strict quarantine measures
- WHO risk assessment: "low"

## Pathways to 5+ non-passenger cases
- Household transmission from Saint Helena group: expected ~2-3 cases
- Flight contacts (KL592): expected ~0-1 cases
- Healthcare workers: expected ~0-1 cases
- TOTAL expected: ~2-4 (below the threshold of 5)

## Analysis
Probability significantly below the 36% base rate because:
1. ANDV H2H transmission is very inefficient — even in close ship quarters, attack rate was low
2. Zero non-passenger cases after 17+ days of intensive multinational surveillance
3. Rapid quarantine measures cut off most transmission chains
4. The 5-case threshold is high relative to expected secondary cases
