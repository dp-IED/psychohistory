## Reflection Report: Question 11 — "Will Trump drop out of presidential race?"

### Diagnosis

**Correct prediction (NO)**, but the vault contributed only partial signal. The quarterly timeline files (2024-Q1 through Q4) documented the election arc well — Trump's nomination lock-in (Q1), his conviction (Q2), two assassination attempts and Biden's withdrawal (Q3), and his victory (Q4). However, the vault lacked structured causal frameworks that would explain *why* withdrawal was structurally impossible, not just historically observed. This was a partial freebie: general knowledge about the well-known 2024 outcome was sufficient.

**Three structural gaps identified:**

1. **No thread** connecting the 2024 election narrative across quarters — the events existed in four separate files with no linking narrative
2. **No concept** for the persistence-under-threat dynamic — the mechanism by which legal jeopardy + nomination lock-in + assassination attempt hardening makes candidate withdrawal extremely unlikely
3. **No entity section** on Trump's persistence pattern during the 2024 campaign

### Key Insight for Future Forecasting

The question tests a specific structural dynamic: **major-party nominees facing legal jeopardy cannot drop out** because the stakes are existential (presidency = legal protection). This generalizes beyond Trump to any leader whose legal vulnerability is contingent on holding office. The new procedure step ensures this check is performed on every future candidate-withdrawal question.
