### Key Lessons for Future Forecasts

1. **Sentencing is a distinct sub-domain from trial timing.** The vault correctly tracked when the sentencing would happen but had no framework for predicting *what* sentence would result. Post-conviction dynamics (prosecution posture, officeholder practical obstacles, judge tendencies) are materially different from pre-trial dynamics.

2. **Entity stubs for judges and prosecutors are critical.** The vault cannot forecast sentencing outcomes without documenting the sentencing judge's tendencies and the prosecutor's post-conviction posture. Merchan's caution and Bragg's concession were both directly material.

3. **Zero-byte entity stubs are a trap.** The Donald Trump entity existed by name but was empty. The named-entity sweep procedure (Step 5) correctly requires that every named person in a question have a vault stub — but a stub that exists but provides no information is as bad as no stub at all.
