# 2024-Q2 Summary (April 1, 2024 - June 30, 2024)

## Geopolitics & International Relations

### Middle East

- **April 1** -- Israel attacked the Iranian consulate in Damascus, killing 16 people, including two Iranian generals. This marked a significant escalation in Israel's campaign against Iranian military presence in Syria (Syrian Observatory for Human Rights, April 3, 2024).

- **April 13** -- Iran launched retaliatory strikes against Israel with drones and missiles, marking the first direct Iranian military attack on Israeli territory. Most were intercepted by Israeli, US, UK, and Jordanian defenses (New York Post, April 13, 2024).

- **April 19** -- Israel conducted airstrikes against Iran near Isfahan in response to Iran's April 13 attack, in a calibrated strike that appeared designed to avoid full-scale war while re-establishing deterrence (CNN, April 19, 2024).

- **May 20** -- ICC Chief Prosecutor Karim Khan sought arrest warrants for Israeli PM Benjamin Netanyahu, Defense Minister Yoav Gallant, and Hamas leaders Yahya Sinwar, Ismail Haniyeh, and Mohammed Deif for alleged war crimes and crimes against humanity related to the October 7 attacks and the subsequent Gaza war (BBC News, May 20, 2024).

- **May 24** -- The International Court of Justice ruled that Israel must halt its military offensive in Rafah, southern Gaza (BBC News, May 24, 2024).

- **May 28** -- Spain, Ireland, and Norway formally recognized the State of Palestine, a significant diplomatic step that brought the total number of UN member states recognizing Palestine to over 140 (BBC News, May 28, 2024).

- **June 12** -- A UN Commission of Inquiry found that both Israel and Hamas committed war crimes since October 7, the first such UN inquiry into the conflict (The Guardian, June 12, 2024).

### Iran

- **May 19** -- Iranian President Ebrahim Raisi, Foreign Minister Hossein Amir-Abdollahian, and seven others were killed in a helicopter crash near the Azerbaijan-Iran border in the Varzaqan region. The crash occurred in foggy conditions (BBC News, May 20, 2024).

### Americas

- **April 5** -- Ecuadorian police raided the Mexican embassy in Quito to arrest former Vice President Jorge Glas, who had been granted political asylum by Mexico. Mexico and Nicaragua broke diplomatic relations with Ecuador over the violation of the Vienna Convention (Primicias, April 6, 2024).

- **April 25** -- Haiti's Transitional Presidential Council took power as the new head of state following the resignation of acting PM Ariel Henry, amid ongoing gang violence and instability (Le Nouvelliste, April 25, 2024).

- **May 5** -- José Raúl Mulino was elected President of Panama (Reuters, May 5, 2024).

- **May 19** -- Incumbent President Luis Abinader was re-elected for a second term in the Dominican Republic (BBC, May 20, 2024).

- **June 2** -- Claudia Sheinbaum was elected President of Mexico, becoming the first woman to lead the country (The New York Times, June 2, 2024).

- **June 26** -- A failed coup attempt in La Paz, Bolivia, was led by former General Commander Juan Jose Zuniga. President Luis Arce called on the population to mobilize in defense of democracy, and the attempt was quickly suppressed (CNN, June 26, 2024).

### Europe

- **April 6** -- Peter Pellegrini was elected President of Slovakia in the second round of the presidential election (Politico, April 6, 2024).

- **April 9** -- Simon Harris became Ireland's youngest Taoiseach (prime minister) after Leo Varadkar's resignation, following a Dail vote of 88-69 (RTÉ News, April 9, 2024).

- **April 17** -- Croatia's parliamentary election: Incumbent PM Andrej Plenkovic's HDZ coalition won a plurality but failed to secure a majority (The Guardian, April 18, 2024).

- **May 9** -- North Macedonia held dual elections: right-wing VMRO-DPMNE won a landslide in parliamentary elections, and Gordana Siljanovska-Davkova was elected the country's first female president (AP News, May 9, 2024).

- **May 15** -- Slovak PM Robert Fico was shot and wounded in an assassination attempt while meeting supporters in Handlova (Politico, May 15, 2024).

- **May 26** -- Incumbent President Gitanas Nauseda won a landslide victory for a second term in Lithuania's presidential election (Reuters, May 26, 2024).

- **June 6-9** -- European Parliament elections: The center-right EPP remained the largest group, but far-right parties made significant gains, dealing defeats to French President Macron's alliance and German Chancellor Scholz's SPD (AP News, June 9, 2024).

- **June 25** -- EU formally opened accession negotiations with Ukraine and Moldova, marking a historic step in their European integration (European Council, June 25, 2024).

### Africa

- **April 29** -- Floods and a railway embankment failure near Mai Mahiu, Kenya, killed at least 50 people, adding to wider East African floods that killed 488 and displaced 503,000 (BBC News, April 29, 2024).

- **May 6** -- Mahamat Deby won Chad's presidential election to a full term, succeeding his father Idriss Deby (Yahoo News, May 10, 2024).

- **May 19** -- An attempted coup in the Democratic Republic of Congo, reportedly led by Christian Malanga, was suppressed by government forces in Kinshasa (Al Jazeera, May 19, 2024).

- **May 29** -- South Africa's general election: The ANC failed to win a majority for the first time since the end of apartheid, winning only 40% of the vote (BBC News, May 31, 2024).

- **June 10** -- A plane crash near Chikangawa, Malawi, killed Vice President Saulos Chilima and eight others (AP News, June 11, 2024).

- **June 14** -- The ANC and opposition parties agreed to form a national unity government in South Africa, with Cyril Ramaphosa re-elected as president (BBC News, June 14, 2024).

### Asia-Pacific

- **April 3** -- A magnitude 7.4 earthquake struck off eastern Taiwan, the strongest in 25 years, triggering tsunami warnings in Japan and Okinawa (The Guardian, April 3, 2024).

- **April 10** -- South Korea's legislative election: The opposition Democratic Party won 176 seats, while President Yoon Suk Yeol's People Power Party won only 108 seats in a sharp rebuke to the government (CSIS, April 10, 2024).

- **April 17** -- Solomon Islands general election: Incumbent PM Manasseh Sogavare's OUR Party won a plurality (Al Jazeera, April 24, 2024).

- **May 15** -- Lawrence Wong succeeded Lee Hsien Loong as Prime Minister of Singapore, the first leadership change in 20 years (Reuters, May 15, 2024).

- **June 1** -- India's general election concluded: PM Modi's BJP lost its outright majority, though the National Democratic Alliance retained a majority. The result was a surprising setback for Modi's party (NBC News, June 4, 2024).

- **June 28** -- Mongolia's parliamentary election took place.

### Other

- **May 10** -- The UN General Assembly passed Resolution ES-10/23 granting Palestine additional rights and seating among member states alphabetically (BBC News, May 10, 2024).

- **May 24** -- A major landslide in Enga Province, Papua New Guinea, killed between 160 and over 2,000 people (ABC News Australia, May 31, 2024).

## Military & Security

### Israel-Gaza War (ongoing)

- **April 1** -- Israeli drone strike killed seven World Central Kitchen aid workers delivering food in Gaza, sparking international outrage (Al Jazeera, April 2, 2024).

- **May 7** -- Israeli forces entered Rafah in southern Gaza, expanding ground operations despite international warnings about civilian casualties.

- **May 24** -- ICJ ordered Israel to halt its Rafah offensive, though Israel indicated it would not comply (BBC News, May 24, 2024).

### Campus Protests

- **April 17 onwards** -- Pro-Palestinian protest encampments spread across US university campuses starting at Columbia University, with hundreds of arrests. By May 3, over 2,000 arrests had been made nationally (CNN, May 3, 2024). Columbia cancelled its main graduation ceremony.

### Terror & Security

- **June 23** -- Coordinated attacks in Makhachkala and Derbent in Russia's Republic of Dagestan killed at least 28 people, including police and clergy (Reuters, June 24, 2024).

## Politics & Governance

### United States

- **April 9** -- Arizona Supreme Court upheld an 1864 law banning nearly all abortions; the Arizona legislature later repealed it (The Guardian, April 9, 2024).

- **May 30** -- Donald Trump was found guilty on 34 felony counts of falsifying business records in his New York hush-money trial, the first US president convicted of a crime (NBC News, May 30, 2024).

- **June 4** -- Biden enacted an executive order suspending asylum processing at the Mexico-US border when daily crossings exceed 2,500 (NPR, June 4, 2024).

- **June 11** -- Hunter Biden was convicted on three felony counts related to firearm possession while using narcotics (CNN, June 11, 2024).

- **June 13** -- The Supreme Court ruled in FDA v. Alliance for Hippocratic Medicine to preserve access to the abortion pill mifepristone (NBC News, June 13, 2024).

- **June 19** -- Louisiana became the first state to mandate display of the Ten Commandments in all public school classrooms (ABC News, June 19, 2024).

- **June 27** -- First 2024 presidential debate between Joe Biden and Donald Trump was broadcast on CNN. Biden's poor performance sparked widespread concerns about his age and cognitive fitness, leading to calls from Democrats for him to suspend his campaign (CNN, June 28, 2024).

- **June 28** -- The Supreme Court issued Loper Bright Enterprises v. Raimondo, overruling the 1984 Chevron doctrine and reducing the power of federal agencies (The Guardian, June 28, 2024).

- **June 28** -- The Supreme Court ruled in Moyle v. United States, reinstating emergency abortion access in Idaho (The New York Times, June 27, 2024).

- **June 28** -- Biden pardoned thousands of former service members convicted under the now-repealed military ban on consensual same-sex conduct (AP News, June 26, 2024).

### Georgia

- **May-June** -- Georgia's "Law on Transparency of Foreign Influence" (the "foreign agents" law) sparked massive protests. The US imposed travel bans on Georgian Dream politicians (AP News, June 6, 2024).

### United Kingdom

- **May 22** -- PM Rishi Sunak called a snap general election for July 4, surprising many in his own party.

## Economy & Business

### Energy & Commodities

- **April 17** -- Biden administration announced it would reimpose oil and gas sanctions on Venezuela (Washington Post, April 17, 2024).

- **April 30** -- G7 countries agreed to phase out unabated coal power by 2030-2035 (World Resources Institute, April 30, 2024).

### Corporate

- **April 2** -- General Electric completed its three-way split into GE Aerospace, GE Vernova, and GE HealthCare (Reuters, April 2, 2024).

- **May 1** -- FTC approved ExxonMobil's $60 billion acquisition of Pioneer Natural Resources (CNBC, May 1, 2024).

- **May 2** -- Florida became the first US state to ban cultivated (lab-grown) meat (BBC, May 2, 2024).

- **May 2** -- Rue21 filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy, closing all 540 stores (Fox Business, May 3, 2024).

- **May 6** -- Steward Health Care, a for-profit hospital chain, filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy (WBUR, May 6, 2024).

- **June 1** -- Ticketmaster (Live Nation) disclosed a data breach affecting up to 560 million customers (Reuters, June 1, 2024).

- **June 3** -- GameStop shares surged 21% after "Roaring Kitty" (Keith Gill) posted a $116 million investment on Reddit (Reuters, June 3, 2024).

- **June 12** -- Coach USA (owner of Megabus) filed Chapter 11 bankruptcy (Reuters, June 12, 2024).

- **June 17** -- FTC sued Adobe for hiding termination fees and making subscription cancellation difficult (CBS News, June 17, 2024).

- **June 18** -- Nvidia surpassed Microsoft as the world's most valuable publicly traded company with a market cap exceeding $3.34 trillion, driven by AI chip demand (CNBC, June 18, 2024).

- **June 18** -- EV startup Fisker filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy (Reuters, June 18, 2024).

- **June 24** -- Novo Nordisk announced a $4.1 billion facility in North Carolina to expand production of weight-loss drugs Ozempic and Wegovy (Bloomberg, June 24, 2024).

- **June 27** -- A federal jury ruled the NFL violated antitrust laws with its Sunday Ticket premium package, awarding $4.7 billion in damages (CNBC, June 27, 2024).

## Technology & AI

### Artificial Intelligence

- **May 13** -- OpenAI released GPT-4o ("omni"), a multimodal model capable of processing text, images, and audio with significantly improved latency and reasoning capability (TechCrunch, May 13, 2024).

- **May 14** -- Google introduced AI Overviews in US search results, generating AI-summarized answers at the top of search pages (The Guardian, May 14, 2024).

- **May 8** -- Google DeepMind announced AlphaFold 3, capable of predicting structures of almost all biological molecules and their interactions (Time, May 8, 2024).

- **June 8** -- A paper in Ethics and Information Technology argued that LLMs like ChatGPT "bullshit" rather than "hallucinate," challenging the framing of AI inaccuracies (Scientific American, June 8, 2024).

### Space

- **June 5** -- Boeing's Starliner launched its first crewed flight (CFT) to the ISS carrying astronauts Butch Wilmore and Suni Williams, after years of delays (NASA, June 5, 2024).

- **June 6** -- SpaceX's Starship completed its fourth integrated flight test (IFT-4), with both Super Heavy booster and Starship upper stage achieving soft water landings (The New York Times, June 6, 2024).

- **June 2** -- China's Chang'e 6 successfully landed on the far side of the Moon (BBC News, June 2, 2024).

- **June 4** -- Chang'e 6 lifted off from the lunar far side carrying samples (Reuters, June 4, 2024).

- **June 25** -- Chang'e 6 returned to Earth with the world's first samples from the far side of the Moon (AP News, June 25, 2024).

### Computing & Science

- **April 4** -- DESI (Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument) released the largest 3D cosmic map, finding indications that dark energy may be evolving over time (Wired, April 4, 2024).

- **April 11** -- Scientists reported the first nitrogen-fixing organelle (nitroplast) in a marine alga, a landmark in evolutionary biology (Science, April 12, 2024).

- **May 10** -- A G5 geomagnetic storm (the strongest since 2003) impacted Earth, producing aurora visible at unusually low latitudes across both hemispheres. The storm caused some power grid irregularities and satellite communication disruptions (The New York Times, May 10, 2024).

- **June 5** -- Astronomers identified the slowest-spinning neutron star ever recorded, ASKAP J1935+2148, rotating once every 54 minutes (University of Manchester, June 5, 2024).

- **June 20** -- The Iberian lynx was downgraded from "endangered" to "vulnerable" by the IUCN after populations recovered from 62 mature individuals in 2001 to 648 in 2022 (BBC News, June 20, 2024).

- **June 24** -- Three Super-Earth candidates discovered around HD 48948, a K-type dwarf star 55 light-years away, with one in the habitable zone (University of Exeter, June 24, 2024).

### Cybersecurity

- **June 1** -- Ticketmaster data breach affecting up to 560 million users; linked to Snowflake Inc. cloud vulnerability. Banco Santander also confirmed exposure (Reuters, June 1, 2024).

- **May 8** -- Ascension healthcare network suffered a major cyberattack disrupting operations across the US (CBS News, May 8, 2024).

## Key Themes & Emerging Patterns

1. **Escalation and de-escalation in the Middle East**: The Israel-Iran direct exchange of fire in April marked a historic first, but both sides calibrated strikes to avoid full-scale war. The Gaza conflict continued with Israel's Rafah offensive drawing international legal challenges.

2. **Electoral shockwaves**: India's BJP lost its majority, South Africa's ANC lost its 30-year majority, and the European Parliament saw a rightward shift. The US presidential campaign took a dramatic turn after Biden's poor June 27 debate performance.

3. **AI industry acceleration**: GPT-4o, Google AI Overviews, and AlphaFold 3 were released. Nvidia's market cap surged past $3.3 trillion, reflecting the AI boom's financial impact.

4. **Climate extremes**: Severe floods in Brazil, Kenya, and the Persian Gulf; extreme heat during Hajj; a G5 geomagnetic storm; and the 4th global coral bleaching event highlighted accelerating climate disruption.

5. **Space race intensifies**: China's Chang'e 6 returned the first lunar far-side samples; Boeing Starliner finally launched crew; SpaceX Starship achieved successful test flight milestones.

6. **Democratic backsliding concerns**: Georgia's "foreign agent" law, Nicaragua-Ecuador diplomatic crisis, coups in DRC and Bolivia (both failed), and growing far-right influence in European elections.

7. **Judicial developments**: Trump's conviction, the end of Chevron deference, and international legal actions against both Israel and Hamas signaled an active period for judicial bodies.
