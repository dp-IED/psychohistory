# 2023-Q1: January 1 - March 31, 2023

## GEOPOLITICS & GOVERNMENT

### January

- **Jan 1** -- Croatia adopts the euro and joins the Schengen Area, becoming the 20th Eurozone member and 27th Schengen member. The first Eurozone enlargement since Lithuania's entry in 2015 and first Schengen enlargement since Liechtenstein in 2011. (Source: European Council/Council of the EU, Jul 12, 2022; CNN, Jan 2, 2023)

- **Jan 5** -- Funeral of Pope Benedict XVI at St. Peter's Square, Vatican City, attended by ~50,000 people. (Source: France 24, Dec 31, 2022; The Guardian, Jan 5, 2023)

- **Jan 8** -- Supporters of former Brazilian president Jair Bolsonaro storm the Brazilian National Congress, Supreme Federal Court, and the Presidential Palace of Planalto, following the inauguration of Lula da Silva. (Source: El Pais, Jan 8, 2023; Agencia Brasil, Jan 10, 2023)

- **Jan 8** -- Benin holds parliamentary elections; the Progressive Union for Renewal party wins a plurality. (Source: France 24, Jan 13, 2023)

- **Jan 9** -- Juliaca massacre: Peruvian National Police fire on demonstrators in Juliaca, killing at least 18 and injuring over 100. (Source: Reuters, Jan 10, 2023)

- **Jan 10-17** -- Cold snap in Afghanistan kills 166 people and nearly 80,000 livestock. (Source: Deutsche Welle, Jan 28, 2023)

- **Jan 15** -- Yeti Airlines Flight 691 crashes near Pokhara, Nepal, killing all 72 on board. (Source: BBC News, Jan 15, 2023)

- **Jan 16** -- Tigray War: Amharan Special Forces withdraw from the Tigray Region per an AU-backed peace agreement between the Ethiopian government and the TPLF. (Source: Al Jazeera, Jan 16, 2023)

- **Jan 17** -- Nguyen Xuan Phuc resigns as President of Vietnam amid government scandals. (Source: Reuters, Jan 18, 2023)

- **Jan 18** -- Helicopter crash in Brovary near Kyiv, Ukraine, kills 14 including Ukrainian Interior Minister Denys Monastyrsky. (Source: BBC News, Jan 18, 2023; The Guardian, Jan 18, 2023)

- **Jan 20** -- Trinidad and Tobago elects Christine Kangaloo as president in a 48-22 vote. (Source: Trinidad and Tobago Guardian, Jan 21, 2023)

- **Jan 21** -- Burkina Faso requests French forces to withdraw, suspending a military accord allowing French troops. (Source: Reuters, Jan 22, 2023)

- **Jan 21** -- Tigray War: Eritrean forces withdraw from Shire and other major towns in Tigray. (Source: Reuters, Jan 22, 2023)

- **Jan 25** -- Chris Hipkins succeeds Jacinda Ardern as Prime Minister of New Zealand, six days after her resignation. (Source: The Guardian, Jan 22, 2023; RNZ, Jan 19, 2023)

- **Jan 27-28** -- Petr Pavel wins the second round of the Czech presidential election. (Source: CT24, Jan 29, 2023)

- **Jan 27** -- Israeli military raid in Jenin kills nine Palestinians, followed by incendiary balloon launches and Israeli airstrikes; seven Jewish civilians killed in a synagogue attack in Neve Yaakov. (Source: BBC News, Jan 27, 2023)

- **Jan 30** -- Jamaat-ul-Ahrar suicide bombing at a mosque in Peshawar, Pakistan, kills 85 and injures over 217. (Source: Al Jazeera, Jan 30, 2023; BBC News, Jan 30, 2023)

- **Jan 30** -- Kiribati announces it will rejoin the Pacific Islands Forum. (Source: The Guardian, Jan 30, 2023)

### February

- **Feb 1** -- Lebanese central bank devalues the pound by 90% amid the ongoing financial crisis. (Source: Reuters, Jan 31, 2023)

- **Feb 2** -- Israel and Sudan finalize an agreement to normalize relations. (Source: Reuters, Feb 3, 2023)

- **Feb 3** -- The US announces it is tracking alleged Chinese spy balloons over the Americas; one balloon drifts from Yukon to South Carolina before being shot down Feb 4; a second hovers over Colombia and Brazil. (Source: Al Jazeera, Feb 5, 2023; Business Insider, Feb 5, 2023; Reuters, Feb 13, 2023)

- **Feb 3** -- Norfolk Southern train carrying hazardous materials derails in East Palestine, Ohio. Multiple cars burn for over two days; controlled burn releases hydrogen chloride and phosgene. (Source: The Guardian, Feb 11, 2023; AP, Feb 6, 2023)

- **Feb 5** -- Nikos Christodoulides elected president of Cyprus. (Source: Reuters, Feb 12, 2023)

- **Feb 6** -- 7.8 and 7.7 Mww earthquakes strike southern/central Turkey and northern/western Syria, causing widespread destruction: over 59,000 fatalities and 121,000 injured. (Source: Cumhuriyet, Apr 22, 2023; TRT Haber, Mar 20, 2023; IFRC, Apr 21, 2023)

- **Feb 13** -- Shahabuddin Chuppu elected unopposed as president of Bangladesh. (Source: Prothom Alo, Feb 13, 2023; Bangladesh Election Commission, Feb 13, 2023)

- **Feb 14** -- European Parliament approves ban on new petrol/diesel vehicle sales in the EU from 2035. (Source: Reuters, Feb 14, 2023)

- **Feb 16** -- Russian State Duma votes to withdraw from 21 Council of Europe conventions. (Source: RadioFreeEurope/RadioLiberty, Feb 16, 2023)

- **Feb 17** -- South African Navy hosts 10-day joint military exercise with Russia and China in the Indian Ocean. (Source: BBC News, Feb 17, 2023)

- **Feb 19** -- African Union announces plans for a peace conference to address Libyan instability. (Source: France 24, Feb 19, 2023)

- **Feb 21** -- Vladimir Putin announces Russia is suspending participation in the New START nuclear arms reduction treaty with the US. (Source: BBC News, Feb 21, 2023)

- **Feb 23** -- Oman opens airspace to Israeli airlines for the first time. (Source: Haaretz, Feb 23, 2023)

- **Feb 25** -- Bola Tinubu elected president of Nigeria, defeating Atiku Abubakar and Peter Obi. (Source: The Punch, Mar 1, 2023)

- **Feb 27** -- UK and EU reach agreement (Windsor Framework) on modifications to the Northern Ireland Protocol. (Source: Sky News, Feb 28, 2023)

- **Feb 28** -- Train crash in Thessaly, Greece kills 57 and injures dozens, sparking nationwide protests and strikes over railway conditions. (Source: BBC News, Mar 1, 2023; Al Jazeera, Mar 5, 2023)

### March

- **Mar 2** -- Vo Van Thuong declared new president of Vietnam by the National Assembly with 98.38% of the vote. (Source: Wikipedia citing Vietnamese parliament)

- **Mar 4** -- UN member states agree on a legal framework for the High Seas Treaty, aiming to protect 30% of the world's oceans by 2030. (Source: Wikipedia)

- **Mar 4** -- Burundi deploys 100 troops to DRC to fight M23 and other militias. (Source: Wikipedia)

- **Mar 5** -- Estonian parliamentary election held; two centre-right liberal parties gain absolute majority for the first time. (Source: Wikipedia)

- **Mar 8** -- Allied Democratic Forces jihadist insurgents kill ~35 people with machetes in Mukondi, North Kivu, DRC. (Source: Wikipedia)

- **Mar 10** -- Xi Jinping unanimously re-elected President of China for an unprecedented third term by the National People's Congress. (Source: Wikipedia)

- **Mar 10** -- Iran and Saudi Arabia agree to resume diplomatic relations (severed in 2016) at talks mediated by China. (Source: Wikipedia)

- **Mar 10** -- Angola announces troop deployment to DRC following failed ceasefire with M23 rebels. (Source: Wikipedia)

- **Mar 17** -- International Criminal Court issues arrest warrant for Vladimir Putin and Maria Lvova-Belova for war crimes related to deportation of children from Ukraine -- first ICC warrant against a UN Security Council permanent member leader. (Source: Wikipedia)

- **Mar 19** -- Honduras switches diplomatic recognition of "China" from the Republic of China (Taiwan) to the People's Republic of China. (Source: Wikipedia)

- **Mar 26** -- Large-scale spontaneous protests across Israel after PM Netanyahu fires his defense minister, who criticized the government's judicial overhaul plan. (Source: Wikipedia)

- **Mar 29** -- Brazil and China sign agreement to trade in their own currencies, ceasing US dollar as intermediary. (Source: Wikipedia)

- **Mar 29** -- Burkina Faso resumes diplomatic relations with North Korea after suspending them in 2017. (Source: Wikipedia)

- **Mar 30** -- International Court of Justice rules US violated its Treaty of Amity with Iran by allowing domestic courts to freeze Iranian company assets. (Source: Wikipedia)

## TECHNOLOGY & AI

- **Jan 11** -- NASA's James Webb Space Telescope discovers LHS 475 b, an Earth-like exoplanet -- the first exoplanet observed by JWST. (Source: NASA press release, Jan 11, 2023)

- **Jan 11** -- Energy teleportation demonstrated for the first time using an IBM quantum computer. (Source: research paper; news reports, Jan 11, 2023)

- **Jan 16** -- NASA announces preliminary considerations for future space telescope programs including the Habitable Worlds Observatory. (Source: NASA, Jan 16, 2023)

- **Jan ~20** -- Google's Med-PaLM AI achieves 67.6% accuracy on US Medical License Exam questions. (Source: preprint, Dec 26, 2022; news reports Jan 20, 2023)

- **Feb 8** -- First direct transfer of qubits between quantum computer microchips demonstrated with 99.999993% accuracy. (Source: research publication, Feb 8, 2023)

- **Mar 14** -- OpenAI launches GPT-4, a multimodal large language model capable of processing images and up to 25,000 words. (Source: OpenAI, Mar 14, 2023)

- **Mar ~7** -- Google reveals PaLM-E, an embodied multimodal language model with 562 billion parameters. (Source: Google, Mar 7, 2023)

- **Mar 21** -- Google releases chatbot Bard as a competitor to ChatGPT. (Source: Google, Mar 21, 2023)

- **Mar 22** -- An open letter "Pause Giant AI Experiments" by the Future of Life Institute calls for a 6-month moratorium on training AI systems more powerful than GPT-4, citing "profound risks to society and humanity." Signed by thousands including Elon Musk, Steve Wozniak, and Yoshua Bengio. (Source: Future of Life Institute, Mar 22, 2023)

- **Mar 22** -- Nvidia demonstrates editing of NeRF scenes using natural language commands. (Source: Nvidia, Mar 22, 2023)

## SCIENCE & ENVIRONMENT

- **Jan 3** -- Study shows molecular mechanisms underlying health benefits of intermittent fasting: changes to gene expression in ~80% of mouse genes across at least one tissue. (Source: research publication, Jan 3, 2023)

- **Jan 5** -- Scientists discover a previously unknown meningeal layer in brain anatomy (SLYM), likely playing roles in CSF functions, immune surveillance, and brain diseases. (Source: research publication, Jan 5, 2023)

- **Jan 5** -- Archaeologists report that notational signs from ~37,000 years ago in caves, conveying calendaric meaning about animal behavior, are the first known proto-writing in history. (Source: research publication, Jan 5, 2023)

- **Jan 6** -- Study concludes retroviruses in the human genome can become awakened from dormant states and contribute to aging. (Source: research publication, Jan 6, 2023)

- **Jan 9** -- Researchers demonstrate Stentrode, an open-brain-surgery-free brain implant that records brain activity from a blood vessel, enabling computer operation. (Source: research publication, Jan 9, 2023)

- **Jan 11** -- Cellular bioengineers report development of nonreplicating bacterial 'cyborg cells' using a synthetic hydrogel polymer network. (Source: research publication, Jan 11, 2023)

- **Jan 12** -- David Sinclair-led team shows how DNA breaks drive epigenetic change and how loss of epigenetic information is a cause of aging in mammals, demonstrating ability to drive aging forward and reverse in mice. (Source: research publication, Jan 12, 2023)

- **Jan 12** -- Updated "hallmarks of aging" published a decade after the original framework. (Source: review, Jan 12, 2023)

- **Jan 16** -- Researchers demonstrate redirection of lightning with lasers for the first time. (Source: research publication, Jan 16, 2023)

- **Jan 18** -- Investigation reveals ~90% of rainforest offset credits under the Verified Carbon Standard are likely "phantom credits." (Source: news reports, Jan 18, 2023)

- **Feb 4** -- Cyclone Freddy forms in the Indian Ocean; it will become the longest-lasting tropical cyclone in recorded history, ultimately causing over 1,400 deaths across southeastern Africa. (Source: Washington Post, Mar 7, 2023)

- **Feb 6** -- A previously unknown cell mechanism involved in aging is discovered, explaining how cells 'remember' their identity when dividing (epigenetic memory). (Source: research publication, Feb 6, 2023)

- **Feb 6** -- Astronomers announce the discovery of 12 additional moons of Jupiter. (Source: research announcement, Feb 6, 2023)

- **Feb 8** -- Dwarf planet Quaoar found to have a ring system, observed by CHEOPS space telescope. (Source: research publication, Feb 8, 2023)

- **Feb 8** -- Scientists propose mining lunar soil and launching it toward the Sun to form a space sunshade against global warming. (Source: research publication, Feb 8, 2023)

- **Feb 9** -- Results of CALERIE, the first longevity caloric restriction trial, show two years of CR slowed the pace of aging as measured by one of three aging clocks. (Source: research publication, Feb 9, 2023)

- **Feb 14** -- New record low Antarctic sea ice extent reported by the US National Snow and Ice Data Center, beating the previous record set a year earlier. (Source: NSIDC, Feb 14, 2023)

- **Feb 15** -- Cosmologists report results suggesting black holes are the astrophysical origin of dark energy, potentially solving a major cosmological question and avoiding black hole singularities. (Source: research publications, Feb 15, 2023)

- **Feb 16** -- Effective new method for carbon dioxide removal from the ocean described, deployable via ships, offshore platforms, or aquaculture farms. (Source: research publication, Feb 16, 2023)

- **Mar 1** -- 'Brainoware' (organoid intelligence) demonstrated to solve computational tasks via a preprint, with implications for bioethics and AI limits. (Source: preprint, Mar 1, 2023)

- **Mar 1** -- DART mission results published: first test of asteroidal planetary defense. (Source: multiple papers, Mar 1, 2023)

- **Mar 8** -- First global precise time series of ocean surface plastic pollution released: 82-358 trillion particles weighing 1.1-4.9 million tonnes, with rapid increase since 2005. (Source: research publication, Mar 8, 2023)

- **Mar 10** -- Complete connectome of a fruit fly larva brain mapped: all 3,016 neurons and 548,000 synapses. (Source: research publication, Mar 10, 2023)

- **Mar 14** -- First clear evidence of active volcanism on Venus presented, based on reanalysis of Magellan spacecraft images. (Source: research publication, Mar 14, 2023)

- **Mar 15** -- Structure of first human olfactory receptor (OR51E2) elucidated. (Source: research publication, Mar 15, 2023)

- **Mar 15** -- Offspring from two male mice demonstrated via skin cells differentiated into oocytes. (Source: research publication, Mar 15, 2023)

- **Mar 16** -- First Global Biolabs Report documents rapid rise of high-risk pathogen labs, with BSL-4 facilities doubling within a decade, raising pandemic prevention concerns. (Source: Global Biolabs Report, Mar 16, 2023)

- **Mar 20** -- IPCC releases the synthesis report of its Sixth Assessment Report (AR6) on climate change, summarizing the state of knowledge with assessed confidence levels. (Source: IPCC, Mar 20, 2023)

- **Mar 21** -- Analysis of samples from near-Earth asteroid Ryugu reveals presence of uracil, one of four RNA nucleobases, supporting theories about the origins of life. (Source: research publication, Mar 21, 2023)

- **Mar 22** -- Study confirms 'Oumuamua was a comet after all, with acceleration due to molecular hydrogen release from H2O-rich ice. (Source: research publication, Mar 22, 2023)

## CROSS-DOMAIN PATTERNS & EMERGING TRENDS

1. **AI Boom Intensifies**: ChatGPT's explosive adoption triggers an industry-wide race -- Google releases Bard, OpenAI launches GPT-4, and an open letter calling for a 6-month pause on giant AI experiments garners global attention. The debate over AI safety, regulation, and societal impact reaches mainstream consciousness.

2. **Banking Turmoil**: Silicon Valley Bank's failure (Mar 10) and the emergency UBS-Credit Suisse merger (Mar 19) mark the most serious banking crisis since 2008, driven by interest rate hikes from central banks fighting inflation.

3. **Post-Earthquake Geopolitical Shifts**: China brokers the Iran-Saudi Arabia rapprochement (Mar 10) -- a diplomatic coup that reshapes Middle Eastern dynamics. Xi Jinping begins his unprecedented third term.

4. **Climate Science Consolidation**: The IPCC AR6 synthesis report (Mar 20), record Antarctic sea ice minimum (Feb 14), and evidence of active volcanism on Venus (Mar 15) mark Q1 as a significant period for climate and planetary science.

5. **Central Banks' Inflation Fight**: Coordinated interest rate hikes by the Fed, ECB, and Bank of England (Feb 2) signal a continued aggressive tightening cycle, with ripple effects across global markets.

6. **Russia-Ukraine War Enters Second Year**: The conflict grinds on with the ICC issuing an arrest warrant for Putin (Mar 17) -- an unprecedented step against a permanent UN Security Council member leader. Russia suspends New START treaty participation (Feb 21).

7. **Aging Research Breakthroughs**: Multiple high-profile studies on epigenetic reprogramming, caloric restriction, and cellular aging mechanisms suggest the field is accelerating rapidly, with potential long-term implications for healthcare.
