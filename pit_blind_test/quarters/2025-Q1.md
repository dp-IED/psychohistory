# 2025-Q1 Summary: January 1 – March 31, 2025

## GEOPOLITICS & GOVERNMENT

### Trump Returns to Power
- **January 6** – Congress certifies Donald Trump's electoral victory in a joint session (Washington Post, Jan 6, 2025).
- **January 20** – Donald Trump is inaugurated as the 47th President of the United States in a ceremony moved to the Capitol Rotunda due to extreme cold. The inauguration is unprecedented for hosting foreign leaders including Argentine President Javier Milei, Italian PM Giorgia Meloni, and a Chinese delegation led by Vice President Han Zheng — the first Chinese official to attend a US presidential inauguration (NYT, Jan 20, 2025; AP, Jan 20, 2025).

### US-Russia-Ukraine
- **February 18** – After a four-hour summit in Saudi Arabia, Vladimir Putin announces that Russia and the US have agreed to restore diplomatic relations, with both sides describing it as a first step toward Ukraine war talks (Reuters, Feb 18, 2025).
- **February 28** – A tense Oval Office meeting between Trump, VP JD Vance, and Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy sees Trump accuse Zelensky of "gambling with World War Three." The meeting raises serious doubts about continued US support for Ukraine (BBC, Feb 28, 2025).
- **March 3** – The Trump administration pauses military aid to Ukraine following the February 28 confrontation (CNN, Mar 3, 2025).

### Middle East
- **January 9** – Lebanon's parliament elects army chief Joseph Aoun as president, ending a power vacuum that lasted over two years (France 24, Jan 9, 2025).
- **January 15** – Israel and Hamas approve a ceasefire agreement to end the 15-month Gaza war, with provisions for hostage/prisoner exchanges and humanitarian aid. The ceasefire takes effect January 19 (Reuters, Jan 15, 2025).
- **February 4** – Trump announces that the US will "take over" the Gaza Strip and that the US military will lead reconstruction, in what is described as a shock announcement during a joint press conference with Netanyahu (AP/Reuters/Guardian, Feb 4-5, 2025).
- **March 18** – Israel launches widespread aerial bombardments on the Gaza Strip, killing at least 591 people and effectively ending the January ceasefire agreement (Al Jazeera, Mar 18, 2025).

### Europe
- **January 1** – Poland assumes the Presidency of the Council of the European Union, succeeding Hungary (Polsat News, Jan 3, 2025). Bulgaria and Romania fully join the Schengen Area, lifting land border controls (Euronews, Jan 1, 2025).
- **January 1** – Ukraine halts Russian gas transit through its territory after a five-year deal expires (Al Jazeera, Jan 1, 2025). Ukraine also becomes a state party to the International Criminal Court (Ukrinform, Jan 1, 2025).
- **February 9** – The Baltic states (Lithuania, Latvia, Estonia) synchronize their power grids with continental Europe, completing their disconnection from Russia's IPS/UPS grid (TVN24, Feb 7, 2025).
- **February 12** – Klaus Iohannis resigns as President of Romania, the first Romanian president to do so post-revolution (Observator News, Feb 12, 2025). Konstantinos Tasoulas is elected President of Greece after four rounds of parliamentary voting (Ekathimerini, Feb 12, 2025).
- **February 12** – The Armenian parliament unanimously approves a bill to begin Armenia's accession process to the European Union (Armenpress/Reuters, Feb 12, 2025).
- **February 19** – Croatia completes its accession to the European Economic Area (EFTA, Feb 19, 2025).
- **February 23** – Germany's snap federal election: the centre-right CDU/CSU becomes the largest party, while the far-right Alternative for Germany (AfD) makes major gains to finish second (Onet, Feb 24, 2025).
- **March 16** – North Macedonia nightclub fire in Kočani kills 59 (later revised to 63) and injures 155 (BBC, Mar 16, 2025).

### East Asia & Pacific
- **January 15** – Arrested South Korean President Yoon Suk Yeol is successfully taken into custody after a second police attempt, following his December martial law declaration and impeachment (BBC, Jan 15, 2025).
- **February 27 – March 1** – Imprisoned PKK leader Abdullah Öcalan calls for the organization's dissolution and disarmament; the PKK declares a ceasefire effective March 1 (France 24/ BBC, Feb 27-Mar 1, 2025).
- **March 11** – Former Philippine President Rodrigo Duterte is arrested on an ICC warrant for crimes against humanity related to his war on drugs (Philippine News Agency, Mar 11, 2025).
- **March 11** – Center-right Democrats become the largest party in Greenland's general election, defeating the incumbent Inuit Ataqatigiit (RFE/RL, Mar 9, 2025).
- **March 19** – Nationwide protests erupt across Turkey following the arrest of Istanbul Mayor Ekrem Imamoglu on corruption and terrorism charges (BBC, Mar 19, 2025).

### Africa
- **January 8** – 18 gunmen and a soldier die in an attack on Chad's presidential palace in N'Djamena (AP, Jan 9, 2025).
- **February 15** – Djibouti's Foreign Minister Mahamoud Ali Youssouf is elected Chairperson of the African Union Commission (Reuters, Feb 15, 2025).
- **March 5** – Sudan files an application against the UAE at the International Court of Justice, alleging violations of the Genocide Convention through UAE support for the RSF in Darfur (BBC, Mar 5, 2025).
- **March 8** – Syrian transitional government security forces kill more than 1,000 people in a crackdown in the Alawite region, described as the country's worst violence in years (Reuters/BBC, Mar 8-9, 2025).
- **March 11** – Balochistan Liberation Army militants hijack the Jaffar Express in Pakistan, taking 450 hostages (Hindustan Times, Mar 11, 2025).
- **March 12** – Belize's incumbent People's United Party wins a second term in a landslide general election (CBC, Mar 13, 2025).
- **March 18** – Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud survives an assassination attempt by al-Shabaab (AP, Mar 18, 2025).

### Americas
- **January 12** – Croatian President Zoran Milanovic is re-elected for a second term (Eunews, Jan 13, 2025).
- **February 18** – Nicaragua becomes a diarchy following constitutional reform, with spouses Daniel Ortega and Rosario Murillo as co-presidents (Reuters, AP, Feb 18-27, 2025).
- **March 12** – Belize general election (see above).
- **March 16** – Keith Rowley resigns as Prime Minister of Trinidad and Tobago after nine years; succeeded by Stuart Young (Jamaica Observer, Feb 26, 2025).

### South Asia
- **March 11** – Balochistan Liberation Army militants blow up railway tracks and hijack the Jaffar Express train from Quetta to Peshawar, taking 450 hostages including security personnel and civilians. A multi-day siege ensues (Hindustan Times, Mar 11, 2025).

## ECONOMICS & TRADE

### Trump Tariffs and Trade War
- **January 20** – Upon taking office, Trump begins implementing aggressive tariff policies. Overall US effective tariff rate rises from 2.5% to an estimated 27% by April 2025 — the highest level in over a century (Axios, Jun 5, 2025; Politico, Apr 10, 2025).
- **February 4** – China announces retaliatory export controls and increased tariffs on American imports after Trump's new tariffs on Chinese goods (CNN, Feb 4, 2025).
- **February 11** – Trump orders a probe into copper imports and restores/expands Section 232 tariffs on steel and aluminum, raising aluminum tariff from 10% to 25% and eliminating all exemptions (White House fact sheet, Feb 11, 2025; Reuters, Feb 11, 2025).
- **March 2025** – 25% tariffs on all steel and aluminum imports take effect. Canada, the largest supplier, is most affected (CFR, Feb 14, 2025). Trump also imposes broad tariffs on Canada and Mexico, threatening the deeply integrated North American auto supply chain, though USMCA-compliant vehicles receive a temporary delay (NPR, Mar 4, 2025; WSJ, Mar 6, 2025).
- **March 2025** – The US auto industry warns of significant price increases. Major manufacturers lobby for exemptions (NPR, Mar 3, 2025).

### Markets & Business
- **January 27** – DeepSeek's AI chatbot launch triggers a historic $600 billion single-day drop in Nvidia's market value, the largest single-company decline in US stock market history (Sky News, Jan 28, 2025).

## TECHNOLOGY & SCIENCE

### Artificial Intelligence
- **January 20** – Chinese AI startup DeepSeek launches its R1 model and chatbot, built at a fraction of the cost of Western competitors (reportedly ~$6M vs. $100M+ for GPT-4). By January 27, DeepSeek surpasses ChatGPT as the most downloaded free app on the iOS App Store in the US (NYT, Jan 23-27, 2025; CNBC, Jan 27, 2025).
- **January 27-28** – DeepSeek's breakthrough triggers what analysts call a "Sputnik moment" for the US in AI. The model's cost-efficiency, open-weight approach, and use of weaker export-restricted chips raises questions about the value of massive AI infrastructure spending (The Guardian, Jan 28, 2025; New Yorker, Feb 3, 2025; NPR, Jan 28, 2025).
- **March 24** – DeepSeek releases DeepSeek-V3-0324 under the MIT License, capable of running efficiently on consumer hardware (VentureBeat, Mar 24, 2025).

### Space
- **January 16** – Blue Origin's heavy-lift New Glenn rocket launches successfully on its first attempt, though its first-stage booster fails to land as intended (Scientific American/AP, Jan 16, 2025).
- **March 2** – Firefly Aerospace becomes the first commercial company to successfully land on the Moon with no technical issues, via Blue Ghost Mission 1 (The Guardian/Al Jazeera, Mar 2, 2025).

### De-Extinction & Biotechnology
- **January 15** – Colossal Biosciences and the University of Melbourne create the world's first artificial marsupial womb as part of their thylacine (Tasmanian tiger) de-extinction project (IFLScience, Jan 15, 2025).
- **March 4** – Colossal Biosciences creates "woolly mice" using multiplex gene editing — a step toward de-extinction of the woolly mammoth. The mice were born in October 2024 but disclosed now (bioRxiv/Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory, Mar 4, 2025).

### Archaeology
- **February 18** – Egypt announces the discovery of Thutmose II's tomb by a joint British-Egyptian team — the first royal pharaonic tomb discovered since Tutankhamun's in 1922 (BBC/CBS News, Feb 18-19, 2025).

## DISASTERS & CLIMATE

### Natural Disasters
- **January 7** – A 7.1 magnitude earthquake strikes Tibet, China, killing at least 126 and injuring 338 (BBC, Jan 7, 2025).
- **January 7-10+** – The January 2025 Southern California wildfires become the most destructive in Los Angeles history. Fueled by drought and strong Santa Ana winds, the fires destroy over 13,000 structures including most of Sunset Boulevard, kill at least 29, and force 180,000 to evacuate (BBC/NBC News, Jan 7-10, 2025).
- **January 13** – Comet C/2024 G3 (ATLAS) reaches perihelion and is dubbed the "Great Comet of 2025" (Space.com, Jan 27, 2025).
- **March 21** – South Korea experiences one of its worst wildfires in modern history, destroying over 87,000 hectares in South Gyeongsang Province and killing at least 32 (BBC, Mar 26, 2025).
- **March 28** – A 7.7-magnitude earthquake strikes Myanmar, ultimately resulting in 5,413 killed and 11,402 injured, with extensive damage including a collapsed skyscraper under construction in Bangkok (Reuters/USGS/BBC, Mar 28, 2025).

### Climate
- **January 10** – The Copernicus Climate Change Service reports that 2024 was the world's hottest year on record, and the first calendar year to exceed 1.5°C of global warming above pre-industrial levels (BBC, Jan 10, 2025).

### Industrial Accidents & Fires
- **January 11** – A gas station explosion in Al Bayda, Yemen, kills 40 (Yemen Shabab Net, Jan 18, 2025).
- **January 18** – A fuel tanker explosion near Suleja, Niger State, Nigeria, kills 98 (Barron's/AP, Jan 20, 2025).
- **January 21** – A fire at the Grand Kartal Hotel in Kartalkaya, Turkey, kills 78 and injures 51 (BBC, Jan 21, 2025).
- **February 10** – A bus falls off a bridge over the Las Vacas River in Guatemala City, killing at least 55 (France 24, Feb 10, 2025).

### Aviation Accidents
- **January 1** – New Year's Day attacks include a truck-ramming and shooting in New Orleans (15 dead) and a mass shooting in Cetinje, Montenegro (13 dead) (BBC/Guardian, Jan 1, 2025).
- **January 29** – A mid-air collision between a Bombardier CRJ700 and a military helicopter over the Potomac River near Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport (BBC, Jan 29, 2025).
- **February 25** – A Sudanese Air Force Antonov An-26 crashes in a residential area near Wadi Seidna Air Base, killing all 17 onboard and 29 on the ground (Aviation Safety Network, Feb 26, 2025).

### Other
- **February 4** – A mass shooting at an adult education centre in Orebro, Sweden, kills 11 — the deadliest mass shooting in Swedish history (Guardian/BBC, Feb 4, 2025).

## EMERGING PATTERNS & TURNING POINTS

1. **American trade realignment** – Trump's return initiates the most aggressive US tariff regime in a century, with the effective tariff rate rising from 2.5% to ~27% by end of Q1. The China-US trade war reignites, Canada and Mexico are targeted, supply chains are disrupted.

2. **AI cost disruption** – DeepSeek's January launch upends the AI industry paradigm that assumed massive compute investment was the only path to frontier models. The "$6M model" sends shockwaves through markets and policy circles, triggering a "Sputnik moment" narrative about US-China AI competition.

3. **Gaza ceasefire collapse** – The January ceasefire between Israel and Hamas lasts barely two months before Israel resumes major operations on March 18, killing over 590 in a single day.

4. **Ukraine uncertainty** – The Trump administration's hostile meeting with Zelenskyy on February 28 and subsequent aid pause (March 3) signal a fundamental shift in US policy toward the Russia-Ukraine war.

5. **Private space milestone** – Firefly's Blue Ghost becomes the first commercially flawless Moon landing, while Blue Origin's New Glenn reaches orbit on first attempt — both signaling maturation of private space capabilities.

6. **PKK ceasefire call** – Ocalan's February 27 call for PKK dissolution and the subsequent ceasefire marks the most significant development in the decades-long Turkish-Kurdish conflict in years.

7. **Climate records** – 2024 confirmed as the first calendar year above 1.5°C, as climate-related disasters (LA fires, Myanmar earthquake, South Korea wildfires) dominate the quarter.

8. **European political shift** – Germany's election brings CDU/CSU to power with a strengthened far-right AfD; snap elections and leadership changes across Greece, Romania, and elsewhere signal continued political volatility in Europe.
