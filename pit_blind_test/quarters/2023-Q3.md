# Q3 2023: July - September 2023

## Summary

The third quarter of 2023 was defined by converging planetary-scale crises and breakthroughs. Record-shattering global temperatures, devastating natural disasters, and a cascade of geopolitical ruptures unfolded alongside historic space achievements, the consolidation of generative AI into mainstream use, and coordinated labor action reshaping the entertainment industry. The quarter saw the hottest months ever recorded, a major war in Nagorno-Karabakh, a coup in Gabon, the first Indian Moon landing, and the beginning of what would become the largest Hollywood labor stoppage in decades.

---

## GEOPOLITICS & INTERNATIONAL RELATIONS

### July

**July 3-5: Israel's largest West Bank incursion in two decades.** The IDF launched "Operation Home and Garden" into the Jenin refugee camp, deploying ground forces and armed drones. At least 13 Palestinians were killed and over 100 injured, with thousands displaced. A Tel Aviv vehicle-ramming attack the following day injured nine; Hamas claimed it as retaliation. (Al Jazeera, July 3-5, 2023)

**July 4: Iran joins the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation** as its ninth full member, deepening Eurasian institutional integration. (Reuters, July 4, 2023)

**July 7: Netherlands government collapses.** The fourth Rutte cabinet fell after failing to reach consensus on asylum policy. Mark Rutte announced his retirement from politics three days later. (BBC News, July 7, 2023)

**July 9: New Zealand signs free trade agreement with the EU**, expected to increase EU exports by EUR 4.5 billion annually. (AFP via RTE, July 9, 2023)

**July 10: China and Solomon Islands sign police cooperation agreement**, deepening Beijing's footprint in the South Pacific during PM Manasseh Sogavare's visit. (Reuters, July 10, 2023)

**July 10: EU and US sign new Data Privacy Framework**, replacing the invalidated Privacy Shield. Civil liberties group NOYB immediately announced plans to challenge it. (Reuters, July 10, 2023)

**July 11-12: NATO Vilnius Summit.** Turkey's Erdogan agreed to forward Sweden's accession protocol to parliament. G7 unveiled a long-term security framework for Ukraine encompassing bilateral talks, hardware, training, and intelligence-sharing. (AP, July 11, 2023; Politico EU, July 12, 2023)

**July 17: Black Sea Grain Initiative expires.** Russia refused to renew the deal allowing safe passage of Ukrainian grain exports, which had been in effect since July 2022. (Reuters, July 17, 2023)

**July 18: US soldier Travis King flees across inter-Korean border into North Korea.** The soldier, facing disciplinary action, was believed to be in North Korean custody. The USS Kentucky, a nuclear-capable missile submarine, was simultaneously deployed to Busan for the first time since 1981. (Reuters, July 18, 2023; Navy Times, July 18, 2023)

**July 19: Israel recognizes Moroccan sovereignty over Western Sahara**, deepening normalization ties. (Jerusalem Post, July 17, 2023)

### August

**August 18: US-Japan-South Korea trilateral pact signed** at Camp David -- the Camp David Principles -- formalizing defense cooperation and joint response mechanisms among the three Pacific allies. (Wikipedia, August 18, 2023)

**August 20: Bernardo Arevalo elected president of Guatemala.** The anti-corruption candidate won 58% of the vote in the second round. The election had faced judicial interference attempts, with Arevalo's Semilla party briefly suspended. (AP, August 20, 2023)

**August 22: Thaksin Shinawatra returns to Thailand after 15 years of political exile**, arriving on the same day his Pheu Thai party's candidate, Srettha Thavisin, was elected prime minister. (Wikipedia, August 22, 2023)

**August 30: Military coup in Gabon.** Hours after the announcement of President Ali Bongo Ondimba's re-election, military officers launched a coup, creating the Committee for the Transition and Restoration of Institutions. This ended the Bongo family's 56-year hold on power. (Wikipedia, August 30, 2023)

### September

**September 9: African Union joins G20 as permanent member** during the 18th G20 summit in New Delhi, a significant structural reform of the forum. (Wikipedia, September 9, 2023)

**September 10: Storm Daniel devastates Libya.** The Mediterranean cyclone caused two dams to collapse near Derna, destroying a quarter of the city. At least 5,000 dead with 10,000-100,000 reported missing. (Wikipedia, September 10, 2023)

**September 19: Azerbaijan launches military offensive in Nagorno-Karabakh.** The 24-hour campaign ended with a swift Azerbaijani victory. Artsakh announced dissolution of its government institutions, and over 100,000 ethnic Armenians fled the enclave. (Wikipedia, September 19, 2023)

**September 21: Rupert Murdoch retires**, passing control of News Corp and Fox to his son Lachlan. Murdoch had led the media empire for over seven decades. (Wikipedia, September 21, 2023)

**September 24: France ends military presence in Niger.** President Macron announced the withdrawal of French troops and recall of the ambassador, following the July 26 coup that ousted President Mohamed Bazoum. (Wikipedia, September 24, 2023)

**September 30: Slovak parliamentary election.** Smer-SD, led by former PM Robert Fico, won a plurality on a pro-Russia, anti-American platform, signaling a potential shift in Central European alignment. (Wikipedia, September 30, 2023)

## CLIMATE & ENVIRONMENT

**July 4: Global average temperature record shattered.** Data from the US National Centers for Environmental Prediction showed the global average temperature reached 17.18 degrees C, the highest since 1979. July would later be confirmed as the hottest month on record for globally averaged surface air temperatures by a margin of 0.3 degrees C. (Washington Post, July 4, 2023; Reuters, July 4, 2023)

**July 10: WMO reports hottest week on record** at the beginning of July, attributing it to climate change and early El Nino effects. (AFP via RFI, July 10, 2023)

**July 10: Study published in Nature Medicine estimates 61,672 deaths in Europe from 2022 heat waves.** (AP, July 10, 2023)

**August 1: World's oceans reach new record high temperature of 20.96 degrees C**, exceeding the previous 2016 record. (Wikipedia, August 1, 2023)

**August 8: Hawaii wildfires -- Maui disaster.** A series of wildfires burned 17,000 acres on Maui, killing at least 101 people in the deadliest US wildfire in over a century. The town of Lahaina was largely destroyed. (Wikipedia, August 8, 2023)

**August 16-21: Hurricane Hilary.** The Category 4 hurricane struck Baja California and caused record flooding in Southern California, a rare direct hit for the region. (Wikipedia, August 16-21, 2023)

**August 21: Canadian wildfires force evacuation of 68% of the Northwest Territories**, including the capital Yellowknife. (Wikipedia, August 21, 2023)

**September 8: Morocco earthquake.** A 6.9 magnitude quake struck Marrakesh-Safi province, killing at least 2,960 people and damaging historic buildings including parts of Marrakesh's old city. (Wikipedia, September 8, 2023)

## ECONOMICS & BUSINESS

**July 3: Indian oil refiners begin paying for Russian oil imports in Chinese yuan**, bypassing the US dollar amid Western sanctions on Russia. (Reuters, July 3, 2023)

**July 7: China fines Ant Group nearly $1 billion and Tencent $415 million** for financial regulatory violations, signaling a shift toward routine enforcement after years of regulatory crackdown. (AFP via France 24, July 7, 2023)

**July 11: US federal judge approves Microsoft's $69 billion acquisition of Activision Blizzard**, dismissing the FTC's antitrust injunction request. The largest video game acquisition in history cleared its final major US hurdle. (Reuters, July 11, 2023)

**July 12: IMF approves $3 billion bailout for Pakistan**, averting imminent sovereign default. (AP, July 12, 2023)

**July 18: Bank of Japan maintains ultra-loose monetary policy**, Governor Ueda stating the bank's overall narrative remained unchanged, even as global central banks tightened. (Reuters, July 18, 2023)

**August 10: Tapestry (parent of Coach and Kate Spade) acquires Capri Holdings (Michael Kors, Versace, Jimmy Choo)** for $8.5 billion, consolidating the US fashion luxury sector. (Wikipedia, August 10, 2023)

**September 14: ECB raises eurozone interest rates to an all-time high of 4%** amid persistent continental inflation. (Wikipedia, September 14, 2023)

## TECHNOLOGY & SCIENCE

### Space

**July 1: ESA launches Euclid space telescope** to study dark matter and dark energy from the second Lagrange point, 1.5 million km from Earth. (AFP via ABS-CBN News, July 1, 2023)

**July 5: Final Ariane 5 launch** from Kourou, French Guiana, carrying French and German military satellites, marking the end of a storied 27-year rocket program. (AFP via RFI, July 5, 2023)

**July 14: India launches Chandrayaan-3 lunar mission**, carrying the Vikram lander and Pragyan rover. (Space.com, July 14, 2023)

**August 23: Chandrayaan-3 successfully lands near the lunar south pole**, making India the fourth nation (after US, USSR, China) to soft-land on the Moon and the first to reach the south polar region. (Wikipedia, August 23, 2023)

**August 19: Russia's Luna 25 spacecraft crashes into the Moon** after a technical glitch during orbital maneuver, the country's first lunar mission in 47 years. (Wikipedia, August 19, 2023)

**September 2: ISRO launches Aditya-L1**, India's first solar observation mission. (Wikipedia, September 2, 2023)

**September 6: JAXA launches SLIM lunar lander and XRISM space telescope.** SLIM (Smart Lander for Investigating Moon) carried two mini rovers. (Wikipedia, September 6, 2023)

### Science

**July 1 - Euclid cosmology mission begins** six-year survey of dark energy. (See space above.)

**July 3 - DNA "biological camera":** Researchers demonstrated encoding and storing images as DNA using optogenetic circuits in bacteria, without new DNA synthesis. (Wikipedia, July 3, 2023)

**July 5 - First evolution experiment of synthetic minimal cells** (JCVI-syn3B) showed they regained fitness within ~2,000 generations, demonstrating "life finds a way." (Wikipedia, July 5, 2023)

**July 10 - Dynamic shell fusion demonstrated** as a feasible target for mass production of fusion energy. The highest exoplanet albedo measured (LTT 9779 b, 80% reflectivity) also reported. (Wikipedia, July 10, 2023)

**July 11 - Three possible "dark star" candidates** identified from JWST observations, dating to 320-400 million years after the Big Bang. Berkeley Earth confirmed June 2023 as warmest June since 1850. (Wikipedia, July 11, 2023)

**August 1 - Oceans hit record temperature** of 20.96 degrees C. (See Climate section.)

**August 2 - Ancient human hominin** potentially dating to 300,000 years ago discovered in China. Whale species Perucetus colossus from the Eocene shown to be Earth's heaviest-ever animal (85-340 tonnes). (Wikipedia, August 2, 2023)

**August 10 - Fermilab reports most precise measurement of muon magnetic moment**, wobbling faster than predicted by the Standard Model, hinting at new physics. (Wikipedia, August 10, 2023)

**September 2 - JWST data questions Standard Model of Cosmology**, with astrophysicists reporting tensions with the latest deep-field galaxy studies. (Wikipedia, September 2, 2023)

**September 5 - Ho'oleilana bubble structure identified**, a billion-light-year diameter feature representing the first observation of an individual baryon acoustic oscillation. (Wikipedia, September 5, 2023)

**September 6 - Largest known lithium deposit** discovered in a dormant volcano crater along the Nevada-Oregon border, estimated at 20-40 million tonnes. Specialized astrocytes mediating glutamatergic gliotransmission also discovered. (Wikipedia, September 6, 2023)

**September 11 - JWST detects CO2 and methane on K2-18b**, a potentially habitable exoplanet 8.6 times Earth's mass, suggesting a hycean world covered in oceans. (Wikipedia, September 11, 2023)

**September 14 - NASA releases first public UAP study** and appoints Mark McInerney as first Director of UAP. A new quantum coherence record of 34 milliseconds reported with a superconducting cavity. Genetically engineered marine microorganism shown to break down PET plastic in salt water. (Wikipedia, September 14, 2023)

**September 18 - LCLS-II becomes the world's most powerful X-ray laser** at SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory. (Wikipedia, September 18, 2023)

**September 20 - Oldest wooden structure, 476,000 years old**, discovered in Zambia: two interlocking logs connected by a notch, indicating advanced woodworking by early hominins predating Homo sapiens. (Wikipedia, September 20, 2023)

## SOCIETY, LABOR & CULTURE

**July 14: SAG-AFTRA joins WGA on strike** -- the first Hollywood "double strike" since 1960. The actors' union walked out over compensation, ownership of work, and generative AI protections. The WGA had been on strike since May 2. The "Oppenheimer" red carpet premiere was cancelled. (CNBC, July 13, 2023; Screen International, July 14, 2023)

**July 13: FDA approves Opill** -- the first over-the-counter birth control pill in the United States, manufactured by Perrigo. (AFP via France 24, July 13, 2023)

**July 16: Carlos Alcaraz defeats Novak Djokovic at Wimbledon** in a five-set final, winning the men's singles title and becoming the first non-Big Four player to win since 2002. Marketa Vondrousova won the women's title as the first unseeded Wimbledon champion. (USA Today, July 16, 2023; AFP via ABS-CBN News, July 15, 2023)

**July 20 - August 20: 2023 FIFA Women's World Cup** co-hosted by Australia and New Zealand. Spain defeated England 1-0 in the final on August 20 in Sydney. (Wikipedia)

**August 23: Wagner Group leader Yevgeny Prigozhin killed in plane crash** in Russia, along with founder Dmitry Utkin and eight others, two months after their abortive rebellion. (Wikipedia, August 23, 2023)

**August 31: Johannesburg building fire** kills 77 people in a building taken over by gangs and rented to squatters. (Wikipedia, August 31, 2023)

**September 8 - October 28: 2023 Rugby World Cup** in France, won by South Africa defeating New Zealand in the final for a record fourth title. (Wikipedia)

**September 25: Nagorno-Karabakh gas station explosion** kills an estimated 170 people and injures over 300, as the region's ethnic Armenian population was in the process of evacuating. (Wikipedia, September 25, 2023)

## KEY EMERGING PATTERNS

1. **Earth system signals intensify**: July 2023 as hottest month on record, unprecedented ocean temperatures, extreme weather events (Hawaii fires, Libya floods, Canadian wildfires, Hurricane Hilary) all pointed to accelerating climate breakdown.

2. **BRICS+ and multipolar institutional competition**: Iran joined SCO, African Union joined G20, and Saudi-Iran rapprochement continued -- signaling consolidation of alternative institutional frameworks outside Western-led ones.

3. **Space becomes a multi-player domain**: India's lunar south pole landing, Russia's failed Luna 25, Japan's SLIM, and the final Ariane 5 flight highlighted the widening of space access beyond the traditional US-Russia-China triad.

4. **AI regulation becomes a labor issue**: The Hollywood strikes explicitly cited generative AI as a bargaining issue, setting a precedent for AI's impact on creative labor that would echo across other industries.

5. **Coup belt across Africa**: Niger (July 26) and Gabon (August 30) coups continued the pattern of military takeovers in the Sahel and Central Africa, with French influence retreating.

6. **Nagorno-Karabakh resolved by force**: Azerbaijan's 24-hour offensive and the resulting exodus of 100,000+ ethnic Armenians effectively ended the three-decade conflict over the enclave.

7. **Global monetary tightening at its peak**: The ECB's 4% rate in September represented the highest in eurozone history, even as the Fed paused and the BOJ held ultra-loose, signaling divergence in the global tightening cycle.
