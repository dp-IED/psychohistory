**Reasoning:**
This is a near-structural-certainty market. The 0.4% YES price is actually generous — Golan's true probability is even lower.

**Electoral mathematics:** To become Prime Minister, Golan's party (The Democrats) must either (a) win a plurality of Knesset seats and form a coalition of 61+, or (b) lead a center-left bloc that collectively commands 61+ seats. Neither is mathematically plausible given current polling:
- The Democrats (Labor+Meretz merger) poll at 4-8 seats in most surveys
- The right-religious bloc (Likud + Religious Zionism + Shas + UTJ + Otzma Yehudit) consistently commands 60+ seats
- The combined center-left (Yesh Atid + National Unity + The Democrats + Labor splinters) commands ~25-35 seats
- Even with Arab party support (Ra'am, Hadash-Ta'al at ~10 seats), the center-left maxes out at ~45 seats — 16 short of a majority

**Structural right-wing dominance:** Israeli politics has undergone a generational rightward shift driven by:
1. **Security concerns**: The Second Intifada (2000-2005), Hamas takeover of Gaza (2007), multiple Gaza wars (2008-09, 2012, 2014, 2021, 2023-25), and the Iran threat have made security the dominant electoral issue
2. **Demographic trends**: Higher birth rates among religious Zionist and Haredi communities (right-wing voters) vs secular communities (center-left voters)
3. **Settlement expansion**: The settler population has grown from ~250K (2000) to ~500K+ (2024), expanding the right-wing electoral base
4. **Netanyahu's political genius**: Despite corruption indictments and the October 7 intelligence failure, Netanyahu has repeatedly survived

**Golan-specific constraints:**
- His 2016 Holocaust Remembrance Day speech remains a right-wing attack ad in perpetuity
- The "Democrats" brand carries the weight of Labor's decline
- Gantz, Lapid, and Golan have not unified into a single bloc

**The only viable path** requires ALL of: (1) Netanyahu convicted and barred from office, (2) Likud fractures in the succession fight, (3) Gantz, Lapid, and Golan form a single electoral list, (4) Arab parties join a coalition, (5) external shock discredits the right. Compounding probability well below 1%.
