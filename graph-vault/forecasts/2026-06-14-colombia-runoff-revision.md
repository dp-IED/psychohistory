# Forecast Revision: Colombia 2026 Presidential Runoff (June 21, 2026)

**Post-First-Round Revision** — Supersedes [[forecasts/2026-05-23-colombia-presidential-runoff]] (pre-first-round version)

|-----------|--------|---------------------------|------------------------|

| Others (11 candidates) | 15.4% | — | — |

Turnout: 57.89% (↑2.91pp vs 2022)

## What Changed

The first-round result fundamentally altered the foundation of the May 23 forecast:

1. **de la Espriella, not Cepeda, is the runoff frontrunner** — 2.8pp lead (43.7% vs 40.9%) is a material first-round advantage
2. **Right-wing consolidation happened in round 1**, not between rounds — de la Espriella captured ~44% of the vote directly, nearly matching the combined pre-election right-wing share
3. **Polymarket was profoundly wrong** on first-round winners — this discredits the market's pre-first-round runoff pricing and suggests systematic mispricing of Colombian elections
4. **Cepeda ceiling at ~41% validated** — well below even the vault's 46-48% estimate; the structural left-wing ceiling is real and binding

## Revised Runoff Assessment

### Advantages for de la Espriella

1. **Momentum and media narrative**: Winning 43.7% vs expectations of 15-18% creates a massive credibility and momentum advantage
2. **Historical right-wing runoff gains**: Colombian right-wing candidates consistently gain in runoffs:
   - 2014: Zuluaga +10pp (first: 29.3% → runoff: 45.0%)
   - 2018: Duque +5pp (first: 39.1% → runoff: 54.0%)
   - 2022: Hernández +11pp (first: 28.2% → runoff: 47.3%)
3. **Endorsement cascade**: Valencia (Uribista right), Fajardo (centrist), Peñalosa (center-right) voters flow to de la Espriella — potentially adding 8-12pp
4. **Turnout asymmetry**: Colombian runoff turnout drops 10-15pp, favoring older/rural/right-wing voters who are more reliable
5. **De la Espriella's ceiling**: If he consolidates the full anti-Cepeda vote (57-60% of electorate), his ceiling is ~55-60% — well above the 50% threshold

### Advantages for Cepeda

1. **Petro precedent**: Petro won the 2022 runoff despite getting 40.3% in first round (vs Hernández 28.2%). Cepeda's 40.9% is numerically similar
2. **Centrist disillusionment with de la Espriella**: De la Espriella's populist style may alienate moderate voters (Fajardo supporters) who prefer a stable center-left option over an unpredictable right-wing populist
3. **Anti-Trump parallel**: If de la Espriella is perceived as a Trump-aligned candidate, this could mobilize Democratic/Pacto base voters in a race otherwise defined by turnout
4. **Cepeda's campaign effectiveness**: The first-round result may sharpen Cepeda's message — he now runs as the underdog, which could be more effective than running as the frontrunner

## Polymarket Inefficiency Assessment

The May 23 Polymarket pricing was profoundly wrong on first-round outcomes, and its runoff pricing inherited these errors:

- Pre-first-round: de la Espriella 50.5% to win the presidency (with only 15.8% to win first round)
- This implied: de la Espriella barely makes runoff but then wins — a structurally inconsistent narrative
- Post-first-round: de la Espriella ~65-70% — a more rational price reflecting actual first-round performance
- Key insight: The market initially underweighted the probability of right-wing consolidation in the first round, leading to mispricing of both first-round and runoff outcomes

## Calibration Note

The May 23 vault forecast (Cepeda 0.35-0.40) was based on pre-first-round polling showing Cepeda with a 10-15pp first-round lead. The actual margin was a -2.8pp deficit. The revised estimate (Cepeda 0.30-0.35) reflects:

- Downward adjustment for first-round deficit (was expected to have lead)
- BUT upward adjustment because Cepeda's 40.9% in a fragmented field is stronger than it would be in a consolidated field (Petro 2022 parallel)
- Net: modest reduction from 0.35-0.40 to 0.30-0.35 for Cepeda

The larger lesson: the Vault's first-round margin → runoff probability mapping table was correct in direction but lacked scenarios where Cepeda loses the first round entirely.
