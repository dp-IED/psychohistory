## GEOPOLITICS & CONFLICT

### US-Iran Peace Deal Implementation

The historic US-Iran permanent peace deal announced on June 14, 2026 enters its implementation phase in Q3. Key questions:
- **Sanctions relief sequencing**: Trump's "let the oil flow!" statement signals rapid sanctions rollback, but the bureaucratic timeline for CAATSA waiver implementation is uncertain
- **IRGC status**: The IRGC remains a US-designated terrorist organization — resolving this contradiction is the hardest single implementation challenge
- **Iranian succession**: With Khamenei deceased (Feb 28, 2026 assassination), the internal power struggle between IRGC and civilian factions will determine whether the deal holds
- **Israel's response**: Israel participated in the Feb 28 attack but was not party to the peace deal. Israeli security establishment views on the deal are critical to its durability

**US-Iran diplomatic meeting by July 31**: The post-peace-deal diplomatic sequence requires at least one high-level meeting for implementation logistics (nuclear verification, sanctions relief, diplomatic recognition). ### Sweden General Election (September 13)

Sweden's fixed-term general election on September 13 will determine the next government. Prime Minister Ulf Kristersson (Moderate Party) seeks re-election with a fragile center-right coalition relying on Sweden Democrats (SD) confidence-and-supply. Left bloc led by Magdalena Andersson (Social Democrats) polls slightly ahead (~50-52% vs right bloc ~46-48%). The SD kingmaker paradox — SD is the largest right-bloc party (~20%) but excluded from cabinet — creates coalition fragility.
