# 2025-Q4 (October 1 - December 31) — Point-in-Time Summary

**Compiled from sources available as of 2025-12-31.** No information from after this date is included.

## GEOPOLITICS & CONFLICT

### Israel-Gaza Ceasefire Takes Effect

The quarter opened with a major diplomatic breakthrough in the Gaza conflict. On October 8, US President Donald Trump announced that Israel and Hamas had agreed to a ceasefire and the first phase of his 20-point peace plan to end the Gaza war. Israel's Prime Minister's Office concurrently confirmed the agreement — this was Israel's **first official announcement** of agreeing to the ceasefire. The Israeli cabinet formally ratified the agreement on October 9, and the ceasefire came into effect on October 10, 2025, with the IDF withdrawing to agreed lines. 

> **FORECASTING NOTE**: Israel's "first announcement" of agreeing to the ceasefire was October 8 (PMO confirmation), NOT October 9 (cabinet ratification). These are distinct events: the PMO announces the diplomatic agreement; the cabinet subsequently ratifies it through an internal approval process. Date-specific forecast questions (Polymarket: "Will Israel first announce ceasefire on October X?") resolve on the announcement date, not the ratification date.

Hostage and prisoner releases began in the following days (see [[gaza-ceasefire-negotiations-2025]] for full timeline).

This followed the September 2025 Trump 20-point Gaza proposal, Hamas agreeing to parts of it on October 3, and indirect talks in Sharm el-Sheikh, Egypt on October 6. The ceasefire ended 15 months of war (since October 2023) and approximately 7 months since the January 2025 ceasefire collapsed in March.

The ceasefire was achieved through the [[diplomatic-pressure-tipping-point]] pattern — accumulated international pressure (28-nation statement, famine confirmation, UN genocide finding, Palestinian state recognitions) combined with Trump's peace broker positioning to create conditions for agreement.

## ECONOMICS

### Federal Reserve Rate Decisions

The Fed continued its resumption of the easing cycle in Q4 2025 with two additional 25bp cuts, bringing the total reduction from the September 2024 peak to 200bp (5.50% to 3.50% upper bound). The cuts reflected the Fed's assessment that inflation was converging toward target and that the labor market required policy support. Dissenting votes broadened across the Committee as the cutting cycle matured. [[us-monetary-policy-cycle-2022-2026]]

- **October 29, 2025**: Fed cut 25bp to 3.75-4.00% (10-2 vote). Governor Miran again dissented (favored 50bp) joined for the first time by Governor Schmid (favored holding steady at 4.00-4.25%). The two-dissent vote reflected growing internal division: one faction wanted faster easing, the other wanted to wait for more inflation data. (Source: Federal Reserve Press Release, October 29, 2025) [[federal-reserve-system]] [[jerome-powell]] [[central-bank-forward-guidance]]

- **December 10, 2025**: Fed cut 25bp to 3.50-3.75% (9-3 vote). Miran (favored 50bp) and Schmid (favored hold) were joined by Governor Goolsbee (favored hold), creating the broadest dissent coalition of the cycle. The dot plot released at this SEP meeting signaled 2-3 additional cuts in 2026, with the median projecting rates at 3.00-3.25% by end-2026. (Source: Federal Reserve Press Release, December 10, 2025) [[federal-reserve-system]] [[jerome-powell]] [[central-bank-forward-guidance]]

#### Argentina: Milei's LLA Wins Legislative Landslide
The October 26 legislative election for 127 of the 257 Chamber of Deputies seats delivered a decisive mandate for President Javier Milei's coalition. [[La Libertad Avanza]] (LLA) won 40.66% of the vote and 64 seats (+54 relative to the previous election), becoming the largest bloc in the Chamber. The Peronist successor Fuerza Patria led by [[Axel Kicillof]] won 33.70% and 47 seats. [[Juan Schiaretti]]'s [[Hacemos por Nuestro País|Primero País]] (formerly HNP) won 7.73% and 8 seats (-16), reflecting the collapse of regional Peronist alternatives under Milei-era polarization. The far-left [[FIT-U]] won 3.90% and 3 seats. Turnout was 67.43%. (See [[argentina-milei-realignment]] thread for full analysis.)

This result confirmed a [[populist-coattail-legislative-wave]] pattern: Milei entered office with only 10 LLA seats in 2023, and the 2025 election consolidated his coalition as the dominant legislative force. The far left (FIT-U) was squeezed to marginal vote shares, demonstrating its structural irrelevance under Milei-era polarization.

## KEY THEMES & EMERGING PATTERNS

1. **Ceasefire Phase-in**: The October 8-10 ceasefire marked the most significant Middle East diplomatic development of 2025. Implementation challenges in the post-ceasefire period include humanitarian access, hostage release sequencing, and the political status of Hamas in Gaza.

2. **Trump Peace Broker Legacy**: With the Armenia-Azerbaijan deal (August), Alaska Summit (August), and Gaza ceasefire (October), Trump's second-term foreign policy was defined by high-profile peace brokering. This pattern has direct implications for forecasting Trump-era conflict resolution.

3. **Diplomatic-Pressure-Tipping Validation**: The Gaza ceasefire confirmed the [[diplomatic-pressure-tipping-point]] pattern as a predictive framework for ceasefires in asymmetric conflicts where the superpower patron acts as mediator rather than combatant. The predicted timeline (~6 months from collapse to ceasefire) was accurate.

4. **Argentina Legislative Landslide**: The October 26 election confirmed Milei's [[populist-coattail-legislative-wave]], with LLA winning 64 seats and establishing Argentina's new political equilibrium. FIT-U's 3 seats confirmed far-left irrelevance in the Milei-Kicillof polarization.
