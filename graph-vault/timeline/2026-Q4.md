The June 21, 2026 Colombia runoff result reshapes Latin American politics through Q4. Key questions:
- **If de la Espriella wins**: Colombia's most right-wing government since Uribe (2002-2010). Implications for coca eradication, US-Colombia military cooperation, and Venezuelan migration policy. US-Colombia relations under a Trump-compatible leader.

## US DOMESTIC POLITICS — 2026 MIDTERM ELECTIONS (Nov 3)

**The single most consequential US political event of 2026.** All 435 House seats, 35 Senate seats, and 39 governorships.

### House of Representatives

- **Pre-election balance**: Republicans hold narrow majority (218-217 or similar)
- **Historical pattern**: President's party typically loses ~26 seats (average since 1946)
- **Key factors**: Trump approval rating, tariff/economic sentiment, abortion access as mobilizing issue, AI safety as cross-cutting concern
- **Swing districts**: ~30-40 competitive seats determined by turnout in suburban districts
- **Most likely outcome**: Democrats gain House control (needs net +4-5 seats)
- **Vault structural assessment**: Midterm referendum dynamics favor out-party. Historical average loss of 26 seats far exceeds the slim Republican majority. However, tariff-driven economic anxiety cuts both ways — voters may blame either party.

### Senate

- **Pre-election balance**: Highly competitive — 35 seats up, with vulnerable Dems in red states and vulnerable GOP in blue states
- **Key battlegrounds**: Pennsylvania (open — Casey seat), Wisconsin (open — Baldwin seat), Georgia (Ossoff, D), North Carolina (Tillis, R), Michigan (open — Stabenow seat), Arizona (Kelly, D), Maine (Collins, R)
- **Most likely outcome**: Senate control likely to remain close; either party could win 50-52 seats
- **Trump endorsement factor**: Primary results through Q2 will determine whether GOP candidates are Trump-aligned or establishment; general election effect of Trump involvement is mixed

### California Gubernatorial Election (Nov 3)

- **Matchup**: Xavier Becerra (D) vs. Steve Hilton (R)
- **Structural baseline**: Becerra strong favorite given CA registration advantage (~46% D, ~24% R)
- **Hilton ceiling**: Fox News platform gives name recognition but structural ceiling in deep-blue state
- **Vault structural estimate**: Becerra ~0.65-0.70 baseline, Hilton ~0.30-0.35
- **$24M Polymarket** market active; vault structural analysis favors Becerra
- **Outcome implications**: If Becerra wins, CA continues Newsom-aligned governance; if Hilton wins (major upset), CA shifts right for first time since Schwarzenegger (2003)

### Other State Elections

- 39 gubernatorial races total — key swing states: Pennsylvania, Wisconsin, Michigan, Arizona, Georgia, Nevada
- State legislative control in many states affects 2028 redistricting
- Ballot measures: abortion rights amendments expected in multiple states (post-Dobbs mobilization continues)

### Post-Midterm: Political Implications

- If **Democrats win House**: Trump faces divided government for final two years of term. Investigations, oversight, legislative gridlock. 2027-2028 agenda constrained.
- If **Republicans hold House**: Trump continues unified government. Opportunity for further deregulation, judicial appointments, and policy agenda through 2027.
- Senate control determines judicial confirmation pace (including potential Supreme Court vacancy)
- Midterm outcome is strong leading indicator for 2028 presidential race
