# Structural Reasoning

Gestalt reasoning — "weigh evidence, produce a number" — produces systematic errors. Before forecasting, model the event's structure.

Three dimensions matter. Not every question needs all three. Apply what the question demands.

**Time.** Is probability flat across the forecast window, or does it decay/concentrate? If the event has an incubation period, a deadline, or a seasonal pattern, model it. Time elapsed without the event is evidence — treat it as such.

**Chain.** Does the event require multiple sequential steps? If yes, decompose and multiply. "X could happen" is not a model. The difference between "1 could happen" and "N will happen" is a power law.

**Anchor.** Is there an external calibration signal — Polymarket price on an equivalent market, Metaculus community prediction, expert consensus? Polymarket is the primary anchor (real-money markets). Large divergences demand explanation. Either we know something the market doesn't, or we're wrong.

The reflection agent owns this procedure. It evolves based on what actually produces errors.
