# Duterte ICC Arrest (March 2025)

## Timeline

- **2025-03-11**: Duterte arrested at Ninoy Aquino International Airport upon return from Hong Kong. ICC warrant for crimes against humanity (war on drugs killings, 2016-2022). Marcos administration cooperates with ICC, facilitating the arrest — a dramatic reversal from the Duterte-era policy of withdrawal from the Rome Statute (2019).
- **2025-03-12**: Duterte transferred to The Hague via chartered aircraft. Philippines government provides logistical support.
- **2025-03-14**: Initial appearance before ICC Pre-Trial Chamber I. Charges: crimes against humanity (murder) under Article 7(1)(a) Rome Statute. Duterte pleads not guilty. Pre-Trial Chamber orders continued detention.
- **2025-Q2**: Philippines files jurisdictional challenge (withdrawal from Rome Statute, domestic remedies exhaustion). Pre-Trial Chamber schedules confirmation of charges hearing.
- **2025-Q3**: Confirmation of charges hearing. Pre-Trial Chamber confirms charges, finding sufficient evidence to proceed to trial. Duterte remains in ICC custody.
- **2025-Q4**: Trial preparation phase. Jurisdictional appeals continue. Sara Duterte's political position deteriorates.
- **2026-Q1**: Trial proceedings expected to begin. Duterte's health (age 80+) a monitoring variable.

## Significance for Forecasting

### ASEAN Precedent Break
The arrest shattered the assumption that ASEAN states will never cooperate with ICC prosecution of their own leaders. This has direct implications for forecasts on:
- Myanmar junta accountability (ICC has jurisdiction since 2018 Myanmar withdrawal from Rome Statute is disputed)
- Other Southeast Asian leaders' post-exit legal risk
- ICC enforcement credibility in non-signatory states

### Marcos-Duterte Alliance Fracture
The arrest represents the definitive break of the 2022 Marcos-Duterte electoral alliance. Key implications:
- The Marcos administration calculated that ICC cooperation was worth the Duterte base alienation
- Sara Duterte's political future is uncertain — she could lead a Duterte faction against Marcos in 2028
- A Duterte-apologist third-party candidacy could split the vote in 2028

### ICC Pretrial Custody Validation
Duterte's ongoing detention (12+ months at latest observation) validates the [[domains/global/concepts/icc-pretrial-custody-duration/_concept]] framework: ICC almost never releases defendants before trial, even elderly defendants with health concerns.
