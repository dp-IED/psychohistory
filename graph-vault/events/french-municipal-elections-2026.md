# French Municipal Elections 2026

## Schedule

- **First round**: March 15, 2026
- **Second round**: March 22, 2026

## Overview

Municipal elections in France elect the municipal councils of all ~35,000 communes. While non-partisan at the local level in principle, the elections are closely watched as a national political barometer — the first major electoral test since the 2024 snap legislative elections and the collapse of the Bayrou government.

## Political Context

- PM Sébastien Lecornu had been in office since September 2025 (replacing Bayrou after no-confidence vote)
- The March 1 Iranian drone strike on the French naval base in Abu Dhabi added a national security dimension to the campaign
- Marine Le Pen's December 2025 conviction for EU funds embezzlement (5-year ineligibility) created an uncertain backdrop for RN candidates
- President Macron announced a nuclear weapons stockpile increase on March 2, 2026, framing the campaign around national sovereignty

## Forecasting Relevance

- **Presidential bellwether**: Municipal election results in France correlate with presidential voting patterns in the following year. The 2026 municipals provide the last major electoral data point before the 2027 presidential race.
- **RN local strength**: RN has historically polled higher in national elections than in local races. The 2026 municipals tested whether that gap is closing.
- **Turnout signal**: Municipal elections typically have lower turnout than presidential elections. Turnout patterns across different demographics signal enthusiasm gaps for 2027.
