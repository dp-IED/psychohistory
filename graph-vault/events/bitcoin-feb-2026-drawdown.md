# Bitcoin February 2026 Drawdown ($97K → ~$60K)

## Summary

In early February 2026, Bitcoin entered a sharp drawdown from a peak near ~$97,000 toward a trough around $60,000 — a ~38% correction. The selloff was driven primarily by two compounding factors:

1. **Unfulfilled SBR expectations**: Market disappointment that the Trump administration had not issued any Strategic Bitcoin Reserve executive order after months of anticipation removed a key bullish narrative driver.
2. **Tariff uncertainty overwhelming macro**: Trump administration tariff policy created risk-asset uncertainty that dominated the positive signal from the Fed's Jan 28, 2026 rate cut (25bp to 3.25-3.50%).

The drawdown occurred despite a structurally supportive macro backdrop (Fed easing cycle paused, CPI ~2.5-3.0%, GDP ~2%) and the April 2024 halving's ongoing supply-side effects (daily issuance cut to ~225 BTC).

## Forecasting Significance

This event tests two vault concepts simultaneously:
- [[domains/economics/concepts/crypto-macro-linkages]] — Confirmed that macro drivers (tariffs) can overwhelm crypto-specific positives (SBR expectations, halving supply shock)
- [[domains/economics/concepts/strategic-bitcoin-reserve]] — Demonstrated the risk of market anticipation exceeding policy reality; SBR as campaign promise had created expectation that became a vulnerability

The drawdown's timing relative to the Bitcoin >$72K forecast (cutoff Feb 6, target Feb 13) meant a ~20% rally in 7 days was needed — structurally improbable given the trend and absent a known catalyst.
