# Hezbollah-Israel Ceasefire (November 2024)

## Date
November 26, 2024

## Significance

The Hezbollah-Israel ceasefire on November 26, 2024, was agreed between Israel and Hezbollah (the Iran-backed Lebanese militia) and brokered by the US and France. It was the second of four cascading events between October and January that structurally enabled the Gaza ceasefire:

| Event | Date | Role in Cascade |
|-------|------|-----------------|
| Sinwar killed | Oct 16 | Removed Hamas's most hardline leader; opened negotiation window (day 61-120 = elevated ceasefire probability) |
| Trump elected | Nov 5 | Created transition-window diplomatic compression (Nov 5 → Jan 20) |
| **Hezbollah ceasefire** | **Nov 26** | **Removed Israel's second-front burden; isolated Hamas strategically** |
| Assad regime collapses | Dec 8 | Degraded Iran's land corridor to Hezbollah; removed Hamas's last hope of axis-of-resistance rescue |
| Gaza ceasefire framework | Jan 15 | Multi-phase agreement announced by Biden + Trump + Qatar PM |

## Mechanism

The Hezbollah ceasefire enabled the Gaza agreement through three distinct channels:

1. **Multifront decompression**: Israel had been fighting on multiple fronts (Gaza, Lebanon, Iran, West Bank, Houthis). Securing the northern border with Hezbollah freed military bandwidth, intelligence resources, and leadership attention for Gaza negotiations.

2. **Axis isolation**: Hamas had calculated that the Axis of Resistance (Iran → Syria → Hezbollah → Hamas) would eventually force Israel to accept terms. The Hezbollah ceasefire broke this chain — Hamas's most capable non-state ally was no longer an active threat. Sinwar's death (Oct 16) had already decapitated Hamas's leadership; the Hezbollah ceasefire removed the remaining axis pillar.

3. **Domestic political space**: With the northern front stabilized, Israel's political leadership could take the political risk of a Gaza ceasefire without simultaneously managing an active war in Lebanon. The far-right coalition partners (Ben-Gvir, Smotrich) lost the argument that "we're fighting on all fronts — we can't concede on any."

## Forecasting Relevance

The Hezbollah ceasefire is a canonical example of a **structural precondition event** — it did not directly cause the Gaza ceasefire but removed a key barrier that had made it impossible. Forecasters evaluating Middle East ceasefire probability between Nov 26, 2024 and Jan 15, 2025 should weight this event as:

- **Single factor weight**: ~20-30% of the cumulative probability shift toward a Gaza deal (from ~20% pre-election baseline to ~90% by Jan 15)
- **Interaction with transition-window**: The ceasefire plus the US presidential transition created a compound forcing function — Israel had both a freer hand (no second front) and a deadline (Jan 20 inauguration)
- **Contrast with Assad collapse**: The Assad collapse (Dec 8) was the more dramatic event, but the Hezbollah ceasefire was the earlier and arguably more direct structural precondition — it removed Hamas's most credible military partner

## Preconditions

- October 1, 2024: Iran launches missile barrage at Israel (Operation True Promise II)
- October 16, 2024: Sinwar killed by Israeli forces in Rafah — Hamas leadership decapitation
- October-November 2024: Israel intensifies ground operations in southern Lebanon
- November 5, 2024: Trump wins US presidential election — transition-window diplomacy begins
- November 26, 2024: US-France brokered ceasefire takes effect between Israel and Hezbollah
