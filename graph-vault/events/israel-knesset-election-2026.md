# Israel Knesset Election 2026

## Event Summary

The 26th Knesset election is scheduled for October 27, 2026 — the first national election since the Oct 7, 2023 Hamas attack and the subsequent Gaza war, Iran-Israel conflict, and regional realignment. The election will determine the composition of the 120-seat Knesset and, through coalition negotiations, the next Prime Minister of Israel.

## Key Dates

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| October 27, 2026 | Knesset election day |
| Late Oct-Nov 2026 | Coalition negotiations (typically 4-6 weeks, can extend to 8+) |
| Dec 2026-Jan 2027 | New government sworn in; PM determined |

## Structural Dynamics

### Right-Religious Bloc (Current Floor: ~55 seats)
- Likud under Netanyahu remains the largest right-wing party despite multiple crises (Oct 7 intelligence failure, Gaza war management, International Criminal Court arrest warrant issued Nov 2024)
- Religious Zionist party under Smotrich and Otzma Yehudit under Ben-Gvir have pushed the coalition further right
- Netanyahu's political resilience: survived Oct 7, ICC warrant, ongoing corruption trial, mass protests (2019-2023 judicial overhaul)
- Key unknown: whether Netanyahu seeks re-election or orchestrates an orderly succession within Likud

### Center-Left Bloc (Current Ceiling: ~45 seats)
- Labor party decline from 44 seats (1992) to 4-8 seats currently — near-extinction trajectory
- Yesh Atid (Lapid) remains the largest center party but has not expanded beyond its ~17-24 seat ceiling
- Yair Golan's Democrats party (Labor-Meretz merger) attempts to revive the Zionist left but faces structural headwinds
- The center-left has not won a plurality since 1999 (Barak) and has not formed a government without right-wing coalition partners since then
- Arab-majority parties (Hadash-Ta'al, Ra'am) are potential coalition partners but introduce ideological friction on security issues

### Arab/Mixed Parties (~8-12 seats)
- United Arab List (Ra'am) broke the anti-Netanyahu bloc by joining Bennett-Lapid coalition (2021-2022)
- Hadash-Ta'al remains oppositional but could serve as a blocking majority
- Arab voter turnout dropped sharply in 2022 election (~50%) — key swing variable

## Forecasting Relevance

- **Golan PM probability**: The core assumption driving p=0.004 is that center-left coalition formation requires a ~15-20 seat swing from current right-religious dominance — structural near-impossibility absent a major crisis that discredits the right (analogous to 1999 when Netanyahu's first term collapse cost Likud 13 seats)
- **Ohana PM probability**: The 4-gate chain (Likud win × Netanyahu departure × Ohana selected × coalition approval) compounds to ~0.17%. The election outcome is just the first gate — Ohana must also survive internal Likud competition
- **Electoral volatility risk**: Israeli elections since 2019 have shown high volatility (5 elections in 3.5 years, 2019-2022). If the Knesset dissolves before Oct 27, this schedule shifts
