# French Presidential Election (April 2027)

## Schedule

- **First round**: April 2027 (exact date TBD, typically second Sunday of April)
- **Runoff**: Two weeks later if no candidate wins 50%+1
- **Inauguration**: May 2027

## Electoral System

Two-round system. If no candidate wins 50%+1 of valid votes in the first round, the top two advance to a runoff two weeks later. The threshold is a true majority of votes cast (not registered voters).

## Political Landscape (as of May 22, 2026)

### Frontrunners

1. **Jordan Bardella** (National Rally, far-right): RN president since 2022. Presumptive 2027 candidate after Le Pen's legal disqualification. Young (born 1995), polished communicator, has worked to detoxify RN brand. Projected first-round vote share ~30-35% (based on RN's 2022 ceiling of 41.5% in runoff and Bardella's fresher appeal).

2. **Bruno Retailleau** (The Republicans, center-right): LR president since May 2025. Former interior minister under Barnier. Hardline conservative on immigration, law-and-order. Positioned to consolidate the center-right vote after Macron's Renaissance collapse.

3. **Édouard Philippe** (Horizons, centrist): Former PM (2017-2020), popular, seen as the "continuity Macron" candidate. Would split the center-right vote with Retailleau.

4. **Gabriel Attal** (Renaissance): Youngest former PM (2024). Struggles with Macron fatigue but represents the remaining Macronist core.

### Structural Dynamics

- **Macron term-limited**: First open presidential race since 2017. The incumbent is ineligible for a third consecutive term.
- **RN's highest-ever positioning**: RN received 41.5% in the 2022 runoff and has only strengthened since. Bardella offers a "fresh face" vs Le Pen's baggage.
- **Left fragmentation**: LFI (Mélenchon fading), Socialists, and Greens remain divided. No clear left-wing frontrunner. The New Popular Front (NFP) alliance formed for the 2024 snap legislative elections has weakened.
- **Centrist fragmentation**: Philippe, Attal, and potentially other Renaissance figures split the Macronist vote, reducing the chance any centrist reaches the runoff.
- **Le Pen appeal pending**: July 7, 2026. If overturned, she could re-enter the race. If upheld, Bardella is the clear RN successor.
- **Cohabitation risk**: Legislative elections follow 4-6 weeks after the presidential. A president without a legislative majority would face France's first cohabitation since 1997-2002.

### Cordon Sanitaire

The "republican front" — mainstream parties uniting against the far-right in the runoff — has eroded steadily. In 2002, Chirac defeated Le Pen père 82% to 18%. In 2017, Macron beat Le Pen fille 66% to 34%. In 2022, 59% to 41%. At current trajectory, 2027 could produce the first RN victory or a narrow margin that tests political stability.
