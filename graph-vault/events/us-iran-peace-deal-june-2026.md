# US-Iran Permanent Peace Deal (June 14, 2026)

## Overview

On **June 14, 2026**, Pakistan (serving as mediator) announced that the United States and Iran had reached a permanent peace deal. President Trump posted "let the oil flow!" on social media. Both sides declared an **immediate and permanent termination of military operations on all fronts**.

This deal resolves the Polymarket "US x Iran permanent peace deal" markets that had been running since Q1 2026 with escalating deadlines (May through December). The June 15 deadline market (resolving June 14) had been at ~42% YES with $1.5M volume.

## Background

### The US-Iran War (Feb 28, 2026)

On **February 28, 2026**, the United States and Israel launched a joint military operation codenamed **Operation Epic Fury** (US) / **Roaring Lion** (Israel) targeting:
- Key Iranian military commanders and facilities
- Senior political leadership — including the assassination of Supreme Leader **Ali Khamenei**
- Military infrastructure across Iran

This operation was a major escalation beyond the June 2025 Twelve-Day War (which had targeted nuclear facilities).

### Post-War Negotiations (March - June 2026)

Pakistan played the key mediator role. The negotiations addressed:
- Verified nuclear dismantlement (Iran's program had been degraded since June 2025)
- IRGC status and proxy stand-down commitments
- Sanctions relief framework
- Formal diplomatic recognition

## Immediate Consequences

1. **Oil markets**: Trump's "let the oil flow!" statement suggests sanctions relief and renewed Iranian oil exports — a significant supply-side shock for global energy markets
2. **Middle East alignment**: Iran's reintegration reshapes the regional order — implications for Israel, Saudi Arabia, UAE, and the broader Axis of Resistance
3. **Iranian succession**: With Khamenei deceased, the deal creates a new external environment for the IRGC-civilian power struggle
4. **Trump's legacy**: The peace deal is the capstone of Trump's second-term foreign policy (Alaska Summit → Armenia-Azerbaijan → Gaza → Iran), reinforcing his peace-broker brand ahead of the November 2026 midterms.
