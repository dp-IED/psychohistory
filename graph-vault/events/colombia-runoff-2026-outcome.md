# Colombia 2026 Presidential Runoff (June 21, 2026)

## Event Summary

The second round of Colombia's 2026 presidential election pits **Abelardo de la Espriella** (right-wing, 43.7% in first round) against **Iván Cepeda** (left-wing, 40.9% in first round). This is Colombia's first competitive runoff since the 2014 re-election of Juan Manuel Santos and the most expensive political prediction market in vault coverage at $29.9M.

## Pre-Resolution Context (June 18, 2026) — Updated June 20 (T-24h Final / Election Eve)

### Candidate Positions

| Candidate | First-Round % | Ideology | Coalition | Key Endorsements |
|-----------|--------------|----------|-----------|-----------------|
| Abelardo de la Espriella | 43.7% | Centre-right | Right-wing coalition (post-fragmentation consolidation) | Fragmented first-round right-wing candidates unified behind him |
| Iván Cepeda | 40.9% | Left | Pacto Histórico + Petro coalition | Incumbent president's coalition; polarized left base |

### Key Structural Dynamics

1. **First-round leader advantage**: In Colombia's three prior runoffs (2014, 2018, 2022), the first-round leader won the runoff every time — 3/3 historical record
2. **Right-wing consolidation**: The fragmented right-wing field (5+ candidates in first round, total right vote share ~55%) consolidated behind de la Espriella for the runoff
3. **Cepeda's ceiling problem**: First-round result confirmed the vault's structural estimate (Cepeda ceiling ~46-48%) — he finished at 40.9%, below even the lower bound
4. **Vault structural estimate**: de la Espriella ~0.65-0.70 (from [[forecasts/2026-06-14-colombia-runoff-revision]])
5. **Polymarket market**: Runoff market prices de la Espriella as frontrunner at ~65-70% YES, aligning with vault estimate
