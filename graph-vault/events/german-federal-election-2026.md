# CORRECTION: German Federal Election 2026

**This file contained an error.** The German federal election was held on February 23, 2025 (snap election), not in fall 2026.

- For the **actual election results**, see [[events/german-federal-election-2025]]
- For **current German politics**, see [[domains/europe/_domain]]

### Correction Rationale

The original file was created during vault synthesis and incorrectly assumed the next German federal election would be in fall 2026 (4-year cycle from 2021). In fact:
- The traffic-light coalition (SPD/Greens/FDP) collapsed in late 2024
- Scholz called and lost a confidence motion, triggering a snap election
- The election was held February 23, 2025
- CDU/CSU won 28.5%, AfD 20.8%, SPD collapsed to 16.4%
- Merz became Chancellor via CDU/CSU+SPD grand coalition (April 9, 2025)
- The next election is due on or before March 25, 2029

### Impact on Vault

Any references to a "German federal election 2026" elsewhere in the vault should be updated to reflect the correct February 2025 date. See [[events/_index]] for the corrected event catalog.
