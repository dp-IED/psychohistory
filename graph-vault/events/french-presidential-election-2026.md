# CORRECTION: French Presidential Election 2026

**This file contained an error.** The next French presidential election is in April 2027, not 2026.

- For the **upcoming presidential election**, see [[events/french-presidential-election-2027]]
- For the **2026 municipal elections** (which actually occurred), see [[events/french-municipal-elections-2026]]
- For **current French politics**, see [[domains/france/_domain]]

### Correction Rationale

The original file was created during vault synthesis and incorrectly assumed the French presidential election would occur in April-May 2026 based on a misconception about the electoral calendar. In fact:
- Emmanuel Macron was elected in 2017 and re-elected in 2022
- The French constitution limits presidents to two consecutive terms
- The next presidential election is therefore April 2027
- The 2026 French elections were the municipal elections (March 15 & 22)

### Impact on Vault

Any references to a "French presidential election 2026" elsewhere in the vault should be updated to reflect the correct 2027 date. See [[events/_index]] for the corrected event catalog.
