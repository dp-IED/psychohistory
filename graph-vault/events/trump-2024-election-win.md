# Trump Wins 2024 US Presidential Election

## Date
November 5, 2024

## Significance

Donald Trump's victory in the 2024 US presidential election was a structural pivot point for multiple forecasting domains. It created:

1. **Transition-window diplomatic compression** (Nov 2024-Jan 2025): The ~10-week window between Trump's election and Jan 20 inauguration created aligned incentives between the outgoing Biden administration and incoming Trump administration on ceasefire diplomacy — both wanted a deal before the transition, compressing negotiation timelines that had been stalled for 8 months.

2. **Executive enforcement discretion**: The incoming Trump administration could choose to enforce or not enforce the TikTok divest-or-ban law after taking office, creating a distinct layer of uncertainty separable from SCOTUS's procedural determinism on judicial delay.

3. **Crypto policy pivot**: Trump's pro-crypto campaign positions (pledge to establish a Strategic Bitcoin Reserve, appoint crypto-friendly SEC chair) created policy expectations that initially drove Bitcoin to $97K but later contributed to a drawdown when executive orders didn't materialize immediately — the canonical [[domains/global/concepts/policy-expectation-without-delivery/_concept]] case.

4. **Global populist alignment**: Trump's victory strengthened Javier Milei in Argentina (Milei was first foreign head of state invited to the Jan 20 inauguration), accelerating the LLA coattails that would carry the 2025 midterm elections.

## Preconditions

- June 27, 2024: Biden's poor debate performance triggers party panic
- July 21, 2024: Biden withdraws from race, endorses Harris
- July-August 2024: Harris campaign consolidates Democratic support; veepstakes selects Walz
- September-October 2024: Harris-Trump contest remains competitive; swing states in play
- November 5, 2024: Trump wins 312-226 electoral college; popular vote also Trump (first Republican popular vote win since 2004)

## Forecasting Relevance

The 2024 election outcome is the single most important structural precondition for forecasts spanning late 2024 through at least 2026. Any forecast with a cutoff date after November 5, 2024, should reference this event as a context variable — especially for:

- **Middle East ceasefire dynamics** (transition-window diplomatic compression)
- **US tech/crypto regulation** (SEC chair replacement, SBR policy, antitrust posture)
- **US-NATO relations** (Trump's NATO skepticism)
- **Trade/tariff policy** (Trump's protectionist platform)
- **Global authoritarian alignment** (coordination with Milei, Orbán, Netanyahu, Saudi MBS)
