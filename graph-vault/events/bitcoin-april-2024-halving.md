# Bitcoin April 2024 Halving

## Summary

On or around April 19, 2024, Bitcoin underwent its fourth programmed halving event at block height 840,000. The block reward was reduced from 6.25 BTC to 3.125 BTC per block, cutting daily new issuance from approximately 450 BTC to approximately 225 BTC (~$15M to ~$7.5M at then-prevailing prices).

This was the first halving to occur after Bitcoin reached institutional maturity — spot ETFs launched in January 2024, creating regulated demand channels that did not exist during previous halving cycles (2012, 2016, 2020).

## Forecasting Significance

The halving is a structural supply-side event with well-documented cycle dynamics:

1. **Pre-halving rally**: Typically begins 3-6 months before the event as miners accumulate and speculators front-run the supply crunch.
2. **Post-halving consolidation**: A 1-6 month period of range-bound or declining price as the market absorbs the new supply-demand equilibrium.
3. **Cycle peak**: 12-18 months post-halving — historically coinciding with the next bull market peak (2013, 2017, 2021).

The 2024 halving differs from prior cycles because:
- Spot ETF demand channels existed for the first time, potentially accelerating the post-halving re-price
- Macro environment was restrictive (Fed at 5.25-5.50%, no cuts until September 2024), unlike prior halvings during loose monetary policy
- Institutional adoption (MicroStrategy, corporate treasuries, pension funds) provided a demand floor that prior cycles lacked
