# Colombia Presidential Election 2026

## Schedule
- **First round**: May 31, 2026 — COMPLETED
- **Runoff**: June 21, 2026
- **Inauguration**: August 7, 2026

## Electoral System
Two-round system. If no candidate wins 50%+1 of valid votes in the first round, the top two advance to a runoff three weeks later. No candidate reached 50% threshold.

## First Round Results (May 31, 2026)

| Candidate | Party/Alliance | Vote % |
|-----------|---------------|--------|
| **Abelardo de la Espriella** | Defenders of the Homeland (Ind.) | **43.7%** |
| **Iván Cepeda Castro** | Historic Pact (Alliance for Life) | **40.9%** |
| Others (11 candidates) | Various | 15.4% |

**Turnout**: 57.89% (↑2.91pp vs 2022)

### Key Takeaways

1. **Massive Polymarket inefficiency**: Cepeda was priced at 82.5% to win first round but finished second (40.9%). The market was systematically wrong. De la Espriella at 15.8% won with 43.7% — a 27.9pp market-underestimation.

2. **Right-wing consolidation in round 1**: The vault's May 23 analysis predicted consolidation *between rounds*. Instead, it happened in round 1, with de la Espriella capturing ~44% of the vote directly — close to the combined pre-election right-wing polling share.

3. **Cepeda ceiling validated at ~41%**: The structural left-wing ceiling analysis held. Cepeda underperformed even the vault's 46-48% head-to-head estimate — actual first-round share was 40.9%.

4. **Pre-election polling failure**: Polls showing Cepeda at 35-38% and de la Espriella at 15-18% were significantly off, suggesting systematic methodological issues (likely social desirability bias against populist right candidates, similar to Trump 2016/2020 polling errors).

## Runoff Dynamics (June 21, 2026)

- De la Espriella enters as frontrunner with 2.8pp lead and momentum
- Historical runoff gains for right: 2014 Zuluaga +10pp, 2018 Duque +5pp, 2022 Hernández +11pp
- Endorsement cascade from eliminated right-wing/centrist candidates (Valencia, Fajardo, Peñalosa) expected to flow to de la Espriella
- Cepeda at 40.9% faces path similar to Petro 2022 (40.3% in first round, won runoff 50.4%) but against a stronger right-wing opponent
- Runoff turnout (typically 10-15pp lower) favors right-wing base

## Market Anomaly — Post-First-Round Assessment

The Polymarket pricing revealed severe structural inefficiency:
- Pre-first-round: Cepeda 82.5% first-round, de la Espriella 15.8%
- Actual: de la Espriella 43.7% first round, Cepeda 40.9%
- Post-first-round runoff pricing: de la Espriella ~65-70% (vs pre-first-round 50.5%)
- The market initially priced Cepeda as 82.5% frontrunner who would then lose the runoff — a narrative that collapsed when Cepeda couldn't even win the first round

This is a major calibration data point for the [[domains/global/concepts/market-vault-structural-divergence]] concept and documents how structural polling errors propagate into prediction market prices.
