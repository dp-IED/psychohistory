# Freedom Movement (GS)

The Freedom Movement (Gibanje Svoboda) is a centrist, pro-European liberal party founded by Robert Golob in 2021. It won a historic landslide in 2022, unseating Janez Janša's SDS government. However, the party's cohesion was fragile — built around Golob's personal popularity and anti-Janša sentiment rather than a strong party organization.

## 2026 Collapse

The 2026 election saw GS fall from 41 to 29 seats — a 12-seat loss. Despite remaining the largest single party, the loss of coalition partner SD (6 seats) and Levica (5 seats) left GS unable to reach 46 seats without the Democrats (6 seats). When Logar's Democrats withdrew from negotiations, GS's governing path collapsed.

## Forecasting Relevance

GS illustrates the **one-term liberal party erosion** pattern: freshman liberal parties with broad anti-incumbent appeals often cannot sustain their initial seat share at the next election. The party's future is uncertain — going into opposition may allow rebuilding, but Golob has lost his governing mandate and the party's electoral brand was tied to his PM persona.
