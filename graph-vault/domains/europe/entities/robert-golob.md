# Robert Golob

Robert Golob is the outgoing Prime Minister of Slovenia (2022-2026) and leader of the Freedom Movement (GS). He entered politics after a career in the energy sector, where he served as CEO of GEN-I, a state-owned electricity trading company.

## Political Trajectory

- **2022**: Entered politics as a political novice; formed GS as a centrist liberal alternative. Won 41 seats in a landslide, unseating Janez Janša's SDS government. The victory was widely seen as a rejection of Janša's polarizing style rather than an endorsement of Golob's program.
- **2022-2026**: Governed as a GS+SD+Levica coalition. Focused on normalizing relations with EU institutions, climate policy, and COVID recovery.
- **2026**: Election saw GS fall from 41 to 29 seats. After Logar's Democrats withdrew from coalition negotiations (accusing Golob of "political deception"), GS failed to reach 46 seats and announced it would go into opposition.

## Forecasting Significance

Golob's trajectory illustrates the **one-term majority erosion** pattern common in European center-left/liberal parties:
- Large freshman majorities tend to erode at the next election (GS -12 seats)
- Coalition formation depends on swing parties (Logar's 6 Democrats seats were decisive)
- A leader whose coalition collapses may become caretaker for an extended period (Slovenia has no constitutional deadline for government formation)
- The GS→opposition move is historically unusual for a party that remained the largest single party
