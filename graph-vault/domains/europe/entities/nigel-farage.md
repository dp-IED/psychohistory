# Nigel Farage

## Summary

Nigel Farage is a British right-populist politician, leader of Reform UK, and a central figure in the Brexit movement. He served as a Member of the European Parliament for South East England from 1999 to 2020 and as leader of the UK Independence Party (UKIP) and later the Brexit Party (which became Reform UK). He has been a persistent force on the right flank of British politics, pressuring the Conservative Party from the outside and drawing significant working-class and Euroskeptic support.

## Significance

Reform UK under Farage polls at 12-18% and is the third force in British politics, capable of splitting the right-wing vote and affecting Conservative recovery prospects. Any forecast about UK general elections, Conservative leadership, or the Labour government's electoral position must account for Farage's Reform UK as a structural factor in the British electoral landscape. Farage himself is a perennial candidate whose movements influence the Conservative Party's strategic positioning.
