# Rachel Reeves

## Summary

Rachel Reeves is a British Labour Party politician serving as Chancellor of the Exchequer under Prime Minister Keir Starmer since July 2024. She is the first woman to hold the office. A fiscal conservative within Labour, Reeves has pursued a policy framework emphasizing economic stability, fiscal rules, and gradual public investment. Her tenure has been defined by managing the UK's post-Brexit economic adjustment, NHS funding pressures, and the constraints of near-record tax burdens.

## Significance

Reeves is the second-most-powerful figure in the Starmer government after the PM himself. Her fiscal stance — combining tight spending rules with targeted investment — sets the parameters for Labour's domestic agenda. Any forecasting about UK economic policy, tax changes, public spending, or Labour government stability must account for Reeves's approach and political positioning. She is also a potential successor to Starmer if a leadership vacancy emerges.

## Timeline

- **2021-2024**: Shadow Chancellor of the Exchequer
- **2024-07-05**: Appointed Chancellor of the Exchequer
- **2024-10**: Delivers first Budget — tax increases and spending cuts to meet fiscal rules
- **2025**: Continues to defend stringent fiscal rules amid sluggish growth (~1-1.5%)
