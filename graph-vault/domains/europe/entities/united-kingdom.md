# United Kingdom

United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland. A sovereign country in northwestern Europe, comprising the island of Great Britain (England, Scotland, Wales) and the northeastern part of the island of Ireland.

## Political System

- **Parliamentary constitutional monarchy**: Westminster-style parliamentary system with a fused executive (PM and cabinet drawn from Commons majority)
- **Fixed-term Parliaments**: General elections every 5 years (2024, 2029) — but snap elections possible via Early Parliamentary General Election Act
- **First-past-the-post**: Single-member districts produce durable two-party dominance (Labour vs Conservative), with strong regional variation (SNP in Scotland, Reform UK as insurgent right-wing populist)
- **Current government**: Labour Party under PM Keir Starmer (landslide majority from July 2024 election, ~400+ seats)
- **Next scheduled election**: 2029, but a leadership change within the governing party would make the new PM automatically — no general election required

## Climate

- Temperate maritime climate dominated by Atlantic westerlies
- Mild winters and cool summers relative to latitude due to North Atlantic Drift
- Gulf Stream / AMOC moderation keeps UK 5-10°C warmer than continental locations at similar latitude
- High interannual variability driven by NAO (North Atlantic Oscillation) phase
- February record high: 21.2°C at Kew Gardens (London, 2019)
- February average high range: 7-10°C (north to south)
