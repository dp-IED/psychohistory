# Keir Starmer

Sir Keir Starmer became Prime Minister following Labour's landslide victory in the July 2024 general election. His tenure has been defined by navigating post-Brexit UK-EU relations, managing public finances after the Truss/Kwarteng fiscal crisis, addressing NHS waiting lists, and maintaining Labour coalition unity between the party's moderate and left wings.

## Key Context (as of mid-2026)

- **Approval ratings**: Labour has experienced a typical mid-term decline in polling, though Starmer's personal ratings remain higher than his party's
- **Parliamentary majority**: Labour holds a substantial majority (est. 150+ seats) from the 2024 election
- **Internal party dynamics**: Ongoing tension between Starmer's centrist leadership and the party's left wing over fiscal policy, welfare reform, and green investment
- **Key challenges**: Stagnant economic growth, NHS performance, immigration levels, and post-Brexit trade frictions
