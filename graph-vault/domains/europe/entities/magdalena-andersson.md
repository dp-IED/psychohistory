# Magdalena Andersson

## Structural Position
- **Role**: Leader of the Opposition; Party Leader, Social Democratic Party (S)
- **Previous**: Prime Minister of Sweden (November 2021 – October 2022)
- **Party**: Social Democrats (Socialdemokraterna, S) — Sweden's largest party historically
- **Next election**: September 13, 2026 — frontrunner to become PM

## Political Profile
- **Age**: 59 (born January 1967)
- **Style**: Pragmatic, experienced, fiscally moderate
- **Key policies**: Welfare state defense, climate transition, crime (tough-on-crime pivot from traditional S position), NATO consensus
- **Historic**: Sweden's first female PM (2021-2022)

## Electoral Position (2026)
- Social Democrats polling ~30-34% (largest single party)
- Left bloc polling ~50-52% vs right bloc ~46-48%
- Path to PM: left bloc wins majority → Andersson forms government with Left Party (V) and Green Party (MP) support
- Key strength: experience and name recognition; voters trust S on welfare/healthcare
- Key vulnerability: crime/gang violence under S-led governments (2014-2022) despite recent tough-on-crime pivot
