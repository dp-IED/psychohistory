# Rishi Sunak

## Summary

Rishi Sunak became Prime Minister of the United Kingdom on October 25, 2022, winning the Conservative Party leadership contest unopposed after Liz Truss resigned. He became the first person of color and the first Hindu to hold the office. Sunak had previously served as Chancellor of the Exchequer under Boris Johnson (2020-2022), where he oversaw the COVID-era furlough scheme. He resigned from Johnson's government in July 2022, triggering the mass ministerial walkout that ultimately forced Johnson from office. Sunak's premiership began with the promise of economic stability and fiscal responsibility after the Truss mini-budget crisis.

## Significance

Sunak's succession after the Truss crisis represented the Conservative Party's third PM in two months and the fourth consecutive Conservative PM since the 2016 Brexit referendum. Unlike Truss, Sunak prioritized fiscal discipline and market confidence, appointing Jeremy Hunt as Chancellor who had already reversed most of Truss's mini-budget. Sunak's government took office facing a severe cost-of-living crisis, double-digit inflation, and a deeply divided Conservative Party.
