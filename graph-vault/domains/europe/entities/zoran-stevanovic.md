# Zoran Stevanović

Zoran Stevanović is the leader of Resni.ca, an anti-establishment party that entered the Slovenian National Assembly for the first time in the March 22, 2026 election. His election as Speaker of the Assembly with 48 votes on April 10 made him a critical figure in the government formation crisis.

## Political Profile

- **Ideology**: Anti-establishment, anti-corruption, direct democracy rhetoric
- **EU stance**: Eurosceptic but not explicitly exit-advocating; platform focused on national sovereignty
- **Role**: As Speaker, controls the legislative agenda and procedural schedule — can accelerate or delay government formation votes

## Forecasting Significance

Stevanović/Resni.ca represents the **anti-system kingmaker** archetype:
- A small anti-system party (5 seats) holds disproportionate power in a hung parliament
- Supporting a PM candidate while refusing to join the coalition creates a confidence-and-supply arrangement — uniquely unstable
- The Speaker role amplifies the 5 seats' influence; an anti-system Speaker can shape the legislative agenda even without being in government
- This pattern is emerging across European hung parliaments (Slovakia 2023, Spain 2023, Slovenia 2026)
