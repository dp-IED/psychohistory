# Kwasi Kwarteng

## Summary

Kwasi Kwarteng was appointed Chancellor of the Exchequer on September 6, 2022, by newly installed Prime Minister Liz Truss. On September 23, 2022, he delivered a "mini-budget" — officially a Fiscal Statement — that announced approximately £45 billion in unfunded tax cuts, including the abolition of the 45% top rate of income tax and a reversal of the National Insurance hike. The announcement triggered a historic sell-off in UK government bonds, a sharp depreciation of the pound sterling, and forced the Bank of England to intervene with an emergency bond-buying program on September 28.

## Significance

Kwarteng is the architect of what markets have dubbed a "fiscal event" that severely damaged UK economic credibility. As of September 30, 2022, his position is tenuous — political and market pressure is mounting on Truss to reverse course or replace him.
