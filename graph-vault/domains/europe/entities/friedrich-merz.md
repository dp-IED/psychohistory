# Friedrich Merz

Chancellor of Germany (CDU/CSU). Merz became Chancellor following the February 23, 2025 snap federal election, leading a CDU/CSU+SPD grand coalition formed on April 9, 2025. The next federal election is due on or before March 25, 2029.

## Forecasting Relevance

- **Re-election probability** (2029 cycle) depends on economic management (US tariff exposure, Chinese manufacturing competition, energy costs), immigration policy, and coalition dynamics
- **EU/NATO implications**: Merz represents the mainstream conservative center-right aligned with EU institutional stability and NATO burden-sharing, in implicit opposition to AfD's Eurosceptic positioning
- **Relationship to other European forecasts**: The German political trajectory under Merz and the French presidential election (April 2027) together define the European political direction through the second Trump term
