# Democrats (Slovenia)

The Democrats (Demokrati Anzeta Logarja) are a center-right political party founded by Anže Logar after his departure from SDS in October 2024. Logar had been widely seen as Janša's natural successor, but the succession failed, leading to a party split that fundamentally reshaped Slovenian politics.

## 2026 Election

In its first election, the Democrats won 6 seats — a strong performance for a party founded just 16 months earlier. The 6 seats are disproportionately influential because they are the decisive swing bloc in a 90-seat hung parliament where no coalition can reach 46 without them.

## Forecasting Significance

The Democrats' success tests the hypothesis that breakaway factions from dominant populist parties can succeed as independent forces. Unlike similar breakaways in Hungary (Fidesz) or Poland (PiS), Logar's Democrats survived their first electoral test. The key forecasting question is whether the Democrats remain independent or eventually reunite with SDS.
