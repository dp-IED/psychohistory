# Italy

G7 member, NATO founding member, EU founding member. Parliamentary republic with a bicameral legislature.

## Current Government (2026)

- **Prime Minister**: Giorgia Meloni (Brothers of Italy, FdI) — since October 2022
- **Coalition**: FdI + Lega (Salvini) + Forza Italia (Tajani) — right-wing coalition
- **President**: Sergio Mattarella (since 2015, re-elected 2022)

## Foreign Policy

- **NATO**: 2% GDP defense spending target; hosts US military bases (Aviano, Naples, Sigonella)
- **EU**: Meloni has moderated from pre-election Euroscepticism; constructive EU engagement post-2023
- **Mediterranean**: Active in EUNAVFOR MED (Operation Irini); Libya stabilization; migration patrols
- **Strait of Hormuz**: Italy participated in EMASoH (European Maritime Awareness in the Strait of Hormuz) since 2020; Italian frigate deployments are routine for maritime security
- **Ukraine**: Consistent support for Ukraine; arms supplies; sanctions on Russia
- **Middle East**: Historically balanced between Israel and Palestinian Authority; energy diversification away from Russian gas toward North Africa/Azerbaijan

## Military

- Navy (Marina Militare): Aircraft carrier Cavour, amphibious assault ships, ~10 frigates/destroyers
- Deployable naval assets in Mediterranean at any given time: 3-5 surface combatants
- Naval base: Taranto (primary), La Spezia, Augusta

## Strait of Hormuz Posture

Italy has participated in EMASoH (European-led maritime surveillance mission) since 2020. This is a non-escalatory monitoring mission — distinct from US-led Sentinel or UK-led missions. Italian warships transiting Hormuz occurs within this framework. A dedicated "send warships through the Strait of Hormuz" market typically means a NEW deployment announcement beyond routine patrols, which requires political authorization.
