# Angela Rayner

## Summary

Angela Rayner is a British Labour Party politician serving as Deputy Prime Minister and Secretary of State for Housing, Communities and Local Government under Prime Minister Keir Starmer since July 2024. She represents the soft-left wing of the Labour Party and serves as a bridge between the leadership and the trade union movement. Her position as Deputy PM and party deputy leader makes her a central figure in Labour internal dynamics.

## Significance

Rayner is Labour's senior-most internal faction manager, responsible for maintaining cohesion between Starmer's centrist leadership and the party's left-wing and union base. Any scenario involving Labour leadership transition, internal party rebellion, or coalition dynamics in a hung parliament would involve Rayner as a pivotal actor. Her relationship with Starmer and the unions is a key indicator of Labour government stability.
