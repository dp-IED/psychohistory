# Janez Janša

Janez Janša is the leader of the Slovenian Democratic Party (SDS) and a three-time former Prime Minister. After the March 22, 2026 parliamentary election produced a hung parliament, he submitted a mandate candidacy on May 19, 2026 seeking to form a center-right coalition government.

## Political Profile

- **Ideology**: Center-right, national conservative, Eurosceptic (within EU framework)
- **Governance style**: Polarizing — accused of democratic backsliding during his third term (2020-2022), including pressure on judiciary and media
- **Foreign policy**: Strongly pro-NATO, pro-Ukraine, critical of EU bureaucracy but not exit-advocating
- **Hungarian alignment**: Close personal and political ties with Viktor Orbán; SDS has been a member of the European Conservatives and Reformists (ECR) party group

## Forecasting Significance

Janša's effort to form a government after the 2026 hung parliament tests several forecasting concepts:
- **Incumbent comeback after electoral decline**: SDS lost the 2022 election but has now returned to governing contention. This is a canonical case of the populist successor returns to power after defeat pattern.
- **Fragmented center-right coalition formation**: Requires stitching together SDS (28), NSi (9), Democrats (6), and Resni.ca (5) — four parties with distinct ideological profiles
- **Hung parliament resolution timeline**: Slovenia's constitution lacks a strict deadline for government formation, creating uncertainty about how long the crisis can persist

## Timeline

- **2004-2008**: First term as PM — led center-right coalition; oversaw Slovenia's EU presidency (2008)
- **2012-2013**: Second term — cut short by corruption allegations (confirmed by anti-corruption commission)
- **2018**: SDS wins most seats but cannot form government; Šarec becomes PM
- **2020-2022**: Third term — appointed during pandemic; controversial media law targeting RTV Slovenija
- **2022**: Lost election to Robert Golob's Freedom Movement (GS) — SDS 27%, GS 34.5%
- **2024-10-14**: Anže Logar leaves SDS, founding Democrats
- **2026-03-22**: Election — SDS wins 28 seats, second place
- **2026-05-06**: Janša begins coalition formation talks
- **2026-05-19**: Submits mandate candidacy with 48 signatures
