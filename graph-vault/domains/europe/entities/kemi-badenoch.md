# Kemi Badenoch

## Summary

Kemi Badenoch is a British Conservative Party politician and former Cabinet minister under Rishi Sunak (Business and Trade Secretary, 2023-2024). She has been a leading figure in the Conservative Party's right wing and a contender for the party leadership following the 2024 general election defeat. A social conservative and economic liberal, Badenoch represents the faction that argues the Conservatives lost in 2024 because they abandoned conservative principles rather than because they moved too far right.

## Significance

Badenoch (alongside Robert Jenrick) represents the likely future of the Conservative Party leadership. The direction the Conservatives take — whether toward the center or further right — will determine the UK's political alignment over the next electoral cycle. Badenoch's ascendancy would signal a rightward consolidation of the Tory party, while a more centrist leader would signal a bid to recapture moderate voters lost to Labour and Liberal Democrats.
