# Anže Logar — Historical Note

> **This page is a corrected historical stub.** The pre-March 2026 version of this entity described Logar as an SDS member, MEP, and PM frontrunner — which was materially wrong after October 2024. This stub records the corrected facts.

## Corrected Biography

Logar left the Slovenian Democratic Party (SDS) on **14 October 2024**, ending his association with Janez Janša's party. He founded **Democrats (Demokrati Anzeta Logarja)** on **14 November 2024**.

In the 22 March 2026 parliamentary election, Logar's Democrats won **6.69% of the vote and 6 seats** in its first electoral contest. Logar ran in the Logatec constituency but did not win a seat. Despite not winning a personal seat, his party holds a pivotal position in the hung parliament.

Logar negotiated with both incumbent PM Robert Golob (GS) and opposition leader Janez Janša (SDS) during the post-election coalition talks. His negotiations with Golob collapsed in mid-April 2026, with GS accusing Logar of "political deception."

**Current role**: Swing party leader in government formation negotiations. The Democrats' 6 seats are decisive for any majority coalition.

## Forecasting Significance

Logar's trajectory represents a counter-example to the "populist succession" pattern observed in other European center-right parties (Fidesz, PiS). Instead of inheriting SDS from Janša, Logar's departure split the center-right vote. This makes Logar a case study in **failed party succession → factional breakaway → hung parliament dynamics**.
