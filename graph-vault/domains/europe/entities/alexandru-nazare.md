# Alexandru Nazare

## Summary

Alexandru Nazare is a Romanian economist and politician, former Minister of Finance (2020-2021) under Prime Minister Florin Cîțu (PNL). He is a member of the National Liberal Party (PNL). As of June 2026, he is listed as a candidate in Polymarket's "Next Prime Minister of Romania" market with 7.0% implied probability (~$99K volume).

## Current Position (June 2026)

- **Role**: Candidate for Prime Minister of Romania (PNL-affiliated)
- **Market probability**: 15.3% Yes on Polymarket ($99K volume, as of June 23) — surged from 6.3% (Δ+9.0pp)
- **Leading candidates**: Sorin Grindeanu (PSD, ~41%), Dan Motreanu (PNL, ~13%), Ilie Bolojan (PNL/caretaker acting president, ~11%)
- **Party dynamics**: PNL holds the acting presidency (Bolojan) and has multiple PM candidates. PSD (Grindeanu) leads the market, reflecting the largest parliamentary party's advantage.

## Background

- **Minister of Finance** (Dec 2020 - Jul 2021): Served in the Cîțu government, overseeing Romania's COVID recovery budget and EU Resilience Facility negotiations.
- **Member of European Parliament** (interim, 2021)
- **Technocratic profile**: Economist background, seen as a competent but not charismatic figure.
- **PNL internal dynamics**: PNL has been fractured since the 2024 election annulment and leadership changes. Nazare represents the party's technocratic wing rather than its political machine.

## Forecasting Significance

- **Rising mid-tier candidate**: At 15.3% (surged from 6.3%), Nazare has gained momentum. The +9pp swing suggests either: (a) PNL coalition dynamics shifting toward a Nazare premiership, (b) coordinated market buying based on insider information, or (c) broader PM race repricing.
