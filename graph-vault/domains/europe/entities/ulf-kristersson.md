# Ulf Kristersson

## Structural Position
- **Role**: Prime Minister of Sweden (since October 2022)
- **Party**: Moderate Party (Moderaterna, M) — leader since 2017
- **Government**: Center-right coalition (M + Christian Democrats + Liberals) with Sweden Democrats (SD) confidence-and-supply
- **Next election**: September 13, 2026 — seeking re-election

## Political Profile
- **Age**: 62 (born December 1963)
- **Style**: Technocratic, consensus-oriented, moderate conservative
- **Key policies**: Immigration restriction (post-2015 pivot), crime/gang violence crackdown, NATO integration, energy (nuclear expansion)
- **Coalition management**: Navigates between centrist coalition partners (Liberals, Christian Democrats) and far-right SD support party — a fragile arrangement

## Electoral Position (2026)
- Right bloc polling ~46-48% vs left bloc ~50-52% (as of June 2026)
- SD (far-right) polling as largest right-bloc party (~20%) but excluded from cabinet
- Moderate Party polling ~18-20%, down from 19.1% in 2022
- Key vulnerability: gang violence has continued despite tough-on-crime rhetoric — undermines core campaign promise
