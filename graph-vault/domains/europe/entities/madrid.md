# Madrid

## Summary

Madrid is the capital and largest city of Spain, with a population of ~3.26M (city proper) and ~6.7M (metropolitan area). Located in central Spain on the Manzanares River at an elevation of ~650m, Madrid has a Mediterranean climate with continental influences (Köppen: Csa), characterized by hot, dry summers and cool, sometimes cold winters. Annual precipitation is ~400mm, concentrated in spring and fall, with a high diurnal temperature range due to its elevation and continental position. The weather authority is AEMET (Agencia Estatal de Meteorología). Madrid serves as Spain's political capital (seat of government and monarchy), economic center, and transport hub, connecting the Iberian Peninsula.
