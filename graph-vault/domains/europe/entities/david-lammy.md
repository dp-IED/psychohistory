# David Lammy

## Summary

David Lammy is a British Labour Party politician serving as Foreign Secretary under Prime Minister Keir Starmer since July 2024. An Atlanticist and pro-European, Lammy has positioned UK foreign policy in the Starmer era as firmly aligned with NATO, the EU (on security and foreign policy), and the US. He has focused on maintaining strong Ukraine support, managing UK-EU relations post-Brexit, and addressing the Israel-Gaza conflict and its regional ramifications.

## Significance

Lammy sets the UK's foreign policy direction, which intersects with numerous forecasting domains: UK-EU relations, NATO burden-sharing, Middle East diplomacy, China policy, and the Ukraine war. His pro-European Atlanticism represents a sharp break from the previous Conservative government's approach, particularly on EU engagement.
