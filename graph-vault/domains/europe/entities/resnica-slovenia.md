# Resni.ca (Truth)

Resni.ca (meaning "Truth" in Slovenian) is an anti-establishment political party that entered the Slovenian National Assembly for the first time in the March 22, 2026 election. Its 5 seats give it outsized influence in the hung parliament, and its leader Zoran Stevanović's election as Speaker amplifies that influence further.

## Kingmaker Dynamics

Resni.ca occupies a unique position as an anti-system kingmaker:
- It has stated it will NOT formally join an SDS-led coalition (avoiding the political costs of governing)
- But it supports Janša as PM candidate (providing the necessary votes for a mandate)
- This creates a **confidence-and-supply** arrangement rather than a formal coalition — more fragile, as Resni.ca can withdraw support without collapsing its own government role
- As Speaker, Stevanović controls the legislative agenda, giving Resni.ca procedural leverage beyond its 5 seats

## Forecasting Relevance

Resni.ca exemplifies the **anti-system kingmaker** archetype that is emerging across European hung parliaments:
- Small anti-system parties hold disproportionate power in fragmented legislatures
- They support governments without joining them, avoiding accountability
- The Speaker role amplifies their influence beyond seat count
- This pattern is likely to recur in other European elections as mainstream parties fragment
