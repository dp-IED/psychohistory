# Wes Streeting

Wes Streeting is a British Labour Party politician serving as Secretary of State for Health and Social Care. An MP for Ilford North since 2015, he is considered a potential future Labour leadership contender and a prominent moderate/centre-right figure in the party.

## Political Positioning

- **Faction**: Labour centrist/right — associated with the Progress group and Labour First
- **Key allies**: Rachel Reeves (Chancellor), David Lammy (Foreign Secretary), aligned with Keir Starmer's internal party reforms
- **Notable stances**: Pro-NHS reform, private-sector partnership in healthcare delivery, tough on crime, pro-Israel, anti-Corbyn left
- **Personal background**: First in his family to attend university (Cambridge); openly gay; from working-class East End background
