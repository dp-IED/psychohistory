# Liz Truss

## Summary

Liz Truss became Prime Minister of the United Kingdom on September 6, 2022, after winning the Conservative Party leadership contest against Rishi Sunak. On September 23, Chancellor Kwasi Kwarteng delivered a "mini-budget" announcing approximately £45 billion in unfunded tax cuts, which triggered a severe financial crisis: the pound fell to a record low against the US dollar, UK gilt yields surged, and the Bank of England was forced to launch an emergency bond-buying program on September 28 to restore market stability.

## Significance

Truss's premiership faces an immediate test of market confidence. The mini-budget crisis raises questions about her government's fiscal credibility and longevity. As of September 30, 2022, it is unclear whether she will survive the political fallout — she could become one of the shortest-serving prime ministers in British history if confidence erodes further.
