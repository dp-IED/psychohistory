# Slovenian Democratic Party (SDS)

The Slovenian Democratic Party is the dominant center-right force in Slovenian politics. Under Janez Janša's leadership since 1993, it has governed Slovenia for three non-consecutive terms. The party is ideologically national-conservative and Eurosceptic within an EU framework.

## 2026 Election

The SDS won 28 seats (27.88%) in the 2026 election — a slight gain from 27 seats in 2022. However, Anže Logar's breakaway Democrats (6 seats) fractured the center-right, preventing a straightforward SDS+NSi (37 seats) government. The SDS needs both Democrats and Resni.ca to reach 46 seats.

## Forecasting Relevance

SDS's electoral trajectory tests the durability of dominant populist parties after leadership splits. The Logar defection (October 2024) created a structural split in the Slovenian center-right that mirrors the Fidesz succession crisis and the Czech ODS fragmentation. The question is whether SDS can reabsorb the Democrats or whether Logar establishes a permanent rival center-right party.
