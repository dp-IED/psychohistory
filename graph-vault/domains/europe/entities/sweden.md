# Sweden

## Structural Position
- **Government**: Center-right coalition (Moderates + Christian Democrats + Liberals) with Sweden Democrats (SD) confidence-and-supply
- **Prime Minister**: Ulf Kristersson (Moderate Party) since October 2022
- **Next election**: September 13, 2026 (fixed 4-year term)
- **Parliament**: Riksdag (349 seats, 4% threshold, proportional representation)
- **EU/NATO**: EU member since 1995, NATO member since March 2024

## Political Landscape (2026)
- **Right bloc**: Moderates (M), Christian Democrats (KD), Liberals (L) + Sweden Democrats (SD, far-right, outside cabinet)
- **Left bloc**: Social Democrats (S), Left Party (V), Green Party (MP), Centre Party (C)
- Current polls show left bloc slightly ahead but within margin of error
- Key issues: immigration, crime/gang violence, energy policy, NATO integration
