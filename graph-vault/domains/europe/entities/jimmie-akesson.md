# Jimmie Åkesson

## Structural Position
- **Role**: Party Leader, Sweden Democrats (SD) — since 2005
- **Party**: Sweden Democrats (Sverigedemokraterna, SD) — far-right, largest right-bloc party in polls
- **Government role**: Confidence-and-supply to Kristersson government (not in cabinet)
- **Next election**: September 13, 2026

## Political Profile
- **Age**: 47 (born May 1979)
- **Style**: Populist, confrontational, anti-establishment
- **Key policies**: Immigration restriction (zero asylum), crime (harsh penalties), anti-EU (Swexit referendum), welfare chauvinism
- **Party history**: SD has neo-Nazi roots (founded 1988), Åkesson led professionalization and electoral breakthrough

## Electoral Position (2026)
- SD polling ~19-22% — largest right-bloc party
- Kingmaker role: no right-bloc government possible without SD seats
- Paradox: SD's strength hurts Moderates (vote splitting); if SD becomes too large, centrist coalition partners may defect
- Coalition tension: Liberals and Christian Democrats uncomfortable with SD's far-right positioning; SD wants cabinet seats but coalition partners resist

## Polymarket Relevance
- Åkesson's SD is the structural variable that determines Kristersson's PM chances. If SD support remains stable and centrist parties tolerate the arrangement, Kristersson has a path. If SD surges (taking Moderate votes) or coalition tensions fracture the right bloc, Kristersson loses.
