### Party Breakdown
- **PSD** (Social Democrats): Largest parliamentary party. Grindeanu is the leading PM candidate at 41.4%.
- **PNL** (National Liberals): Multiple candidates (Motreanu, Bolojan, Nazare, Predoiu) splitting party probability. Bolojan is acting president.
- **PSD+PNL probability**: Combined ~70% — market expects PM from the two major parties.
- **Technocrat/Independent probability**: ~29% (Polymarket "next PM is technocrat" at 25.0%).

## Structural Dynamics

### Constitutional Crisis
- December 2024: Constitutional Court annuls presidential election runoff (Georgescu vs Lasconi)
- Ilie Bolojan (PNL) becomes acting president
- No date set for new presidential election
- PM selection operates in constitutional gray zone

### Coalition Arithmetic
- PSD holds parliamentary plurality
- PNL is the junior coalition partner (or rival, depending on configuration)
- UDMR (Hungarian minority party) often plays kingmaker
- AUR (far-right) excluded from mainstream coalitions
- USR (reformist) alienated after 2024 crisis

### Key Scenarios
1. **PSD-led government**: Grindeanu or another PSD figure becomes PM (41% probability)
2. **PNL-led government**: Requires PNL to claim PM slot in coalition negotiations (25-30%)
3. **Technocrat compromise**: An independent/technocrat PM appointed to break deadlock (25%)
4. **Snap elections**: Constitutional crisis forces new parliamentary elections (5-10%)
