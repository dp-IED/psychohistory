# France Domain

## Overview

France is a major European power with a semi-presidential system. The 2027 presidential election (April-May 2027) is the most active forecasting topic in this domain. Emmanuel Macron is term-limited and cannot run again, creating the first open presidential race since 2017.

## Forecasting Relevance

France is a recurring prediction market domain (presidential elections, legislative majorities, European policy influence). The 2027 election is a structural inflection point: first non-Macron race since 2017, RN's highest-ever polling position, and left fragmentation that may produce a three-way runoff scenario.

Key uncertainty factors:
1. Le Pen's legal eligibility for 2027 (EU funds verdict expected 2026)
2. Bardella's announcement timing and campaign positioning
3. Centrist fragmentation: Philippe, Attal, and others splitting the Macronist vote
4. Left unification: can LFI, Socialists, and Greens coalesce behind one candidate?
5. Cordon sanitaire erosion: will the republican front hold against a stronger RN?
6. Cohabitation risk: legislative elections 4-6 weeks after presidential; a President without a legislative majority would face the first cohabitation since 2002
