# Sébastien Lecornu

## Summary

Sébastien Lecornu (born June 11, 1986) is a French politician serving as Prime Minister since September 2025. He succeeded François Bayrou after the Bayrou government was ousted by a no-confidence vote on September 9, 2025. Lecornu previously served as Minister of the Armed Forces (2022-2025) under Prime Ministers Élisabeth Borne and Gabriel Attal.
