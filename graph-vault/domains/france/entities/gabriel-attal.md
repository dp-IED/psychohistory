# Gabriel Attal

## Overview

Gabriel Attal (b. 1989) is a French centrist politician who served as Prime Minister of France from January to September 2024 — the youngest PM in French history and the first openly gay head of government. A member of Renaissance (formerly En Marche!), he served as Minister of National Education before becoming PM. After the July 2024 snap legislative election produced a hung parliament, he resigned as PM but remains a key figure in Macron's party.

## Political Position

- **Party**: Renaissance
- **Role**: Former Prime Minister (Jan-Sep 2024), former Education Minister
- **Previous**: Government Spokesperson, Budget Minister, MP for Hauts-de-Seine
- **Ideology**: Centrist, pro-EU, Macronist continuity, socially liberal, education reformer
- **Base**: Younger voters, Macron loyalists, Parisian professional class

## 2027 Presidential Candidacy Status

As of May 2026, Attal has NOT formally declared a 2027 candidacy. He is considered a potential Renaissance candidate but faces structural challenges:

1. **Youth and experience**: At 37 (in 2027), Attal would be France's youngest-ever presidential candidate if elected. His brief 8-month premiership provides limited executive track record.
2. **Centrist field congestion**: The centrist space is crowded with potential Macron-successor candidates: Édouard Philippe (Horizons), Bruno Le Maire (Renaissance/Renaissance-aligned), and potentially others. Attal's claim to the Renaissance nomination is not exclusive.
3. **Name recognition**: As former PM, Attal has high name recognition and favorable ratings among Macron voters, but limited penetration outside the centrist bubble.
4. **Political rhythm**: Attal is 37 — he may calculate that his political future extends beyond 2027, making a 2027 run premature if polling suggests a first-round exit.
