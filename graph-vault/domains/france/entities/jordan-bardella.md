# Jordan Bardella

## Overview

Jordan Bardella (b. 1995) is the president of the French far-right party National Rally (Rassemblement National, RN) since November 2022, succeeding Marine Le Pen. He led the RN's European Parliament election campaign in June 2024, which won 31.4% of the vote — the highest for any French party. He is widely considered the RN's presumptive candidate for the 2027 French presidential election, though Marine Le Pen has not formally ruled out running herself.

## Political Position

- **Party**: Rassemblement National (RN), formerly Front National
- **Role**: Party President (since Nov 2022)
- **Previous**: MEP (2019-2024), RN Vice President, parliamentary candidate
- **Ideology**: Far-right, French nationalist, anti-immigration, Euroskeptic (softened in 2024 campaign to "Europe of nations")

## 2027 Presidential Candidacy Status

As of May 2026, Bardella has NOT formally announced his candidacy for the 2027 French presidential election. The RN's candidate selection process is controlled by Marine Le Pen, who leads the party's parliamentary group and remains its dominant figure. The decision is expected to hinge on:

1. **Le Pen's legal situation**: If Le Pen's 2027 eligibility is compromised by legal rulings, Bardella becomes the automatic candidate
2. **Party strategy**: Whether the RN calculates Bardella's youth and "new face" appeal will outperform Le Pen's experience
3. **Polling dynamics**: Head-to-head polling vs Macron's successor and left-wing candidates
