# Marine Le Pen

## Overview

Marine Le Pen (b. 1968) is a French far-right politician, three-time presidential candidate (2012, 2017, 2022), and president of the National Rally (RN) parliamentary group in the National Assembly. She is the daughter of Jean-Marie Le Pen, founder of the National Front (now RN). She led the RN from 2011 to 2022, stepping down as party president to focus on her parliamentary role, but remains the party's dominant figure and gatekeeper for the 2027 presidential nomination.

## Political Position

- **Party**: Rassemblement National (RN), formerly Front National
- **Role**: President of RN parliamentary group (since 2022); party president 2011-2022
- **Previous**: MEP (2004-2017), party founder's daughter
- **Ideology**: Far-right, sovereignist, anti-immigration, Euroskeptic, welfare chauvinist
- **Electoral trajectory**: 2012 (17.9%, 3rd place), 2017 (21.3% → 33.9% runoff), 2022 (23.1% → 41.5% runoff)

## 2027 Presidential Candidacy Status

As of May 2026, Le Pen has NOT formally announced a 2027 candidacy. Her eligibility is uncertain due to ongoing judicial proceedings:

1. **EU parliamentary assistant funds case**: Le Pen and 24 other RN officials are on trial for allegedly using EU parliamentary funds to pay party staff. Prosecutors seek a 5-year ban from public office and a potential prison sentence. A ruling is expected in 2026. If she is convicted and the ban takes effect before 2027, she would be ineligible.
2. **Party leadership dynamics**: Le Pen controls the RN's candidate selection. She has maintained ambiguity about her own candidacy while Jordan Bardella (party president since 2022) positions as the presumptive successor.
3. **Strategic calculus**: Le Pen may choose to step aside in favor of Bardella regardless of legal outcome — his youth and less polarizing image could outperform her in a runoff. However, her personal ambition and party dominance (she holds the parliamentary group) make a voluntary withdrawal uncertain.

## Forecasting Significance

Le Pen's legal eligibility and candidacy decision is the single biggest uncertainty factor in the 2027 French presidential election. Key variables:
- Timing of the EU funds verdict (before or after the official campaign period begins)
- Whether the court imposes an immediate or suspended electoral ban
- Whether Le Pen pre-empts a ban by endorsing Bardella, or fights to run
- The effect of a conviction on RN voter turnout (victimization vs abandonment)
