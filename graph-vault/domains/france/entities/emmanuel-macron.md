# Emmanuel Macron

President of France since 2017. Leader of Renaissance (formerly En Marche!).

## Significance

Central figure in French politics, EU integration, and European response to geopolitical crises. **Term-limited** and cannot run in the 2027 presidential election — this makes him a lame-duck incumbent for forecasting purposes, with his influence over succession and centrist coalition cohesion as the primary forecasting variables.

## 2027 Election Context

Macron is constitutionally term-limited (Article 6, Fifth Republic: maximum two consecutive terms). His departure creates an open presidential race for the first time since 2017. Key forecasting implications:

- **Successor dynamics**: Macron's Renaissance coalition lacks a clear successor. Potential Macron-aligned candidates include Édouard Philippe (Horizons), Gabriel Attal, and Bruno Le Maire. The centrist vote will likely fragment without Macron's personal appeal.
- **Lame-duck influence**: As of May 2026 (~11 months from the election), Macron's legislative influence is constrained by his term-limited status and RN's dominant legislative position.
- **EU leadership vacuum**: Macron has been the driving force behind EU strategic autonomy, defense spending, and Ukraine support. His succession could shift France's EU posture, affecting European forecasting across multiple domains.

## Timeline

- 2017-05-14: Elected President of France (defeated Marine Le Pen in runoff)
- 2022-04-24: Re-elected (defeated Marine Le Pen, 58.5% to 41.5%)
- 2024-06-09: Called snap legislative election after EU Parliament defeat
- 2024-07-07: Snap election resulted in hung parliament (RN largest bloc ~37%, left alliance NFP second)
- 2025-present: Governing with minority support, RN as dominant opposition
- 2027-05: Must leave office (term-limited)
