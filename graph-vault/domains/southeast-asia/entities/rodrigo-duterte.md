# Rodrigo Duterte

## Summary

Rodrigo Duterte was President of the Philippines from 2016 to 2022, known for his "war on drugs" that killed thousands. On March 11, 2025, he was arrested on an International Criminal Court (ICC) warrant for crimes against humanity for extrajudicial killings during the drug war. He was transferred to The Hague and remains in ICC custody pending trial as of Q2 2026.

## Significance for Forecasting

Duterte's ICC arrest and ongoing detention create multiple forecast-relevant dynamics:

1. **ICC precedent**: First former head of state from an Asian democracy to face ICC prosecution. The case tests the ICC's legitimacy and effectiveness in a region where China and ASEAN states have resisted international legal oversight.

2. **Philippine domestic politics**: Duterte's detention reshapes the Marcos-Duterte alliance. Sara Duterte (vice president and Duterte's daughter) faces a political choice: distance from father to preserve her 2028 candidacy, or champion his cause and risk association with the drug war.

3. **Public opinion dynamics**: Duterte retains substantial domestic support (the drug war was popular). ICC detention may increase his political stock — martyrdom effect — or erode it as the evidence becomes public. Monitoring Philippine polling on the ICC and Duterte is essential.

4. **State cooperation precedent**: The Marcos Jr. government cooperated with the ICC arrest, a significant departure from Duterte-era hostility (Philippines withdrew from Rome Statute in 2019, effective March 2019). This cooperation may have been influenced by US pressure, Marcos's own political calculations, or the ICC's legal persistence.
