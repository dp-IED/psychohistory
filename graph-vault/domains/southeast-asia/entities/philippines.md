# Philippines

## Summary

The Republic of the Philippines is an archipelagic state in Southeast Asia (~115M population), a US treaty ally, and a claimant in the South China Sea dispute. Its political system features elite family dynasties, a presidential system with a single six-year term (no re-election), and a bicameral Congress (Senate elected nationally, House from districts).

## Significance for Forecasting

1. **US alliance and basing**: The Philippines is a frontline state in US-China strategic competition. The Enhanced Defense Cooperation Agreement (EDCA) grants US access to 9+ military bases, making the Philippines a critical platform for any Taiwan contingency or South China Sea operation.

2. **South China Sea**: The 2016 UNCLOS arbitration ruling (Philippines v. China) is the legal foundation for challenging China's nine-dash line claims. Chinese maritime militia and PLAN activity around Scarborough Shoal, Second Thomas Shoal, and Sabina Shoal has escalated after a period of post-2016 deterrence. The Philippines' enforcement capacity and willingness to invoke the Mutual Defense Treaty are critical escalation variables.

3. **Elite family politics**: The Marcos-Duterte alliance (forming the "UniTeam" coalition that won in 2022) is the dominant political alignment. The alliance's durability depends on the 2028 presidential succession, the Duterte ICC case, and Sara Duterte's political ambitions.

4. **Economic vulnerability**: As a middle-income country with significant exports to the US (electronics, semiconductors, agricultural products), the Philippines is exposed to Trump tariff policies. Overseas Filipino Workers (OFW) remittances (~10% of GDP) provide a buffer but also expose the economy to labor-market conditions in the Gulf states and the US.

5. **Domestic security**: Communist insurgency (NPA/CPP, diminished but still active), Islamist militancy in Mindanao (remnants of Maute group, ASG after Marawi 2017), and the drug war legacy.

## Key Governance Periods

- **2016-2022**: Rodrigo Duterte presidency (war on drugs, pivot to China, ICC withdrawal)
- **2022-2028**: Bongbong Marcos presidency (pivot to US, EDCA expansion, tariff exposure, Duterte arrest)
- **2025-05-12**: Midterm elections — Marcos's Alyansa wins Senate majority
- **2028**: Next presidential election (Sara Duterte as potential frontrunner)
