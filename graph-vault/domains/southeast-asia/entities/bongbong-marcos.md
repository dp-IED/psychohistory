# Ferdinand "Bongbong" Marcos Jr.

## Summary

Ferdinand Romualdez Marcos Jr. (commonly known as Bongbong Marcos or BBM) is the 17th and current President of the Philippines, elected in May 2022 in a landslide. He is the son of former dictator Ferdinand Marcos Sr., who was ousted in the 1986 People Power Revolution. Marcos Jr. inherited a political coalition that includes the Duterte family (Sara Duterte is his vice president) and a mix of traditional political clans and military allies.

## Significance for Forecasting

Marcos Jr.'s presidency is forecast-relevant along several dimensions:

1. **Elite family alliance**: The Marcos-Duterte alliance is the foundation of his governing coalition. The Duterte ICC arrest creates tension — Marcos cooperated with the arrest, potentially straining the 2028 electoral alliance with Sara Duterte.

2. **US-Philippines alliance**: Marcos leaned toward the US more than Duterte did, expanding EDCA bases (Enhanced Defense Cooperation Agreement) and allowing more US military access during Taiwan Strait tensions. This is a key variable in South China Sea and potential Taiwan contingency scenarios.

3. **Domestic governance**: Marcos faces inflation, the Liberation Day tariff shock's impact on Philippine exports, and the post-Duterte recalibration of the drug war.

4. **2025 midterms**: The May 2025 Philippine midterm elections (which Marcos's Alyansa coalition won) tested the Marcos-Duterte coalition's durability.

## Key Dates

- **1957-09-13**: Born in Batac, Ilocos Norte
- **1980s-2010s**: Various provincial and national offices (governor, congressman, senator)
- **2016**: Ran for vice president, lost to Leni Robredo by narrow margin
- **2022-05-09**: Elected president in landslide (58.8%), with Sara Duterte as VP
- **2022-06-30**: Inaugurated as 17th President
- **2025-03-11**: Government cooperates with ICC arrest of Rodrigo Duterte
- **2025-05-12**: Alyansa coalition wins Senate majority
