# Sara Duterte-Carpio

## Summary

Sara Zimmerman Duterte-Carpio (commonly known as Sara Duterte) is the 15th and current Vice President of the Philippines and the daughter of former President Rodrigo Duterte. She was elected in 2022 as Bongbong Marcos's running mate under the UniTeam alliance. She served as Secretary of Education (2022-2025) concurrently with the vice presidency.

## Significance for Forecasting

Sara Duterte is the central figure in the Marcos-Duterte alliance's durability and the most likely 2028 presidential candidate:

1. **2028 presidential frontrunner**: As Duterte's daughter and the current VP, she is the natural candidate for the Duterte wing of the coalition. Her father's ICC arrest is a double-edged sword: it could galvanize the Duterte base (martyrdom effect) or hurt her with moderate voters who see the drug war as indefensible.

2. **Coalition loyalty test**: Did she support or condemn her father's ICC arrest? Did the Marcos administration consult her before cooperating with the ICC? These answers determine whether the Marcos-Duterte alliance survives to 2028.

3. **Education portfolio**: As Education Secretary, she inherited a system with learning losses from the pandemic, chronic underfunding, and the controversial K-12 curriculum. Her performance in this role is a primary determinant of her broader political viability.
