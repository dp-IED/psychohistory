# Southeast Asia Domain

## Overview

Southeast Asia comprises 11 states (Brunei, Cambodia, East Timor, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam) organized under ASEAN. The region's forecasting relevance spans:

- **Geopolitical rivalry**: US-China competition for influence via South China Sea disputes, ASEAN centrality, infrastructure financing, and military basing. The Philippines (US ally, Visiting Forces Agreement) and Vietnam (partial US partner, China threat) are key swing states.

- **Domestic political volatility**: Democratic backsliding (Thailand, Cambodia, Myanmar coup), elite family dynasties (Philippines: Marcos-Duterte; Indonesia: Widodo dynasty), Islamist populism, and communist-party authoritarianism.

- **Economic transformation**: Supply chain relocation from China ("China + 1" strategy), critical mineral processing (Indonesia/Philippines nickel), semiconductor assembly (Malaysia, Singapore, Vietnam), and digital economy growth.

- **Maritime security**: South China Sea disputes, UNCLOS arbitration (Philippines v. China, 2016), maritime militia tactics, Scarborough Shoal/Second Thomas Shoal flashpoints, piracy in the Singapore Strait.

- **Conflict zones**: Myanmar civil war (post-2021 coup), separatist insurgencies (southern Thailand, Philippines' Bangsamoro/Mindanao), Thailand-Cambodia border skirmishes.
