# Iran Domain

## Overview

Covers the Islamic Republic of Iran — its domestic politics, nuclear program, regional proxy network, and relations with the US, Israel, and Gulf states. The domain has become dramatically more active for forecasting following the June 2025 Twelve-Day War (US/Israeli strikes on Iranian nuclear facilities), the rapid ceasefire, and the subsequent US-Iran permanent peace deal negotiations — one of the fastest enemy-to-diplomacy transitions in modern history.

### Active Thread (Most Important)

The [[domains/iran/threads/us-iran-post-war-diplomacy/_thread]] tracks the US-Iran diplomatic track following the Twelve-Day War. The Polymarket deadline cascade resolved YES on June 14, 2026, with a Pakistan-mediated permanent peace deal. Khamenei was assassinated on February 28, 2026 (Operation Epic Fury). The succession fight between Mojtaba Khamenei (IRGC-backed) and clerical-establishment alternatives is the dominant uncertainty for 2026-2027.

### Second Critical Thread: Supreme Leader Succession

Khamenei was assassinated on February 28, 2026 (Operation Epic Fury). His succession is the single most consequential uncertainty in the MENA region for 2026-2027:

| Scenario | Probability (Implied) | Notes |
|----------|----------------------|-------|
| IRGC Continuity (Mojtaba Khamenei) | 66% (PM: $2.3M) | Market baseline — IRGC consolidation over clerical independence |
| Civilian-Clerical Compromise | 20-30% | Alireza Arafi (1.7%, $927K), Abbas Araghchi (4.8%, $279K) |
| Power Struggle / Rapid Turnover | 10-20% | Multiple claimants; first successor ousted within 1-2 years |
| Regime Fragmentation | 5-15% | IRGC vs civilian government becomes openly hostile |

The succession outcome determines everything: nuclear negotiation posture, proxy reconstitution speed, ceasefire durability, and Gulf-state alignment trajectory. See [[domains/iran/entities/ali-khamenei]] and [[domains/iran/entities/mojtaba-khamenei]] for detailed succession analysis.
