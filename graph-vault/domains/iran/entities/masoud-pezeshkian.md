## Summary
President of Iran (2024-). Inaugurated in July 2024. His tenure was defined by the escalating Iran-Israel conflict. During the Twelve-Day War, he reportedly sustained light injuries to his leg during an airstrike on a Supreme National Security Council meeting on June 15. As a relative moderate, Pezeshkian represented the diplomatic face of the Iranian regime during the crisis, though ultimate military authority rested with Khamenei.

## Significance for Forecasting
Pezeshkian's role illustrates the limited agency of elected presidents in the Iranian system during wartime. For forecasting, distinguishing which Iranian officials have real decision-making power (Khamenei, IRGC commanders) versus which are diplomatic figureheads (president, foreign minister) is critical for assessing ceasefire probability.
