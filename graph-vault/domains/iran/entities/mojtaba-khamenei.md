# Mojtaba Khamenei

## Summary

Mojtaba Khamenei is the second son of Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei and the leading candidate to succeed his father as Supreme Leader of Iran. Never holding elected office, he operates through informal influence within the IRGC, the Office of the Supreme Leader, and the Basij paramilitary. He is the clear frontrunner in Polymarket's "Iran leader end of 2026?" contract at **66.45% YES** ($2.3M volume as of May 2026).

## Key Attributes

- **IRGC Patronage**: Deep ties to the IRGC Quds Force and intelligence apparatus, cultivated over two decades as his father's informal gatekeeper
- **Clerical Credentials**: Studied at Qom seminary under Ayatollah Mesbah Yazdi (hardline ideologue); sufficient for Assembly of Experts approval
- **No Electoral Track Record**: Never contested or held elected office — governed entirely through the Supreme Leader's office infrastructure
- **Generational Outsider**: If appointed, would be the first Supreme Leader without personal memory of the 1979 Revolution (he was 9 at the time)
- **Succession Risk**: His elevation would represent IRGC consolidation over clerical independence, potentially triggering pushback from traditional seminaries and civilian pragmatists
