# Iran Nuclear Enrichment — Post-War Status

## Current State (June 2026)

Iran's nuclear enrichment infrastructure was **physically destroyed** during the June 2025 Twelve-Day War:
- **Fordow Fuel Enrichment Plant**: B-2 strike, confirmed destroyed (IAEA verification pending)
- **Natanz Enrichment Complex**: Centrifuge halls destroyed; underground halls damaged
- **Isfahan Nuclear Technology Center**: Research reactors and conversion facility destroyed

Breakout timeline extended from weeks (pre-war, with 60% enriched stockpile) to **years** (post-war, zero enrichment capacity, zero stockpile above 3.67%).

## Key Distinction: Capacity vs. Commitment

- **Capacity**: Iran cannot enrich uranium at scale — physical destruction is complete
- **Commitment**: Iran has NOT formally agreed to permanently end enrichment as a diplomatic/treaty commitment
- The June 14, 2026 peace deal terminates military operations but does NOT explicitly address enrichment renunciation
- Iran's historical position (pre- and post-revolution): enrichment is a sovereign right under NPT Article IV

## Post-Khamenei Policy Uncertainty

- Khamenei assassinated Feb 28, 2026 (Operation Epic Fury)
- Succession fight: Mojtaba Khamenei (IRGC-backed) vs clerical-establishment alternatives
- No post-Khamenei leader has the political capital to permanently renounce enrichment
- Enrichment renunciation would be weaponized by succession rivals as "capitulation to the Great Satan"
