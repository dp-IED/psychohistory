# PPA Tour

The **Professional Pickleball Association (PPA) Tour** is the premier professional pickleball tour in the United States. Founded in 2022, it has rapidly expanded as pickleball has grown into a major participation sport.

## Forecasting Significance

- **Emerging market**: Pickleball forecasting on Polymarket is nascent, with very thin liquidity (typically <$500 USDC per market)
