# PPA Pickleball Tour – Emerging Forecasting Domain

## Domain Status

Pickleball forecasting on Polymarket is **nascent**. The PPA Tour appears in thin-liquidity tournament and match markets. This thread tracks the development of pickleball as a forecasting domain and catalogs the analytical tools needed.

## Structural Characteristics

| Factor | Assessment |
|--------|-----------|
| Liquidity | <$500 USDC typical — noise market territory |
| Partnership stability | Low — pairs shift frequently between tournaments |
| Rating system | DUPR (less granular than ATP/WTA rankings) |
| Tournament format | Variable (round-robin pools, knockout) |
| Observable history | Very limited for most pairings |

## The Sports Pairing Chemistry Challenge

PPA doubles markets are the best current example of the [[domains/global/concepts/sports-pairing-chemistry]] problem: when a doubles pairing has never played together before, individual skill statistics are near-useless for forecasting. The correct prior for any untested pairing winning a tournament is the base rate of newly-formed pairs in the same event, which tends to be very low.
