# Domain: Religion

## Scope

This domain covers religious institutions whose leadership transitions, governance changes, or institutional decisions are forecast-relevant. The primary focus is the **Catholic Church** (the world's largest religious institution with 1.4 billion members, sovereign state status via Vatican City, and recurring prediction-market questions about papal succession, health, and institutional direction).

## Coverage Rationale

Religious institutions are a **structural blind spot** in most geopolitical forecasting vaults. Yet:
- The Catholic Papacy is the world's oldest continuous institution with a defined succession mechanism
- Papal transitions are high-impact global media events that prediction markets routinely ask about
- Leadership health forecasting applies to popes the same way it applies to heads of state
- The Pope exercises significant soft power, diplomatic influence, and institutional authority
