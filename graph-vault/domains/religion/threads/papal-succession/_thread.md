# Papal Succession Thread

## Context

This thread tracks the health decline, death, and succession of Pope Francis (2013-2025), along with the election of Pope Leo XIV. Papal transitions are rare events — this was the first papal transition since 2013 (when Benedict XVI resigned), the first papal death since 2005 (John Paul II), and the first papal conclave since 2013. The transition is historically significant: the first pope from the Americas (Francis) was succeeded by the first US citizen pope (Leo XIV).

## Timeline

### 2024: Health Decline Phase

- **2024 Q1-Q4**: Pope Francis's health visibly deteriorates. He increasingly uses a wheelchair, cancels international travel, and delegates public appearances. Multiple respiratory infections reported. Vatican press office issues increasingly cautious health bulletins.
- **Late 2024**: Speculation intensifies about Francis's ability to continue. Some Vatican observers note he has rejected the idea of resigning (unlike Benedict XVI), stating he intends to "die in office."

### 2025: Death and Conclave

- **2025 April 21** (Easter Monday): Pope Francis dies at 07:35 CEST at Domus Sanctae Marthae, Vatican City, at age 88. His last public appearance was Easter Sunday.
- **2025 April 26**: Papal funeral at St. Peter's Square. Delegations from 164 countries attend, including 82 heads of state. Estimated 250,000 public attendees.
- **2025 May 7-8**: Papal conclave. Cardinal Robert Francis Prevost is elected on the 4th ballot, choosing the name Leo XIV.
- **2025 May 8**: Pope Leo XIV is announced to the world with the traditional "Habemus Papam" from St. Peter's Basilica.
- **2025 May**: Pope Leo XIV is inaugurated as Bishop of Rome.

## Succession Mechanics (For Forecasting)

The papal succession process is structured and predictable:

1. **Death/Resignation** → The Pope dies or resigns
2. **Sede Vacante** (vacant see) → The Cardinal Camerlengo administers the Church; the Roman Curia's authority lapses
3. **Novemdiales** → 9 days of mourning between death and conclave
4. **Conclave convenes** → Between 15-20 days after the death, in the Sistine Chapel
5. **Voting rounds** → 4 rounds per day (2 morning, 2 afternoon); 2/3 majority required
6. **Election** → White smoke (fumata bianca) when elected; "Habemus Papam" announcement
7. **Inauguration** → Mass to inaugurate the new pontificate

**Typical timeline**: From death to new pope ≈ 2-3 weeks. From the gold_28 question perspective, if Francis died on April 21, the new pope was elected on May 8 — only 17 days later. Any question asking "will there NOT be a new pope in 2025?" resolves NO if Francis died before approximately mid-December (allowing time for the post-death process).

## Forecasting Implications

- If the question asks about a "new pope," the trigger is the SAME pope dying or resigning, not the new pope being inaugurated in a separate process
- The post-death timeline is short (2-3 weeks), so the window for "no new pope" closes almost immediately after the current pope's death or resignation
- The question "Will there not be a new Pope in [year]?" is effectively asking: "Will Pope [current] survive through [year]?" — because if they die at any point, the conclave process ensures a successor within weeks
