# Middle East and North Africa

Geopolitical forecasting domain covering the MENA region: conflict dynamics,
ceasefire negotiations, great-power competition (US, Russia, China), Iran's
proxy network (Axis of Resistance), and regional state behavior.

## Key Threads

- **Israel-Hamas war and ceasefire** — Oct 2023 through Jan 2025 ceasefire,
  including the transition-window diplomacy that enabled it.

## Key Concepts

- **Transition-window ceasefire diplomacy** — US presidential transitions create
  a narrow window where both outgoing and incoming administrations have aligned
  incentives to push for a ceasefire, dramatically increasing the probability of
  agreement within the ~2-week window before inauguration.

## Events

- [[events/assad-regime-collapse-december-2024]] — Catalytic event degrading Iran's Axis of Resistance, enabling Jan 2025 Gaza ceasefire
- [[events/hezbollah-ceasefire-november-2024]] — Hezbollah-Israel ceasefire, removed second-front burden, isolated Hamas as part of 4-event cascade toward Gaza deal

## Coverage Status

This domain has been seeded from the seed30 blind-build experiment and significantly expanded through post-hoc structural analysis. Current coverage spans four primary sub-domains:

- **Israel-Hamas conflict** — Comprehensive: ceasefire cycles, negotiation dynamics, domestic Israeli politics, operational actors. Covers Oct 2023 through present with thread, 11 entities, 3 procedures, and 8+ concepts.
- **Iran-Israel shadow war to direct conflict** — Complete arc from asymmetric warfare (2020-2024) through Twelve-Day War (June 2025) and post-war succession dynamics. Covers escalation thresholds, proxy network structure, IRGC decision-making, and nuclear negotiations outlook.
- **Gaza ceasefire negotiations** — Multi-phase agreement framework with four-date cascade tracking, mediator-vs-party announcement distinction, collapse-and-restoration pattern, and canonical cases for procedural-certainty and pre-negotiated-framework-activation concepts.
- **Turkish monetary policy normalization** — EM central bank credibility normalization framework under Erdogan regime; TCMB governance, historical regimes, and political constraint dynamics.
- **Israeli domestic politics** — Coalition dynamics, party system fragmentation, Netanyahu's political durability, Likud succession, center-left decline, far-right influence. Thread covers Israeli PM succession forecasts.
- **Syria post-Assad transition** — HTS-led governance under Ahmed al-Sharaa, international recognition constraints, terrorist designation barrier, regional competition for influence.
- **Hezbollah** — Entity exists at [[domains/global/entities/hezbollah]] (general coverage). Post-Nasrallah reconstitution capacity, proxy autonomy dynamics post-Khamenei, Israel-Lebanon withdrawal implications. Entity enriched with cross-references to MENA domain, Lebanon entity, and withdrawal forecast.

**Remaining coverage gaps**: Houthi Red Sea escalation and its interaction with Gaza ceasefire phases; Palestinian state recognition cascade (partially addressed by [[domains/mena/entities/saudi-arabia]] — the Saudi normalization angle is now covered; the Palestinian institutional capacity dimension remains open).
