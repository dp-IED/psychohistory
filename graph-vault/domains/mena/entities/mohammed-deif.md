**Mohammed Deif** (born 1965, birth name Mohammed Diab Ibrahim al-Masri) was the commander of Hamas's military wing, the Al-Qassam Brigades, from 2002 until his death in 2024. He was one of Israel's most wanted figures for over two decades, surviving multiple assassination attempts. Deif was the architect of the October 7, 2023 attack on Israel.

**Status**: Deif's death was announced by Israel on August 1, 2024, following a July 13, 2024 airstrike in Khan Younis. Hamas never officially confirmed his death, but multiple sources in Hamas and regional intelligence agencies assessed he was killed. The ICC issued an arrest warrant for Deif (alongside Netanyahu and Gallant) on November 21, 2024 for war crimes, though he was presumed dead by that point.

**Forecasting significance**: Deif's death (like Sinwar's) was part of the leadership decapitation cascade that removed hardline decision-makers from Hamas's military command. His death made the remaining Hamas leadership more willing to consider ceasefire terms. The uncertainty around his death (Hamas never confirmed) created a period where ceasefire negotiations were hampered by ambiguity — Israel had to treat Deif as possibly alive in tunnel command.

**Key points**:
- Commander of Al-Qassam Brigades for 22+ years
- Survived 7+ Israeli assassination attempts before July 2024
- Mastermind of Oct 7, 2023 attack
- Presumed killed in Israeli airstrike July 13, 2024 (confirmed by Israel Aug 1)
- ICC arrest warrant issued Nov 21, 2024
- His removal contributed to Hamas's weakened negotiation position in late 2024
