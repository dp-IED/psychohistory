# Egypt

## Type
Nation-state; key diplomatic mediator in the Israel-Hamas conflict.

## Significance for Forecasting
Egypt shares a border with Gaza (Rafah crossing) and has been the traditional
mediator between Israel and Palestinian factions for decades. Its intelligence
service (General Intelligence Service, GIS) is the primary operational
negotiation channel. Egypt's core interest is preventing a Gaza spillover into
Sinai and maintaining its peace treaty with Israel.

## Key Dynamics
- Rafah crossing is the only Gaza border not controlled by Israel — gives Egypt
  leverage over humanitarian aid and evacuation
- Egypt's GIS director (Abbas Kamel) is the lead negotiator
- Egypt's security interest is preventing Hamas or other militant groups from
  operating in Sinai
- Works in coordination with Qatar and the US as the "mediation trio"
- Cairo is a major negotiation venue alongside Doha
