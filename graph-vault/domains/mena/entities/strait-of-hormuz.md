# Strait of Hormuz

The Strait of Hormuz is the world's most critical oil transit chokepoint. Approximately 20-21 million barrels per day of crude oil and petroleum products transit the Strait — roughly 20% of global consumption.

## Strategic Context

- **Width**: 33 km at narrowest point (between Iran and Oman)
- **Transit Lanes**: 2 nautical mile-wide inbound/outbound lanes with a 2 nm buffer zone
- **Iranian Control**: Iran's coastline dominates the northern side of the Strait; IRGC Navy has extensive anti-access/area-denial (A2/AD) capabilities
- **Omani Side**: Musandam Peninsula provides geographic balance

## Current Tensions (2026)

- **Post-Twelve-Day War (June 2025)**: Iran-Israel/US conflict raised Hormuz closure risk to highest since 1980s Tanker War
- **Ceasefire Holding**: As of May 2026, the Iran ceasefire is holding (99.9% on Polymarket)
- **Iranian Threats**: Iran has periodically threatened to close the Strait if attacked; IRGC fast-attack craft and anti-ship missiles deployable within hours
- **US Naval Presence**: US 5th Fleet (Bahrain) maintains continuous patrol presence; carrier strike groups rotate through
- **European Maritime Missions**: EMASoH (European-led) and individual nation contributions (UK, France, Italy) for maritime domain awareness

## Naval Deployment Frameworks

- **EMASoH**: European Maritime Awareness in the Strait of Hormuz — surveillance/non-escalatory mission led by France, later rotated. Italy, Denmark, Netherlands, Germany contribute.
- **Operation Sentinel**: US-led coalition focused on escort and deterrence
- **UK Maritime Component**: Historically HMS Montrose/HMS Lancaster deployments; independent command but coordinates with US/European allies

## Market-Relevant Dynamics

Naval deployments through Hormuz are typically:
1. **Routine rotations**: Pre-scheduled frigate/destroyer deployments announced 4-8 weeks in advance
2. **Crisis deployments**: Rapid reaction to seizure events (Iran has seized multiple tankers since 2019)
3. **Escalation signaling**: Major new deployment announcements signal heightened tension

The Polymarket "send warships through Hormuz by [date]" markets capture the intersection of these dynamics. A short-window deployment (30 days) requires either a pre-scheduled rotation already in the pipeline OR a crisis trigger event.
