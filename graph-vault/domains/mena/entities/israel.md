# Israel

## Type
Nation-state; key US ally in the Middle East.

## Significance for Forecasting
Israel is a central actor in MENA conflict dynamics. Its security cabinet and
full cabinet must approve ceasefire agreements. Its prime minister (Netanyahu)
has significant discretion over war/ceasefire decisions but faces coalition
constraints from far-right factions.

## Key Dynamics
- Coalition government structure gives small parties outsized influence on
  security decisions.
- Hostage families are a powerful domestic political force that can push for or
  against ceasefire.
- Defense establishment (IDF, Mossad, Shin Bet) often has different preferences
  from political leadership.
- US relationship is the single most important external factor in Israeli
  security decisions.

## Key Events Timeline
- Oct 7, 2023: Hamas attack triggers Gaza war
- Jan 15-17, 2025: Approves ceasefire with Hamas
- Mar 18, 2025: Resumes combat
