# Lebanon

Republic of Lebanon — Eastern Mediterranean nation. Parliamentary democracy with confessional power-sharing system. Shares border with Israel (south) and Syria (east/north).

## Political System

- **President**: Joseph Aoun (since January 2025)
- **Prime Minister**: Nawaf Salam (since January 2025)
- **Parliament Speaker**: Nabih Berri (since 1992)
- **System**: Confessional parliamentary republic — President must be Maronite Christian, PM Sunni Muslim, Speaker Shia Muslim
- **Hezbollah**: Shia Islamist political party and militant group, Iran-backed, designated terrorist organization by US/EU/UK/Arab League. Holds significant political and military power in Lebanon, particularly in the south (bordering Israel).

## Israel-Lebanon Dynamics

- **Hezbollah-Israel conflict**: Ongoing tensions since 2006 Lebanon War. Hezbollah maintains significant rocket/missile arsenal aimed at Israel.
- **Post-Twelve-Day War (June 2025)**: The Iran-Israel Twelve-Day War (June 2025) severely degraded Hezbollah's patron (Iran/IRGC). With Khamenei's removal (by March 2026) and IRGC command decimated, Hezbollah operates with increased autonomy — a "proxy autonomy risk" where actions are less constrained by Tehran.
- **Israeli ground presence**: Israel conducted ground operations in southern Lebanon following the escalation cycle. The presence of Israeli ground forces in Lebanese territory is tied to the broader Iran-Israel conflict resolution.
- **Diplomatic engagement**: Polymarket markets show 87% probability of Israel-Lebanon diplomatic meeting by June 30, 2026 — indicating active diplomacy. However, normalization before 2027 is priced at only 19%.

## Economic Situation

- Lebanon faces severe economic crisis since 2019: currency collapse, banking sector insolvency, >80% poverty rate.
- IMF bailout negotiations have stalled multiple times over structural reforms.
- The economic crisis constrains Hezbollah's domestic political position and reduces Lebanon's capacity for prolonged military confrontation.
