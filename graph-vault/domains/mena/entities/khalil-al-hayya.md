**Khalil al-Hayya** (born 1960) is a senior Hamas political leader who became the organization's lead negotiator in ceasefire talks following the death of Yahya Sinwar. He served as Sinwar's deputy in Gaza and was a member of Hamas's political bureau, representing the Gaza-based leadership in external negotiations.

**Forecasting significance**: Al-Hayya is the key figure to track for any future Israel-Hamas negotiation. After Sinwar's death (Oct 16, 2024), al-Hayya became the primary conduit for ceasefire negotiations with Israel, engaging with Qatari and Egyptian mediators. He is considered more pragmatic than Sinwar but remains aligned with the organization's core military objectives. Leadership succession dynamics within Hamas directly affect ceasefire probability — a more militant successor would reduce likelihood of diplomacy.

**Key roles**:
- Lead Hamas negotiator in Gaza ceasefire talks (Oct 2024–present)
- Head of Hamas politburo in Gaza (post-Sinwar)
- Primary interlocutor with Qatari and Egyptian mediators
- Spokesperson for Hamas ceasefire positions
