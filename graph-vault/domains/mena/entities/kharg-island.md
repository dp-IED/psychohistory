# Kharg Island (جزیره خارگ)

## Structural Position
- Iran's primary oil export terminal (~90% of Iranian crude exports)
- Located in Persian Gulf, ~25km off Iranian coast
- Island with terminal infrastructure, storage tanks, loading jetties
- Critical chokepoint: any disruption to Kharg Island = Iranian oil exports halted
- Heavily defended by IRGC Navy and coastal defense systems

## Strategic Significance
- Iran's economic lifeline: even partial disruption causes fiscal crisis
- 12-Day War (June 2025): Kharg Island was not seized by US/Israeli forces but may have been targeted
- Post-war: under Iranian control, but vulnerability exposed
- US blockade of Strait of Hormuz (in place since June 2025 war) restricts shipping in/out
- Blockade lifting (predicted by June 30 at 92% YES) would restore normal operations

## "No longer under Iranian control" Scenarios
- **Military seizure**: US/Israeli amphibious operation — extremely unlikely given Iran-Israel ceasefire at 99.9%
- **IRGC/internal coup**: Factional takeover by IRGC dissidents — possible but unverifiable
- **Blockade de facto control**: If US blockade prevents ALL Iranian use of the terminal, one could argue it's not "under Iranian control" functionally — but market resolution criteria likely requires territorial seizure
- **Khamenei succession chaos**: If Khamenei dies and IRGC factions fight, temporary loss of command possible

## Polymarket Context
- Market: "Kharg Island no longer under Iranian control by June 30?" at 0.9% YES ($995K volume)
- The 0.9% prices near-impossibility: 15 days remaining, ceasefire holding at 99.9%, zero operational indicators of seizure
- Even 0.9% may be generous — military seizure of Iran's primary oil terminal would effectively restart the war
- Most likely outcome: NO (Kharg remains under Iranian control). The 0.9% captures tail risk of Khamenei succession chaos enabling brief factional loss of control
