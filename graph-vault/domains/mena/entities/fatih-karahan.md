# Fatih Karahan

## Overview

Fatih Karahan is the current Governor of the Central Bank of the Republic of Turkey (TCMB), appointed on February 3, 2024, following the resignation of Hafize Gaye Erkan. He previously served as the TCMB's Deputy Governor in charge of research and monetary policy, joining the bank during the normalization era under Erkan.

## Background

- Previously an economist at Amazon and the Federal Reserve Bank of New York
- Holds a PhD in economics from the University of Pennsylvania
- Academic background in macroeconomics and monetary policy
- Appointed Deputy Governor in July 2023 as part of the post-election economic team overhaul
- Promoted to Governor when Erkan resigned amid a media controversy over nepotism allegations

## Forecasting Significance

- Karahan is widely viewed as a credible orthodox economist, continuing the policy direction set under Simsek's oversight
- His appointment was seen by markets as a signal of policy continuity (USD/TRY and CDS spreads barely moved on the announcement)
- He maintained the hawkish stance: rates were raised to 45% (Jan 2024), held, then raised further through 2024 as inflation persistence exceeded expectations
- The key forecasting question for any TCMB decision is whether Karahan has Erdogan's backing for the rate path. If Erdogan signals discontent, Karahan's independence — and the normalization itself — is at risk

## Key Dynamics

- **Continuity with Erkan's legacy**: Karahan was Erkan's deputy and co-authored the tightening strategy. His appointment signaled the normalization would continue.
- **Simsek as institutional shield**: Finance Minister [[mehmet-simsek]] acts as the political buffer between the TCMB and Erdogan. As long as Simsek remains in his role, Karahan's operational independence is protected.
- **Inflation report as primary signal**: Karahan's quarterly Inflation Report press conferences are the closest thing to Fed-style forward guidance for the TCMB. His inflation forecasts and tone signal the rate path.
