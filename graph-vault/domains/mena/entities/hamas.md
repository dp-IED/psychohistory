# Hamas

## Type
Palestinian Sunni-Islamist militant group; de facto governing authority in Gaza
from 2007 until 2025.

## Significance for Forecasting
Hamas is the primary adversary in the Gaza conflict. Its political leadership
is distributed across Gaza, Qatar, and Turkey. Ceasefire negotiations require
Hamas's acceptance, mediated through Qatar and Egypt.

## Key Dynamics
- Political and military wings have different decision-making structures.
- Leadership decapitation (killing of Sinwar, Oct 2024) can change negotiating
  posture dramatically.
- Hamas has accepted ceasefire frameworks (May 5, 2024) that Israel rejected,
  and vice versa — stated positions relative to a given framework are
  essential context.
- Military degradation does not necessarily equal willingness to negotiate;
  Hamas's political goals (permanent ceasefire, Israeli withdrawal,
  reconstruction) remain consistent.

## Key Personnel
- **Yahya Sinwar** (killed Oct 16, 2024): Most intransigent leader; his death
  was a key enabling condition for the Jan 2025 ceasefire.
- **Ismail Haniyeh** (killed Jul 31, 2024): Political leader based in Qatar.
- **Khaled Meshaal**: Former political leader; post-war leadership uncertain.

## Key Events Timeline
- Oct 7, 2023: Leads attack on Israel
- May 5, 2024: Accepts three-phase ceasefire framework
- Aug 6, 2024: Yahya Sinwar becomes political chief after Haniyeh's death
- Oct 16, 2024: Sinwar killed by Israel
- Jan 15, 2025: Accepts ceasefire variation of May 2024 framework
