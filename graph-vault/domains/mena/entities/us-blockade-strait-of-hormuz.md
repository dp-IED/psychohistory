# US Blockade of the Strait of Hormuz (2025-2026)

## Overview

The US-imposed naval blockade of the Strait of Hormuz was one of the immediate military consequences of the June 2025 Twelve-Day War between the US/Israel and Iran. Following the US bombing of Iranian nuclear facilities (June 22, 2025), the US 5th Fleet (Bahrain) imposed a blockade to interdict Iranian naval assets, prevent IRGC fast-attack craft sorties, and enforce the ceasefire terms. The blockade's announced lifting is a key signaling event in the US-Iran diplomatic normalization track.

## Imposition Timeline

- **June 22-24, 2025**: Twelve-Day War — US strikes Iranian nuclear sites; ceasefire announced June 24
- **Late June 2025**: US 5th Fleet announces enhanced maritime interdiction operations in the Strait of Hormuz — effectively a blockade
- **July-August 2025**: Blockade enforced; IRGC naval assets confined to port; Iranian oil exports disrupted
- **September 2025 onward**: Partial easing — humanitarian and civilian traffic restored; military interdiction continues

## Blockade Status as of May 2026

- **Ceasefire holding**: 99.9% on Polymarket (no Iranian violation since June 2025)
- **Blockade still technically active**: US 5th Fleet maintains enhanced presence; IRGC naval operations remain restricted
- **Iranian compliance**: No Iranian attempt to challenge the blockade since war's end
- **Oil transit**: Civilian oil tanker traffic has normalized (~20M barrels/day transit), but under US naval overwatch

## Lifting Scenarios

### Scenario A: Full Peace Deal → Immediate Lifting (probability: ~55%)
A US-Iran permanent peace deal (executive agreement or framework declaration) would include blockade termination as a near-automatic provision. Trump would announce the lifting as part of the deal announcement.

### Scenario B: Limited De-escalation → Conditional Lifting (probability: ~35%)
Even without a "permanent peace deal," the US could announce blockade lifting as a confidence-building measure tied to Iranian compliance milestones (verified nuclear constraints, IRGC stand-down commitments). This scenario explains the 92% YES price vs 53.5% for the peace deal by June 30 — the blockade can be lifted independently and earlier.

### Scenario C: No Deal, Blockade Continues (probability: ~8%)
The 92% YES market price implies only ~8% chance of this scenario. Would require either breakdown in negotiations or Khamenei succession crisis that freezes diplomatic progress.

## Forecasting Significance

1. The blockade has been in place for ~11 months. The longer it persists without incident, the more it normalizes as background condition — lifting becomes procedurally simpler.
2. Trump's diplomatic pattern (Alaska Summit, Armenia-Azerbaijan, Gaza ceasefire) favors announcing de-escalatory steps. Lifting the blockade is a low-cost, high-visibility peace signaling move.
3. The blockade lifting does NOT require Senate ratification (unlike a formal peace treaty) — it's an executive action under the President's Commander-in-Chief authority.
4. Watch for: any announcement of reduced US 5th Fleet posture, Iranian compliance certification, or joint US-Iran statement between now and June 30.
