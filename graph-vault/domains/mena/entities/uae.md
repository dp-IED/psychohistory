# United Arab Emirates (UAE)

## Structural Position
- **Leadership**: President Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan (MBZ) since May 2022
- **GCC member**: Founding member of Gulf Cooperation Council (1981)
- **Economic model**: Oil-funded diversification (DP World, Emirates, Abu Dhabi Investment Authority)
- **Foreign policy**: Independent, assertive; Abraham Accords signatory (2020)

## GCC Dynamics
- GCC members: Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain
- 2017-2021 Qatar blockade: UAE/Saudi/Bahrain/Egypt severed ties with Qatar
- Post-AlUla reconciliation (Jan 2021): blockade lifted, but tensions persist
- UAE-Saudi competition: economic diversification rivalry (NEOM vs Dubai), OPEC+ quota disputes
- UAE has pursued independent foreign policy: withdrew from Yemen war (2019), normalized with Israel (2020), engaged with Iran

## GCC Exit Probability Assessment
- UAE has the most independent foreign policy of any GCC state
- GCC exit would require: (a) major diplomatic rupture with Saudi Arabia, (b) UAE calculation that bilateral deals outperform multilateral bloc, (c) no loss of economic benefits
- Against exit: GCC provides institutional cover, unified currency peg coordination, Saudi market access
- Base rate: 0 state exits from GCC since 1981 founding (43 years)
- Polymarket: 9.5% YES ($98K vol) — appears appropriately low given institutional inertia
