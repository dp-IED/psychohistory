# Benjamin Netanyahu

## Type
Political leader; Prime Minister of Israel (2009–2021, 2022–present).

## Significance for Forecasting
Netanyahu is the single most consequential decision-maker in Israeli
war/ceasefire policy. He has the authority to accept or reject ceasefire
frameworks but must have security cabinet approval. His political survival
depends on a coalition that includes far-right factions (Ben-Gvir, Smotrich)
who oppose any ceasefire with Hamas. He is also facing corruption trials,
creating legal-political cross-pressures.

## Key Dynamics
- **Ceasefire calculus**: Netanyahu rejected permanent ceasefire for 8 months
  (May 2024 – Jan 2025) before accepting a variation of the same deal. The
  change was driven by US transition pressure, Sinwar's death, and military
  objectives achieved.
- **Coalition constraint**: Far-right coalition partners can threaten to
  collapse the government over a ceasefire. Netanyahu navigates this by
  framing deals as "temporary" or "phased" rather than permanent.
- **US relationship**: Has historically used US pressure as both a constraint
  and an excuse for coalition management ("I had no choice").
- **Legal jeopardy**: Corruption trials mean any decision that alienates
  coalition partners carries existential political risk.

## Key Events Timeline
- Oct 7, 2023: Presides over worst security failure in Israeli history
- 2024: Repeatedly rejects permanent ceasefire proposals
- Jan 15, 2025: Accepts ceasefire framework
- Jan 17, 2025: Secures cabinet approval for the deal
- Mar 18, 2025: Orders resumption of combat
