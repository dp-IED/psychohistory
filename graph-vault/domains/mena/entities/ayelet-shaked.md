# Ayelet Shaked

## Structural Position
- Israeli politician, formerly of Yamina/New Right party
- Former Minister of Interior (2021-2022)
- Former Minister of Justice (2015-2019)
- Former Knesset member (2013-2022)
- Failed to pass electoral threshold in 2022 election (Yamina collapsed)

## Political Trajectory
- Rose as secular right-wing alternative to Netanyahu within the religious-Zionist camp
- Co-founded New Right with Naftali Bennett in 2018 after splitting from Jewish Home
- Served in Bennett-Lapid government (2021-2022) — burned bridges with Netanyahu camp
- 2022 election: Yamina failed to cross 3.25% electoral threshold → out of Knesset
- Has not held elected office since 2022
- Attempted comeback runs unsuccessful; political brand damaged by Bennett association

## Path to PM
- Israeli PM must command Knesset majority (61+ seats)
- Current obstacles:
  1. No Knesset seat (failed to pass threshold in 2022)
  2. Political base collapsed (Yamina voters went to Likud/Religious Zionism)
  3. Netanyahu dominates right-wing; no space for secular right alternative
  4. Personal unpopularity: associated with Bennett government that included Ra'am (Arab party)
- Even a Knesset return would be from a small party (2-4 seats), not a PM contender
- Market probability: 0.2% — appropriately near-zero
