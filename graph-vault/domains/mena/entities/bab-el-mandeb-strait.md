# Bab el-Mandeb Strait

The Bab el-Mandeb Strait ("Gate of Tears") is a strategic chokepoint connecting the Red Sea to the Gulf of Aden and Indian Ocean. Approximately 6.2 million barrels per day of crude oil and petroleum products transit the strait — roughly 12% of global seaborne trade.

## Strategic Context

- **Width**: ~26 km at narrowest point (between Yemen and Djibouti/Eritrea)
- **Depth**: 30-310 meters
- **Peril Island**: Divided into two channels — western (26 km wide, 310m deep) and eastern (3 km wide, 30m deep)
- **Yemeni Control**: Houthi rebels control Yemen's Red Sea coast including key ports (Hodeidah, Salif)
- **Djibouti/Eritrea**: Provide counterbalance on African side

## Houthi Threat (2023-2026)

- **Anti-shipping campaign**: Since November 2023, Houthis have attacked 100+ commercial vessels in the Red Sea/Bab el-Mandeb region with drones, anti-ship missiles, and unmanned surface vessels
- **"Effective closure":** Even without physically sealing the strait, the threat has reduced Red Sea transit by 50-70% (Jan 2024 peak). Shipping companies (Maersk, MSC, Hapag-Lloyd) rerouted around Cape of Good Hope
- **US/UK strikes**: Operation Prosperity Guardian (Dec 2023) and subsequent strikes have degraded but not eliminated Houthi capability
- **Ceasefire intersections**: Houthi attacks initially tied to Gaza war; Israel-Hamas ceasefire (Jan 2025) led to partial reduction but attacks continue
- **Escalation triggers**: US-Iran Twelve-Day War (June 2025) escalated Houthi attacks on Red Sea shipping; post-ceasefire (Feb 2026) reduction observed

## Market-Relevant Dynamics

- **Effective closure**: The strait is "effectively closed" when commercial shipping insurance rates make transit uneconomical OR when major carriers announce indefinite rerouting
- **De facto closure**: Does not require physical blockade — the insurance premium + threat calculus creates economic closure
- **Normalization**: Requires sustained period (4-8 weeks) without attacks before carriers resume transit
- **US-Iran deal impact**: The June 14, 2026 US-Iran peace deal may reduce Houthi attacks if Iran reduces weapons/Intel support — but Houthis have autonomous capability and local grievances
