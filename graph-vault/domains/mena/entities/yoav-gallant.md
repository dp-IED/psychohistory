**Yoav Gallant** (born 1958) served as Israel's Minister of Defense from December 29, 2022 until his dismissal in November 2025. He was a key member of Israel's war cabinet during the Gaza war and was subject to an ICC arrest warrant (issued November 21, 2024) alongside Netanyahu for alleged war crimes in Gaza.

**Forecasting significance**: Gallant was a crucial actor in Israel's ceasefire decision-making. He was considered more moderate than Netanyahu on ceasefire terms and was reported to have pushed for a hostage deal in mid-2024. His relationship with Netanyahu deteriorated over the course of the war, with Netanyahu dismissing him in November 2025. The Gallant-Netanyahu dynamic illustrates a pattern in war coalitions: the defense minister often becomes the internal advocate for ceasefire while the prime minister balances coalition politics. Tracking Gallant's position (retained, dismissed, replaced by a hawk) provides a leading indicator for Israel's ceasefire posture.

**Key points**:
- Member of Israel's war cabinet throughout the Gaza war (Oct 2023–2025)
- ICC arrest warrant issued Nov 21, 2024 for war crimes in Gaza
- Publicly clashed with Netanyahu over ceasefire terms and hostage deal prioritization
- Dismissed as Defense Minister in November 2025 — replaced by a more hawkish figure (Benjamin Netanyahu served as interim Defense Minister, then Israel Katz)
- His dismissal is a leading indicator that Israel was hardening its military posture post-January 2025 ceasefire collapse
