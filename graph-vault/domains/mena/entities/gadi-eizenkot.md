**Role:** Former IDF Chief of General Staff (2015-2019), Israeli politician
**Party:** National Unity (alliance with Benny Gantz)
**Position:** Member of Knesset; former minister without portfolio in war cabinet (Oct 2023-Jun 2024)

## Structural Position

Eizenkot is a **centrist former military chief** in the National Unity party. He served as IDF Chief of Staff during a period of relative stability (post-2014 Gaza war, pre-2023 conflict). His military credentials give him security-establishment credibility without the polarizing effect of Netanyahu.

## Path to Prime Minister

- **Election victory**: National Unity would need to win the most seats and form a coalition. Currently opposition.
- **Coalition dynamics**: Israeli PMs are chosen by Knesset coalition-building, not direct election. Eizenkot could emerge as a compromise candidate if neither Netanyahu (Likud) nor Gantz commands a majority.
- **Netanyahu succession**: If Netanyahu steps down or Likud loses power, Eizenkot could be a unity candidate acceptable to both center and right.
- **War cabinet legacy**: His service in the Oct 2023 war cabinet (alongside Gantz, Netanyahu, Gallant) established him as a national-security figure above partisan politics.
