# Qatar

## Type
Nation-state; key diplomatic mediator in the Israel-Hamas conflict.

## Significance for Forecasting
Qatar has hosted Hamas's political leadership (including Haniyeh, Meshaal)
since 2012, giving it unique access and leverage. Its role as a US-designated
Major Non-NATO Ally and its relationship with both the US and Iran make it the
indispensable intermediary in Gaza ceasefire negotiations. The Qatari PM
(Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani) was a primary negotiator of all ceasefire
frameworks.

## Key Dynamics
- Houses Hamas political leadership — gives it unique communication channel
- Works in coordination with Egypt and the US as the "mediation trio"
- US-Joint force base (Al Udeid) means US has significant leverage over Qatar
- Threatened with reevaluation of US-Qatar relationship if Hamas didn't
  negotiate (Apr 2024)
- Doha is the venue for most ceasefire negotiations

## Key Events Timeline
- Oct–Nov 2023: Mediates first temporary ceasefire and hostage deal
- May 5, 2024: Qatari PM co-drafts three-phase ceasefire framework
- Jan 2025: Mediates final ceasefire acceptance
