# Amir Ohana

## Identity

Amir Ohana (born 1976) — Israeli politician, member of Likud party. Current Speaker of the Knesset (since December 2022). Former Minister of Public Security (2019-2020) and Minister of Justice (2020-2021).

## Political Position

- **Party**: Likud (Netanyahu's party)
- **Current role**: Knesset Speaker — one of the most powerful institutional positions in Israeli politics
- **Ideology**: Right-wing, close Netanyahu ally. First openly gay Knesset Speaker.
- **Netanyahu relationship**: Longtime loyalist, considered part of Netanyahu's inner circle.

## PM Succession Relevance

- **Next Israeli election**: Scheduled October 27, 2026 ([[events/israel-knesset-election-2026]])
- **Ohana as PM candidate**: Not currently positioned as the frontrunner to succeed Netanyahu. Other Likud figures (Yariv Levin, Israel Katz, Nir Barkat) are more frequently mentioned as potential successors.
- **Market assessment**: Polymarket prices Ohana as next PM at just 0.5% YES ($990K volume) — near-impossible according to market consensus.
- **Key blockers**:
  1. Netanyahu has not announced retirement/resignation
  2. Ohana's Knesset Speaker role gives institutional power but not electoral base
  3. Likud leadership succession would be contested — Ohana is not the heir apparent
  4. If Likud loses the election, the next PM would come from a different party
  5. The 0.5% price reflects the compounding improbability: Likud winning + Netanyahu stepping down + Ohana being chosen over more senior figures
