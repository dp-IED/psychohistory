# Syria Post-Assad Transition

Thread tracking Syria's transition following the collapse of the Assad regime on December 8, 2024, including HTS-led governance, international recognition, constitutional process, and regional dynamics.

## Scope
Covers: HTS transitional governance, Ahmed al-Sharaa's leadership, international diplomatic engagement, Turkey-Iran-Saudi competition for influence, Israel-Syria security dynamics, constitutional reform process, and reconstruction.

## Historical Context
- **Dec 8, 2024**: HTS-led forces capture Damascus; Assad flees to Moscow
- **2025**: Gradual international recognition; EU and regional engagement
- **2025–2026**: HTS transitions from rebel group to governing authority

## Current State (June 2026)

### Governance
- De facto leader: Ahmed al-Sharaa (Abu Mohammad al-Julani), head of HTS
- Transitional government apparatus controlling Damascus and major population centers
- Constitutional process ongoing but undefined

### International Status
- HTS remains designated as a terrorist organization by US, UN, and EU
- Limited engagement through regional partners (Turkey, Saudi Arabia)
- Trump administration has not established direct diplomatic channel
- Polymarket: 32.5% probability Trump speaks to al-Sharaa in June 2026

### Key Constraints
1. **Terrorist designation**: Blocks direct US engagement and formal diplomatic recognition
2. **Israel factor**: Israel conducts strikes in Syria, opposes HTS normalization
3. **Regional competition**: Turkey (primary backer), Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar vying for influence
4. **Internal consolidation**: Al-Sharaa must balance domestic rebel factions, civil society, and international demands
