## Summary

The May 2024 ceasefire framework was a three-phase proposal drafted by the US, Egypt, and Qatar that aimed to end the Gaza war through a phased hostage-prisoner exchange leading to a permanent ceasefire. Hamas accepted the framework on May 5, 2024; Israel's government rejected a permanent ceasefire under Netanyahu. Despite failing at the time, the May 2024 framework became the structural template for the January 2025 ceasefire — the same basic deal that was politically impossible in May 2024 became achievable 8 months later when leadership and regional conditions shifted.

**Forecasting significance**: The May 2024 framework is the canonical case of a pre-negotiated framework awaiting political activation. The deal existed; what changed was the conditions (Sinwar's death, Hezbollah ceasefire, Trump transition). This pattern — a framework exists but needs leadership change or political realignment to activate — is not limited to Israel-Hamas and applies wherever a detailed proposal has been tabled but lacks the political conditions for acceptance.

## The Three Phases

1. **Phase 1 (6 weeks)**: Hostage-prisoner exchange — women, elderly, wounded hostages released; Palestinian prisoners released; partial Israeli troop withdrawal from populated areas; surge in humanitarian aid
2. **Phase 2**: Permanent ceasefire — remaining living hostages released; full Israeli withdrawal from Gaza; sustainable calm
3. **Phase 3**: Gaza reconstruction — major reconstruction plan overseen by US, Egypt, Qatar; remains of deceased hostages returned

## Why It Failed in May 2024

- **Sinwar's intransigence**: Hamas's Gaza leader wanted a permanent ceasefire with full withdrawal — terms Israel would not accept while Hamas remained militarily viable
- **Netanyahu's coalition constraints**: Far-right coalition partners (Ben-Gvir, Smotrich) threatened to collapse the government if Netanyahu agreed to a permanent ceasefire before Hamas was destroyed
- **No regional forcing function**: Hezbollah and Iran were still active threats; Assad still in power; no transition deadline

## Why It Succeeded in January 2025

The framework was structurally unchanged. What changed was the activation conditions:
- Sinwar killed (Oct 16, 2024) — Hamas's most intransigent decision-maker removed
- Netanyahu's war aims achieved (Hamas degraded, Rafah captured) — reduced opposition to ceasefire
- Hezbollah ceasefire (Nov 27) — removed northern front concern
- Assad collapse (Dec 8) — degraded Iran's Axis of Resistance
- Trump inauguration deadline (Jan 20) — created forcing function for both sides

## Forecasting Lessons

- **Distinguish framework existence from framework activation**: A pre-negotiated framework lowers the bar for a future ceasefire — the question becomes "will the political conditions align?" not "can they reach a deal?"
- **Track the blocking condition**: In May 2024, the blocking condition was Sinwar's control of Hamas decision-making. Once that changed, the framework became activatable.
- **The activation delay is a proxy for condition severity**: The 8-month gap (May 2024 to Jan 2025) between framework creation and activation reflects how many conditions needed to change and how resistant the blocking actors were.
