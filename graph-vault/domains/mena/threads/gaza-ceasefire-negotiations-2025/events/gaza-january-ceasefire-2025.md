## Summary

Israel and Hamas accepted a ceasefire framework on January 15, 2025 (effective January 19), ending the first phase of the 15-month Gaza war. The deal framework was publicly announced by US President Joe Biden, President-elect Donald Trump, and Qatar's Prime Minister on January 15. Israel's security cabinet formally approved the deal on January 17 (Party Announcement). The ceasefire took effect January 19.

**⚠️ IMPORTANT FOR FORECASTING**: The January 15 date was the MEDIATOR announcement (US/Qatar announcing the framework Israel and Hamas had accepted in principle). The January 17 date was ISRAEL'S formal announcement (cabinet approval). Forecast questions asking "Israel announces ceasefire by date X?" resolve on the January 17 date, not January 15. See [[concepts/ceasefire-announcement-ratification-gap]] for the full framework and [[concepts/public-framework-announcement-commitment/_concept]] for the commitment-trap mechanism.

## Preconditions

1. Leadership decapitation cascade: Ismail Haniyeh (July 31, 2024) then Yahya Sinwar (Oct 16, 2024) killed
2. Hezbollah ceasefire (Nov 26, 2024) — Hamas lost its most powerful ally
3. Assad regime collapse (Dec 8, 2024) — Iran's Axis of Resistance severely degraded
4. Trump's impending inauguration (Jan 20, 2025) — created deadline effect on Biden admin
5. Witkoff-McGurk coordination (Nov 2024-Jan 2025) — bipartisan transition pressure on Israel

## Collapse

The ceasefire collapsed on March 18, 2025 when Israel launched widespread aerial bombardments, killing at least 591 people — see [[events/gaza-ceasefire-collapse-march-2025]].
