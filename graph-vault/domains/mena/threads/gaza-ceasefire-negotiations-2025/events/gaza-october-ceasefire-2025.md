## Summary

The final Gaza ceasefire, announced on October 8, 2025, ending the 15-month war that had resumed in March 2025 after the collapse of the January ceasefire. The ceasefire was first officially announced by the Israeli Prime Minister's Office on October 8, concurrently with Trump's announcement. The Israeli cabinet ratified it on October 9. This 1-day gap between announcement and ratification created a [[concepts/forecast-resolution-criteria-gotchas]] trap — date-specific questions resolving on "first announcement" require the October 8 date, not October 9 ratification.
