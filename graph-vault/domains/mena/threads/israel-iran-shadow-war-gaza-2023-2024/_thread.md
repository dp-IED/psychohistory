## Overview

From the October 7, 2023 Hamas attack through March 31, 2024, Israel and Iran did not yet conduct acknowledged airstrikes on each other's sovereign territory, but fought through proxies and adjacent fronts: Gaza, Hezbollah's northern border, Houthi Red Sea operations, and Israeli action in Syria. This thread captures only what was observable **on or before 2024-03-31** — the conjuncture immediately before the first direct exchange.

## Chronicle (PIT: through 2024-03-31)

### 2023-Q4 — War opens, proxies activate
- **Oct 7, 2023**: Hamas attack; Israel declares war. [[timeline/2023-Q4]]
- **Oct 31, 2023**: Israel intercepts Houthi ballistic missile — first claimed space-combat intercept. [[timeline/2023-Q4]]
- **Dec 18, 2023**: Houthis escalate Red Sea attacks on commercial shipping; major carriers suspend transits. [[timeline/2023-Q4]]
- **Dec 29, 2023**: South Africa files ICJ genocide case against Israel. [[timeline/2023-Q4]]

### 2024-Q1 — Humanitarian crisis, maritime war, Iran cross-border signal
- **Jan 12, 2024**: US-led coalition strikes Houthi targets in Yemen (Operation Prosperity Guardian). [[timeline/2024-Q1]]
- **Jan 16–18, 2024**: Iran strikes Pakistan (Balochistan); Pakistan retaliates on Iranian territory — first Iranian sovereign territory struck by a state since the 1980s war, then rapid de-escalation. [[timeline/2024-Q1]]
- **Jan 26 – Mar 25, 2024**: ICJ provisional measures; Gaza casualties exceed 30,000; UNSC ceasefire resolution (Mar 25) — fighting continues. [[timeline/2024-Q1]]
- **Mar 31, 2024**: Red Sea disruption ongoing; no Israel–Iran direct territory exchange yet reported in open sources.

## Conjuncture (as of 2024-03-31)

**Binding constraints**
- Israel's government is domestically committed to destroying Hamas capabilities in Gaza; ceasefire pressure has not halted operations.
- Iran faces legitimacy pressure after Kerman (Jan 3) and cannot appear passive if Israeli action kills senior IRGC figures in the region.
- US seeks to limit regional spillover but has already conducted strikes in Yemen and Iraq/Syria (Feb 2) — precedent for kinetic response to proxy attacks.

**Active escalation channels**
1. **Gaza → axis solidarity**: Houthis tie Red Sea attacks explicitly to Gaza; US/UK counter-strikes sustain a maritime combat loop.
2. **Cross-border strike normalization**: Iran–Pakistan exchange showed both sides will hit sovereign territory for militant pretext; Israel routinely strikes Syria-linked targets (open-source pattern through Q1, not yet consulate-scale on record at cutoff).
3. **Deterrence failure dynamic**: Each proxy response that imposes cost on Israel (shipping, northern front) increases incentive for Israel to strike Iranian capability directly rather than only proxies.

**Near-term trigger logic (knowable at cutoff, no outcome named)**
If Israel kills high-value Iranian personnel in a third country (Syria/Lebanon) or Iran's proxies impose a mass-casualty event on Israel, historical pattern in 2023–2024 regional wars points to **rapid tit-for-tat within days to weeks**, not months of diplomacy first.

**Calibrated stance at 2024-03-31:** Probability of **at least one** Israeli airstrike on Iranian sovereign territory before **2024-06-30** should be **high (≥0.65)** unless a durable Gaza ceasefire removes axis pressure. Proxy-only equilibrium is unstable given Q1 trajectory.

**Do not** treat "unprecedented" as a strong downward adjustment — unprecedented events are exactly what escalation ladders produce after proxy phases.

### Narrow-Window Calibration: Israel Attack Iran by Feb 15, 2024

This thread's chronicle enables a specific calibration for the question "Will Israel attack Iran by February 15, 2024?" (a 21-day window from Jan 25 to Feb 15):

**P(Israel strikes Iranian soil in this window) was ~0.03-0.05 at the Jan 25, 2024 cutoff**, for the following reasons:

1. **No tripwire**: The key trigger that sparked direct escalation — the Damascus consulate strike (April 1, 2024) — had not occurred. No major IRGC commander had been killed by Israel in this period. Without a tripwire event within or immediately before the window, the base rate for a first-ever direct strike is structurally low.

2. **Short window constraints**: Minimum Israeli decision-to-execution time for a sovereign-state strike is 2-5 weeks (intelligence prep, cabinet authorization, US notification, operational planning). A 21-day window starting Jan 25 meant a decision deadline of ~Jan 20 — before the window even opened. No trigger occurred before Jan 20.

3. **Bandwidth exhaustion**: IDF was at combat intensity in Gaza, alert along the Lebanon border, and supporting Red Sea naval operations. Initiating a direct strike on Iran (a peer-state adversary with advanced air defenses) would require significant operational reallocation.

4. **US containment**: Biden administration posture in Jan-Feb 2024 was explicitly containment-focused. The US struck Houthi targets (Jan 12) and Iranian-linked militias in Iraq/Syria (Feb 2) — absorbing proxy retaliation itself — but repeatedly warned Israel against wider war.

5. **Shadow war stage**: Iran-Israel were at Stage 1-2 on the [[concepts/shadow-war-to-direct-escalation]] ladder. The transition to Stage 3-4 requires a trigger event that, as of Feb 15, 2024, had not occurred.

**The outcome (NO) validates this calibration.** The correct prediction reflects the structural improbability of a first-ever direct strike within a narrow window without a preceding tripwire event.
