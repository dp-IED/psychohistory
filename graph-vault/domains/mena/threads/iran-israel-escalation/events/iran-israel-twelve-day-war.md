## Summary

A full-scale conventional war between Israel and Iran lasting from June 13 to June 24, 2025. Israel launched Operation Rising Lion on June 13 — large-scale airstrikes on Iranian nuclear facilities (Fordow, Natanz, Isfahan), military bases, and IRGC command infrastructure. Iran retaliated with Operation True Promise III (ballistic missiles and drones targeting Israeli cities). The war was terminated when the United States directly entered the conflict on June 22, striking three Iranian nuclear sites with B-2 Spirit bombers, followed by Trump's ceasefire announcement on June 23.

## Key Dates

- June 12: IAEA Board declares Iran non-compliant on nuclear obligations
- June 13: Operation Rising Lion begins; IRGC commander Hossein Salami killed
- June 22: US direct entry — B-2s strike Fordow, Natanz, Isfahan; Iran strikes US bases in Qatar and Iraq
- June 23: Trump announces ceasefire; Israeli security cabinet ratifies same day
- June 24: Ceasefire takes effect
