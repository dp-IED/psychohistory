# East Asia Domain

## Overview

East Asia includes Greater China (PRC, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macau), Japan, the Korean Peninsula, and Mongolia. The region covers the world's second and third largest economies (China, Japan), the world's most volatile geopolitical flashpoint (Taiwan Strait), and a nuclear-armed North Korea with active missile testing.

For forecasting, the most active domains are:

- **China domestic politics and leadership**: Xi Jinping's leadership stability, CCP factional dynamics, succession planning at the 2027 20th Party Congress. Thread: [[domains/east-asia/threads/xi-jinping-leadership-stability/_thread]]. China GDP growth and property sector crisis are recurring Polymarket question classes.
- **Taiwan electoral dynamics**: Presidential and legislative elections in Taiwan's single-round plurality system, where opposition fragmentation is the dominant structural variable. The 2024 presidential election (Lai Ching-te, DPP, 40.05%) is the canonical case of a [[domains/east-asia/concepts/divided-opposition-plurality-win/_concept]].
- **Cross-strait relations and invasion scenarios**: Military posturing, diplomatic signaling, economic interdependence, and escalation risks between China and Taiwan. Threads: [[domains/east-asia/threads/taiwan-cross-strait-relations/_thread]], [[domains/east-asia/threads/china-taiwan-invasion-scenarios/_thread]], [[domains/east-asia/threads/taiwan-strait-crisis-2022/_thread]].
- **North Korea**: Nuclear and missile testing patterns, denuclearization negotiations, leadership succession.
- **South Korea**: Bank of Korea monetary policy (rate decisions, won stability constraints), household debt risks, political dynamics. Entities: [[domains/east-asia/entities/south-korea]], [[domains/east-asia/entities/bank-of-korea]].
- **Japan**: Third-largest global economy, BOJ monetary normalization, constitutional revision debate. Gap: no Japan entity stub exists yet — flagged for future reflection.
