# South Korea

## Structural Position
- Population: ~52M. GDP: ~$1.8T (12th globally)
- Government: Presidential republic. President Yoon Suk Yeol (PPP, since 2022)
- Alliances: US-ROK Mutual Defense Treaty, trilateral with US-Japan
- Trade: Export-driven. Semiconductors (Samsung, SK Hynix) ~20% of exports. Heavy China exposure.

## Political Dynamics (2026)
- Yoon's approval fluctuated; opposition DPK holds National Assembly majority (Apr 2024 elections)
- Political gridlock constrains fiscal policy -> more pressure on monetary policy
- 2027 presidential election is next major political event

## Economic Context
- Export growth slowed 2025 (China slowdown + US tariff uncertainty)
- Household debt elevated (~105% GDP)
- Bank of Korea cautious on rate cuts due to won stability + household debt

## Central Bank: Bank of Korea (BoK)
- Current base rate: verify (was 3.50% through 2024; began easing cycle early 2025)
- Next meeting: July 16, 2026
- Dual mandate: price stability + financial stability
- Won/dollar constraint: aggressive cuts risk capital outflows
- Governor: Rhee Chang-yong (term expires April 2026)

## Forecasting Implications
- BoK dynamics similar to EM central banks: forward guidance less structured than Fed
- Export sensitivity to China = China stimulus or slowdown impacts rate path
