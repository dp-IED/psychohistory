# China (PRC)

## Summary

The People's Republic of China is the world's second-largest economy (~$18T GDP nominal, 2025), ruled by the Chinese Communist Party (CCP) under General Secretary Xi Jinping (since 2012). It is a nuclear-armed UN Security Council permanent member with the world's largest military by personnel. China is the primary geopolitical rival of the United States, with active flashpoints in the Taiwan Strait, South China Sea, and technology/semiconductor competition.

## Significance for Forecasting

- **GDP growth trajectory**: China's GDP growth has slowed from ~10% (pre-2012) to ~4-5% (2024-2026), driven by property sector deleveraging, demographic decline, and US tariff barriers. GDP growth above/below specific thresholds is a recurring Polymarket question class. Q2 2026 consensus: 4.8-5.2%.
- **Property sector crisis**: The Evergrande/Country Garden collapse (2021-2024) continues to weigh on consumer confidence and local government finances. Recovery is slow and incomplete.
- **Taiwan Strait risk**: China's military modernization and increasing cross-strait pressure make Taiwan the most dangerous geopolitical flashpoint. PLA exercises around Taiwan increased markedly post-2024.
- **Technology competition**: US chip export controls (2022-2025) and China's indigenous semiconductor push (SMIC, Huawei) are a structural economic variable affecting both countries.
- **Demographic cliff**: China's population peaked in 2022 and is declining. Working-age population shrinkage of ~3-4M/year constrains long-term growth.
- **POF (Politburo/CCP) decision cycle**: Major policy shifts (stimulus, property rescue, regulatory crackdowns) are announced at Politburo meetings and Central Economic Work Conferences, which follow a predictable annual calendar.

## Timeline

- **1949**: PRC founded under Mao Zedong
- **1978**: Deng Xiaoping economic reforms begin
- **2012**: Xi Jinping becomes General Secretary
- **2018**: Constitutional term limits removed; Xi becomes president-for-life in effect
- **2021**: Evergrande default begins property crisis
- **2022**: Population peaks and begins declining
- **2024**: Third Plenum economic reform package; stimulus measures announced
- **2025**: US tariff escalation (Liberation Day); China retaliates
- **2026 Q1**: GDP growth 5.3% (above consensus)
- **2026 Q2**: GDP growth expected 4.8-5.2%, data release July 15
