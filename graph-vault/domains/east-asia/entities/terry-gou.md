# Terry Gou

## Summary

Terry Gou (Guo Taiming) is a Taiwanese billionaire business magnate and philanthropist, founder of Foxconn (Hon Hai Precision Industry Co., Ltd.), the world's largest electronics manufacturer. Born in Taiwan to mainland Chinese parents, Gou built Foxconn into a global manufacturing powerhouse producing iPhones, PlayStation consoles, and other consumer electronics for Apple, Sony, and other major brands. His net worth has been estimated at $6-10 billion.

Gou entered politics in 2019, running unsuccessfully in the Kuomintang's presidential primary. In 2023, he launched an independent presidential campaign for the 2024 election, qualifying for the ballot with over 900,000 signatures. However, he withdrew on the registration deadline (November 24, 2023) after the KMT-TPP opposition alliance negotiations collapsed, and did not endorse any candidate.

## Significance for Forecasting

Gou's entry and withdrawal in the 2024 election was a significant but transitory factor. His campaign drew votes from both KMT and TPP in Q3 2023 polls, and his withdrawal after the KMT-TPP talks collapsed simplified the race back to a three-way contest. Future forecasting about Taiwanese elections must assess whether Gou or other business figures could run as spoilers in future cycles. Key variables: the Foxconn tax investigation launched by China in October 2023 (a potential tool of PRC electoral influence), Gou's age (73 in 2023), and the viability of an independent business-centric platform in Taiwan's two-party system.
