# Lai Ching-te (賴清德)

## Summary

Lai Ching-te is a Taiwanese politician who has served as President of Taiwan since May 20, 2024. He won the 2024 presidential election as the Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) candidate with 40.05% of the vote — a plurality win enabled by opposition fragmentation between the Kuomintang (33.49%) and the Taiwan People's Party (26.46%).

Prior to the presidency, Lai served as Vice President under Tsai Ing-wen (2020-2024) and as Premier of Taiwan (2017-2019). He was previously a physician and legislator, and served as Mayor of Tainan (2010-2017). Lai is known for his explicit pro-sovereignty stance, which is more assertive than Tsai's more cautious posture.

## Significance for Forecasting

Lai's 2024 victory exemplifies the [[domains/east-asia/concepts/divided-opposition-plurality-win/_concept]]: a front-runner winning with <50% because the opposition vote is split between two candidates. His presidency is consequential for cross-strait relations given his more assertive sovereignty positioning compared to Tsai Ing-wen. Future Taiwan election forecasts must account for Lai's incumbency advantage and the continued existence of the TPP as a vote-splitting force.
