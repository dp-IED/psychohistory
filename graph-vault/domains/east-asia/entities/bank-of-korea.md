# Bank of Korea (BoK)

## Position
- Central bank of South Korea, established 1950
- Governor: Rhee Chang-yong (since Apr 2022, term ends Apr 2026)
- Monetary Policy Board: 7 members (Governor + Senior Deputy + 5 external)

## Recent Policy History
- Hiking cycle: 0.50% (2021) -> 3.50% (Jan 2023) = 300bp tightening
- Held at 3.50% through end of 2024
- Easing cycle began 2025 (specific timeline verify needed)
- Next meeting: July 16, 2026

## Policy Constraints
1. Won/dollar: Primary constraint. Aggressive easing -> won depreciation -> imported inflation + capital outflows
2. Household debt: ~105% GDP. Rate cuts risk re-igniting property speculation
3. Fed dependency: BoK rarely moves opposite direction from Fed. Fed plateau at 4.25-4.50% limits BoK room
4. Export cycle: Semiconductor cycle dominates. China stimulus -> less pressure to cut
5. Governor transition: Rhee term expires Apr 2026 -> successor may change policy direction
