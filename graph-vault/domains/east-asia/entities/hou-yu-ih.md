# Hou Yu-ih (侯友宜)

## Summary

Hou Yu-ih is a Taiwanese politician and former police officer who served as the Kuomintang's (KMT) presidential candidate in the 2024 election. He received 33.49% of the vote, finishing second behind Lai Ching-te (DPP, 40.05%). Prior to his presidential campaign, Hou served as Mayor of New Taipei City (2018-present), the most populous city in Taiwan.

Hou's candidacy was characterized by a relatively late campaign start (the KMT nominated him in May 2023 after an extended primary process) and difficulty consolidating the anti-DPP vote against Ko Wen-je's third-party challenge. His background as a law-and-order police official positioned him as a moderate, but the KMT's China-leaning brand and his relatively low national profile before the campaign limited his appeal beyond the traditional KMT base.

## Significance for Forecasting

Hou's 33.49% vote share — below the historical KMT baseline — illustrates the structural squeeze the KMT faces when a centrist third party (TPP) competes for the same anti-DPP vote. Future KMT candidates must either rebuild the party's appeal to young urban voters (the TPP's base) or negotiate a pre-election alliance. Hou's candidacy is a case study in the limits of the KMT's electoral ceiling under three-party competition.
