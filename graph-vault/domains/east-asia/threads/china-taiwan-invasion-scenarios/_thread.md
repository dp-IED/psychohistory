# China-Taiwan Invasion Scenarios

## Thread Summary

This thread tracks the probability, timelines, and scenarios for a Chinese military invasion of Taiwan. It consolidates military, diplomatic, and economic indicators to assess whether China will attempt to take Taiwan by force before the end of 2026.

**Polymarket reference:** "Will China invade Taiwan by end of 2026?" — 7.4% Yes, $23.4M volume (as of 2026-05-20)

## Key Dynamics

### 1. Military Readiness Indicators
- **PLA exercises near Taiwan** — frequency, scale, and proximity of military drills
- **Grey-zone operations** — increased patrols, ADIZ enforcement, simulated blockade
- **Force posture** — deployment of DF-21/DF-26 anti-ship ballistic missiles, aircraft carrier groups
- **Amphibious capability** — Type 075 LHDs, civilian ferry conversion programs, landing drills

### 2. Diplomatic Signals
- **Xi's public statements** — unification timelines, peaceful vs. forceful rhetoric
- **US response posture** — security assurances to Taiwan, arms sales, naval presence
- **Third-party mediation** — ASEAN, EU positions on status quo changes
- **Cross-strait dialogue** — any revival of official/unofficial communication channels

### 3. Economic Deterrents
- **Cost of war** — estimated economic disruption, global supply chain damage
- **Semiconductor dependency** — TSMC concentration risk; China's self-sufficiency timeline
- **Sanctions vulnerability** — potential US/EU/JP sanctions regime magnitude
- **Domestic stability** — Chinese economic conditions affecting regime risk appetite

### 4. Escalation Pathways
- **Grey-zone suffocation** — incremental pressure without formal invasion (blockade, denial of access)
- **Hybrid warfare** — information operations, cyberattacks, coercive diplomacy
- **Full amphibious assault** — large-scale invasion requiring months of visible preparation
- **Limited operation** — seizure of outlying islands (Kinmen, Matsu, Penghu) as coercive bargaining

### 5. Key Threshold Events
- PLA announces "blockade drills" near Taiwan
- US aircraft carrier group permanently stationed in Taiwan Strait
- China builds airfields within 100km of Taiwan
- Cross-strait trade agreement terminated
- Taiwan formally requests US troop deployment

## Probability Assessment

The market prices a 7.4% chance of invasion by end of 2026. This reflects:

**Arguments for higher probability:**
- Xi's "no promise" on peaceful reunification (2024)
- PLA capability growth (3 LHDs, 6 Type 052D destroyers since 2020)
- US deterrence credibility questions after Afghanistan/Taiwan strategic ambiguity
- China's growing economic self-sufficiency reduces vulnerability to sanctions

**Arguments for lower probability:**
- Massive economic cost (estimated $2-5T GDP impact)
- No visible PLA preparation for amphibious assault
- US security commitment to Taiwan is bi-partisan (Taiwan Policy Act)
- Domestic Chinese economic challenges consume leadership attention
- Taiwan's 2M military-age population and defensive preparations
