# South Africa (Republic of South Africa)

## Structural Position
- Third largest economy in Africa (~$400B GDP nominal), most industrialized
- Constitutional parliamentary republic; President elected by National Assembly
- ANC in government since 1994 but lost absolute majority in May 2024 election (40.2%)
- Government of National Unity: ANC + DA + IFP + smaller parties (May 2024-2029)
- Key sectors: mining (gold, platinum, coal), finance, manufacturing, tourism

## Key Actors
- **Cyril Ramaphosa**: President (2018-), ANC leader. Stepped down as ANC president Dec 2027?
- **John Steenhuisen**: DA leader, Agriculture Minister in GNU. Key partner in coalition stability.
- **Julius Malema**: EFF leader, leftist populist. Advocates land expropriation, nationalization.
- **Jacob Zuma**: Former President (2009-2018), formed MK party in Dec 2023, split ANC vote in 2024.
- **Gayton McKenzie**: Patriotic Alliance leader, significant role in GNU.

## Forecasting Relevance
- **ANC electoral trajectory**: 2024 election (40.2%) was first time ANC fell below 50%. 2029 election will test whether ANC can recover or if coalition politics is permanent. Key variable: Ramaphosa succession and GNU durability.
- **GNU stability**: ANC+DA+IFP coalition is historically unprecedented. Breakup scenarios: DA withdrawal over policy, EFF entering, or ANC going it alone with smaller parties.
- **EFF radicalization risk**: Malema's land reform and nationalization platform tests investor confidence. EFF growth trajectory (6.3% in 2014 → 10.8% in 2019 → 9.5% in 2024) stalled in 2024 due to Zuma's MK party.

## Key Market Examples
- No major Polymarket markets active as of June 2026, but structural relevance for: ANC future majority, GNU breakdown, Ramaphosa succession, EFF or MK government participation.

## Vulnerabilities
- Eskom power crisis (load shedding has eased but structural deficit remains)
- High unemployment (~33%, youth ~60%)
- Inequality (Gini coefficient ~0.63, highest in world)
- Crime and security perception
- Land reform uncertainty
- ANC internal factionalism (Ramaphosa vs Zuma/MK defectors)
