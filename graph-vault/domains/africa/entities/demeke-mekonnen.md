# Demeke Mekonnen

## Structural Position
- Former Deputy Prime Minister of Ethiopia (2012-2024)
- Former Minister of Foreign Affairs (2020-2024)
- Previously TPLF member who broke with the party during Tigray war
- Removed from Deputy PM position in February 2024 reshuffle
- Now positioned as opposition/alternative candidate for Prime Minister

## Political Trajectory
- Served as Deputy PM under both Hailemariam Desalegn (2012-2018) and Abiy Ahmed (2018-2024)
- Key figure during Tigray war: as ethnic Tigrayan in federal government, helped legitimize Abiy's position
- February 2024 removal signaled Abiy consolidating power and sidelining TPLF-aligned figures
- Has positioned himself as a potential alternative to Abiy, drawing support from disaffected factions

## Path to PM
- PM is elected by House of Peoples' Representatives (majority vote)
- Would require: (1) Prosperity Party majority to defect from Abiy, OR (2) opposition coalition to win majority, OR (3) Abiy to step down/be removed
- All three paths are structurally difficult: Abiy has consolidated party control, Prosperity Party machine is loyal, no unified opposition exists
- Market probability: 1.1% — appropriately low
