# Nigeria (Federal Republic of Nigeria)

## Structural Position
- Most populous country in Africa (~230M), largest economy ($1.1T GDP PPP)
- Federal presidential republic; President Bola Tinubu (APC) elected 2023, 4-year term
- Bicameral National Assembly: Senate (109 seats) + House of Representatives (360 seats)
- 36 states + Federal Capital Territory; multi-ethnic federation (Hausa-Fulani, Yoruba, Igbo)
- OPEC member, ~1.5M bpd oil production, but petroleum sector is ~90% of exports

## Key Actors
- **Bola Tinubu**: President since May 2023, APC. Running for re-election 2027. Economic reforms (fuel subsidy removal, forex unification) have mixed results.
- **Rabiu Kwankwaso**: NNPP standard-bearer, strong in Kano/Northwest
- **Atiku Abubakar**: PDP elder, perennial candidate (6th run in 2023)
- **Peter Obi**: Labour Party, 2023 third-place finisher; "Obidient" youth movement; strong Southeast base
- **Nyesom Wike**: FCT Minister, ex-Rivers Governor, PDP defector to APC in 2023

## Forecasting Relevance
- **2027 presidential election**: Tinubu seeking second term. Key variables: economic recovery trajectory, currency stability, security improvement in Northwest/Southeast, APC primary cohesion. PDP fragmentation (Wike defection, Atiku succession) reduces opposition competitiveness.
- **Polymarket (est.)**: "Who wins 2027 Nigerian presidential election?" will be a major market as 2027 approaches.
- **Coup risk**: West African coups (Mali, Burkina Faso, Niger 2020-2023) create regional contagion concern, but Nigerian civil-military relations are structurally different (larger professional army, stronger democratic institutions, no post-colonial military coup tradition in 4th Republic).

## Vulnerabilities
- Oil theft and pipeline sabotage in Niger Delta
- Herder-farmer conflicts (Middle Belt)
- Boko Haram/ISWAP insurgency in Northeast
- Separatist agitation in Southeast (IPOB/ESN)
- Currency crisis: Naira depreciated ~70% under Tinubu
- Inflation ~30% after subsidy removal
