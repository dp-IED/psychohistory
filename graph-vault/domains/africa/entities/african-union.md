# African Union (AU)

## Structural Position
- Continental body of 55 member states, founded 2002 (successor to OAU)
- Headquarters: Addis Ababa, Ethiopia
- Key institutions: AU Commission (executive), Peace and Security Council (PSC), Pan-African Parliament, African Court on Human and Peoples' Rights
- Chairperson of the AU Commission: Mahamoud Youssouf (Djibouti), elected 2025
- AU Chairperson (rotating annually among heads of state): currently held by [varies by year]

## Forecasting Relevance
- **AU peacekeeping missions**: AUSSOM (Somalia, successor to ATMIS), regional interventions in Sahel. AU mission effectiveness is a key variable for conflict forecasting.
- **AU-EU relations**: Partnership on migration, counterterrorism, trade (AfCFTA implementation).
- **AU-ICC relationship**: African state withdrawal dynamics (Kenya, South Africa, Nigeria all have debated withdrawal). Duterte precedent may reshape AU-ICC dynamics.
- **AU chair election**: Each AU Commission chair election (next ~2028) tests continental power dynamics between regional blocs.

## Role in Active Conflicts
- Mediating role in Sudan civil war (RSF vs SAF)
- Somalia stabilization (AUSSOM transition from ATMIS)
- Sahel security vacuum post-French withdrawal (limited AU capacity)
- Ethiopia-Somalia tensions over Somaliland port deal
- Great Lakes region stability (DRC, Rwanda tensions)
