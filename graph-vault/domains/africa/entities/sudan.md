# Sudan (Republic of the Sudan)

## Structural Position
- Northeast African country, strategic Nile and Red Sea position
- Population ~50M, transition from 30-year Omar al-Bashir dictatorship (1989-2019)
- Current state: full-scale civil war since April 2023 between SAF (Sudanese Armed Forces, led by Gen. Abdel Fattah al-Burhan) and RSF (Rapid Support Forces, led by Gen. Mohamed Hamdan "Hemedti" Dagalo)
- No functioning central government; multiple factions controlling different regions
- Humanitarian catastrophe: ~8M displaced, famine conditions in multiple regions

## Key Actors
- **Abdel Fattah al-Burhan**: SAF commander, de facto head of state (Sovereignty Council). Controls Port Sudan and eastern Sudan. Seeking international legitimacy but unwilling to compromise on RSF integration.
- **Mohamed Hamdan "Hemedti" Dagalo**: RSF commander, former Janjaweed leader. Controls Darfur, Khartoum parts, Kordofan. Facing ICC charges for war crimes. Strong UAE links.
- **Abdalla Hamdok**: Former PM (2019-2021, brief 2023), civilian figure seeking democratic transition. Limited control, represents civilian coalition (Forces for Freedom and Change).

## Forecasting Relevance
- **Civil war trajectory**: SAF vs RSF conventional phase largely stalemated. Key variables: UAE support for RSF, Iranian drones for SAF, SPLM-N and SLM factional shifts.
- **Humanitarian markets**: Famine severity, displacement numbers, ceasefire mediation outcomes (AU, IGAD, Saudi-US mediation tracks).
- **Regional impact**: Sudan destabilization affects Egypt (Nile water, refugees), Ethiopia (border, refugees), South Sudan (oil transit), Chad (Darfur spillover), Red Sea shipping.
- **RSF as new power broker**: Hemedti's transformation from militia leader to potential national power-sharer creates a forecasting angle for militia-to-state transitions across Africa.

## Vulnerabilities
- Darfur genocide risk (RSF ethnically targeted non-Arab groups)
- Famine: IPC Phase 5 confirmed in multiple locations
- State collapse: No single actor controls entire territory
- Proxy war dynamics: UAE (RSF) vs Egypt/Iran (SAF)
- Gold smuggling to UAE funds RSF operations
