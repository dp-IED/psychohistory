# Ethiopia (Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia)

## Structural Position
- Second most populous country in Africa (~126M)
- Federal parliamentary republic; PM is head of government
- Ruling party: Prosperity Party (successor to EPRDF), led by PM Abiy Ahmed
- 2026 general election held; Prosperity Party expected to win majority
- Ethiopia Tigray conflict (2020-2022) ended with Pretoria Agreement; Tigray reintegration ongoing
- Oromia and Amhara conflicts continue at lower intensity

## Key Actors
- **Abiy Ahmed**: PM since 2018, Nobel Peace Prize 2019, Prosperity Party leader. Centralized power, marginalized TPLF. Won 2021 election (opposition boycotted in some regions)
- **Demeke Mekonnen**: Deputy PM (2012-2024), former Foreign Minister. Was TPLF member but sided with Abiy during Tigray war. Removed as Deputy PM in 2024 reshuffle. Now contesting for PM?
- **Temesgen Tiruneh**: Deputy PM (2024-present), former intelligence chief
- **TPLF**: Tigray People's Liberation Front, former dominant party, now regional opposition after Pretoria Agreement

## Recent Developments (as of June 2026)
- 2026 general election held; Prosperity Party maintains majority
- Economy growing ~7% but forex shortages, inflation ~25%
- Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam (GERD) operational; negotiations with Egypt/Sudan ongoing
- Somaliland port deal (January 2024) provides naval access; tensions with Somalia

## Political Structure
- Parliamentary system: PM elected by House of Peoples' Representatives (lower house)
- House of Federation (upper house) represents ethnic regions
- Ethnic federalism system creates persistent regional autonomy tensions
- PM serves 5-year term; no constitutional term limits
