# Kenya (Republic of Kenya)

## Structural Position
- Largest economy in East Africa (~$120B GDP nominal, ~$350B PPP)
- Presidential system; President elected by plurality (50%+1 requirement reduces to simple majority in second round)
- Bicameral Parliament: Senate (67 seats) + National Assembly (349 seats)
- Devolved government: 47 counties with elected governors and assemblies
- Ruto administration (2022-2027): Kenya Kwanza coalition government

## Key Actors
- **William Ruto**: President since Sep 2022 (50.5% in disputed election). Kenya Kwanza coalition. Pursuing "Hustler Fund" economic agenda.
- **Raila Odinga**: 5-time presidential candidate, Azimio coalition leader. Lost 2022 (48.8%). Ongoing role as opposition elder statesman. AU Commission Chair bid 2024-2025 (lost to Mahamoud Youssouf).
- **Musalia Mudavadi**: Prime Cabinet Secretary, influential Luhya leader.
- **Gachagua/Ethuro**: Key deputies shaping 2027 Ruto succession dynamics.

## Forecasting Relevance
- **2027 presidential election**: Ruto eligible for second term. Opposition consolidation will determine competitiveness. Key variables: cost of living, debt sustainability, Ruto's popularity trend.
- **Raila factor**: At 81 (2026), Raila's likely last presidential run. Succession within Azimio coalition is an open question.
- **Kenya as regional bellwether**: Kenya's election cycle (every 5 years, 2017/2022/2027) creates predictable political windows. Post-election litigation pattern (2017 nullification) creates institutional uncertainty.

## Vulnerabilities
- Public debt (~70% GDP), high debt service costs
- Cost of living (fuel, food prices)
- Ethnic balancing politics (Kikuyu-Kalenjin alliance vs Luo-Luhya opposition axis)
- Floods/drought cycle affecting agriculture
- Youth unemployment (~40%)
