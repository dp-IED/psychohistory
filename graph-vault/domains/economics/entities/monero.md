# Monero (XMR)

Monero is a privacy-focused cryptocurrency utilizing ring signatures, stealth addresses, and RingCT to obscure transaction details. It is the largest privacy coin by market capitalization.

## Current Status (May 2026)

- **Price**: ~$400
- **Market Cap**: ~$7.3B
- **All-time High**: ~$517 (May 2021)
- **2026 High-Low Range**: ~$280-$480

### Price Catalysts

1. **Privacy coin regulatory pressure**: Monero faces ongoing delisting pressure from centralized exchanges due to AML/KYC compliance requirements. Binance delisted XMR in several jurisdictions in 2024.
2. **ETF/Institutional adoption**: As a privacy coin, Monero is unlikely to see ETF products — its privacy features are incompatible with surveillance-sharing requirements that ETF issuers need.
3. **Macro sensitivity**: XMR correlates weakly with BTC compared to other altcoins, making it less sensitive to the macro liquidity cycle.
4. **Halving**: Monero has no supply cap but has a tail emission; its emission schedule is not a significant price catalyst.
