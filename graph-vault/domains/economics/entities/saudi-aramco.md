# Saudi Aramco

World's largest oil company by production (~12M bpd capacity) and historically the most valuable public company. State oil monopoly of Saudi Arabia, listed on Tadawul (Riyadh) since Dec 2019.

## Market Position

- **Market cap**: ~$1.8T (March 2026), fluctuates with oil prices and global equity markets
- **Competitors for "second-largest"**: Apple (~$2.8T May 2026), Microsoft (~$2.6T), Nvidia (~$2.1T), Alphabet, Amazon
- **Key dynamic**: "Second-largest company by market cap" means greater than Apple OR Microsoft. Aramco at $1.8T vs Apple $2.8T = Aramco would need ~55% gain to surpass Apple, OR Apple would need to decline ~35% to drop below Aramco.

## Why Market Prices Near-Zero (0.1% YES)

- Aramco's market cap is oil-price correlated (Brent ~$65-70/bbl Jun 2026, down from $80+)
- Saudi Vision 2030 spending creates fiscal demands that compete with Aramco dividends
- OPEC+ production policy constrains Aramco's upside (Saudi Arabia is swing producer, bears burden of cuts)
- Apple/Microsoft/Nvidia all have market caps well above Aramco's
- To become second-largest by June 30 (13 days), Aramco would need a catastrophic US tech crash (30%+ drop) OR an oil supply crisis driving Brent to $120+ — neither plausible in 13 days
- Saudi Arabia has been cutting production under OPEC+ agreements, reducing Aramco's revenue base
