# Cathie Wood

## Summary

Catherine Duddy Wood (born 1955) is an American investment manager and the founder, CEO, and CIO of ARK Invest, an investment management firm focused on disruptive innovation. Wood became a prominent and controversial figure in the investment world for her high-conviction, concentrated bets on technology and crypto assets, particularly during the 2020-2021 pandemic boom when ARK's flagship ARKK ETF returned over 150%. Her subsequent performance during 2022's tech downturn (ARKK fell ~67% from peak to trough) generated intense debate about her investment methodology, but her position on crypto and blockchain technology proved structurally prescient.

## Significance for Forecasting

Cathie Wood is significant for forecasting primarily through her consistent advocacy for spot Bitcoin ETF approval and the role her firm's application played in establishing the statutory deadline that forced SEC action:

1. **First-mover deadline-setter**: ARK Invest, under Wood's leadership, filed the earliest spot Bitcoin ETF applications and maintained the first statutory final deadline (January 10, 2024). This deadline became the forced-approval date after the Grayscale DC Circuit ruling stripped the SEC of its basis for denial.

2. **Crypto price forecasting indicator**: Wood's public Bitcoin price targets ($1M+ by 2030, repeated across multiple years) serve as a useful contrarian indicator. While consistently bullish, her targets provide an upper-bound reference for crypto market sentiment and a benchmark for assessing how far market pricing diverges from maximalist projections.

3. **Narrative driver**: Wood's media presence and Congressional testimony on crypto regulation (she testified before the House Financial Services Committee in 2023) shaped the public narrative around Bitcoin ETFs and crypto adoption. Her influence on the regulatory debate, while difficult to quantify, was a contributing factor in maintaining political pressure for ETF approval.

## Timeline

- **2014**: Founds ARK Invest
- **2020-2021**: ARKK ETF gains ~150% during pandemic tech boom; Wood becomes a celebrity investor
- **2021**: ARK files for ARK 21Shares Bitcoin ETF — among the earliest spot Bitcoin ETF applications
- **2022**: ARKK falls ~67% as tech stocks correct; Wood remains committed to innovation/crypto thesis
- **2023**: Testifies before House Financial Services Committee on digital assets regulation; maintains Bitcoin price targets
- **2024-01-10**: SEC approves 11 spot Bitcoin ETFs on ARK 21Shares' deadline
- **2025**: Post-Gensler SEC era; ARK's role shifts from catalyst to routine participant
- **2025-2026**: ARKK recovers partially; Wood continues to promote innovation investment themes
