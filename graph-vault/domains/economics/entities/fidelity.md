# Fidelity

## Summary

Fidelity Investments, founded in 1946, is one of the world's largest asset managers with over $5 trillion in assets under management. Alongside BlackRock, Fidelity is a dominant incumbent institutional ETF issuer. Fidelity filed for spot Bitcoin ETF (FBTC) and spot Ethereum ETF (FETH) applications in 2023-2024, and both were approved as part of the SEC's regulatory precedent cascade. Fidelity's involvement in crypto ETFs was a strong signal of institutional demand — as a tier-1 incumbent asset manager, its participation structurally raised the probability of SEC approval because the SEC cannot easily deny products from a firm of Fidelity's regulatory sophistication and market influence.

## Significance for Forecasting

Fidelity is a tier-1 institutional applicant whose participation in any crypto ETF product is a leading indicator of approval probability. When forecasting whether a crypto ETF will be approved:

1. **Check whether Fidelity (or BlackRock) has filed**: If yes, the approval probability baseline rises to >60% even without court compulsion, because the SEC faces disproportionate reputational and legal risk denying a major incumbent.
2. **Monitor Fidelity's regulatory posture**: Fidelity has a sophisticated regulatory affairs team and typically secures approvals through process compliance rather than litigation — its applications are procedurally bulletproof, leaving the SEC few technical grounds for denial.
3. **Fidelity's crypto stance**: Fidelity has been crypto-friendly since ~2018 when it launched Fidelity Digital Assets, a dedicated crypto custody and trading division serving institutional clients. This pre-existing infrastructure means Fidelity's ETF products have a ready distribution channel.

## Timeline

- **2018**: Fidelity launches Fidelity Digital Assets — crypto custody and trading for institutions
- **2023-06**: Fidelity files for spot Bitcoin ETF (FBTC)
- **2023-11**: Fidelity files for spot Ethereum ETF (FETH)
- **2024-01-10**: SEC approves FBTC (spot Bitcoin ETF)
- **2024-05-23**: SEC approves FETH 19b-4 proposal
