# Tether

**Type**: Private company, issuer of USDT stablecoin

## Forecasting Relevance

- USDT is the largest stablecoin by market capitalization (~$100B+ range in 2025-2026)
- Tether's reserve composition (treasury bills, commercial paper, loans, other assets) is a recurring source of market uncertainty — any reserve crisis could trigger a systemic stablecoin de-pegging event affecting the entire crypto market
- USDT trade volume dominates BTC trading pairs on Binance and other non-US exchanges
- Tether's relationship with US regulators and banks affects crypto market liquidity and exchange stability
- Tether's periodic attestations (reserve reports) are market-moving events

## Key Facts
- Founded: 2014
- Issuer: Tether Limited (British Virgin Islands)
- Primary stablecoin: USDT (pegged 1:1 to USD)
- Competing stablecoins: USDC (Circle), DAI (MakerDAO), FDUSD

## Systemic Risk Factors
- Regulatory scrutiny from US DOJ, CFTC, NYAG (settled with NYAG in 2021, ongoing compliance requirements)
- Reserve transparency concerns — attestations not full audits
- Banking relationships vulnerability — loss of correspondent banking access could disrupt redemptions
