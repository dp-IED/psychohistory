# XRP

## Summary

XRP is the native cryptocurrency of the XRP Ledger (XRPL), a decentralized blockchain technology developed by Ripple Labs Inc. Created in 2012 by David Schwartz, Jed McCaleb, and Arthur Britto, XRP functions as a bridge currency for cross-border payments and settlements. Unlike Bitcoin's proof-of-work or Ethereum's proof-of-stake, the XRPL uses a unique Federated Consensus algorithm that enables fast (3-5 second) and low-cost transactions. Ripple Labs holds a significant portion of XRP's total supply (100 billion coins, with ~55 billion in circulation and the rest in escrow), which has been a central point of regulatory and market debate.

## Significance for Forecasting

XRP is the third-largest cryptocurrency by market capitalization and has unique characteristics that make it a distinct forecasting domain:

1. **SEC Lawsuit Resolution**: The SEC v. Ripple lawsuit (filed December 2020) was the most consequential legal case for crypto securities classification in the US. The July 2023 ruling (Judge Analisa Torres) that XRP is NOT a security when sold to retail investors on exchanges was a landmark precedent for the entire crypto industry, though the SEC appealed. The case's resolution path directly affects XRP's price trajectory.

2. **ETF Approval Timeline**: Following the [[regulatory-precedent-cascade]] from Bitcoin ETF (Jan 2024) to Ethereum ETF (May/Jul 2024), XRP is a leading candidate for the next crypto ETF approval. Multiple asset managers (including WisdomTree, Bitwise, Canary Capital) filed for XRP ETFs in 2024-2025. The post-Gensler SEC (from Jan 2025) is considered more favorable to crypto ETF products. An XRP ETF approval would be a major price catalyst.

3. **Regulatory Status**: XRP's unique legal history — being deemed "not a security" for retail sales but a security for institutional sales by the Torres ruling — creates a dual regulatory status that affects its availability on exchanges, market structure, and institutional adoption.

4. **Market Profile**: XRP has high retail ownership concentration, significant volatility around legal/regulatory events, and a developing correlation with broader crypto market cycles. Its price has shown particular sensitivity to SEC-related news events.

## Timeline

- **2012**: XRP Ledger launched by David Schwartz, Jed McCaleb, Arthur Britto
- **2013**: Ripple Labs formed to commercialize XRP
- **2020-12-22**: SEC files lawsuit against Ripple Labs, alleging XRP is an unregistered security
- **2023-07-13**: Judge Analisa Torres ruling — XRP is NOT a security when sold to retail investors on exchanges; IS a security when sold to institutional investors
- **2024-01-10**: SEC approves spot Bitcoin ETFs — creates regulatory precedent for crypto ETFs
- **2024-05-23/07-22**: SEC approves spot Ethereum ETFs — extends regulatory precedent cascade
- **2024-08-07**: Judge Torres enters final judgment ordering Ripple to pay $125M civil penalty
- **2024-10**: SEC files notice of appeal of Torres ruling (Second Circuit)
- **2025-01**: Gensler resigns as SEC Chair; new SEC leadership potentially more favorable to crypto
- **2025**: Multiple XRP ETF filings submitted to SEC (WisdomTree, Bitwise, Canary Capital, 21Shares)
- **2026-02-05/06**: XRP flash crash to $1.12-1.14 range, lowest levels in months
- **2026-03-18**: Fed holds rates at 3.25-3.50% — pause after easing cycle
- **2026-03-21**: XRP trades $1.40-1.45 range, well above $1.20 level

## Price Context (as of Q1 2026)

XRP entered 2026 trading in the $1.50-1.60 range before a flash crash in early February took it to $1.12. The price recovered to $1.40-1.50 by mid-March and traded in that range following the March 18 Fed rate hold decision. Key support levels: $1.30 (February/March lows), $1.40 (psychological level), $1.20 (prior crash zone). Key resistance: $1.55-1.60 (early 2026 highs), $1.70-2.00 (post-lawsuit rally levels).
