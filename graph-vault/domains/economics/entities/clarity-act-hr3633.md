# Digital Asset Market Clarity Act (H.R.3633)

The "Digital Asset Market Clarity Act of 2025" (H.R.3633) is a comprehensive crypto market structure bill introduced in the U.S. House of Representatives. It addresses token classification, exchange registration, and SEC-CFTC jurisdiction over digital assets.

## Status (May 2026)

- **Introduced**: 2025, 119th Congress
- **Current stage**: Committee
- **Polymarket**: 56.5% YES at $992K volume — moderate optimism for passage by Dec 31, 2026

## Key Provisions

1. **Token classification**: Defines when a digital asset is a security vs commodity, providing a clear framework that the SEC's enforcement-first approach lacked
2. **Exchange registration**: Creates a registration pathway for crypto exchanges with both SEC and CFTC
3. **Jurisdictional clarity**: Draws clear lines between SEC and CFTC authority over digital assets
4. **Stablecoin provisions**: Complementary to the STABLE Act

## Political Landscape

- Bipartisan cosponsorship as of early 2026
- Trump administration's pro-crypto SEC (post-Gensler, Atkins era) supports the legislative effort
- 2026 midterm elections create both urgency (pre-election window) and risk (post-election lame duck)
- Stablecoin legislation (STABLE Act) has parallel momentum and may move first

## Forecasting Significance

The Clarity Act is the single most important piece of crypto legislation in the 119th Congress. Its passage or failure will define the US regulatory environment for digital assets through the remainder of the decade.
