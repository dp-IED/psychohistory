# VanEck

## Summary

VanEck is a US-based investment management firm founded in 1955, known for pioneering thematic and alternative investments including gold ETFs, emerging market funds, and cryptocurrency-linked products. VanEck was a key applicant in the crypto ETF approval cascade, filing for a spot Bitcoin ETF (denied in 2021) and later a spot Ethereum ETF. VanEck's Ethereum ETF application had one of the earliest statutory deadlines among the 2024 applicants, making it the procedural "canary" — its May 23, 2024 19b-4 deadline was the date that forced SEC action on the entire Ethereum ETF class. VanEck launched its spot Ethereum ETF (ETHV) in July 2024 following S-1 approval.

## Significance for Forecasting

VanEck's role in the regulatory precedent cascade is as the **applicant with the earliest deadline** for Ethereum ETF approval. When a court ruling compels SEC approval of a product class, the earliest-filing applicant's statutory deadline determines the forced-approval date. For Ethereum ETFs, VanEck's deadline was May 23, 2024 — this date anchored the entire cascade chronology. Understanding which applicant has the earliest deadline is essential for forecasting "by what date" questions.

## Timeline

- **2021**: VanEck files for spot Bitcoin ETF; SEC denies in November 2021
- **2023**: VanEck files for spot Ethereum ETF
- **2024-05-23**: SEC approves VanEck's 19b-4 proposal along with 7 other Ethereum ETF applicants
- **2024-07-22**: S-1 registration statement approved; ETHV begins trading July 23
- **2025+**: VanEck advocates for XRP ETF and other crypto-linked products
