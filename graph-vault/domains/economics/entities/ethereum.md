# Ethereum

## Summary

Ethereum is a decentralized, open-source blockchain with smart contract functionality, created by Vitalik Buterin in 2015. It is the second-largest cryptocurrency by market capitalization and serves as the foundational layer for decentralized finance (DeFi), NFTs, and most blockchain applications.

## Significance for Forecasting

Ethereum's price and ecosystem health are driven by distinct factors from Bitcoin:

1. **Smart contract platform competition**: Ethereum competes with Solana, Avalanche, and other L1s for developer mindshare, TVL, and application activity. EIP-4844 (proto-danksharding, March 2024) improved L2 scalability.
2. **DeFi and stablecoin activity**: ETH's utility as the gas token and collateral backbone of DeFi gives it endogenous demand beyond speculative investment.
3. **ETF flows**: Spot Ethereum ETFs (approved May 2024, launched July 2024) created institutional exposure, though net flows have been more volatile than BTC ETFs.
4. **Regulatory posture**: The SEC's classification of ETH as a non-security (vs. many other tokens) provides relative regulatory clarity. The CFTC's parallel jurisdiction over ETH derivatives creates a split regulatory framework.
5. **Staking yield and supply**: The post-Merge proof-of-stake mechanism (~4% staking yield) and net issuance near zero make ETH structurally different from BTC's proof-of-work halving cycles.

## Key Price Levels (2026 PIT)

- All-time high: ~$4,800 (November 2021)
- Post-ETF approval range: $2,500-$4,000 (2024-2025)
- Early 2026 levels: ~$1,800-$2,200
- ETH/BTC ratio: declining trend since 2022 (L1 competition, L2 fragmentation)

## ETF Approval Timeline

The spot Ethereum ETF approval followed the regulatory precedent cascade [regulatory-precedent-cascade] established by the Bitcoin ETF (Jan 2024):

| Date | Event | Significance |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 2023-Q4 | BlackRock, Fidelity, VanEck, and others file for spot Ethereum ETFs | Institutional applicants trigger the cascade |
| 2024-05-23 | SEC approves 19b-4 forms for 8 Ethereum ETFs (VanEck, BlackRock, Fidelity, Grayscale, ARK 21Shares, Invesco Galaxy, Franklin Templeton, Bitwise) | Legal precedent from Bitcoin ETF makes denial untenable |
| 2024-07-22 | SEC approves S-1 registration statements (Division of Corporation Finance) | Paperwork phase completed ~60 days after 19b-4 |
| 2024-07-23 | Trading begins on NASDAQ, NYSE Arca, and CBOE BZX | Two business days before the July 26 question deadline |

**Key forecasting pattern**: The ~60-day gap between 19b-4 (May 23) and S-1 (July 22) was the critical timeline variable for "begins trading by" questions. Unlike Bitcoin ETFs (where 19b-4 and S-1 were approved on the same day), Ethereum's S-1 process took 2 months — consistent with the "accepting inevitability" SEC posture where the agency approves 19b-4 under legal compulsion but takes normal procedural time on S-1 registration.
