# BlackRock

## Summary

BlackRock is the world's largest asset manager, with over $10 trillion in assets under management. Founded in 1988 by Larry Fink, BlackRock is the dominant incumbent institutional ETF issuer, with over 575 ETF products approved and a near-zero denial rate. BlackRock's entry into the crypto ETF race (June 15, 2023, filing for the iShares Bitcoin Trust) was a pivotal moment in the regulatory timeline — it signaled that institutional capital was committed to crypto ETFs and structurally raised the probability of SEC approval.

BlackRock's significance in regulatory forecasting extends beyond crypto: its ETF approval track record and regulatory relationships make it a **bellwether applicant** for any novel financial product. When BlackRock files for a product, the approval probability baseline rises significantly because the SEC faces disproportionate reputational and legal risk from denying a firm of BlackRock's stature.

## Significance for Forecasting

1. **Institutional applicant leading indicator**: BlackRock's entry into any ETF product race is a strong signal that approval is structurally more likely. In the crypto ETF context, BlackRock's filing (alongside Fidelity's) turned a legal battle (Grayscale v. SEC) into a market inevitability.

2. **The BlackRock effect on timing**: BlackRock's applications are procedurally robust and hard for the SEC to deny on technical grounds. This compresses the SEC's procedural room for delay — once BlackRock has filed, the SEC's options narrow to: (a) approve, or (b) deny and face a lawsuit they're likely to lose.

3. **Fee competition dynamic**: BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT) charged only 0.25% (with a 0.12% promotional rate for the first $5B), compared to Grayscale GBTC's 1.5%. This fee competition drove significant GBTC outflows post-approval and concentrated ETF trading volume in IBIT and FBTC.

## Timeline

- **1988**: Founded by Larry Fink
- **2009**: BlackRock acquires Barclays Global Investors (iShares ETF business)
- **2023-06-15**: BlackRock files for iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT) — the first major incumbent to file for a spot crypto ETF
- **2024-01-10**: SEC approves IBIT along with 10 other spot Bitcoin ETFs
- **2024-01-11**: IBIT begins trading on NASDAQ; records $1B+ in first-day volume
- **2024-05-23**: SEC approves BlackRock's Ethereum ETF (ETHA) 19b-4 proposal
- **2024-07-22/23**: ETHA S-1 approved; begins trading
