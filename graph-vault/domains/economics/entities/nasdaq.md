# NASDAQ

## Summary

The NASDAQ Stock Market (National Association of Securities Dealers Automated Quotations) is the world's second-largest stock exchange by market capitalization, founded in 1971 as the world's first electronic stock exchange. It is owned by Nasdaq, Inc. (NDAQ). NASDAQ is the primary listing venue for technology companies and is one of three exchanges (alongside NYSE and CBOE) where spot crypto ETFs trade. NASDAQ listed the first spot Bitcoin ETFs (January 2024) and the first spot Ethereum ETFs (July 2024), including BlackRock's IBIT and ETHA.

## Significance for Forecasting

1. **ETF listing venue**: NASDAQ is one of the three US exchanges authorized to list spot crypto ETFs (Bitcoin, Ethereum, and potentially XRP/Solana). Questions about "begins trading on NASDAQ" resolve when the exchange lists the product — which typically occurs the business day after S-1 effectiveness.

2. **Market structure indicator**: NASDAQ's ETF listing fees, market maker relationships, and data dissemination infrastructure affect ETF liquidity and trading volumes. Products listed on NASDAQ typically have access to NASDAQ's electronic market-making ecosystem.

3. **Regulatory gatekeeper**: As a self-regulatory organization (SRO), NASDAQ must file 19b-4 rule changes with the SEC to list novel products. The 19b-4 filing is the first formal step in any ETF approval process — the exchange proposes to list, the SEC approves or denies.

## Timeline

- **1971**: Founded as the world's first electronic stock exchange
- **2024-01-10**: SEC approves 19b-4 rule changes for NASDAQ to list spot Bitcoin ETFs
- **2024-01-11**: Trading begins for spot Bitcoin ETFs on NASDAQ (IBIT, FBTC, etc.)
- **2024-05-23**: SEC approves NASDAQ's 19b-4 rule changes to list spot Ethereum ETFs
- **2024-07-23**: Trading begins for spot Ethereum ETFs on NASDAQ (ETHA, FETH, ETHV, etc.)
