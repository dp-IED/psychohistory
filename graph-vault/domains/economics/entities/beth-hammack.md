# Beth M. Hammack

## Summary

President of the Federal Reserve Bank of Cleveland since August 2024, succeeding Loretta Mester. Hammack previously served as co-head of Goldman Sachs' global funding division and treasurer of Goldman Sachs, with over 30 years of experience in financial markets.

## Significance for Forecasting

Hammack is notable for dissenting at the **December 18, 2024 FOMC meeting** — her first dissent after only 4 months on the job. At that meeting, the FOMC cut rates by 25bp to 4.25-4.50% (11-1 vote), and Hammack dissented in favor of maintaining the rate. This made her the second FOMC dissenter of the easing cycle (after Michelle Bowman in September 2024) and the first Cleveland Fed president to dissent since 2017.

Hammack represents a structurally hawkish bloc on the FOMC, aligned with the view that inflation risks remain elevated and that the Fed should proceed cautiously with additional cuts. Her dissent pattern — voting against a cut within months of joining the Committee — signals an independent-minded hawk with conviction. For any future question about FOMC split votes, hawkish opposition to the easing cycle, or internal division over the pace of cuts following the H1 2025 tariff-induced pause, Hammack is a key actor to track.

## Timeline

- **2024-08**: Assumes office as President of the Cleveland Fed (voting FOMC member in 2024)
- **2024-09-18**: Votes with the majority on 50bp cut (no dissent in first meeting)
- **2024-11-07**: Votes with the majority on 25bp cut (unanimous 12-0)
- **2024-12-18**: **Dissents** — votes against 25bp cut (preferred to hold at 4.50-4.75%)
- **2025**: Voted with majority on all H1 2025 holds and H2 2025 cuts
