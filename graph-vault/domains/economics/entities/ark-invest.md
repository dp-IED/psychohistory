# ARK Invest

## Summary

ARK Invest is an American investment management firm founded by Cathie Wood in 2014, focused on disruptive innovation in areas including genomics, fintech, crypto, artificial intelligence, and space. ARK gained prominence for its high-conviction, concentrated portfolio approach and became the first major ETF issuer to file for a spot Bitcoin ETF (ARK 21Shares Bitcoin ETF, filed jointly with 21Shares) in 2021. ARK's Bitcoin ETF application was among the earliest and had the first final SEC decision deadline (January 10, 2024), making it the first-mover in the approval timeline.

## Significance for Forecasting

ARK Invest is significant because its Bitcoin ETF application created the pivotal deadline that forced the SEC's hand. Under SEC rules, the agency had a maximum of 240 days to decide on the ARK 21Shares spot Bitcoin ETF application, with a final deadline of January 10, 2024. Because the SEC could not deny the application without contradicting the Grayscale DC Circuit ruling, this deadline effectively became the date when spot Bitcoin ETFs were approved. ARK's application functioned as the "clock" that gave the Grayscale ruling concrete temporal force — without the court-ordered consistency principle AND the impending final deadline, the SEC could have delayed indefinitely.

The ARK case illustrates a broader forecasting principle: when a regulator is compelled by a court to approve a product class, the specific product with the earliest final decision deadline becomes the vehicle through which the approval occurs. The applicant with the nearest statutory deadline functions as the "canary in the coal mine" — its approval unlocks all subsequent similar products.

## Timeline

- **2014**: Founded by Cathie Wood
- **2021**: ARK files for ARK 21Shares Bitcoin ETF (joint with 21Shares) — one of the earliest spot Bitcoin ETF applications
- **2023-08**: DC Circuit's Grayscale ruling creates legal framework for Bitcoin ETF approval; ARK's application has the earliest final SEC deadline
- **2023-12**: ARK amends its Bitcoin ETF filing to include Coinbase surveillance-sharing agreement, meeting SEC requirements
- **2024-01-10**: SEC approves 11 spot Bitcoin ETFs on ARK 21Shares' final deadline day — the deadline forced the decision
- **2024-05-23**: SEC approves 19b-4 forms for spot Ethereum ETFs
- **2024-07-23**: Ethereum ETFs begin trading
