# Coinbase

## Summary

Coinbase is the largest cryptocurrency exchange in the United States, founded by Brian Armstrong and Fred Ehrsam in 2012. It went public via direct listing on NASDAQ in April 2021 (COIN), becoming the first major crypto company to list on a US stock exchange. Coinbase has been a central figure in US crypto regulation: the SEC sued Coinbase in June 2023 for operating as an unregistered exchange, broker, and clearing agency. Coinbase has actively advocated for clearer crypto regulatory frameworks in the US and served as the custodian for several spot Bitcoin and Ethereum ETFs approved in 2024.

## Significance for Forecasting

Coinbase's legal battle with the SEC is a leading indicator for broader US crypto regulatory direction. As the primary US crypto exchange and a custodian for institutional crypto ETF products, Coinbase's regulatory status directly affects crypto market structure and product availability.
