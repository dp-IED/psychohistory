# MicroStrategy

**Type**: Publicly-traded business intelligence and software company (NASDAQ: MSTR)

## Forecasting Relevance

- Largest publicly-traded corporate holder of Bitcoin (holdings grew through 2024-2026 via continuous ATM equity offerings and convertible note issuances)
- Founder and Executive Chairman [[entities/elon-musk|Michael Saylor]] is a prominent Bitcoin advocate whose acquisition strategy influences Bitcoin market dynamics
- MSTR share price trades as a leveraged Bitcoin proxy — MSTR/BTC correlation delta is a watched metric by crypto analysts
- MicroStrategy's convertible note issuances to fund BTC purchases create a unique capital structure that affects both equity and crypto markets
- Company's Bitcoin holdings represent a significant portion of total BTC supply, making its buy/sell decisions macro-relevant for Bitcoin price forecasting

## Key Facts
- HQ: Tysons Corner, Virginia
- Founded: 1989
- Bitcoin first purchased: August 2020
- Cumulative BTC holdings (est. 2026): >200,000 BTC
