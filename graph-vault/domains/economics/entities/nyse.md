# New York Stock Exchange (NYSE)

## Summary

The New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) is the world's largest stock exchange by market capitalization, founded in 1792 under the Buttonwood Agreement. Owned by Intercontinental Exchange (ICE), the NYSE is one of three US exchanges (alongside NASDAQ and CBOE) that list spot crypto ETFs. The NYSE listed Grayscale's Bitcoin Trust (GBTC) conversion to a spot ETF and Grayscale's Ethereum Trust (ETHE) conversion, as well as Bitwise's crypto ETF products.

## Significance for Forecasting

1. **ETF listing venue**: The NYSE, alongside NASDAQ and CBOE, is one of the authorized venues for spot crypto ETF trading. The "begins trading on the NYSE" resolution condition requires that a product is listed on NYSE Arca (the exchange's electronic trading platform for ETFs).

2. **Grayscale relationship**: The NYSE is the listing venue for Grayscale's converted ETF products (GBTC, ETHE). Given that Grayscale's products had significant AUM pre-conversion, NYSE's role in the crypto ETF ecosystem is disproportionately tied to Grayscale.

3. **19b-4 filing agent**: Like NASDAQ, NYSE Arca files 19b-4 rule changes with the SEC to list novel products. NYSE Arca filed for rule changes to list Grayscale's Bitcoin ETF conversion and various Ethereum ETF products.

## Timeline

- **1792**: Founded under Buttonwood Agreement
- **2006**: Merges with Archipelago (becomes publicly traded); later acquired by ICE
- **2024-01-10**: SEC approves NYSE Arca's 19b-4 rule changes for spot Bitcoin ETFs (GBTC and others)
- **2024-01-11**: GBTC begins trading as spot ETF on NYSE Arca
- **2024-05-23**: SEC approves NYSE Arca's 19b-4 rule changes for spot Ethereum ETFs
- **2024-07-22**: SEC approves S-1 for ETHE conversion
- **2024-07-23**: Spot Ethereum ETFs (ETHE, etc.) begin trading on NYSE Arca
