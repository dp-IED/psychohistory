# Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC)

## Summary

The body within the Federal Reserve System that sets US monetary policy — specifically the target federal funds rate and the direction of open market operations. The FOMC consists of 12 voting members: the 7 members of the Board of Governors (of which the Chair, Vice Chair, and Vice Chair for Supervision are the most senior), the President of the Federal Reserve Bank of New York (who holds a permanent vote), and 4 of the remaining 11 Reserve Bank presidents who serve one-year rotations.

The FOMC meets 8 times per year on a published schedule. Four of these meetings (March, June, September, December) are "SEP" meetings where the Summary of Economic Projections (the "dot plot") is published alongside the rate decision. All meetings include a press conference by the Chair (since 2011, expanded to every meeting in 2019).

## Significance for Forecasting

The FOMC's published schedule makes rate decisions time-bound — every forecasting question about Fed rate changes should be mapped to a specific FOMC meeting date. The rotating voting membership (4 of 11 regional presidents vote each year) can shift the Committee's hawkish/dovish balance. Notable dissents (like Michelle Bowman's dissent for 50bp over 25bp in September 2024, the first governor dissent since 2005) signal internal committee dynamics that may affect future decisions.

## Timeline

- **1933**: FOMC formally established
- **1971**: FOMC begins publishing policy decisions
- **1994**: FOMC begins announcing rate decisions immediately after meetings
- **2011**: Chair begins holding press conferences (4x/year initially, expanded)
- **2019**: Press conferences after every meeting
- **2024-09-18**: First governor dissent since 2005 — Michelle Bowman votes against 50bp cut
- **2024-11-07**: 25bp cut to 4.50-4.75% (12-0)
- **2024-12-18**: 25bp cut to 4.25-4.50% (11-1, Hammack dissents)
- **2025-01-29**: Hold at 4.25-4.50% (12-0) — first post-Trump-inauguration meeting
- **2025-03-19**: Hold at 4.25-4.50% (11-1, Waller dissents on balance sheet)
- **2025-05-07**: Hold at 4.25-4.50% (12-0) — first meeting after Liberation Day tariffs
- **2025-06-18**: Hold at 4.25-4.50% (12-0)
- **2025-09-17**: 25bp cut to 4.00-4.25% — first 2025 cut (11-1, Miran wanted 50bp)
- **2025-10-29**: 25bp cut to 3.75-4.00% (10-2, Miran wanted 50bp, Schmid wanted hold)
- **2025-12-10**: 25bp cut to 3.50-3.75% (9-3, Miran wanted 50bp, Schmid and Goolsbee wanted hold)
