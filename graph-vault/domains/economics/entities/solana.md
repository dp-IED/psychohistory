# Solana

High-throughput layer-1 blockchain (~65K TPS theoretical, ~3K actual). Proof-of-History consensus. Major DeFi/NFT ecosystem; primary competitor to Ethereum for developer activity. SOL is the native token.

## Key Dynamics (2025-2026)

- **Spot ETF Approval**: SEC approved Solana spot ETFs in early 2025, alongside Bitcoin/Ethereum ETFs. This expanded institutional access but also introduced correlation with traditional risk assets.
- **Price Path**: SOL peaked ~$295 in Jan 2025 (ETF euphoria), declined through 2025-2026 alongside broader crypto drawdown. Current ~$130 (Jun 2026).
- **Ecosystem**: Dominant in memecoin trading (Pump.fun), DePIN (Helium migration), and payments (Solana Pay). NFT volume down ~80% from 2024 peak.
- **Network Reliability**: Multiple outages in 2022-2024; Firedancer validator client (Jump Crypto) partially deployed 2025, improving reliability. No major outage since Sep 2024.
