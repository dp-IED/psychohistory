# Bitcoin

## Summary

Bitcoin is a decentralized digital currency created by the pseudonymous Satoshi Nakamoto in 2009. It is the first and largest cryptocurrency by market capitalization. Bitcoin operates on a proof-of-work blockchain with a fixed supply cap of 21 million coins.

## Significance for Forecasting

Bitcoin is the anchor asset for the entire cryptocurrency market. Its price movements are driven by:

1. **Macro liquidity cycles**: Bitcoin has become increasingly correlated with traditional risk assets and is sensitive to Fed policy, dollar strength, and global liquidity conditions.
2. **Regulatory developments**: US SEC posture on crypto ETFs, enforcement actions, and legislation directly affect Bitcoin prices.
3. **Political factors**: The Trump administration's Strategic Bitcoin Reserve (SBR) proposal was a key catalyst for Bitcoin's 2024-2025 rally. Failure to deliver on SBR contributed to early 2026 sell-off.
4. **Halving cycles**: The April 2024 halving reduced block rewards from 6.25 to 3.125 BTC, historically a bullish structural factor.
5. **ETF flows**: The January 2024 spot Bitcoin ETF approvals created a new institutional demand channel. Net ETF flows are now a significant price driver.
