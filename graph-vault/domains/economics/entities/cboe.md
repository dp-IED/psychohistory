# CBOE Global Markets

## Summary

CBOE Global Markets (Chicago Board Options Exchange) is the world's largest options exchange and a key venue for crypto ETF trading. Founded in 1973, CBOE operates CBOE BZX Exchange, which is one of the three US exchanges (alongside NASDAQ and NYSE Arca) authorized to list spot crypto ETFs. CBOE BZX filed the initial 19b-4 rule changes for spot Bitcoin ETFs and spot Ethereum ETFs. Fidelity's FBTC and FETH trade on CBOE.

## Significance for Forecasting

1. **ETF listing venue**: CBOE BZX Exchange is the third authorized venue for spot crypto ETFs alongside NASDAQ and NYSE Arca. The question "begins trading on the CBOE" resolves when a product is listed on CBOE BZX.

2. **19b-4 filing history**: CBOE was the first exchange to file for spot Bitcoin ETF listings (repeatedly denied until the Grayscale ruling). CBOE also filed the first spot Ethereum ETF 19b-4 proposals. The exchange's persistent filings across multiple cycles made it a procedural test case for the regulatory precedent cascade.

3. **Crypto derivatives leader**: Beyond ETFs, CBOE is the primary venue for crypto futures and options (Bitcoin futures, Ether futures, and later options on Bitcoin ETFs). This derivatives market serves as the basis for the "CME correlation" argument that compelled spot ETF approvals.

## Timeline

- **1973**: Founded as the Chicago Board Options Exchange
- **2010**: Becomes CBOE Holdings; later renamed CBOE Global Markets
- **2024-01-10**: SEC approves CBOE BZX 19b-4 rule changes for spot Bitcoin ETFs
- **2024-01-11**: FBTC and other CBOE-listed Bitcoin ETFs begin trading
- **2024-05-23**: SEC approves CBOE BZX 19b-4 rule changes for spot Ethereum ETFs
- **2024-07-23**: FETH and other CBOE-listed Ethereum ETFs begin trading
