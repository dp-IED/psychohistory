# Circle

**Type**: Private company, issuer of USDC stablecoin

## Forecasting Relevance

- USDC is the second-largest stablecoin, with stronger US regulatory compliance than USDT
- Circle is a regulated US-based company (licensed in multiple US states, registered as a money services business with FinCEN)
- USDC is the primary stablecoin on Solana, Base, and Ethereum DeFi ecosystems
- Circle's reserve composition (US Treasury bills, cash, repo agreements) is more transparent than Tether's — monthly attestations from Deloitte
- Circle's IPO filing (confidentially submitted to SEC) is a indicator of crypto regulatory maturation
- USDC plays a central role in Polymarket settlement — all USDC on Polymarket is bridged USDC from Circle's smart contract

## Key Facts
- Founded: 2013 (as OpenCoin, later renamed Circle Internet Financial)
- HQ: Boston, Massachusetts
- CEO: Jeremy Allaire
- Primary stablecoin: USDC (pegged 1:1 to USD)
- Competing stablecoins: USDT (Tether), DAI (MakerDAO)

## Regulatory Edge vs. Tether
- Full US regulatory compliance (BitLicense NY, MSB registration)
- Reserves in cash and short-duration US Treasuries only
- Monthly attestation by Deloitte
- Accountable to US legal system — can freeze addresses if required by law enforcement
