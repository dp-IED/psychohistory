# Abstract Chain

Abstract is a blockchain project focused on consumer crypto applications, built on the ZK Stack (ZKsync technology). Founded by the team behind Pudgy Penguins, Abstract aims to onboard mainstream consumers through engaging applications rather than traditional DeFi infrastructure.

## Token Launch

As of May 2026, Abstract has not yet launched its governance token. The Polymarket question "Abstract FDV above $200M one day after launch?" at 76.5% YES ($99K volume) reflects market optimism about a high-FDV launch.

- Comparable L2 token launches: zkSync (~$800M FDV at TGE), StarkNet (~$1B+), Scroll (~$500M+)
