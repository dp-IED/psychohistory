# Chicago Mercantile Exchange (CME)

**Type**: Regulated derivatives exchange (part of CME Group, NASDAQ: CME)

## Forecasting Relevance

- CME Bitcoin futures and options are the primary regulated Bitcoin derivatives in the US market
- The SEC's Bitcoin ETF approval (January 2024) was critically based on CME Bitcoin futures market surveillance — the "significant market" test from the Grayscale court ruling hinged on CME's correlation with spot Bitcoin prices
- CME Bitcoin futures basis (premium/discount to spot) is a key sentiment indicator for institutional crypto positioning
- CME open interest in Bitcoin and Ether futures is watched as a proxy for institutional crypto engagement
- Any disruption to CME's Bitcoin futures market (regulatory, technical, or competitive) would have cascading effects on the entire US crypto ETF ecosystem
- CME Group also offers micro Bitcoin/Ether futures and options on futures

## Key Facts
- HQ: Chicago, Illinois
- Bitcoin futures launched: December 2017
- Bitcoin options launched: January 2020
- Ether futures launched: September 2023
- Average daily volume (Bitcoin futures, 2025): tens of thousands of contracts
