# Bitwise Asset Management

## Summary

Bitwise Asset Management, founded in 2016, is a crypto-native asset manager specializing in cryptocurrency index funds and ETFs. Bitwise launched the Bitwise Bitcoin ETF (BITB) in January 2024, approved alongside 10 other spot Bitcoin ETFs. Bitwise is distinct from incumbent issuers (BlackRock, Fidelity) as a crypto-native firm — its entire business model is crypto-focused, giving it domain expertise but less institutional regulatory heft.

## Significance for Forecasting

1. **Crypto-native bellwether**: Unlike BlackRock/Fidelity (incumbents with diversified ETF businesses), Bitwise is a pure-play crypto asset manager. Its ETF products have smaller AUM but higher conviction from crypto-native investors.
2. **Fee structure**: BITB charges 0.20% (with 0% promotional first $1B) — among the lowest in the category — making it a competitive pricing signal for the commoditization of BTC ETF products.
3. **Product pipeline indicator**: Bitwise's ETF filings (e.g., Bitwise Bitcoin and Ethereum Equal Weight Strategy ETF, Bitwise Ethereum ETF) signal the direction of crypto-native institutional product development, distinct from incumbent product strategies.
4. **S-1 approval as benchmark**: Bitwise's S-1 approval timeline (same day as the 10 other issuers, Jan 10, 2024) tracks the regulatory-pipeline dynamics documented in the [[regulatory-precedent-cascade]] concept.

## Timeline

- **2016**: Founded by Hunter Horsley (CEO) and Hong Kim (CTO)
- **2019**: Bitwise withdraws first spot Bitcoin ETF application after SEC concerns
- **2021**: Re-files for spot Bitcoin ETF
- **2023-2024**: Amended filing with Coinbase surveillance-sharing agreement
- **2024-01-10**: SEC approves BITB along with 10 other spot Bitcoin ETFs
- **2024-01-11**: BITB begins trading; $238M first-day volume
