# US Drone Security Incidents

## Overview

A thread tracking unauthorized drone incursions over US military installations, mystery drone waves over US civilian areas, and the evolving US counter-drone policy response. The thread spans two distinct but partially overlapping phenomena: (1) verified incursions over sensitive military sites by unmanned aerial systems (UAS), and (2) mass sociogenic events where widespread public reports of mystery drones are investigated and attributed to routine explanations. These phenomena interact because genuine security concerns can be masked by civilian mass hysteria (the "hide in the noise" dynamic) and because each mystery drone wave triggers renewed legislative efforts to expand counter-drone authorities.

## Key Events

### 2023

- **2023-Dec**: Significant unauthorized drone incursions occur over Langley Air Force Base, Virginia. Multiple drones operate over the base for an extended period, evading detection and response. The incident prompts a response involving US government aircraft and becomes a catalyst for counter-drone technology development. No perpetrator is identified.

### 2024

- **2024-Aug**: Drone sightings over Plant 42, a classified USAF aircraft manufacturing plant in California, are confirmed by the Air Force. New FAA airspace restrictions imposed.

- **2024-Oct**: USNORTHCOM hosts "Falcon Peak" counter-drone technology demonstration in Colorado. One system tested had been used in combat in the Russo-Ukrainian war. The trials are a direct response to the Langley AFB incursions.

- **2024-Nov (UK/Germany)**: Unidentified drones reported over US bases in the UK (November) and over Ramstein Air Base and arms factories in Germany (December).

- **2024-Nov-13**: First confirmed drone sighting at Picatinny Arsenal, NJ — the beginning of the Northeast mystery drone wave. A contractor reports "a light rising straight up from the tree line and toward the arsenal."

- **2024-Nov-Dec**: Mystery drone wave spreads across New Jersey, then New York, Pennsylvania, Connecticut, Maryland, Massachusetts, Virginia, and other states. Thousands of reports. Most are later attributed to misidentified aircraft, authorized drones, hobbyist drones, and astronomical objects (constellation Orion, planet Venus).

- **2024-Dec-09**: Chinese national arrested for flying a drone over Vandenberg Space Force Base, California — attempting to board a flight to China at SFO.

- **2024-Dec-10**: Hill Air Force Base, Utah, acknowledges "unidentified drone swarms" over multiple US military bases.

- **2024-Dec-11**: NJ Assemblywoman Dawn Fantasia shares notes from legislative briefing: officials describe drones up to 6 feet in diameter, operating 6-7 hours nightly, with coordinated behavior.

- **2024-Dec-12**: White House's John Kirby states none of the visual observations can be verified; most are lawfully-operated manned aircraft. No evidence of security threat or foreign origin.

- **2024-Dec-12**: Multiple drone incursions over Marine Corps Base Camp Pendleton, CA (6 incursions Dec 9-15).

- **2024-Dec-16**: Joint FBI/DHS/DoD/FAA statement: 5,000+ reports reviewed, "nothing anomalous" found. Most sightings have routine explanations.

- **2024-Dec-17**: TSA documents reveal major sightings already debunked internally by Dec 17 — findings not shared with the public until released later via FOIA, prolonging public concern.

- **2024-Dec-18**: FAA bans drone flights over 22 NJ communities and parts of NY.

- **2024-Dec**: Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, OH, experiences airspace closures due to small drone incursions.

- **2024-Dec**: Nuclear Regulatory Commission alert about lost trace-radioactive object triggers "nuclear search" theory. The object is recovered by mid-December without drones. NNSA states its emergency response unit does not use drones for radiological detection.

### 2025

- **2025-Jan-28**: White House Press Secretary Karoline Leavitt states the drones were primarily FAA-authorized research drones and hobbyist/private drones — not a threat.

- **2025-Feb**: General Gregory Guillot (NORAD/USNORTHCOM) testifies: 350 drone incursions over 100+ US military bases in 2024. Requests expanded counter-drone authorities.

- **2025-Sep**: Second annual Falcon Peak counter-drone evaluation. Guillot announces a new rapid reaction force to respond to drone incursions over military bases, using systems from Anduril and others.

- **2025-Q3-Q4**: Legislation introduced but not passed: DRONE Act (grants for law enforcement drone acquisition), DEFENSE Act (state/local counter-UAS authority at mass gatherings). Senator Cotton successfully introduces counter-drone amendment to 2026 NDAA.

## Key Findings from the 2024-25 Wave

1. **Two separate phenomena coexisted**: (a) a mass sociogenic event where thousands of civilians misidentified aircraft/stars as drones, and (b) genuine unauthorized drone incursions over military bases. These must be analyzed separately.

2. **The "hide in the noise" dynamic**: Foreign Policy Research Institute analysis suggests that genuine adversary drone operations can be concealed within waves of false reports — making it harder for defenses to distinguish real threats.

3. **FOIA transparency gap**: TSA documents showing that major sightings were internally debunked by Dec 17 were withheld until months later, prolonging public concern and fueling calls for expanded counter-drone powers — a dynamic worth tracking for future incidents.

4. **Counter-drone legislation cycles**: Each major drone incident triggers renewed legislative efforts. The 2023 Langley incursion led to the 2024 Falcon Peak trials. The 2024 Northeast wave led to the 2025 DRONE Act and DEFENSE Act. The cycle repeats and is a predictable pattern.

## Status

Active. Counter-drone legislation remains in committee. The rapid reaction force was stood up in Sep 2025. Drone incursions over military bases continue. Future mystery drone waves are likely and would trigger the same dynamics.
