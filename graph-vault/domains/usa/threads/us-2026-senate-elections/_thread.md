# 2026 US Senate Elections

The 2026 US Senate elections are held on November 3, 2026, with 33 of the 100 Senate seats up for election. The winners serve six-year terms beginning January 3, 2027. This election determines Senate control for the remainder of Trump's second term.

## Stakes

- **Current Senate**: 53 Republicans, 45 Democrats, 2 Independents (both caucus with Democrats) → effective 53-47 Republican majority
- **Seats up**: 20 Democratic (including 2 independents), 13 Republican
- **Net change needed for flip**: Democrats need +4 seats (or +3 + White House tiebreaker) to take control
- **Key defensive states (Dems)**: Maine (King independent/Independent), Georgia (Ossoff), Michigan (Peters), Minnesota (Smith), New Hampshire (Shaheen), Virginia (Warner)
- **Key defensive states (GOP)**: Alaska (Murkowski/Sullivan), North Carolina (Tillis), Ohio (Moreno), Texas (Cornyn)
