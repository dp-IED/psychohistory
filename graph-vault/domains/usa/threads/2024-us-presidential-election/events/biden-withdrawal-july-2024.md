## Summary

President Joe Biden announced his withdrawal from the 2024 presidential race on July 21, 2024, following a cascade of party pressure after a poor debate performance against Donald Trump on June 27, 2024. He endorsed Vice President Kamala Harris, who became the Democratic nominee. This was the first time an incumbent president eligible for re-election withdrew so late in the campaign cycle.

## Trigger Cascade

1. June 27, 2024: Poor debate performance against Trump triggers party panic
2. July 2024: Donor pressure, elected official defections, media calls to withdraw
3. July 21, 2024: Formal withdrawal announcement + Harris endorsement
4. August 19-22, 2024: Democratic National Convention confirms Harris as nominee

## Forecasting Significance

- Canonical example of [[concepts/incumbent-withdrawal-cascade]]: latent vulnerability (age 81, ~39% approval) + trigger event (debate) + elite defection cascade
- Distinguishes from [[concepts/leadership-persistence-under-threat]]: Biden lacked compounding legal jeopardy, making withdrawal possible when political pressure crossed threshold
- Pre-cascade vulnerability was knowable: Q1 2023 polling showed majority of Democrats preferred another nominee (see [[entities/joe-biden]])
