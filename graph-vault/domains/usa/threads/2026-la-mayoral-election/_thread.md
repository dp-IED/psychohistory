# 2026 Los Angeles Mayoral Election

## Overview

The 2026 Los Angeles mayoral election is scheduled for June 2, 2026 (primary) with a runoff in November if no candidate receives >50%. Los Angeles, the second-largest US city (population ~3.8M), uses a nonpartisan primary system where the top two candidates advance regardless of party.

## Incumbent

- **Karen Bass** (elected 2022, first female mayor of LA) is eligible for re-election
- Bass defeated developer Rick Caruso 54.8% to 45.2% in the 2022 runoff
- Key issues: homelessness, housing affordability, public safety, 2028 Olympics preparation

## Dynamics

- **Celebrity effect**: Los Angeles has a history of celebrity mayoral candidates — actor Clint Eastwood (elected mayor of Carmel-by-the-Sea 1986), television host and actor Ronald Reagan (governor before presidency). However, the LA mayor's office requires serious administrative capacity.
- **Nonpartisan primary**: The top-two system means a celebrity candidate can advance to the runoff with a fragmented field even with 20-25% of the vote.
- **Homelessness crisis**: The dominant issue in LA politics. Any candidate must present a credible homelessness plan.
- **Name recognition**: Spencer Pratt's celebrity gives him significantly higher name recognition than most first-time candidates, but negative name recognition could also be a factor.
