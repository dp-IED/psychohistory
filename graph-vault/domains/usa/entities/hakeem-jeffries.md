# Hakeem Jeffries

## Summary

Hakeem Sekou Jeffries is the House Minority Leader (2023–present), representing New York's 8th congressional district (2013–present). Jeffries succeeded Nancy Pelosi as Democratic leader after the 2022 midterms. In the 118th and 119th Congresses, where Republicans held a narrow House majority with an internally divided caucus, Jeffries effectively became the most powerful figure in congressional appropriations — providing the decisive votes for virtually every major funding bill.

## Significance for Forecasting

Jeffries is the single most predictive single variable for US government shutdown questions in the 118th-119th Congress context:

1. **De facto governing majority**: Because the House Freedom Caucus can block Republican-only bills in the Rules Committee, virtually all government funding legislation requires Democratic votes. Jeffries controls the Democratic caucus vote. When Jeffries supports a CR, it passes. When he opposes, it fails.

2. **The "Green Card" signal**: Jeffries developed a signaling mechanism — literally holding up a green card on the floor to alert Democrats they could vote for a Republican-sponsored procedural measure. This became the reliable indicator that a bipartisan funding pathway was open.

3. **No vote wasted**: Jeffries consistently delivered Democratic votes strategically — never providing more than needed, never fewer. He gave exactly the number of votes required to pass the Dec 2024 American Relief Act, the Mar 2025 full-year CR, and the Apr 2024 foreign aid package.

4. **Bipartisan governance without coalition**: Jeffries never formed a formal governing coalition. He provided votes bill-by-bill, extracting concessions (earmarks, policy riders) each time. This gives Democrats maximum leverage without shared responsibility for governing.

## Forecasting-Relevant Attributes

- **Vote delivery reliability**: When Jeffries commits Democratic votes, near-100% delivery. When he does not, Democratic caucus is unified in opposition.
- **Concession price**: Jeffries's price for Democratic votes on CRs includes: no abortion/gun riders, clean disaster aid, level or increased non-defense spending, earmarks for Democratic members. Price is predictable and stable.
- **Leadership style**: Disciplined, message-controlled. Avoids media speculation. Communicates through whip operation and floor signals.
- **2024 foreign aid package**: Jeffries personally negotiated the legislative path and delivered majority of votes for $95B Ukraine/Israel/Taiwan aid package — a significant power projection by a minority leader.

## Timeline

- **2013-2023**: House member. Rising through Democratic leadership. Chair of House Democratic Caucus.
- **2023-Jan**: Elected House Minority Leader. Narrow Republican majority (222-213) gives him outsized influence.
- **2023-Sep 30**: Provides decisive votes for CR that funds government, effectively choosing to save McCarthy from shutdown. McCarthy is ousted anyway (Oct 3).
- **2024-Mar 6-23**: Provides Democratic votes for both FY2024 minibus packages.
- **2024-Sep 25**: Democratic votes provide majority for clean CR.
- **2024-Dec 20**: Provides Democratic votes for American Relief Act after two failed Republican attempts.
- **2025-Mar 14**: Does NOT whip Democrats to vote for full-year CR, but allows members to vote individually. 9 Democrats provide decisive Senate cloture votes.
- **2025**: Role continues as pivotal coalition partner.
