# Pamela Evette

## Position
- Lieutenant Governor of South Carolina (since 2019)
- Party: Republican
- 2026 bid: Candidate for SC Governor (Republican primary, June 9, 2026)

## Background
- Businesswoman; founded Quality Business Solutions (QBS), a payroll/HR services company
- First female Lt. Governor of SC (independently elected, not on governor's ticket)
- Ran with McMaster in 2018, re-elected 2022

## 2026 Governor Race
- McMaster term-limited -> open seat

- Key question: does she consolidate the establishment lane?
- Market: 31% YES to win primary -> competitive field, not clear frontrunner
