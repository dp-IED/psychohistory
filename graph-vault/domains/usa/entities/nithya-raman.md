# Nithya Raman

City Councilmember for LA's 4th District (Silver Lake, Los Feliz, Hollywood, Sherman Oaks area). Progressive champion elected in 2020 by defeating incumbent David Ryu — the first time an LA City Council incumbent had been unseated in 17 years. Re-elected in 2024 against moderate challenger Ethan Weaver (supported by police union/LAPD).

## Political Profile

- **Ideology**: Progressive left — homeless services reform (not criminalization), tenant protections, police accountability, transit-oriented development
- **Coalition**: Young renters, progressive activists, Hollywood creative class, unhoused advocacy groups
- **Opposition**: LAPD union (consistently opposes her), homeowner associations, moderate Democrats aligned with Karen Bass
- **Structural Position**: Only the second-ever LA City Council member to win re-election against a police-union-backed challenger

## 2026 Mayoral Context

If Karen Bass seeks re-election, Raman's mayoral path is blocked — she would not challenge an incumbent progressive (Bass is also progressive-left aligned). If Bass declines (age 72, facing homelessness crisis dissatisfaction), Raman enters a likely field including:
- Rick Caruso (self-funded, 2022 runner-up with 47% in runoff)
- Kevin de León (tarnished but name-recognized)
- Other councilmembers

Raman's strengths: activist base turnout, young voter enthusiasm, progressive donor network
Raman's weaknesses: limited citywide name recognition, police union opposition, geographic concentration in eastside districts
