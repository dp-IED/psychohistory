# Hillary Clinton

Former U.S. Secretary of State (2009-2013), former U.S. Senator from New York (2001-2009), and the 2016 Democratic presidential nominee. The first woman to win a major-party presidential nomination in U.S. history. Has not held elected office since 2013 and has repeatedly stated she will not run for office again.
