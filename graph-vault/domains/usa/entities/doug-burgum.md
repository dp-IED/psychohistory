# Doug Burgum

## Summary

Douglas James Burgum is an American businessman and politician serving as the 33rd Governor of North Dakota since 2016. He was a finalist in Donald Trump's 2024 Vice Presidential selection process.

Burgum entered the 2024 Republican presidential race as a long-shot moderate alternative but dropped out in December 2023 before the primaries began. He was then considered for Trump's VP slot — with his executive experience, Midwestern identity, and business background fitting the traditional governing model. He was ultimately passed over for JD Vance, whose more aggressive populist-nationalist style better reinforced Trump's campaign message.
