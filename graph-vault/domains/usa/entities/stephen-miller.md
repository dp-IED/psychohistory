# Stephen Miller

## Summary

Stephen Miller is an American far-right political advisor who served as a senior advisor to President Donald Trump (2017-2021) and returned as Deputy Chief of Staff for Policy in Trump's second term. Miller is the intellectual architect and primary policy driver of Trump's immigration agenda — both the restrictionist legal immigration policies and the aggressive border enforcement approach.

## Role in Immigration Policy

- **Primary author** of Trump's 2017 "Buy American, Hire American" executive order (EO 13788) which tightened H-1B enforcement, directed agencies to propose reforms, and increased scrutiny of specialty occupation classifications
- **Advocate for "merit-based" immigration** that would radically reduce overall legal immigration levels, eliminate the diversity visa lottery, and restrict family-based sponsorship to nuclear family only
- **Key figure** behind the travel ban (EO 13769), the public charge rule, the "Remain in Mexico" policy (Migrant Protection Protocols), and the Covid-era border closure (Title 42 public health order)
- **Drafted** the 2017 RAISE Act (Reforming American Immigration for Strong Employment) — legislation supported by Trump that proposed cutting legal immigration by 50% — which was introduced but never passed

## Stated Positions on H-1B

Miller has consistently argued that H-1B undermines American workers and wages, particularly in tech. Key statements and positions:

- H-1B should be dramatically scaled back or restructured to eliminate the lottery and instead prioritize "the highest-skilled and highest-paid applicants"
- The program is dominated by outsourcing firms (TCS, Infosys) which use it to replace American workers with cheaper foreign labor
- Has advocated for ending H-1B-dependent outsourcing (H-1B "body shops") through regulatory action
- In his second term role, he is positioned to push for maximum restriction on all immigration channels, including high-skilled

However, he faces countervailing pressure from the tech/libertarian wing of the Trump coalition ([[entities/elon-musk]], Vivek Ramaswamy, Peter Thiel) who advocate for expanding high-skilled immigration.

## Influence Assessment

Miller's influence is strongest on:
- Refugee admissions and asylum processing
- Public charge rule and welfare-immigration linkage
- Interior enforcement and ICE priorities
- Border wall and deterrence policies
- Legal immigration restriction framing

His influence is weaker on:
- High-skilled immigration when opposed by tech coalition (his H-1B restriction agenda succeeded in first term partly because the tech coalition was weaker)
- Legislative immigration reform (Congress has resisted comprehensive immigration changes since 2013 Gang of Eight)

## Significance for Forecasting

Miller is the highest-probability actor to push for H-1B restrictions or elimination. His proximity to Trump, his track record of turning immigration ideas into executive actions, and his position as Deputy Chief of Staff for Policy mean he is the person drafting and advancing any immigration-related EO. Understanding where Miller succeeds versus where he is blocked by coalition counter-pressure is essential for forecasting immigration policy outcomes in the second Trump term.
