# Merrick Garland

## Summary

Merrick Brian Garland is an American jurist and lawyer serving as the 86th US Attorney General since 2021. He previously served as a judge on the US Court of Appeals for the DC Circuit (1997-2021), including as Chief Judge (2013-2020). He was famously nominated to the Supreme Court by President Obama in March 2016 but was denied a hearing by the Republican-controlled Senate.

As Attorney General, Garland appointed Special Counsel Jack Smith in November 2022 to oversee the two federal investigations into Donald Trump (the January 6/election interference investigation and the Mar-a-Lago classified documents investigation). He also oversaw major antitrust enforcement actions (including the DOJ lawsuit against Google) and the prosecution of over 1,400 January 6 defendants.

## Significance for Forecasting

Garland's role in the Trump prosecutions established the framework for how the DOJ handles investigations into a political opponent of the sitting president. His decision to appoint a Special Counsel — rather than prosecuting directly — created a structural tension between independence (Smith operated autonomously) and accountability (Garland was subject to political pressure). For future forecasts about federal prosecutions of political figures, Garland's model of "appoint a Special Counsel and remain at arm's length" is likely to be the default precedent.

Garland's own judicial background (including having been Merchan's colleague on the DC Circuit) adds a layer of institutional credibility that insulated the DOJ from claims of political prosecution, though not from the delay-and-moot dynamics that ultimately ended the federal cases.

## Timeline

- **1997-2021**: Judge, DC Circuit Court of Appeals
- **2016**: Nominated to Supreme Court by Obama; no hearing held by Senate
- **2013-2020**: Chief Judge, DC Circuit
- **2021**: Confirmed as Attorney General
- **November 18, 2022**: Appoints Jack Smith as Special Counsel
- **2023-2024**: Oversaw the two federal Trump prosecutions
- **January 2025**: Smith resigns after releasing final report; both federal cases effectively concluded
