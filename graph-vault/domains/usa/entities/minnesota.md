# Minnesota

## Summary

Minnesota is a Midwestern US state with D+1 Cook PVI (trending bluer). Governor Tim Walz (DFL) won re-election in 2022 by 7.7%. Senator Tina Smith (DFL) is retiring in 2026, creating an open Senate seat. The DFL (Democratic-Farmer-Labor Party) is the state affiliate of the Democratic Party and has held the governorship since 2011 and both Senate seats since 2009.

## Current Status (June 2026)

- **Governor**: Tim Walz (DFL, since 2019), term-limited 2026
- **US Senators**: Amy Klobuchar (DFL, since 2007), Tina Smith (DFL, retiring 2026)
- **Open Senate seat**: Smith retirement creates competitive primary and general election
- **DFL Senate frontrunner**: Lt. Governor Peggy Flanagan at 73.5%
- **Governor race 2026**: Open seat — both parties have contested primaries

## Forecasting Significance

- **Senate open seat**: Minnesota open Senate seats attract strong candidates. The DFL nominee will be favored but the state's D+1 lean means Republicans can compete in wave years.
- **DFL primary dynamics**: Flanagan (73.5%) leads but the 26.5% residual is real. Rep. Angie Craig (MN-02, swing district), AG Keith Ellison, and St. Paul Mayor Melvin Carter have been mentioned as potential candidates.
- **General election**: D+1 with Walz coattails (term-limited, high approval). Generic ballot advantage to DFL but not prohibitive. Republican bench: former state senator Scott Jensen (2022 gov nominee, lost by 7.7%), Rep. Tom Emmer (MN-06, safe R), businessman.
- **Trend**: Minnesota has moved steadily Democratic in presidential elections but retains competitive down-ballot races. Presidential margins: D+7.1 (2020), D+1.5 (2016), D+7.7 (2012).
