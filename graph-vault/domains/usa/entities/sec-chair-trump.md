# SEC Chair (Trump Appointee)

## Summary

The current SEC Chair was appointed by President Donald Trump following Gary Gensler's resignation on January 20, 2025. This SEC chair has taken a markedly different approach to crypto regulation than the Gensler era, shifting from an "enforcement-first" posture toward regulatory clarity and accommodation of crypto industry interests. The new leadership has reviewed pending enforcement actions against Coinbase, Binance, and Kraken, settling several on terms more favorable to the exchanges, and has not initiated major new crypto enforcement actions. The shift has been a key variable in crypto market performance since 2025, supporting Bitcoin's price and the approval of additional crypto-linked ETFs.

## Significance for Forecasting

- **Crypto ETF expansion**: The SEC chair's crypto posture directly determines the pace and breadth of crypto ETF product approvals beyond Bitcoin and Ethereum (e.g., XRP, Solana ETFs). The earliest statutory deadline for XRP ETF filings is projected for mid-2026, making the SEC chair's stance a critical variable for those approval timelines.
- **Regulatory regime signal**: The shift from Gensler to a Trump-appointed SEC chair represents a structural regime change for US financial regulation. Any future change in SEC leadership (resignation, removal, or end of term) would be a major event for crypto markets.
- **Enforcement trajectory**: The SEC enforcement posture under the Trump-appointed chair — whether it continues to ease or re-tightens — is a key leading indicator for crypto industry risk assessment. Major enforcement actions or their absence directly affect exchange valuations and market confidence.
- **Congressional interaction**: The SEC chair's willingness to work with Congress on crypto market structure legislation (the STABLE Act and similar proposals) determines whether legislative clarity supplements or substitutes for regulatory accommodation.
