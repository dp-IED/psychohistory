# Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC)

The NRC is an independent US government agency responsible for regulating civilian use of nuclear materials, including nuclear power plants, research reactors, and radioactive materials. The NRC issues alerts and notifications about lost or missing radioactive material. Its notifications can trigger public speculation that may become the basis for forecasting questions.

## Relevance to Forecasting

- **Lost material alerts**: In early December 2024, the NRC issued an alert that an object with trace radioactivity had been lost in the Northeast US. This alert provided the factual basis for the "drones searching for nuclear weapons" speculation — though the NRC alert did NOT state or imply that drones were involved.
- **Recovery confirmation**: The object was recovered by mid-December 2024. No drones were used in the recovery.
