# Steve Hilton

## Summary

Steve Hilton is a British-American conservative political commentator, former Fox News host, and political strategist. He was a senior advisor to UK Prime Minister David Cameron (2010-2012) and later hosted "The Next Revolution" on Fox News (2017-2023). Hilton is a Republican candidate for the 2026 California gubernatorial election (Polymarket: 8.35% YES, $1.3M volume as of May 22, 2026).

## Significance for Forecasting

Hilton is the leading Republican candidate for California Governor in 2026. His candidacy is significant for several reasons:

1. **Fox News platform**: His Fox News hosting gave him name recognition with California's ~24% Republican electorate — a base that any Republican must consolidate to be competitive.

2. **Top-two primary advantage**: In a fractured Democratic field with 15+ candidates, Hilton's consolidated Republican base (85% probability to advance from primary) practically guarantees he will be one of the two general election candidates. The top-two system means he doesn't need to win a Republican primary — he just needs to be one of the top two vote-getters.

3. **"California populist" positioning**: Hilton's Fox News persona combined criticism of California's Democratic establishment with populist economic messaging. This hybrid approach targets the 24% NPP (no party preference) voters who are critical to any Republican path to victory in California.

4. **Structural headwinds**: Despite his advantages, a Republican winning statewide in California remains extremely difficult. The last Republican to win a California gubernatorial election was Arnold Schwarzenegger (2006 re-election). California voted against Trump by 20+ points in 2024.
