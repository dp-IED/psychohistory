# Alex Padilla

## Summary

Alejandro "Alex" Padilla is an American politician serving as a United States Senator from California since January 2021, when he was appointed by Governor Gavin Newsom to fill the vacancy created by Kamala Harris's election as Vice President. He won a full term in 2022 (61.5% of the vote). Prior to the Senate, Padilla served as California Secretary of State (2015-2021) and as a Los Angeles City Councilmember.
