## Summary

Nancy Pelosi served as Speaker of the US House of Representatives from 2007-2011 and 2019-2023, the first woman to hold the office. She remained a powerful Democratic figure after stepping down from leadership in January 2023, serving as Speaker Emerita.

During the July 2024 crisis over Biden's candidacy, Pelosi played a critical behind-the-scenes role in pressuring Biden to withdraw from the presidential race. Multiple reports indicated she was instrumental in creating the conditions for Biden's exit — privately communicating to donors, elected officials, and ultimately to Biden himself that continued candidacy would damage the party and down-ballot candidates. Her involvement was significant because she had been a longtime Biden ally and her defection from public support was a signal that withdrawal was inevitable.

## Significance for Forecasting

### The "Elder Statesman" Defection Signal
Pelosi's role in the Biden withdrawal illustrates a recurring pattern: when the party's elder statesperson or most respected institutional figure breaks with an incumbent, it catalyzes a cascade of elite defections. The signal works because:
- The elder statesperson cannot be dismissed as a disloyal upstart or factional opponent
- Their defection implies institutional judgment, not personal ambition
- Other party figures receive psychological permission to follow
- The media narrative shifts from "allies remain loyal" to "party elders urge withdrawal"

For future forecasts: when a respected institutional elder (former speaker, former president, senior committee chair) publicly or privately signals withdrawal pressure, the cascade probability increases substantially.

### Key Dates
- **November 2022**: Steps down from House Democratic leadership after Republicans win House majority
- **January 2023**: Becomes Speaker Emerita
- **July 2-20, 2024**: Works behind the scenes to pressure Biden to withdraw after poor debate performance
- **July 21, 2024**: Biden withdraws from 2024 presidential race
- **November 2024**: Retires from Congress at end of term after 40 years of service
