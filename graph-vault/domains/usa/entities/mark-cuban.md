# Mark Cuban

Billionaire entrepreneur, investor on Shark Tank, owner of the NBA's Dallas Mavericks (2000-2023). Was mentioned as a potential 2024 Democratic VP candidate due to his business credentials and media profile, though the speculation never reached the level of formal vetting.

## Significance for VP Forecasting

Cuban represents the "celebrity outsider" archetype in VP speculation — a recurring pattern where non-politicians are floated for VP to add business credibility or media appeal. His inclusion on the "another man" exclusion list demonstrates the Polymarket pattern of capturing every remotely plausible candidate:

- Cuban had no elected-office experience, making him a historically unusual VP pick
- He had no formal ties to the Democratic campaign infrastructure
- His inclusion on the list was a "catch-all" — expanding the exclusion list to cover every conceivable candidate who might be proposed, rather than a reflection of actual VP consideration
- This is the weakest category of exclusion-list entry: plausible-to-mention but not plausible-to-select
