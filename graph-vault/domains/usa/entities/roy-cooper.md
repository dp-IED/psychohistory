# Roy Cooper

## Summary

Roy Asberry Cooper III is an American politician and attorney serving as the 75th Governor of North Carolina since 2017. He was on Kamala Harris's short list for Vice President in 2024.

Cooper has governed a purple state through a period of Republican legislative supermajorities, wielding veto power to moderate conservative legislation. His experience as state Attorney General (2001-2017) provides a law-enforcement background that offered potential campaign advantage. Cooper was reportedly eliminated from VP consideration early in the process due to North Carolina's state law conflict over running mates — a recurring structural constraint in VP selection.
