# Xavier Becerra

## Summary

Xavier Becerra is an American politician and lawyer who served as the 25th US Secretary of Health and Human Services (2021-2025) under President Biden, California Attorney General (2017-2021), and US Representative for California's 34th congressional district (1993-2017). He is the frontrunner for the 2026 California gubernatorial election (Polymarket: 67.65% YES, $903K volume as of May 22, 2026).

## Significance for Forecasting

Becerra is the single most important actor in the 2026 California gubernatorial election. His frontrunner status derives from:

1. **Statewide name recognition**: As CA Attorney General (elected 2018 with 63.6%) and HHS Secretary, Becerra has higher name recognition than any other declared candidate. The only potential candidate who could match or exceed his recognition is Kamala Harris (who has not entered the race).

2. **Institutional support**: Becerra has deep ties to the California Democratic establishment, organized labor, and Latino political organizations. The CA Democratic Party endorsement carries significant weight in the primary.

3. **Fundraising capacity**: His HHS tenure connects him to healthcare industry donors; his Congressional tenure connects him to traditional Democratic donor networks.

4. **Latino electorate**: Latinos are ~40% of California's population and ~30% of the electorate. Becerra would be the first Latino governor of California since Romualdo Pacheco (1875).

## Vulnerabilities

1. **HHS tenure during COVID-19**: His oversight of the federal pandemic response could be attacked by both anti-mandate Republicans and pandemic-skeptic independents. California's progressive base may also fault HHS for insufficient action on healthcare costs.

2. **Limited executive experience**: While he ran the CA DOJ (4,500 employees), HHS is a $1.7 trillion agency with 80,000+ employees. His HHS management record could face scrutiny.

3. **"Washington insider" label**: After 28 years in DC (24 in Congress + 4 at HHS), Becerra may face the "career politician" critique. California elected Schwarzenegger (2003) and Newsom (2018) partly on outsider/change messaging.

## Timeline

- **1958**: Born in Sacramento, California
- **1993-2017**: US Representative (CA-30, later CA-34)
- **2017-2021**: 33rd California Attorney General
- **2021-2025**: 25th US Secretary of Health and Human Services
- **2025-present**: Declared candidate for California Governor
