# Spencer Pratt

Spencer Pratt (b. 1983) is an American reality television personality, social media influencer, and podcast host. He gained fame on MTV's The Hills (2006-2010) alongside his wife Heidi Montag. He is now a candidate for Mayor of Los Angeles in the 2026 election.
