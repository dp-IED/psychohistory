# Fani Willis

## Summary

Fani Taifa Willis is an American attorney serving as the District Attorney for Fulton County, Georgia (Atlanta) since 2021. She brought a sweeping RICO indictment against Donald Trump and 18 co-defendants in August 2023 for alleged efforts to overturn Georgia's 2020 presidential election results.

Willis's prosecution became embroiled in a disqualification scandal when it emerged she had a personal romantic relationship with Special Prosecutor Nathan Wade, whom she had appointed to lead the case. The relationship was disclosed during a January 2024 hearing on a motion to disqualify, and in December 2024, the Georgia Court of Appeals disqualified Willis and her entire office from the case, throwing the prosecution into prolonged appeals.

## Significance for Forecasting

The Willis disqualification saga illustrates a structural vulnerability in high-profile state prosecutions of political figures: when a single local prosecutor brings charges against a national political figure, any personal or professional vulnerability becomes a weapon for disqualification. This pattern — the DA-as-liability — affects forecasts about:

- **Georgia case viability**: With Willis disqualified, the Georgia prosecution faces prolonged delays regardless of the merits. The case may never proceed to trial.
- **State-level prosecution risk**: Willis's experience may deter other state prosecutors from bringing charges against national political figures, knowing that any personal flaw will be exploited.
