# NY-15 Congressional District

## Summary

New York's 15th Congressional District covers the South Bronx (New York City). It is one of the most Democratic congressional districts in the United States (D+35 Cook PVI). The current representative is Ritchie Torres (D, since 2021).

## Current Status (June 2026)

- **Incumbent**: Ritchie Torres (D, since 2021)
- **Cook PVI**: D+35 — among the top 10 most Democratic districts nationally
- **2026 primary**: Torres (95.3%) vs Amanda Septimo (0.4%) — near-certain incumbent renomination
- **General election**: Safe Democratic — district will not flip

## Demographics

- **Population**: ~720,000
- **Demographics**: ~68% Hispanic/Latino, ~28% Black, ~2% White, ~2% Asian
- **Median income**: ~$30K (among lowest nationally)
- **Key neighborhoods**: Mott Haven, Hunts Point, Belmont, Melrose, Morrisania

## Forecasting Significance

- **Safe seat**: D+35 means the Democratic nominee wins the general election regardless of national environment. The only competitive stage is the Democratic primary.
- **Primary dynamics**: Incumbent Torres faces token opposition (Septimo at 0.4%). Incumbents in safe D+ seats with strong fundraising and no scandal are near-deterministic primary winners.
- **Midterm context**: Even in a strong Republican midterm (D+3-5 nationally), NY-15 would remain Democratic by 25+ points.
