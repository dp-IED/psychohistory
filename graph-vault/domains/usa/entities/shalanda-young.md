# Shalanda Young

## Role

Shalanda Young served as Director of the Office of Management and Budget under President Joe Biden from March 2022 to January 2025. She was the first Black woman to lead OMB. Previously served as OMB Deputy Director (2021-2022) and as Staff Director for the House Appropriations Committee (2017-2021).

## Forecasting Relevance

Young is the central actor in a critical forecasting variable: **the OMB Director's discretion over whether a funding lapse triggers a formal government shutdown**.

### The December 2024 Decision

On December 20, 2024, the existing CR expired. The replacement bill (American Relief Act, 2025) had passed Congress but was not yet signed by President Biden. The funding technically lapsed between CR expiration and bill signing.

Young had the authority to:

- **Activate formal shutdown procedures** (furlough non-excepted employees, cease operations) — which would have made the Dec 2024 episode an unambiguous formal shutdown
- **Not activate procedures** (direct agencies to continue normal operations because passage was imminent) — which she chose, making the episode a "technical funding lapse" under standard government definitions

Her choice not to activate procedures meant the event's classification depended entirely on how the question's resolution text defined "shutdown." Polymarket questions that define shutdown broadly (any funding lapse = shutdown) would resolve YES; questions that require OMB activation (furloughs, formal procedures) would resolve NO.

### Institutional Background

Young's background is distinctive: she spent four years as House Appropriations Committee Staff Director before entering the executive branch. This means she had deep personal relationships with both Democratic and Republican appropriations staffers — relationships that likely informed her judgment that passage was genuinely imminent (as opposed to a prolonged stalemate). Her institutional knowledge of congressional timelines may have been a factor in her decision not to activate shutdown procedures.

## Forecasting Variables

When a question asks about a US government shutdown, Young's role (or the role of the sitting OMB Director) is relevant to:

1. **Dec 2024-specific questions**: Her specific decision-making context (imminent passage, House-Senate dynamics, relationship with Congress) determines whether the event was a "shutdown" under various definitions.

2. **General OMB-Director variable**: The sitting OMB Director's institutional background (House vs. Senate staff, executive branch career, political appointment) affects their likelihood of activating shutdown procedures during a funding gap.

3. **Transition-period dynamics**: During the lame-duck period (Nov 2024 - Jan 2025), Young served under Biden. After Jan 2025, Russell Vought took over under Trump. The transition of OMB Directors changes the institutional posture on shutdown declarations.
