# Ritchie Torres

## Summary

Ritchie Torres is a Democratic Congressman representing New York's 15th Congressional District (the Bronx) since 2021. He is the first openly gay Afro-Latino member of Congress. As of June 2026, he is the near-certain Democratic nominee for re-election in NY-15 at 95.3% on Polymarket (~$10K volume).
