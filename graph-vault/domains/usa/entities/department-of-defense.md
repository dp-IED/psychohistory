# Department of Defense (DoD)

The US Department of Defense is the cabinet department responsible for national security and military affairs, encompassing the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Space Force, and other defense agencies. In domestic security context, the DoD operates under the Posse Comitatus Act (which restricts military involvement in law enforcement) but retains authority to protect military installations. The DoD's counter-drone posture and public statements on incursions are directly relevant to forecasting questions about airspace security.

## Relevance to Forecasting

- **Military base incursions**: The DoD tracks unauthorized drone incursions over military installations. In Feb 2025, General Gregory Guillot (NORAD/USNORTHCOM) reported 350 incursions over 100+ US bases in 2024.
- **Joint statements**: The DoD participates in interagency statements on domestic security incidents. The Dec 16, 2024 joint statement with DHS/FBI/FAA found nothing anomalous in 5,000+ civilian reports.
- **Counter-drone authorities**: DoD operates under Title 10 U.S. Code Section 130i for counter-drone operations. General Guillot requested expanded authorities in 2025.
- **Falcon Peak**: DoD-hosted annual counter-drone technology evaluation (Oct 2024, Sep 2025).
- **Rapid reaction force**: Established Sep 2025 to respond to drone incursions over military bases.
