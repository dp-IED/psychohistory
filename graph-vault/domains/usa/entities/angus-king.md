# Angus King

Angus King is the junior United States Senator from Maine, serving since 2013. He is an independent who caucuses with the Democratic Party. Prior to the Senate, he served as Governor of Maine from 1995 to 2003 (two terms as an independent).

## Key Facts

- **Born**: March 31, 1944 (age 82 in 2026)
- **Senate term**: 2013-2027 (current term ends January 3, 2027)
- **Caucus**: Democratic (aligns with Democrats for committee assignments and organization)
- **Committee assignments**: Armed Services, Intelligence, Energy & Natural Resources
- **2026 election**: King's seat is up in 2026. He would be 82 and has not yet announced retirement.

## Political Profile

- Centrist independent with strong bipartisan credentials
- Key swing vote in the 50-50 Senate of 2021-2022
- Focus on energy independence, national security, and fiscal responsibility
- Maine's unique ranked-choice voting system affects the electoral dynamics
