# Clarity Act

## Overview

The "Clarity for Digital Tokens Act" (commonly called the "Clarity Act") is proposed US federal legislation establishing a comprehensive regulatory framework for digital assets. See [[domains/usa/threads/clarity-act/_thread]] for full legislative analysis.

## Key Provisions (Expected)

- **Digital asset taxonomy**: Classifies tokens as digital commodities, digital securities, or payment stablecoins
- **Regulatory jurisdiction**: Defines SEC vs CFTC oversight boundaries; may establish a new self-regulatory organization
- **Exchange registration**: Creates a federal registration framework for crypto trading platforms
- **Stablecoin regulation**: Reserve requirements, redemption rights, and prudential standards for stablecoin issuers
- **Consumer protection**: Disclosure requirements, custody standards, and anti-manipulation provisions
- **Innovation provisions**: Safe harbor for token projects, regulatory sandboxes, decentralized network exemptions
