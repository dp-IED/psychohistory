# Matt Gaetz

## Summary

Matt Gaetz is an American politician and member of the House Freedom Caucus known for procedural brinkmanship and alignment with Donald Trump. He is most significant for filing the motion to vacate (October 2, 2023) that removed Speaker Kevin McCarthy — the first successful removal of a House Speaker in US history. This act directly affected the Biden impeachment inquiry trajectory: McCarthy was singular in having announced the inquiry without a formal House vote, and his removal created a leadership vacuum during a critical period for the investigation.

## Key Events

- **2023-10-02**: Filed the motion to vacate against Speaker Kevin McCarthy, citing McCarthy's passage of a clean CR with Democratic votes to avert a government shutdown.
- **2023-10-03**: House voted 216-210 to remove McCarthy — 8 Republicans and all 208 Democrats voting present. First successful removal of a Speaker.
- **2023-10-25**: Mike Johnson elected Speaker after three weeks of Republican factional infighting.
- **2024-11-13**: Trump nominated Gaetz for Attorney General — a nomination that would prove highly controversial and ultimately withdrawn on November 21, 2024.

## Significance for Forecasting

- **Indirect effect on impeachment trajectory**: Gaetz's removal of McCarthy had a critical downstream effect on the Biden impeachment inquiry. McCarthy had announced the inquiry unilaterally in September 2023 without a House vote, and his removal paused the inquiry's momentum during the search for a successor. The new Speaker, Mike Johnson, approached the inquiry differently — more methodical, less willing to force a floor vote on articles without clear evidence. The leadership change effectively slowed the impeachment timeline and reduced the probability of articles reaching the floor.

- **The motion to vacate as structural constraint**: Gaetz's successful use of the motion to vacate established that the single-member threshold is a credible threat. This constrained Johnson throughout his speakership: any Speaker who relies on bipartisan support for funding bills or procedural moves faces existential risk from a small defection. This affected the impeachment inquiry because Johnson needed to keep his conference united — pushing articles of impeachment that moderate Republicans opposed could trigger another motion to vacate.

- **Trump nomination as control variable**: Gaetz's nomination as Attorney General (November 2024) — though withdrawn — signaled that extreme procedural disruptors can be rewarded with high office, potentially increasing the frequency of such tactics in future Congresses.
