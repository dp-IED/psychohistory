# Los Angeles

Second-largest US city (pop ~3.8M). Mayor elected in non-partisan primary → runoff system.

## Political Dynamics

- **Current Mayor**: Karen Bass (inaugurated Dec 2022), first woman and second Black mayor
- **Term Limits**: Two 4-year terms; Bass is eligible for re-election in 2026
- **Key Issues 2026**: Homelessness crisis (>46,000 unhoused), housing affordability, public safety (LAPD staffing), wildfire recovery (Jan 2025 Palisades/Eaton fires)

## 2026 Mayoral Election

Open seat if Bass does not seek re-election. If she runs, she is the heavy favorite as incumbent. Key challengers emerge if she declines:
- **Nithya Raman**: City Council District 4 (Silver Lake, Los Feliz, Hollywood). Progressive champion, MIT urban planning degree. Elected 2020, re-elected 2024 against moderate challenger. Focus on homeless services reform, tenant protections, police accountability.
- Other potential candidates: Rick Caruso (2022 runner-up, self-funded real estate developer), Kevin de León (former councilmember, tarnished by leaked audio scandal)
