# Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez

U.S. Representative for New York's 14th congressional district (2019-present). A progressive Democratic figure and member of "The Squad," known for her advocacy of the Green New Deal, Medicare for All, and progressive economic policy.

## 2024 Election Relevance

- Was on the "another woman" exclusion list for the 2024 Democratic VP nominee question
- While a polarizing figure, her inclusion on the exclusion list reflected her prominence rather than realistic VP consideration
- Represented the progressive-left option for ticket composition, though strategists considered her too polarizing for the national ticket
