# Transportation Security Administration (TSA)

The TSA is a component of the Department of Homeland Security responsible for transportation security, including aviation security. During the 2024 Northeast drone wave, the TSA conducted forensic analysis of multiple high-profile drone reports, debunking them as misidentified commercial aircraft.

## Relevance to Forecasting

- **Forensic analysis**: The TSA analyzed footage from several high-profile "drone" incidents and determined they were actually commercial aircraft, propeller planes in turbulence, and helicopters. These findings were documented internally by December 17, 2024, but were not publicly released until months later via FOIA.
- **FOIA transparency dynamic**: The TSA's delayed disclosure of debunked sightings prolonged public concern — a pattern worth tracking for future incidents.
- **Key findings from TSA analysis (2024 drone wave)**:
  - Coast Guard vessel "followed by drones": Actually commercial jets making S-shaped maneuvers approaching JFK
  - "Gray mist spraying" over Clinton, NJ: Actually a Beechcraft Baron 58 in turbulence creating wingtip vortices
  - Salem Nuclear Power Plant sighting: Coincided with a UH-60 Black Hawk, Cessna C150, and commercial flight paths
  - RVCC medical helicopter incident: Three aircraft approaching Solberg Airport misidentified as drones
