# Tim Scott

U.S. Senator from South Carolina (2013-present) and 2024 Republican presidential candidate. Was discussed as a potential VP pick for Donald Trump but did not advance to the reported finalist pool (Vance, Rubio, Burgum).

## Political Career

- **U.S. House of Representatives** (2011-2013)
- **U.S. Senate** (2013-present): Appointed by Governor Nikki Haley in 2013, won special election in 2014, re-elected in 2016 and 2022. The first Black Republican elected to the Senate from the South since Reconstruction. Serves on Banking, Finance, and HELP committees.
- **2024 Presidential Campaign**: Ran for president, positioning as a positive conservative message. Withdrew in November 2023 before the primaries and endorsed Trump.

## 2024 Veepstakes Significance

Scott was frequently discussed as a VP contender due to his demographic profile (Black conservative), his strong fundraising network, and his positive-message contrast with Trump's confrontational style. His failure to advance to the finalist pool — despite the obvious balancing value he would have added — demonstrates that the reinforcement model (amplifying Trump's message) completely dominated any balancing logic. Vance was chosen because he was the most effective media surrogate for Trump's message, not because he balanced the ticket.
