# Elizabeth Warren

U.S. Senator from Massachusetts (2013-present) and former presidential candidate (2020). A leading progressive voice in the Democratic Party, known for her work on consumer financial protection, antitrust enforcement, and banking regulation.

## 2024 Election Relevance

- Was on the "another woman" exclusion list for the 2024 Democratic VP nominee question
- Was widely discussed as a potential VP contender in 2020 (Biden ultimately selected Harris)
- Remains a prominent senator and policy influencer through the 2024 cycle
