# Josh Shapiro

## Summary

Joshua David Shapiro is an American politician serving as the 48th Governor of Pennsylvania since 2023. He was a leading finalist in Kamala Harris's 2024 Vice Presidential selection process and is widely discussed as a potential 2028 presidential candidate.

Shapiro emerged as a Democratic national figure through his tenure as Pennsylvania Attorney General (2017-2023), where he oversaw the release of the grand jury report on Catholic Church sexual abuse and fought Trump administration policies. As governor, he navigated a divided state government (Democratic governor, Republican-controlled House/Senate) to deliver a $48.3B budget with education funding increases. His high approval ratings (55%+ in swing-state Pennsylvania) and strong fundraising network make him a prominent figure in Democratic national politics.
