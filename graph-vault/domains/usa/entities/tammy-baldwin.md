# Tammy Baldwin

U.S. Senator from Wisconsin (2013-present), former U.S. Representative (1999-2013). The first openly gay U.S. Senator and a progressive Democrat with strong labor and healthcare advocacy record.

## 2024 Election Relevance

- Was on the "another woman" exclusion list for the 2024 Democratic VP nominee question
- Represented the Wisconsin swing-state regional option for VP selection
- Her LGBTQ+ identity was a potential historic selection angle though likely seen as identity-concentrating by strategists
