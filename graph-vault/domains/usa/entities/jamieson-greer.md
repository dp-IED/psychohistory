## Summary

Jamieson Greer is an American trade lawyer who served as United States Trade Representative (USTR) in President Donald Trump's second term. He previously served as Chief of Staff to USTR Robert Lighthizer in Trump's first term (2017-2021), where he was involved in the US-China trade war, the USMCA negotiation, and Section 232 steel/aluminum tariff actions. Greer's appointment signaled continuity with the first-term protectionist trade approach. As USTR, Greer was responsible for negotiating trade disputes and Section 301 tariff actions, including managing the ongoing US-China trade war and EU-US trade negotiations.

## Forecasting Significance

Greer's presence as USTR affects the probability of tariff actions: his background as Lighthizer's protégé means the administration has experienced trade professionals who can navigate Section 232 and Section 301 processes efficiently, reducing institutional friction for new tariff actions.
