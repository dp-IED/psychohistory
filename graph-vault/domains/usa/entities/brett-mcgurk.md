## Summary

Brett McGurk served as the White House National Security Council Coordinator for the Middle East and North Africa under President Joe Biden (2021–2025). He was the principal US diplomatic point-person for the Israel-Hamas ceasefire negotiations throughout 2024–2025, and played a pivotal role orchestrating the January 2025 ceasefire breakthrough by coordinating with Trump's incoming envoy Steve Witkoff during the 2024–2025 presidential transition.

## Significance for Forecasting

McGurk is a **career diplomatic operator** who served across multiple administrations (George W. Bush, Obama, Trump I, Biden), making him one of the few US diplomats with continuity across partisan transitions. His significance for forecasting:

1. **Institutional memory**: McGurk's tenure across administrations means he knew the negotiation history from the May 2024 proposal forward, enabling him to rapidly transition from one administration to the next.
2. **Bipartisan continuity**: His ability to work with both the Biden and Trump teams meant the ceasefire framework didn't break during the transition — a rare structural advantage.
3. **Transition diplomat pattern**: McGurk exemplifies a class of career diplomats who enable policy continuity during presidential transitions. Their presence is a leading indicator of transition-window diplomatic breakthroughs.

## Key Role in Jan 2025 Ceasefire

- **May 2024–Jan 2025**: Lead US negotiator on the three-phase ceasefire framework
- **Nov 2024–Jan 2025**: Coordinated directly with Trump envoy Steve Witkoff to align both administrations' positions — a precedent-setting level of transition cooperation
- **Jan 15, 2025**: The ceasefire framework was announced after months of McGurk-led shuttle diplomacy, with the transition coordination as the final catalyst
