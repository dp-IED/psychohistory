# Barack Obama

44th President of the United States (2009-2017), U.S. Senator from Illinois (2005-2008), and the first African American president. Was (implausibly) included on the "another man" exclusion list for the 2024 Democratic VP nominee question — a sign that Polymarket market creators sometimes include obviously-ineligible figures to pad the exclusion list.

## Political Career

- **U.S. Senate** (2005-2008): Served less than one term before running for president.
- **President** (2009-2017): Two-term presidency. Key achievements: Affordable Care Act (2010), Dodd-Frank financial regulation (2010), Operation Neptune Spear (killing of Bin Laden, 2011), Iran nuclear deal (2015), Paris Climate Agreement (2015), normalization of relations with Cuba (2014-2016), American Recovery and Reinvestment Act (2009).
- **Post-presidency**: Has remained politically active through the Obama Foundation; has endorsed Democratic candidates including Biden (2020, 2024) and Harris (2024). Served as a campaign surrogate but notably stayed neutral during the 2020 Democratic primaries until after the field narrowed.

## Significance for VP Forecasting

Obama's inclusion on the "another man" list is a structural insight about Polymarket exclusion lists: they often include figures who are constitutionally or practically ineligible (a former president cannot be VP without amending the Constitution, as the 12th Amendment prohibits anyone ineligible for the presidency from serving as VP). The inclusion of a former president reveals that market creators prioritize covering every possible interpretation of "another" over accuracy — and that forecasters must separately evaluate the plausibility of each exclusion-list entry. A list that includes impossible candidates is longer than it should be, and the effective exclusion list is shorter than the nominal one.
