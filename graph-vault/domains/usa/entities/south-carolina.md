# South Carolina

## Structural Position
- Electoral votes: 9 (reliably Republican; Trump +17.6 in 2024)
- Governor: Henry McMaster (R, term-limited 2026)
- Senators: Tim Scott (R), Lindsey Graham (R)
- 2026 Governor race: Open seat. Republican primary June 9, 2026. General Nov 3, 2026.
- Key trait: Republican primary = de facto general election. Democrat hasn't won governorship since 1998. Runoff required if no candidate exceeds 50%.

## Political Dynamics

- Trump endorsement decisive in Republican primaries
- 2026 primary field: Pamela Evette (Lt. Gov), potentially Alan Wilson (AG), Mark Burns
- Evette has incumbency advantage but not automatic — SC Lt. Gov is independently elected

## Recent Developments (as of May 2026)

- McMaster endorsed Evette
- Trump has not yet endorsed in the primary
