# Susan Collins

Susan Collins is the senior United States Senator from Maine, first elected in 1996. She is a moderate Republican known for crossing party lines on key votes. She is the most senior Republican woman in the Senate.

## Key Facts

- **Born**: December 7, 1952
- **Senate term**: 1997-2027 (current term ends January 3, 2027)
- **Committee assignments**: Appropriations, Health, Education, Labor & Pensions
- **2026 election**: Not up for re-election in 2026 (last re-elected 2020)

## Political Profile

- Moderate Republican with strong Maine constituency
- Known for decisive votes on Supreme Court confirmations (voted for Kavanaugh and Gorsuch, voted against ACB)
- Key swing vote on appropriations and health care
- Survived competitive 2020 re-election against Sara Gideon, winning 51-49
