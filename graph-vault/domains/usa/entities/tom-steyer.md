# Tom Steyer

## Summary

Thomas Fahr Steyer is an American hedge fund manager, philanthropist, environmental activist, and politician. He founded Farallon Capital (1986-2012) and was a leading Democratic donor and climate activist through NextGen America. Steyer ran for President of the United States in the 2020 Democratic primaries, spending over $250 million of his own money, and later served as a key impeachment advocate. He is the frontrunner for the 2026 California gubernatorial election (Polymarket: 31.6% YES at $3.3M volume, as of May 18, 2026).

## Significance for Forecasting

Steyer represents a distinct candidate type: the self-funded billionaire activist. His 2020 presidential campaign demonstrated both the power of unlimited self-funding (sustained campaign when grassroots-funded rivals dropped out) and its limits (billionaire status as a liability in a Democratic primary dominated by Warren/Sanders progressives). His 2026 California gubernatorial campaign tests whether the pattern holds in a statewide election where media markets are expensive but the electorate is more moderate than the national Democratic primary electorate.

California governors since 1990: Pete Wilson (R), Gray Davis (D), Arnold Schwarzenegger (R), Jerry Brown (D), Gavin Newsom (D). The state has trended reliably Democratic. An open-seat race (Newsom term-limited) with a crowded field structurally advantages candidates with high name recognition and self-funding capacity.

## Timeline

- **1957**: Born in New York City
- **1986**: Founds Farallon Capital Management
- **2012**: Retires from Farallon; shifts to full-time philanthropy and political activism
- **2013**: Co-founds NextGen Climate (later NextGen America), focused on climate advocacy and youth voter turnout
- **2017**: Leads effort to impeach Donald Trump; launches Need to Impeach campaign
- **2019-2020**: Runs for Democratic presidential nomination; spends $250M+; suspends campaign after Super Tuesday (Feb 2020)
- **2025-2026**: Expected candidate for California Governor
