# Chad Bianco

## Summary

Chad Bianco is the Sheriff of Riverside County, California, and a Republican candidate for the 2026 California gubernatorial election. He has gained statewide attention for his law-and-order stance and criticism of California's criminal justice reform policies, particularly Proposition 47 and Proposition 57.

## Significance for Forecasting

Bianco represents the right-flank Republican alternative to Steve Hilton in the 2026 California governor race. While Hilton is the leading Republican candidate (8.35% YES), Bianco (1.75% YES) appeals to the law-and-order Republican base that may find Hilton's "California populist" positioning too moderate. In a top-two primary, Bianco's viability depends on whether he can consolidate the conservative Republican vote or whether the conservative base coalesces around Hilton.

## Political Profile

- Riverside County Sheriff since 2019
- Vocal critic of California's criminal justice reforms, particularly Prop 47 (theft thresholds) and Prop 57 (parole for nonviolent offenders)
- Gained national attention for confrontations with California's Democratic leadership over COVID-19 restrictions, sanctuary state policies, and homeless encampment enforcement
- Endorsed by law enforcement unions and conservative groups
- Struggles with name recognition outside Southern California and limited fundraising compared to Hilton's Fox News platform

## California Governor Context

- Republican candidate in the June 2, 2026 top-two primary
- Polymarket win odds: 1.75% YES ($1.9M market)
- Primary advancement odds: must finish in top two of a 30+ candidate field
- The Hilton vs Bianco dynamic determines whether Republicans consolidate behind one candidate or split the conservative vote
- Structural headwind: California's Democratic registration advantage (~46% D vs ~24% R) makes any Republican general election win extremely difficult
