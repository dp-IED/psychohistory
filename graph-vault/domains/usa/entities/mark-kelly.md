# Mark Kelly

U.S. Senator from Arizona (2020-present), retired NASA astronaut, and U.S. Navy captain. Was a finalist in the 2024 Democratic VP selection process, widely reported as among the top contenders before the pick narrowed to Shapiro and Walz.

## Political Career

- **Astronaut**: Flew four Space Shuttle missions (2001-2011), commanded STS-124 and STS-134. Served as a U.S. Navy captain and test pilot.
- **U.S. Senate**: Won Arizona special election in 2020 (defeating Martha McSally), re-elected in 2022 (defeating Blake Masters). Has governed as a moderate Democrat in a swing state.
- **Personal**: Husband of former U.S. Representative Gabby Giffords (D-AZ), who survived an assassination attempt in 2011. Both have been leading gun control advocates.

## 2024 Veepstakes Significance

Kelly was considered a strong VP contender because of his swing-state value (Arizona), national security credentials (astronaut, Navy), and moderate profile. He was eliminated from consideration when Harris narrowed to Walz and Shapiro — reportedly because the campaign prioritized message reinforcement over electoral map expansion.
