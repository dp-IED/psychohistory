# Wes Moore

Governor of Maryland (2023-present), author, and former CEO of the Robin Hood Foundation. Was mentioned as a potential VP candidate in 2024 speculation, though he was not a reported finalist.

## Political Career

- **Governor of Maryland** (2023-present): Won in 2022 by 32 points in a safely Democratic state. First Black governor of Maryland. Has focused on economic development, education, and service programs.
- **Author**: Wrote "The Other Wes Moore" (2010), a bestseller about two men with the same name and different life outcomes.
- **Military service**: U.S. Army veteran, served in Afghanistan as a paratrooper and captain.

## 2024 Veepstakes Significance

Moore represents the "rising star" category of VP speculation — a popular first-term governor with strong communication skills and a compelling personal narrative. However, Maryland is safely Democratic (no swing-state value), and Moore was too new to statewide office (less than 2 years as governor) for a VP pick. His speculation reflected his potential as a future national candidate (post-2028) rather than 2024 viability.
