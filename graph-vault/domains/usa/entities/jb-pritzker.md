# JB Pritzker

Governor of Illinois (2019-present), billionaire heir to the Hyatt hotel fortune, and a prominent Democratic donor and figure. Was discussed as a potential Democratic VP contender or presidential candidate but did not advance to the formal vetting stage of the 2024 process.

## Political Career

- **Governor of Illinois** (2019-present): Won in 2018, re-elected in 2022. Signed major legislation on cannabis legalization, minimum wage increase, reproductive rights, and infrastructure. Has positioned Illinois as a "sanctuary state" for abortion access post-Dobbs.
- **Philanthropy**: Major donor to Democratic causes and candidates through his Pritzker investment firm and personal wealth. Co-founded the Pritzker Group, a private investment firm.

## 2024 Veepstakes Significance

Pritzker represents the archetype of a candidate whose veepstakes profile is based on donor access and Midwestern positioning rather than electoral coalition expansion (Illinois is Safe D). His elimination from consideration demonstrates that private-sector wealth and donor-network value are subordinate to electoral map logic in VP selection. The reinforcement model did not favor a governor whose state was already safely Democratic.
