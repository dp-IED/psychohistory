# Scott McAfee

## Summary

Scott McAfee is a Fulton County Superior Court Judge in Georgia, presiding over the Georgia election interference case (Trump et al. v. Willis). He was appointed to the bench in 2023 by Governor Brian Kemp.

McAfee has overseen the disqualification proceedings against DA Fani Willis, ruling in March 2024 that Willis could remain on the case if Special Prosecutor Nathan Wade stepped aside — a compromise ruling that was subsequently appealed. The Georgia Court of Appeals overruled this decision in December 2024, disqualifying Willis entirely. McAfee's handling of the case has been characterized by procedural caution — managing a high-profile case with multiple defendants while avoiding the appearance of partisan bias.

## Significance for Forecasting

McAfee's role demonstrates the unique constraints on state trial judges in politically charged cases: they must balance procedural fairness, public confidence, and the risk of reversal on appeal, all while operating without the institutional insulation that federal judges have (Georgia judges are elected). His cautious approach provides a baseline for how state judges handle high-profile political prosecutions.
