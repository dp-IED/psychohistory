## Summary

Governor of Michigan since 2019. Previously served in the Michigan House of Representatives (2001-2006) and Michigan Senate (2006-2015), including as Senate Minority Leader. Gained national prominence for her leadership during the COVID-19 pandemic and for her role in Michigan's response to a 2020 kidnapping plot against her. Co-chair of the Biden-Harris 2024 campaign. Frequently mentioned as a future presidential or vice-presidential candidate.

## Significance for Forecasting

### Swing-State Executive
Whitmer's position as governor of Michigan — a critical swing state — makes her a perennial candidate for VP consideration and a potential future presidential candidate. Michigan's 15 electoral votes and its return to the Democratic column in 2020 and 2024 (despite Trump winning nationally in 2024) underscore her value as a coalition-building figure.

### Post-2024 Positioning
After the Harris-Walz loss, Whitmer is positioned as a leading contender for the 2028 Democratic nomination. She represents the party's Midwest-rooted moderate wing with progressive governance credentials.

## Key Dates
- **August 23, 1971**: Born in Lansing, Michigan
- **2001-2006**: Michigan House of Representatives
- **2006-2015**: Michigan Senate (Senate Minority Leader 2011-2015)
- **2018**: Elected Governor of Michigan (defeated Republican Bill Schuette)
- **2020**: Gained national profile during COVID-19 pandemic; FBI disrupted militia plot to kidnap her
- **2022**: Re-elected Governor (defeated Republican Tudor Dixon by 10.5 points)
- **2024**: Co-chair of Biden-Harris campaign; widely discussed as potential Harris VP but not selected
