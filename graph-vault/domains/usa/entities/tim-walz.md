# Tim Walz

Governor of Minnesota (2019-present) and Democratic nominee for Vice President of the United States (2024). Selected by Kamala Harris as her running mate on August 6, 2024, after a veepstakes process that narrowed from a finalist pool of Josh Shapiro, Mark Kelly, Andy Beshear, Roy Cooper, and Walz himself.

## Political Career

- **U.S. House of Representatives** (2007-2019): Represented Minnesota's 1st congressional district. Served on Agriculture, Veterans' Affairs, and Transportation committees. Known for bipartisan work on veterans issues and farm policy.
- **Governor of Minnesota** (2019-2025): Won election in 2018, re-elected in 2022. Governed through COVID-19 pandemic, George Floyd protests and aftermath, and a period of split or Democratic trifecta control. Signed notable legislation on paid family leave, free school meals, abortion rights codification, and marijuana legalization.
- **2024 Vice Presidential Nominee**: Chosen by Kamala Harris as the Democratic VP nominee. The selection was notable for its reinforcement model rationale — Walz amplified Harris's Midwestern authenticity and plain-spoken working-class appeal rather than expanding her coalition geographically or demographically.

## 2024 Veepstakes Significance

Walz is the canonical case of a candidate who:
1. Was NOT the media frontrunner (Shapiro and Kelly were)
2. Emerged late in the process (2-3 days before the pick)
3. Fit the reinforcement model (message amplification over coalition expansion)
4. Had a clean vetting file (no reported vetting problems)
5. Was on the comprehensive exclusion list for the "another man" Polymarket question — making the [[concepts/comprehensive-exclusion-list-forecast]] framework the decisive forecasting tool: a dark horse was structurally improbable because the exclusion list captured every plausible candidate
6. Is the actual pick who resolves the "another man" question to NO: he WAS on the exclusion list, so "another" (unlisted) man did not become VP

His selection provides the strongest evidence that veepstakes forecasting should discount media speculation and focus on message alignment, vetting clearance, and the reinforcement/balancing framework.
