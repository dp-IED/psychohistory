# Department of Homeland Security (DHS)

The US Department of Homeland Security is the cabinet department responsible for public security, including counter-terrorism, border security, immigration, and cybersecurity. DHS oversees the Transportation Security Administration (TSA), Customs and Border Protection (CBP), and the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA). The Secretary of Homeland Security is typically the lead cabinet official for domestic security incidents.

## Relevance to Forecasting

- **Counter-drone authority**: DHS has limited counter-drone detection and mitigation authority under the FAA Reauthorization Act. Secretary Mayorkas repeatedly called for expanded authorities during the 2024 drone wave.
- **Public confirmation role**: The DHS Secretary is a key source for official government statements on domestic security incidents.
- **TSA investigations**: TSA (a DHS component) played a key role in debunking specific 2024 drone incidents, analyzing footage that showed misidentified commercial aircraft.

## Key Figures

- **Alejandro Mayorkas** (Secretary 2021-2025): Confirmed some drone sightings but attributed most to misidentified manned aircraft; called for expanded counter-drone powers.
- **Secretary (Trump admin 2025-)**: Posture on counter-drone authorities to be determined.
