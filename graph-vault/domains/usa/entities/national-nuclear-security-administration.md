# National Nuclear Security Administration (NNSA)

The NNSA is a semi-autonomous US Department of Energy agency responsible for maintaining the US nuclear weapons stockpile, nuclear nonproliferation, and nuclear emergency response. Its Office of Emergency Response includes nuclear incident response teams. The NNSA's public statements are directly relevant to forecasting questions involving lost nuclear material or radiological search operations.

## Relevance to Forecasting

- **Radiological search denial**: During the 2024 Northeast drone wave, the NNSA publicly stated that its emergency response unit does NOT use drones for radiological detection and was NOT flying drones in the region. This statement was a critical data point for the "nuclear weapons search" question — it directly denied the theory at the agency level.
- **Material tracking**: The NNSA tracks nuclear material under its jurisdiction. The ability to find lost material is operationally relevant.
- **Public confirmation authority**: An NNSA statement confirming or denying a radiological search is a key resolution source for questions about lost nuclear material.
