# Robert F. Kennedy Jr.

## Summary

Environmental lawyer, vaccine skeptic, and member of the Kennedy family. Ran as an independent candidate in the 2024 US presidential election, initially polling as high as 10-15% in early 2024 before declining. Withdrew from the race in August 2024 and endorsed Donald Trump.

## 2024 Presidential Campaign

- **Announced**: April 2023, initially running as a Democratic primary challenger to Biden
- **Switch to independent**: October 2023, left the Democratic primary to run as an independent
- **Peak polling**: ~15% in November 2023 (Quinnipiac); declined to ~8-10% by mid-2024
- **Ballot access**: Secured ballot access in ~20 states before withdrawal; was on track for 30+
- **Withdrawal**: August 23, 2024 — suspended campaign, endorsed Trump
- **Pre-withdrawal polling in NM**: ~5-7% (below winning threshold by wide margin)

## Forecasting Significance

RFK Jr. was the most prominent non-major-party candidate in 2024, creating a case study in how an independent candidate with high name recognition (Kennedy name), substantial funding (~$30M raised), and a broad ballot access effort can still not reach viability in any state. His withdrawal before Election Day demonstrated the structural fragility of third-party campaigns — the organizational and financial demands of maintaining a 50-state campaign were unsustainable even for a well-funded independent.

Key lesson for forecasting: A third-party candidate polling at 10-15% nationally 12 months before the election has a negligible chance of winning any state, even before accounting for late-campaign collapse or withdrawal. The polling ceiling effect (decline as election approaches) is stronger than any individual candidate's appeal.
