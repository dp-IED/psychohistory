# Chase Oliver

## Summary

Libertarian Party activist and 2024 presidential nominee. A relatively unknown figure outside libertarian circles, Oliver's campaign represented the Libertarian Party's fourth consecutive presidential bid since 1992.

## 2024 Presidential Campaign

- **Announced**: May 2024 (nominated at LP convention)
- **Party**: Libertarian Party
- **Peak polling**: <1% nationally
- **Ballot access**: Secured in ~35-40 states (Libertarian Party has established ballot access from previous cycles)
- **Platform**: Minimal government, non-interventionist foreign policy, drug decriminalization, free markets
- **Pre-withdrawal polling in NM**: <1%
- **Withdrawal status**: Did not withdraw; remained on ballot through November

## Forecasting Significance

The Libertarian Party has been the most consistently visible third party in US presidential elections since 1992, appearing on ballots in most states and polling 1-4% nationally. Despite 30+ years of continuous electoral participation and high ballot access rates, no Libertarian candidate has won a single state. The party's small-government ideology appeals across party lines but lacks the concentrated base needed to overcome the two-party floor in any state.

Key lesson: Ballot access consistency is not a predictor of state-level breakthrough. A third party can achieve near-universal ballot status and still maintain a structural ceiling below 5%.
