# Tim Ryan

Former U.S. Representative from Ohio (2003-2023) and Democratic candidate for U.S. Senate in Ohio (2022). Was briefly mentioned in 2024 Democratic VP speculation, though he was not a serious contender.

## Political Career

- **U.S. House of Representatives** (2003-2023): Represented Ohio's 13th district (Youngstown/Akron area). Served on Appropriations Committee. Ran for House Minority Leader in 2016 (lost to Nancy Pelosi).
- **2022 Senate campaign**: Lost to Republican J.D. Vance by 6 points (47%-53%) in a race that saw external spending from both parties. The candidacy highlighted the challenges of running as a moderate Democrat in a state trending hard Republican.
- **Presidential campaign**: Briefly ran for the 2020 Democratic presidential nomination, positioning as a Rust Belt moderate.

## 2024 Veepstakes Significance

Ryan represents the "former House member who lost a statewide race" category — his VP speculation was minimal and reflected recognition of Ohio's electoral importance rather than his personal viability. His 2022 loss to Vance structurally weakened his case for a national ticket (he couldn't win Ohio at the top of the ticket, and no longer held any office). His inclusion on the "another man" exclusion list is notable primarily for demonstrating how the list captured marginal figures.
