# US Department of Commerce

## Summary

The US Department of Commerce is the cabinet-level department responsible for promoting economic growth, collecting economic data, and enforcing international trade law. It houses the Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA), the Census Bureau, the International Trade Administration (ITA), the Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS — export controls), and the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA).

For forecasting purposes, the Department of Commerce is most relevant as:
1. **The parent of the BEA** — which produces GDP and economic data used in prediction market resolutions
2. **The home of BIS** — which administers export controls on advanced technology (semiconductors, AI hardware) to China and other adversaries
3. **The enforcer of trade remedy laws** — anti-dumping and countervailing duty investigations

## Significance for Forecasting

The Department of Commerce's trade and economic data releases are important inputs for GDP, trade, and tariff-related forecast questions. The Census Bureau (within Commerce) produces the trade balance data (imports/exports) that feeds into GDP calculations. The BIS produces the export control regulations that affect the technology sector.
