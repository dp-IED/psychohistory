## Summary

50th Vice President of the United States, taking office on January 20, 2025 under President Donald Trump. Previously served as a US Senator from Ohio (2023-2025). Author of the bestselling memoir *Hillbilly Elegy* (2016), which became a cultural touchstone for understanding the white working-class Trump voter.

Vance was selected as Trump's running mate on July 15, 2024, at the Republican National Convention. His selection — a first-term senator with a relatively short political career — represented a strategic bet on ideological loyalty, youth (age 39 at selection), and combative media skills. Vance shifted from a Never Trump position in 2016 to one of Trump's most vocal defenders in the Senate, embodying the transformation of the post-2016 Republican Party.

## Significance for Forecasting

### VP Selection as Ideological Reinforcement Model
Vance's selection represents the strongest modern example of a VP pick chosen primarily for ideological reinforcement rather than geographic or demographic balancing. Trump passed over more conventional picks with swing-state appeal (Doug Burgum, Marco Rubio) in favor of a candidate who amplified his message rather than extending his coalition. This pattern — VP as "mini-me" rather than "balancer" — is significant for forecasting future VP selections in populist/insurgent campaigns.

### Senator-to-VP Two-Year Path
Vance's trajectory from first-term senator (sworn in January 2023) to VP nominee (July 2024) — a span of only 18 months — establishes a benchmark for how quickly political ascent can happen in the modern era when aligned with a dominant party leader.
