# Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI)

The FBI is the primary federal law enforcement and domestic intelligence agency of the United States, operating under the Department of Justice. In the context of domestic security incidents, the FBI typically leads the interagency investigation into anomalous events, coordinating with DHS, DoD, FAA, and state/local law enforcement.

## Relevance to Forecasting

- **Lead investigator for mass sociogenic events** (mystery drone waves, UFO flaps): The FBI established the tip line and led the investigation into the 2024 Northeast drone wave, reviewing 5,000+ reports and concluding nothing anomalous was found.
- **Joint statements with DHS/DoD/FAA**: The FBI is typically one of the agencies signing joint statements resolving investigative findings.
- **Counter-drone authority**: The FBI has some counter-drone authorities under existing law, but lacks jurisdiction beyond specific circumstances.
- **Public confirmation authority**: An FBI Director or official statement would be a key resolution source for questions about domestic security incidents.

## Key Events

- 2024-Dec-03: FBI initiates investigation into NJ drone sightings, requesting public reports
- 2024-Dec-10: FBI reports 3,000+ tips received via tip line
- 2024-Dec-16: FBI joins DHS/DoD/FAA joint statement concluding nothing anomalous
