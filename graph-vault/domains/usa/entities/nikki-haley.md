# Nikki Haley

## Summary

Nimrata Nikki Haley is an American politician and diplomat who served as the 116th Governor of South Carolina (2011-2017) and US Ambassador to the United Nations under President Trump (2017-2018). She was the last major challenger to Trump in the 2024 Republican presidential primaries.

Haley entered the 2024 race in February 2023, positioning as the traditional-conservative/foreign-policy-hawk alternative to Trump and DeSantis. She outperformed expectations in the primaries, winning Vermont (Super Tuesday) and coming within 11 points of Trump in New Hampshire — the strongest non-Trump showing in any primary state. She suspended her campaign on March 6, 2024, after Super Tuesday, and eventually endorsed Trump.
