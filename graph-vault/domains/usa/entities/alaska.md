# Alaska

## Overview

Alaska is the 49th US state, admitted in 1959. Population ~733,000 (2023), the third-least populous state. Governed by a Republican trifecta (governor + both legislative chambers). Uses a nonpartisan top-four primary and ranked-choice general election system since 2020 (Ballot Measure 2).

## Political Profile

- **Governor**: Mike Dunleavy (R), term-limited in 2026
- **Senators**: Lisa Murkowski (R, moderate), Dan Sullivan (R) — Class 2 seat up in 2026
- **US House**: Nick Begich III (R, at-large district)
- **Presidential voting**: Trump +10.1% in 2024, Trump +10.0% in 2020
- **Party registration**: Republican ~26%, Unaffiliated ~58%, Democratic ~13%

## 2026 Elections

- **Governor**: Open seat (Dunleavy term-limited). Expected to be competitive under ranked-choice voting
- **US Senate**: Dan Sullivan (R) up for re-election — solid Republican seat
- Key dynamic: ranked-choice voting has allowed moderate candidates to win (Murkowski 2022, Peltola 2022 special)
