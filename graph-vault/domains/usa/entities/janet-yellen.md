# Janet Yellen

## Role in Debt Ceiling Episodes

As Treasury Secretary during the 2023-2025 period, Yellen:

- **Initiated extraordinary measures**: Sent the first letter to Congress when the debt ceiling was reached in January 2023, and when it reinstated in January 2025.
- **Negotiated the Fiscal Responsibility Act**: Played a key role in the technical design of the FRA's debt ceiling suspension mechanism and the extraordinary-measures runway that enabled the 6-month negotiation window.
- **Communicated X-date estimates**: Issued multiple letters updating Congress on the projected exhaustion date, providing the operational timeline that framed the 2023 negotiation.
- **Managed the transition**: Oversaw the debt ceiling reinstatement on January 2, 2025, during the lame duck period, ensuring Treasury had sufficient runway for the incoming administration.

## Forecasting Significance

Yellen's public communications are a key forecasting data point. She:
- Has been more precise and frequent with X-date updates than some predecessors
- Signaled early when extraordinary measures would be exhausted, reducing market panic
- Coordinated closely with the Fed (her former institution) on debt ceiling contingency planning

## Background

- PhD in Economics from Yale
- Chair of the Federal Reserve (2014-2018) — first woman in that role
- Secretary of the Treasury (2021-2025)
- Known for dovish monetary policy stance and careful communication style
- Widely respected institutionally, giving her X-date estimates high credibility
