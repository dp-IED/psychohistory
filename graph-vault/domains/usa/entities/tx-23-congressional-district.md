# TX-23 Congressional District

Texas's 23rd congressional district. Sprawling west Texas district stretching from San Antonio suburbs westward to El Paso, covering ~800 miles of US-Mexico border. Largest geographic congressional district in Texas by area.

## Demographics
- Population: ~780,000
- Hispanic: ~67% (majority Hispanic district)
- Urban/rural: Mix of San Antonio suburban/exurban and vast rural border counties
- Key counties: Bexar (partial — San Antonio suburbs), El Paso (partial), plus 27 rural border counties

## 2026 Midterm Context
- Gonzales is a strong incumbent with growing margins (+4% → +17% → +14%)
- District's Republican trend reflects Hispanic realignment toward GOP in South Texas
- Democratic path to victory requires: (a) D+5-7 national environment, (b) top-tier Hispanic Democratic challenger, (c) significant investment from DCCC
- Even in a Democratic wave year (2018), Hurd won by 0.4% — this district has Republican resilience
- Border security is the dominant issue; Democrats struggle to message on border enforcement in this district
