# Meta Platforms

Meta Platforms, Inc. (formerly Facebook, Inc.) is a US multinational technology conglomerate founded by Mark Zuckerberg in 2004. It owns Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger, and the Quest VR platform, among other assets.

## Antitrust Exposure

### FTC v. Meta (Case 1:20-cv-03590-JEB)
- **Filed**: December 9, 2020
- **Court**: US District Court for the District of Columbia (Judge James Boasberg)
- **Claims**: Meta maintained monopoly in "personal social networking" (PSN) through:
  1. Anticompetitive acquisitions: Instagram (2012) and WhatsApp (2014)
  2. Anticompetitive conduct: Restricting third-party developer access (platform policies)
  3. "Buy or bury" strategy: Acquiring potential competitors before they could become threats
- **Remedy sought**: Divestiture of Instagram and WhatsApp; prohibition on future anticompetitive acquisitions; behavioral remedies

### Meta/Within Challenge (2022-2023)
- FTC challenged Meta's acquisition of Within Unlimited (VR fitness app Beat Saber)
- FTC won preliminary injunction in federal court
- Meta abandoned the acquisition
- Notable: This was a pre-consummation challenge that succeeded where the Instagram/WhatsApp post-consummation challenge faces higher hurdles

### EU Digital Markets Act (DMA) Designation
- Meta designated as a "gatekeeper" platform under EU DMA (2023)
- Subject to interoperability, data access, and anti-self-preferencing obligations
- EU enforcement is separate from US antitrust and faces lower procedural barriers

## Legal Strategy
Meta has defended against FTC v. Meta with arguments including:
- Market definition too narrow (social media is broader than "personal social networking")
- No anticompetitive harm shown (Instagram and WhatsApp have thrived under Meta ownership)
- Acquisitions were reviewed and cleared at the time
- Competition from TikTok, YouTube, Snapchat, and others contradicts monopoly claims
- Divestiture is infeasible (Instagram/WhatsApp deeply integrated into Meta's infrastructure)

## Forecasting Relevance
As the defendant, Meta's legal posture directly affects divestiture probability. Key factors: (1) Meta has signaled it will litigate to the fullest extent, expecting appeals to last years; (2) change in administration reduces remedy pressure; (3) technical integration of Instagram and WhatsApp makes divestiture operationally complex even if ordered.
