# Ron DeSantis

## Summary

Ronald Dion DeSantis is an American politician serving as the 46th Governor of Florida since 2019. He was the leading Republican presidential primary challenger to Donald Trump in the 2024 election cycle. DeSantis entered the race in May 2023 as the most formidable pre-primary alternative to Trump — combining Trump-style cultural conservatism with traditional Republican governance credentials — but never closed the polling gap. He suspended his campaign on January 21, 2024, after a distant second-place finish in the Iowa caucuses, and endorsed Trump.

DeSantis's 2024 campaign is a case study in the difficulty of challenging a dominant party figure. Despite strong positioning (two-term governor of a swing-state-turned-red state, high name recognition from COVID-era culture-war battles, $130M+ in fundraising), DeSantis was unable to consolidate anti-Trump voters because (a) Trump's legal jeopardy created existential loyalty among the base, (b) DeSantis's awkward retail style couldn't compete with Trump's media dominance, and (c) the primary calendar (Iowa, New Hampshire, then Trump-friendly states) disadvantaged anyone who couldn't beat Trump early.
