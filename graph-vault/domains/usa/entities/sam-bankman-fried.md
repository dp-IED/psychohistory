# Sam Bankman-Fried

## Summary

Sam Bankman-Fried (SBF) was the founder and CEO of FTX, a cryptocurrency exchange that grew to be one of the largest in the world, reaching a valuation of $18 billion. By November 2022, FTX collapsed in a matter of days after revelations that customer funds had been misappropriated and loaned to Alameda Research, SBF's affiliated trading firm. The collapse led to the bankruptcy of FTX and over 100 affiliated entities, with up to $8 billion in customer funds missing. As of December 31, 2022, SBF had resigned as CEO, and federal prosecutors were investigating the collapse. He was widely seen as having committed one of the largest financial frauds in history.

## Significance

SBF's rise and fall is the defining cautionary tale of the crypto industry's 2020-2022 boom-bust cycle. FTX's collapse -- after SBF had cultivated an image as a responsible industry figure who testified before Congress and donated to political campaigns -- destroyed trust in centralized crypto exchanges and triggered cascading bankruptcies across the industry (BlockFi, Genesis, and others). The case intensified regulatory scrutiny of cryptocurrency markets globally.
