# Amy Klobuchar

U.S. Senator from Minnesota (2007-present) and former presidential candidate (2020). Known for her moderate-to-liberal record, emphasis on bipartisanship, and Upper Midwest electoral appeal.

## 2024 Election Relevance

- Was on the "another woman" exclusion list for the 2024 Democratic VP nominee question
- Was considered a potential VP contender in 2020 (finalist before Biden selected Harris)
- Represented the Midwest regional balancing option for VP selection
