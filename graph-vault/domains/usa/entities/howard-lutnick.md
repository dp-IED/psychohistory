## Summary

Howard Lutnick is an American billionaire businessman who served as United States Secretary of Commerce in President Donald Trump's second term. He was previously CEO of Cantor Fitzgerald, which lost 658 employees in the September 11, 2001 attacks (including his brother Gary Lutnick). Lutnick rebuilt the firm and became a prominent Trump ally and donor. As Commerce Secretary, Lutnick oversaw the Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS), which administers Section 232 tariff investigations and export controls. His role was central to executing Trump's tariff policies, including the Section 232 steel/aluminum tariff expansion (Feb 2025) and sector-specific tariff investigations.

## Forecasting Significance

Lutnick's background as a Wall Street CEO (not a traditional trade hawk) creates an interesting dynamic: he has business-world credibility but must implement protectionist trade policy. His role in administering Section 232 investigations means any new sector-specific tariff (autos, semiconductors, pharmaceuticals) would flow through Commerce.

## Timeline

- **2025**: Confirmed as Commerce Secretary in Trump's second term
- **Feb 2025**: Section 232 steel/aluminum tariff expansion announced under Lutnick's Commerce Department
- **2025-2026**: Continued administration of tariff policies, industry exemption requests
