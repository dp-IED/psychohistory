# J.B. Pritzker

Governor of Illinois (2019-present), billionaire heir to the Hyatt hotel fortune, and prominent Democratic donor and fundraiser. Was mentioned as a potential VP finalist in the 2024 Democratic selection process, though he was never a leading contender.

## Political Career

- **Governor of Illinois** (2019-present): Won in 2018 by 15 points; re-elected in 2022 by 12 points. Governed through COVID-19, progressive legislative agenda (minimum wage $15, legalized marijuana, reproductive rights protections), and managed Illinois's fiscal recovery.
- **Democratic donor network**: Member of the Pritzker family (net worth ~$3.6B); has been a major Democratic donor and bundler. His personal wealth was considered both an asset (self-funding potential) and a liability (vulnerable to "buying the election" attacks in a national race).

## 2024 Veepstakes Significance

Pritzker represents the "potential self-funder" archetype in VP selection. His wealth and Midwestern profile generated VP speculation, but several factors limited his viability: Illinois is safely Democratic (no swing-state value), his billionaire status was a general-election liability for a campaign emphasizing working-class appeal, and his progressive record in Illinois could create cross-pressure in a national campaign.
