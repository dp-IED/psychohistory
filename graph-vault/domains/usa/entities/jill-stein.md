# Jill Stein

## Summary

Physician and environmental activist, two-time presidential nominee of the Green Party of the United States (2012, 2016, 2024). Known for running left-wing campaigns on environmental, anti-war, and anti-corporate platforms. Her 2016 campaign was the subject of post-election analysis about potential spoiler effects in swing states.

## 2024 Presidential Campaign

- **Announced**: November 2023
- **Party**: Green Party
- **Peak polling**: ~1-2% nationally
- **Ballot access**: Secured ballot access in ~30+ states (Green Party had automatic ballot status in some states)
- **Platform**: Climate emergency, Green New Deal, Medicare for All, ceasefire in Gaza, free tuition
- **New Mexico status**: On ballot; Green Party has established infrastructure in NM from previous cycles
- **Pre-withdrawal polling in NM**: ~1-2% (negligible)

## Forecasting Significance

Jill Stein represents the "entrenched minor-party" category — a candidate with automatic ballot access in many states due to previous electoral performance but with a hard ceiling at 1-3% of the vote. The Green Party has contested every presidential election since 1996 and has never exceeded 3% nationally. Their organizational footprint is limited to specific states (CA, OR, MA, ME, MI, WI) and does not provide a path to winning any state.

Key lesson: An entrenched third party in the US has a demonstrated structural ceiling (1-3%) that is even lower than the general third-party ceiling. The Green Party's 1996-2024 trajectory shows stable non-viability, not growth.
