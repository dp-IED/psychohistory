# Tammy Duckworth

U.S. Senator from Illinois (2017-present), former U.S. Representative (2013-2017), and Army helicopter pilot who lost both legs in the Iraq War. A Purple Heart recipient and prominent veterans advocate within the Democratic Party.

## 2024 Election Relevance

- Was on the "another woman" exclusion list for the 2024 Democratic VP nominee question
- Her military service and Asian-American identity were seen as potential VP selection assets
- Represents the veterans/military credential option for ticket balancing
