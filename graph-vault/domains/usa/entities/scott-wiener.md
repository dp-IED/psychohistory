# Scott Wiener

## Summary

Scott Wiener is a California State Senator representing District 11 (San Francisco). First elected to the State Senate in 2016, previously served on the San Francisco Board of Supervisors (2011-2016). Wiener is a progressive Democrat who has authored some of California's most significant (and controversial) technology regulation bills.

## Significance for Forecasting

Wiener is the most prominent author of AI safety legislation at the state level, making him a key entity for forecasting state-level AI regulation:

1. **SB 1047 author**: Wiener authored SB 1047 (Safe and Secure Innovation for Frontier Artificial Intelligence Models Act), the first major AI safety bill to pass a state legislative chamber. Understanding Wiener's approach, coalition-building style, and relationship with industry is essential for forecasting future iterations of AI safety legislation.

2. **Authorial pattern**: Wiener's legislative approach combines broad liability frameworks with specific safety testing requirements. His bills tend to be first-of-their-kind and face heavy industry opposition. This pattern — progressive legislator authors groundbreaking tech regulation — is itself a forecasting input.

3. **Legislative persistence**: Wiener has a history of reintroducing modified versions of failed bills, learning from previous defeats. The trajectory of his AI legislation (SB 1047 in 2024, revised versions in 2025+) tracks the broader evolution of state-level AI regulation.

4. **Coalition building**: Wiener's support coalition for SB 1047 included AI safety researchers (Yoshua Bengio, Geoffrey Hinton, Stuart Russell) and existential risk advocacy groups. His opposition included major tech companies and some moderate Democrats. The composition of these coalitions is a leading indicator of a bill's trajectory.

## Timeline

- **2020**: Author of SB 145 (sex offender registration reform) — notable for his willingness to take on controversial issues.
- **2023**: Announces intention to introduce AI safety legislation.
- **2024-Q1**: Introduces SB 1047. Bill passes California Senate 32-1.
- **2024-Q2 to Q3**: Leads amendment process in Assembly, modifies bill to address industry concerns. Bill ultimately fails to pass Assembly before Aug 31 deadline.
- **2025-Q1**: Introduces revised AI safety legislation incorporating lessons from SB 1047's failure.
