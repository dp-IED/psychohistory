# Stephen Cloobeck

## Summary

Stephen Cloobeck is an American businessman, philanthropist, and former reality TV personality. He founded Diamond Resorts International, a timeshare company that grew to become one of the largest in the industry before being acquired by Apollo Global Management. Cloobeck is a Democratic donor and has filed as a candidate for the 2026 California gubernatorial election.
