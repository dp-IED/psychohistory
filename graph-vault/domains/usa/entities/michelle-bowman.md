# Michelle Bowman

## Summary

Member of the Federal Reserve Board of Governors since 2018. A former state bank commissioner of Kansas, Bowman was appointed to the Fed Board by President Donald Trump. She serves as the Fed's point person on community banking issues. Bowman emerged as the most hawkish FOMC voter during the 2024-2025 easing cycle, repeatedly dissenting against rate cuts that she considered too large or premature.

## Significance for Forecasting

Bowman's dissenting votes are significant for two reasons: (1) she was the first Fed governor (as distinct from regional bank president) to dissent since 2005, making her votes a stronger signal of internal Board dynamics; (2) her hawkish dissent pattern during the cutting cycle suggests a persistent faction within the FOMC that favors tighter policy, which may constrain the pace of future cuts. Tracking Bowman's voting record provides insight into the hawk-dove balance of the Committee.
