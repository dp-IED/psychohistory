# Katie Porter

## Summary

Katie Porter is a former US Representative for California's 47th congressional district (Orange County) who served from 2019 to 2025. She gained national prominence for her aggressive questioning of corporate executives and government officials in committee hearings using whiteboard visual aids. Porter ran for the US Senate seat vacated by Dianne Feinstein in 2024, losing the primary to Adam Schiff.

## Significance for Forecasting

Porter is a potential Democratic candidate for the 2026 California gubernatorial election. Her progressive policy positions, strong small-donor fundraising base, and high name recognition among Democratic primary voters make her a potentially significant candidate despite low current Polymarket win odds (0.6% YES).

## Political Profile

- First elected to Congress in 2018 as part of the "blue wave" that flipped Orange County
- Law professor before entering politics (UC Irvine School of Law)
- Known for consumer protection advocacy and oversight of pharmaceutical companies, banks, and government agencies
- Progressive policy platform: Medicare for All, Green New Deal, housing affordability, campaign finance reform
- Strong social media presence and small-donor fundraising machine
- 2024 Senate primary loss to Adam Schiff demonstrated limited statewide appeal but strong base support

## California Governor Context

- Progressive lane in a fractured Democratic field for the 2026 CA governor race
- If Porter runs, she would need to consolidate progressive and grassroots support against Becerra's institutional establishment backing
- Her 2024 statewide Senate primary experience provides organizational infrastructure
- Structural disadvantage: California's expensive media markets require either a strong small-donor machine (which she has) or personal wealth (which she does not have, unlike Steyer)
