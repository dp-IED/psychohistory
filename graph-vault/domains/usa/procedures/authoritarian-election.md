# Authoritarian Election/Referendum

Estimate whether an opposition candidate "wins" an election in an authoritarian context — distinguishing winning the vote from assuming office.

## When

Questions about elections in authoritarian or semi-authoritarian states (Venezuela, Russia, Iran, Belarus, etc.).

## Approach

1. Identify resolution criteria: does "win" mean winning the vote or assuming office?
2. Check opposition PVT (parallel vote tabulation) capability
3. Assess regime manipulation capacity: ballot stuffing, vote suppression, result fabrication
4. Apply [[concepts/authoritarian-electoral-facade]]: separate win-vote from take-office

## Calibration

- Opposition has PVT + regime has no plausible denial → opposition likely won regardless of official result
- Regime controls all levels → P(regime candidate "wins") > 0.9
- Win-vote vs take-office: markets more likely resolve on vote outcome than inauguration
