# Divided Opposition Plurality Win

**Redirect**: This concept's canonical home is at [[domains/east-asia/concepts/divided-opposition-plurality-win/_concept]], where the Taiwan 2024 presidential election serves as the primary example. The concept applies broadly to any single-round plurality system where opposition fragmentation produces a low-plurality winner (UK 2005, India 1996, US 1992, Taiwan 2000).

Key rule: When a front-runner polls at 35-45% with the opposition split across 2+ candidates in a single-round plurality system, forecast a win at 85-95% confidence absent last-minute opposition coordination.
