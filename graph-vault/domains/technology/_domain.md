# Technology Domain

## Overview

Covers the intersection of technology innovation, regulation, and forecasting. Focus areas include AI safety regulation, social media platform dynamics (TikTok, algorithmic amplification), national security tech bans, and the structural factors that determine whether proposed tech regulations become law.

## Active Threads

### Federal AI Safety Regulation
The US thread tracking legislative efforts for federal AI safety regulation (see [[domains/usa/threads/us-ai-safety-regulation-federal/_thread]]). The core pattern: sustained legislative effort producing zero enacted comprehensive regulation despite multiple bills and bipartisan working groups.

- [[domains/global/entities/openai]] — Self-regulation preference, opposed to mandatory safety testing
- [[domains/global/entities/anthropic]] — Supports regulation, advocate for mandatory safety frameworks
- [[domains/global/entities/google-deepmind]] — Mixed: research safety advocacy vs. competitive pressure
- [[domains/usa/entities/meta-platforms]] — Open-source advocate, opposes compute-based regulation

- **Generational replacement**: Technology adoption follows generational cohort replacement

- **Technological hubris**: Overconfidence in technology as natural disaster mitigation
