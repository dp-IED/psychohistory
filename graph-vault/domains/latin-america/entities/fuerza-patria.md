# Fuerza Patria

## Overview

Fuerza Patria (Motherland Force) is the Kirchnerist successor faction to the broader Peronist coalition Unión por la Patria (UP), formed after UP's defeat in the 2023 Argentine general election. Led by Buenos Aires Province Governor [[axel-kicillof]], Fuerza Patria represents the left-leaning Kirchnerist wing of Peronism — the faction associated with Cristina Fernández de Kirchner's political tradition.

The coalition's creation marks the fragmentation of the Peronist umbrella into two competing factions: Fuerza Patria (Kirchnerist left) and [[hacemos-por-nuestro-pais|Hacemos por Nuestro País / Primero País]] (conservative Peronist right led by Juan Schiaretti). This split reflects Peronism's recurring pattern of fragmentation after presidential defeats.

## 2025 Legislative Election

In the October 26, 2025 Chamber of Deputies election, Fuerza Patria won 33.70% of the vote and 47 seats — a decline of only 1 seat from the combined Peronist baseline (despite losing the entire HNP/Primero País wing). The result was paradoxical: the Peronist vote fragmented but the Kirchnerist faction held its floor. Voters who left Schiaretti's HNP defected to [[la-libertad-avanza|Milei's LLA]] rather than to Fuerza Patria, indicating that the Córdoba-based conservative Peronist base was ideologically closer to Milei than to Kicillof.

Kicillof's 47 seats placed Fuerza Patria as the second-largest bloc — far behind LLA's 64 but well ahead of any other opposition force. This position makes Kicillof the de facto leader of the Peronist opposition, but also leaves open questions about Peronist reconstitution for the 2027 presidential election.

## Forecasting Significance

Fuerza Patria's trajectory is the central variable for any Argentina forecasting question post-2025:
- **Can it reconstitute the broader Peronist movement** under a single candidate by 2027? The fragmentation-reconstitution cycle is Peronism's canonical pattern — see [[concepts/peronist-fragmentation-reconstitution]].
- **Can Kicillof expand beyond the Kirchnerist floor?** His 33.70% in 2025 was the Peronist base vote. Expanding requires absorbing HNP/PPA voters who defected to Milei — a structurally difficult task given the ideological distance.
- **What is the relationship with the Buenos Aires Province apparatus?** Kicillof remains governor of the country's largest province (37% of the electorate), which provides a strong organizational base for 2027.
