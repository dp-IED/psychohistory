# Corina Yoris

Corina Yoris Villasana is a Venezuelan academic and politician who was the originally designated replacement candidate for [[maria-corina-machado|María Corina Machado]] after Machado was barred from holding public office in 2023. Yoris's candidacy was blocked by the [[cne-venezuela|CNE]] in March 2024, creating a procedural crisis that led to [[edmundo-gonzalez|Edmundo González]] being registered as a placeholder candidate — who ultimately became the unified opposition standard-bearer for the July 2024 presidential election.

## Role in the 2024 Election

Yoris was selected by the [[plataforma-unitaria|PUD]] opposition coalition as Machado's replacement because she was seen as a credible, low-controversy academic figure who could inherit Machado's support base. However, the CNE's online registration system "crashed" when the PUD attempted to register her, and in-person registration was refused — a clear act of procedural obstruction by the regime-controlled electoral commission.

The CNE's blocking of Yoris created a public crisis that forced the opposition to find an alternative. The PUD then registered Edmundo González (a retired diplomat with minimal political profile) to meet the registration deadline, initially as a temporary placeholder. The regime's success in blocking Yoris was a Pyrrhic victory — it forced the opposition to settle on González, who proved as effective a vote-magnet as Yoris would have been, while the procedural obstruction further delegitimized the CNE.

## Forecasting Significance

- The CNE's blocking of Yoris revealed the regime's strategy: use procedural obstruction rather than legal challenges to eliminate opposition candidates. This is a distinct tactic from the administrative disqualification used against Machado.
- The Yoris episode demonstrates the importance of **backup candidate planning** in authoritarian elections. The PUD had multiple potential replacement candidates ready, a level of contingency planning that is a positive signal for opposition organizational capacity.
- The failed Yoris registration was a leading indicator that the CNE would commit outright fraud on election day — once procedural channels are exhausted, the regime must resort to falsification.

- **2024 March**: CNE blocks Yoris registration through procedural obstruction; González registered as alternative.
