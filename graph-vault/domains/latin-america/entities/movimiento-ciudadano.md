# Citizens' Movement (Movimiento Ciudadano, MC)

## Summary
Movimiento Ciudadano (Citizens' Movement, MC) is a Mexican political party founded in 1999 under the name "Convergencia" (Convergence) before rebranding in 2011. It is positioned as a centrist, social-liberal alternative to both the MORENA left-populist coalition and the PAN-PRI-PRD traditional opposition. In the 2024 presidential election, MC nominated [[domains/latin-america/entities/jorge-alvarez-maynez]] as its candidate, who placed third with approximately 10% of the vote.

## Significance for Forecasting
MC's role as a third-party spoiler in Mexican presidential elections is a structurally important dynamic:

- **Vote-splitting mechanism**: MC's refusal to join the PAN-PRI-PRD "Fuerza y Corazón por México" coalition in 2024 ensured a three-way presidential race that split the anti-MORENA vote. In Mexico's single-round plurality system, this fragmentation structurally guaranteed MORENA's victory regardless of the combined opposition vote share.

- **Tactical positioning**: MC has positioned itself as an "anti-establishment, anti-corruption, pro-reform" centrist party — appealing to voters who dislike both MORENA's populism and the PAN-PRI brand toxicity. This wedge positioning has given MC a small but durable 5-15% vote share in federal elections.

- **Future alignment risk**: MC's insulation from the PAN-PRI coalition makes it a potential future pivot partner. If MORENA dominance weakens, MC could become a kingmaker or (less likely) a competitive presidential contender. MC's trajectory is a leading indicator of whether the Mexican party system is returning to competition or fragmenting further.

## Key Data
- 2018 presidential: Did not run a candidate (endorsed Ricardo Anaya, PAN-led coalition)
- 2021 midterms: ~7% of national vote, 27 seats in Chamber of Deputies
- 2024 presidential: Álvarez Máynez with ~10% of vote, 27 seats in Chamber (projected)
- State governors: 2 (Nuevo León, Jalisco) as of 2024
