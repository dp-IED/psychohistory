# Juan Schiaretti

## Summary

Juan Schiaretti is an Argentine politician who served as Governor of Córdoba province (2015-2023) and was a presidential candidate in 2023. A Peronist dissident who broke with the main Peronist coalition, he led the Hacemos por Nuestro País (HNP) coalition in 2023, which rebranded as Primero País for the 2025 legislative election. Schiaretti represented the conservative/developmentalist wing of Peronism, distinct from the left-leaning Kirchnerist faction.

In the 2023 presidential election, Schiaretti won 6.73% of the vote (third place behind Milei and Massa), winning only in Córdoba province where his long tenure as governor provided strong name recognition. In the 2025 legislative election, his Primero País coalition won 7.73% and 8 Chamber seats — down from 24 seats in 2021-2023, reflecting the fragmentation of the broader Peronist vote under Milei's polarization.

## Significance for Forecasting

Schiaretti exemplifies the "regional Peronist dissident" archetype — a figure with strong local power but no capacity to scale to national dominance. In a polarized two-bloc contest (Milei vs Kicillof's Fuerza Patria), centrist/regional alternatives get squeezed to low-single-digit national vote shares. Any forecast about Argentine politics should treat Schiaretti or similar centrist Peronist figures as marginal players in national contests, capable of winning seats regionally but structurally incapable of a plurality.
