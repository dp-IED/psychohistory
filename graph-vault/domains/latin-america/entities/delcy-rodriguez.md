# Delcy Rodríguez

## Summary

Delcy Rodríguez Gómez is a Venezuelan politician serving as Vice President of Venezuela (since 2018) and a senior member of the [[psuv|PSUV]]'s negotiator/pragmatist faction alongside her brother [[jorge-rodriguez|Jorge Rodríguez]]. She previously served as Minister of Foreign Affairs (2014-2017) and President of the Constituent Assembly (2017-2018). As Vice President, she has been the regime's point person for international trade and sanctions-related financial management, particularly with allies in China, Russia, and Turkey.

Rodríguez was notably involved in the [[barbados-agreement-2023|Barbados Agreement]] process, representing the regime's interest in sanctions relief. She has been a target of US and EU sanctions since 2018 due to her role in the Constituent Assembly's usurpation of the National Assembly's powers.

## Significance for Forecasting

- The Rodríguez siblings' influence within the PSUV is a proxy for the pragmatic faction's strength. When both hold prominent negotiation roles, the regime is in sanction-relief-seeking mode. When they are sidelined or publicly attacked by hardliners, the regime is consolidating for repression.
- Delcy's foreign affairs experience makes her a key contact point for international diplomatic engagement with Venezuela. Her presence at international events signals regime openness to negotiation.
- Her sanctions designation means her involvement in any agreement creates legal complications — counterparties (including US officials) face sanctions risk from direct dealings with her. This is a material constraint on any US-Venezuela negotiation track.

## Timeline

- **2014-2017**: Minister of Foreign Affairs under Maduro.
- **2017-2018**: President of the National Constituent Assembly.
- **2018-present**: Vice President of Venezuela (appointed by Maduro).
- **2018 onward**: Designated for US sanctions (OFAC).
- **2023**: Involved in Barbados Agreement negotiations, focusing on sanctions-relief provisions.
- **2025**: Continues as VP; manages relations with China, Russia, Cuba.
