# Jair Bolsonaro

Far-right former President of Brazil (2019-2022).
Lost 2022 re-election to Lula by 1.8% — the narrowest margin in Brazil's history.
Convicted by the Superior Electoral Court and barred from office until 2030.
His party (PL) remains the largest in Congress, and he maintains a loyal base (~25-30% of voters).

## Forecasting Relevance

Bolsonaro is ineligible for 2026 but his endorsement will shape the right-wing primary. His base may transfer to [[tarcisio-de-freitas]] or a like-minded candidate. Key questions:

1. **Endorsement strategy**: Will Bolsonaro endorse Tarcisio or a more hardline candidate? His choice determines right-wing consolidation.
2. **Base transferability**: Can Tarcisio hold Bolsonaro's ~25-30% while expanding to centrists? Bolsonaro's base is deeply personal — may not transfer cleanly.
3. **Legal developments**: Bolsonaro's 27-year sentence for coup-plotting (September 2025 conviction) could be reduced on appeal, potentially affecting his political influence and movement dynamics.
4. **Post-election role**: Barred from 2030 as well — Bolsonaro's long-term relevance depends on family members' political careers (sons Flavio and Eduardo Bolsonaro) and movement durability.
