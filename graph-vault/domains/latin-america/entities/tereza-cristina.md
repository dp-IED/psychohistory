# Tereza Cristina

## Profile

- **Full name:** Tereza Cristina Corrêa da Costa Dias
- **Born:** 1954 (age ~72 in 2026)
- **Party:** Progressistas (PP) — Centrão bloc
- **Current position:** Senator for Mato Grosso do Sul (2023-present)
- **Previous:** Minister of Agriculture, Livestock and Food Supply (2019-2022, Bolsonaro administration)
- **Background:** Agribusiness engineer, large landowner

## Political Career

- Federal Deputy for Mato Grosso do Sul (2015-2023)
- Agriculture Minister under Jair Bolsonaro (2019-2022) — one of the few ministers to serve the full term without controversy
- Elected to Senate in 2022

## 2026 Presidential Election

- Filed as a pre-candidate for the PP party
- Viewed as a placeholder candidate — the Centrão traditionally negotiates support rather than running serious presidential campaigns
- Base is limited to agribusiness sector; no national name recognition
- Polymarket YES: 0.4% ($994K volume) — negligible probability

## Forecasting Relevance

- Likely to withdraw and endorse a right-wing frontrunner (Flávio Bolsonaro or Tarcisio de Freitas) in exchange for PP ministerial positions
- Agribusiness caucus (bancada ruralista) is the most powerful congressional bloc — her candidacy signals PP's desire to be at the negotiation table
- Track whether she withdraws before the August 2026 candidate registration deadline — early withdrawal signals right-wing consolidation
