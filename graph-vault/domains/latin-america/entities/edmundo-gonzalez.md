# Edmundo González

## Summary

Edmundo González Urrutia is a Venezuelan diplomat and politician who served as the presidential candidate for the [[plataforma-unitaria|Unitary Platform (PUD)]] opposition coalition in the 2024 Venezuelan presidential election. A career diplomat and former ambassador (serving as ambassador to Algeria and Argentina under various governments), González was a late replacement candidate after the frontrunner [[maria-corina-machado|María Corina Machado]] was barred from holding office. He was selected by the PUD in April 2024 as a consensus candidate.

González won the July 28, 2024 election by a wide margin, according to opposition-collected vote tallies, parallel vote tabulation, and independent observers. The [[nicolas-maduro|Maduro]]-controlled National Electoral Council (CNE) claimed Maduro won, but provided no tabulated results. The Carter Center, United Nations, and OAS rejected the official results. Despite winning the vote, González never assumed office — an arrest warrant was issued against him in September 2024, and he fled to Spain where he received asylum.

## Significance for Forecasting

- Demonstrates that opposition candidates in authoritarian contexts can win elections despite the state's control of the electoral apparatus, when opposition vote-monitoring infrastructure exists.
- The case of González is a canonical example of the [[concepts/authoritarian-electoral-facade]] concept — where the election result reveals the regime's vulnerability but the regime refuses to concede power.
- His candidacy shows that even a low-name-recognition replacement candidate endorsed by a popular barred opposition figure can defeat an incumbent.
