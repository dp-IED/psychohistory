# Argentina Chamber of Deputies (Cámara de Diputados de la Nación Argentina)

## Overview

The Chamber of Deputies is the lower house of the Argentine National Congress. It has 257 seats, with half (127-130) renewed every two years in staggered elections. The electoral system is proportional representation using the D'Hondt method, with multi-member districts corresponding to the 23 provinces plus the autonomous city of Buenos Aires. Minimum age for deputies: 25.

## Electoral Significance

Argentina's staggered legislative elections mean that each midterm (when only half the seats are contested) serves as a verdict on the incumbent president halfway through their term, while presidential-year elections renew the full chamber. The 2025 midterm (October 26, 2025) determined 127 of the 257 seats.

## 2025 Composition

The 2025 election produced the following distribution:

- [[fuerza-patria]] (Kicillof): 47 seats (-1)
- [[hacemos-por-nuestro-pais]] / Primero País (Schiaretti): 8 seats (-16)

## Forecasting Relevance

The Chamber of Deputies composition determines legislative capacity for any Argentine president. Key forecasting dimensions:
- **Quorum and supermajority thresholds**: 129 = quorum; 172 (2/3) = constitutional amendments, impeachment approval; 129 = simple majority for ordinary legislation.
- **The narrow-majority pattern**: Since 2001, no single party has held an absolute majority (129 seats) in the Chamber without coalition partners. The post-2025 configuration (LLA at 64, needing coalition allies) fits this pattern.
- **The staggered renewal effect**: Each midterm shifts the balance by electing only half the seats, creating multi-cycle realignment patterns. Milei's 2025 gain of 54 seats + a favorable 2027 presidential-year election creates a possible multi-cycle dominance.
