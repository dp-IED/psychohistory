# Jorge Rodríguez

## Summary

Jorge Rodríguez Gómez is a Venezuelan politician and psychiatrist who serves as the President of the National Assembly of Venezuela (since 2021) and was the lead regime negotiator in the [[barbados-agreement-2023|Barbados Agreement]] talks with the opposition. Alongside his sister [[delcy-rodriguez|Delcy Rodríguez]] (Vice President), he represents the **negotiator/pragmatist faction** within the [[psuv|PSUV]] — favoring tactical openings and negotiated agreements with the opposition to relieve international pressure (particularly sanctions), as opposed to the hardliner faction (led by [[diosdado-cabello|Diosdado Cabello]]) that favors outright repression.

Rodríguez's role as chief negotiator means he is the regime's primary interface with international mediators (Norway, US, EU) and the opposition. His political fortunes rise and fall with the negotiation track — when the regime needs sanctions relief, Rodríguez gains influence; when hardliners reassert control after negotiations fail, his influence wanes. This cyclical dynamic is a useful forecasting signal for regime openness to negotiation.

## Significance for Forecasting

- Rodríguez's status as lead negotiator makes him a leading indicator of regime willingness to engage in mediated electoral frameworks. When he is publicly visible leading talks, the regime is in "tactical opening" mode. When he disappears from the negotiation track, hardliners are ascendant and repression intensifies.
- The Rodríguez siblings (Jorge and Delcy) form a powerful family bloc within the PSUV negotiator faction. Tracking their public roles and media profiles provides early warning of factional shifts.
- His medical background (psychiatrist, former Minister of Health) is atypical for a regime security figure and shapes his more pragmatic approach relative to military-aligned hardliners.

## Timeline

- **2012-2014**: Minister of Health under Maduro.
- **2014-2017**: Mayor of Libertador Municipality (Caracas).
- **2017-2020**: Minister of Communication and Information; chief propagandist.
- **2020-present**: Elected to National Assembly; becomes President of the Assembly in 2021.
- **2023-2024**: Leads regime delegation in Barbados Agreement negotiations; co-signs the October 2023 agreement.
- **2025**: Presides over PSUV-controlled National Assembly after opposition boycotts May parliamentary election.
