# Xóchitl Gálvez

## Summary
Bertha Xóchitl Gálvez Ruiz (born 22 February 1963) is a Mexican businesswoman and politician who served as Senator and as the candidate of the "Fuerza y Corazón por México" opposition coalition ([[domains/latin-america/entities/pan]], [[domains/latin-america/entities/pri]], [[domains/latin-america/entities/prd]]) in the 2024 Mexican presidential election. She placed second with approximately 28% of the vote, losing to [[domains/latin-america/entities/claudia-sheinbaum]].

## Significance for Forecasting
Gálvez's candidacy illustrates the structural weakness of the traditional Mexican opposition in the face of a dominant-party realignment. Despite being a compelling candidate (Indigenous background, engineering career, business success, moderate-conservative coalition), she was unable to overcome:

- **The approval-incumbent linkage**: AMLO's 60%+ approval made any opposition race uphill from the start
- **Opposition brand toxicity**: PAN (historically conservative), PRI (historically corrupt/centrist, associated with 2012-2018 government), and PRD (historically left, weakened by MORENA defections) all had negative favorability ratings
- **Vote splitting**: [[domains/latin-america/entities/jorge-alvarez-maynez]] (MC) split the anti-MORENA vote

## Key Data
- Polling: Never above 32% during campaign, consistently 20-30 points behind Sheinbaum
- Coalition: PAN-PRI-PRD "Fuerza y Corazón por México" — ideologically incoherent (PAN is center-right, PRI is centrist/corrupt, PRD is center-left)
- Vote share: ~28% in June 2 election
