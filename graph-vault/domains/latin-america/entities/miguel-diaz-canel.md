# Miguel Díaz-Canel

## Identity
Miguel Mario Díaz-Canel Bermúdez (born April 20, 1960) — Cuban politician, current President of Cuba (since 2018) and First Secretary of the Communist Party of Cuba (PCC) (since 2021). Succeeded Raúl Castro in both roles as the first non-Castro leader since the 1959 revolution.

## Political Position
- **Party**: Communist Party of Cuba (PCC)
- **Current roles**: President of Cuba (2018–present), First Secretary of PCC (2021–present)
- **Ideology**: Communist, Castroism, pragmatic economic reformer
- **Predecessor**: Raúl Castro (handpicked succession after planned transition)

## Structural Position
- **System**: Single-party communist state — PCC monopoly on power, no democratic removal mechanism
- **Power base**: Appointed through managed PCC succession process, not popular election
- **Reform profile**: Has pursued limited economic opening (private sector expansion, digital connectivity) while maintaining one-party control
- **Successor status**: First non-Castro leader since 1959 — but continues Castro-era institutions and policies
