# Abelardo de la Espriella

Colombian lawyer and 2026 presidential candidate. Former ally of Álvaro Uribe who broke with Uribismo and now runs as a populist right-wing independent. Known for combative media presence and anti-establishment rhetoric.

## 2026 Presidential Campaign

### First Round (May 31, 2026)

- **Actual result**: 43.7% — **first place**, decisively outperforming pre-election polling (~18-22%)
- Advanced to June 21 runoff vs Iván Cepeda Castro (40.9%)
- Largest first-round vote share for a right-wing candidate since Álvaro Uribe (62% in 2002 under pre-1991 rules)
- Captured anti-Petro vote across multiple right-wing and centrist blocs, consolidating the fragmented field earlier than expected

- Enters runoff as favorite with 2.8pp first-round lead
- Benefits from historical Colombian pattern: right-wing runoffs consolidate further (2014 Zuluaga +10pp, 2018 Duque +5pp, 2022 Hernández +11pp)
- Endorsement cascade expected from eliminated right-wing candidates (Valencia, Fajardo, Peñalosa)
- Key swing: turnout differential — runoff turnout typically 10-15pp lower, favoring right-wing base
- **Vault probability**: de la Espriella ~0.65-0.70 to win runoff
