# Iván Cepeda Castro

Colombian senator and 2026 presidential candidate for the left-wing Pacto Histórico coalition. Human rights activist and son of communist leader Manuel Cepeda Vargas, assassinated in 1994. Long-time critic of Uribismo and paramilitarism.

## 2026 Presidential Campaign

### First Round (May 31, 2026)

- **Actual result**: 40.9% — **second place** (first round: de la Espriella 43.7%)
- Polymarket priced Cepeda at 82.5% to win first round — the market was profoundly wrong
- Ceiling analysis partially validated: Cepeda's actual 40.9% was below the vault's estimated structural ceiling (46-48%) but well within the first-round range
- Advanced to June 21 runoff despite losing first round

### Post-First-Round (as of June 14, 2026)

- Faces uphill runoff battle starting 2.8pp behind de la Espriella
- Historical pattern suggests right-wing Colombian runoffs gain further (2014 +10pp, 2018 +5pp, 2022 +11pp)
- However: Cepeda's 40.9% in a fragmented field is a stronger first-round showing than Petro's 40.3% in 2022 (who went on to win the runoff)
- **Key swing**: Cepeda needs to consolidate centrist/undecided voters who split among multiple first-round candidates
- **Vault probability**: Cepeda ~0.30-0.35 to win runoff

### Structural Ceiling — Updated (Post-First-Round)

- **Polling ceiling of 46-48% still holds** — Cepeda's 40.9% first-round is well below this ceiling, consistent with the structural analysis
- **Pacto Histórico brand drag validated**: Petro's unpopularity was a real drag, even in a first-round where anti-Petro voters were split
- **Key positive signal**: Cepeda's 40.9% came in a fragmented field where anti-left voters had multiple options. If he can consolidate centrists (Fajardo, Peñalosa voters), his runoff ceiling could be higher than in pre-election head-to-head polling
- **Comparison to Petro 2022**: Petro won runoff with 50.4% after getting 40.3% in first round. Cepeda's 40.9% is numerically similar but faces a stronger right-wing opponent (Hernández was a weaker candidate than de la Espriella)

## Forecasting Significance

Cepeda is the canonical test case for the [[domains/latin-america/concepts/fragmented-right-wing-field/_concept|fragmented right-wing field concept]], the [[domains/global/concepts/plurality-race-reasoning-trap/_concept|plurality-race reasoning trap]], and now also the **polymarket pricing inefficiency** pattern — where market prices for first-round victory reversed profoundly from expectations.

The massive market mispricing (Cepeda 82.5% → actual second place) is a significant calibration signal. It demonstrates that prediction markets can be systematically wrong about multi-round election dynamics when structural polling errors propagate into market prices without correction.
