# Romina Del Plá

## Summary

Romina Del Plá is an Argentine Trotskyist politician and labor organizer for the Workers' Party (Partido Obrero), a component of the FIT-U coalition. She served as a national deputy in the Chamber of Deputies and is known for her work with labor unions, particularly in Buenos Aires province.

## Role in FIT-U

Del Plá represents the Partido Obrero faction within FIT-U and is one of the coalition's most prominent labor-linked figures. Her political base is in provincial labor organizing, making her distinct from the more media-focused figures like Bregman and del Caño. She was part of the FIT-U parliamentary bloc during the 2021-2025 term.

## Forecasting Significance

Del Plá is significant primarily as an indicator of FIT-U's internal faction balance (Partido Obrero vs. PTS) and the coalition's union-based organizing strategy. In any future scenario where FIT-U undergoes a leadership transition or factional split, Del Plá would be relevant as a potential Partido Obrero leader.
