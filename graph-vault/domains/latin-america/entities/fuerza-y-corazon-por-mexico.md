# Fuerza y Corazón por México

## Summary
"Fuerza y Corazón por México" (Strength and Heart for Mexico) was the electoral coalition formed by the [[domains/latin-america/entities/pan]] (National Action Party), [[domains/latin-america/entities/pri]] (Institutional Revolutionary Party), and [[domains/latin-america/entities/prd]] (Party of the Democratic Revolution) to contest the 2024 Mexican presidential election under a single candidate, [[domains/latin-america/entities/xochitl-galvez]]. The coalition was the successor to the "Va por México" coalition that contested the 2021 midterm elections.

## Significance for Forecasting
The coalition illustrates the structural weakness of the traditional Mexican opposition:

- **Ideological incoherence**: The PAN (center-right, conservative, pro-business), PRI (centrist, historically authoritarian, clientelist), and PRD (center-left, social democratic) have fundamentally incompatible policy platforms and voter bases. The coalition was purely negative — built around "stop MORENA" rather than a positive governing vision.

- **Brand toxicity transfer**: Each party brought negative brand associations that cancelled out any opposition message. PAN was associated with the Calderón-era drug war, PRI with the Peña Nieto corruption scandals, and PRD with its collapse and absorption after AMLO's defection. Gálvez's positive personal ratings could not overcome the coalition's combined negative brand equity.

- **Historical precedent**: Previous coalition attempts in Latin America with ideologically diverse partners have similarly failed (e.g., Peru's 2021 anti-Fujimori coalition, Venezuela's 2012 Mesa de la Unidad Democrática after internal fragmentation). The pattern: negative coalitions win elections when the dominant party is weak (approval <40%), but reliably fail against a popular dominant incumbent.

- **Post-election dissolution**: The coalition existed only for the 2024 election cycle and was not maintained as a permanent opposition vehicle. Each party reverted to independent organization after the June 2 defeat.

## Key Data
- Candidate: [[domains/latin-america/entities/xochitl-galvez]] (businesswoman, former Senator, Indigenous-Hñahñu background)
- Vote share: ~28% (vs Sheinbaum ~60%, Álvarez Máynez ~10%)
- Previous iteration ("Va por México", 2021): ~46% for lower house (lost majority to MORENA coalition)
- Coalition members: PAN, PRI, PRD
