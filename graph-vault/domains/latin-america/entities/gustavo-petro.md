# Gustavo Petro

Gustavo Francisco Petro Urrego (born April 19, 1960) — President of Colombia (2022-2026). Colombia's first left-wing president. Former M-19 guerrilla, Bogotá mayor (2012-2015), and Senator.

## Key Policies

- **"Total Peace"**: Pursued negotiations with ELN, FARC dissidents, and other armed groups. Negotiated bilateral ceasefire with ELN (2023-2024). Truce with Clan del Golfo ended in 2024.
- **Drug policy shift**: Moved from forced eradication to voluntary crop substitution. Created tension with US counternarcotics policy.
- **Energy transition**: Halted new oil/gas exploration permits. Pushed renewable energy despite Colombia's oil dependence.
- **Venezuela relations**: Restored diplomatic relations with Maduro government (2022). Colombia-Venezuela border reopened.

## US Relations

- Cool toward the US compared to predecessors (Duque, Santos). Criticized US drug war framework.
- Refused to extradite key narcotraffickers to the US, straining bilateral relations.
- Trump administration has been publicly critical of Petro's policies, particularly drug policy.

## Political Status (May 2026)

- **Lame duck**: Term ends August 2026. Cannot seek re-election (single-term limit).
- **Succession race**: Colombia presidential election May 31 (first round), June 21 (runoff). Iván Cepeda (Pacto Histórico) is Petro's preferred successor but runs independently.
- **Approval**: Mid-to-low 30s. Polarized — strong left base, majority disapproval from center-right.

## Forecasting Relevance

- Colombia's 2026 presidential election is the largest active Polymarket political event ($29.9M).
- Petro's lame-duck status creates an open-seat race with fragmented field.
- US-Colombia relations after June 2026 depend heavily on who succeeds Petro.
