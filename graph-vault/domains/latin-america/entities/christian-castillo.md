# Christian Castillo

## Summary

Christian Castillo is an Argentine Trotskyist politician, sociologist, and intellectual leader of the Workers' Socialist Party (PTS), a component of the FIT-U coalition. He is the PTS's leading intellectual figure and has been a national deputy. Castillo is known for his academic background (sociology professor at the University of Buenos Aires) and his role in shaping FIT-U's ideological and strategic direction.

## Role in FIT-U

Castillo serves as the intellectual and strategic backbone of the PTS faction within FIT-U. Unlike the more publicly visible figures (del Caño, Bregman), Castillo's influence is through internal party strategy, ideological formulation, and coalition coordination. He has been a deputy in the Buenos Aires provincial legislature.

## Forecasting Significance

Castillo is significant primarily as an indicator of FIT-U's strategic direction and internal faction dynamics:
- Any shift in FIT-U's strategic line (e.g., toward coalition with broader left forces) would signal Castillo's influence or decline
- A post-del Caño leadership transition could see Castillo as a PTS candidate for the national leadership role
- His intellectual framing influences whether FIT-U remains doctrinaire Trotskyist or evolves toward a broader left-populist strategy
