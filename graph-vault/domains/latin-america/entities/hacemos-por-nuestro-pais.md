# Hacemos por Nuestro País (HNP) — Historical Reference

> **This entity is a redirect stub.** HNP/Primero País was a Peronist dissident coalition (2023-2025) led by Juan Schiaretti. Its forecasting significance is as a case study in **regional third-way failure in polarized environments**. Full analysis is now in [[argentina-milei-realignment#Key-Dynamics]].

## Key Facts

- Founded 2023 for the general election; Schiaretti won 6.73% nationally (third place)
- Rebranded as Primero País for 2025 legislative election; won 7.73% and 8 Chamber seats
- Dissolved after the 2025 election — a 67% decline from the 24 seats the broader Peronist-aligned coalition had held previously
- Political base was concentrated in Córdoba province; minimal presence outside it
- The coalition's failure illustrates a structurally-limited strategy: regional Peronist alternatives cannot scale up in a polarized Milei-vs-Kicillof contest
