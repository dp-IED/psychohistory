# Party of the Democratic Revolution (PRD)

## Summary
The Party of the Democratic Revolution (PRD) is a center-left political party in Mexico, founded in 1989 by defectors from the PRI including Cuauhtémoc Cárdenas. The PRD was a major force in the 1990s-2000s (won Mexico City mayoralty, nearly won presidency in 2006 with AMLO). After AMLO left to found [[domains/latin-america/entities/morena]] in 2011, the PRD steadily declined. By 2024, it was a minor coalition partner in the PAN-PRI opposition alliance, at risk of losing its registration.

## Significance for Forecasting
The PRD's absorption into the PAN-PRI coalition demonstrates how MORENA's rise destroyed the center-left in Mexican politics. The PRD was AMLO's original party; its collapse after his departure is a case study in how personalist leaders can transfer their support base from one organization to another, hollowing out the original. The PRD's situation by 2024 (polling below the 3% registration threshold) was a leading indicator that the traditional opposition was structurally incapable of competing.
