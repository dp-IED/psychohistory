# Andrés Manuel López Obrador (AMLO)

## Summary
Andrés Manuel López Obrador (AMLO) is a Mexican politician who served as the 65th President of Mexico from December 1, 2018 to September 30, 2024. Founder of the MORENA party, he won the 2018 presidential election with 53% of the vote — the first Mexican president in decades to win an outright majority. His presidency was characterized by a populist left-nationalist agenda, increased social spending, energy nationalism, and the consolidation of MORENA as the dominant party in Mexican politics.

## Significance for Forecasting
AMLO is the central figure in Mexico's dominant-party realignment. His key forecasting-relevant characteristics:

- **Sustained high approval**: Maintained approval ratings of 55-65% throughout most of his presidency, despite mediocre economic growth and rising security challenges. This is unusually high for a single-term president with no re-election available.
- **Successor selection (dedazo)**: Following Mexican tradition (and PRI-era practice), AMLO explicitly anointed [[domains/latin-america/entities/claudia-sheinbaum]] as his preferred successor. In dominant-party systems, the outgoing president's endorsement is the strongest predictor of the next election outcome.
- **Anti-establishment brand while in power**: A structural curiosity — AMLO's political identity was built on anti-establishment populism, yet he governed as the establishment. This complicated the opposition's strategy (attacking him as the establishment without resonance from his base).
- **Constitutional reform push**: Lame-duck period (2024) included pushing judicial reform, electoral reform, and other constitutional changes that may entrench MORENA dominance post-presidency.

## Forensic Note for Forecasting
AMLO's high approval despite mixed governance outcomes resembles the pattern of Hugo Chávez (Venezuela) and Rafael Correa (Ecuador) — personalist leaders whose approval was partially decoupled from objective governance metrics. This pattern is common in Latin American resource-nationalist / welfare-expansion populism.

## Timeline
- 1953: Born in Tepetitán, Tabasco
- 2012: Lost presidential election to Enrique Peña Nieto (PRI)
- 2018: Won presidency with 53%, founded MORENA coalition
- 2023-2024: Lamely endorsed [[domains/latin-america/entities/claudia-sheinbaum]], pushed constitutional reforms
- 2024-09-30: Term ends; Sheinbaum inaugurated October 1
