# Flávio Bolsonaro

## Profile

- **Full name:** Flávio Nantes Bolsonaro
- **Born:** 1981 (age ~45 in 2026)
- **Party:** Partido Liberal (PL)
- **Current position:** Senator for Rio de Janeiro (2019-present)
- **Relationship:** Eldest son of former president Jair Bolsonaro

## Political Career

- State Deputy, Rio de Janeiro (2003-2019)
- Senator (2019-present) — elected with 4.38M votes in the Bolsonaro wave year
- Investigated in "rachadinha" (salary kickback) scandal but case stalled

## 2026 Presidential Election

- Currently the Polymarket favorite on the right at 23.1% YES ($5.8M volume)
- Carries the Bolsonaro brand name but less polarizing than his father
- Jair Bolsonaro is barred from office until 2030 — Flávio is the natural family successor
- Key challenge: consolidating the far-right and center-right into a single candidacy

## Forecasting Relevance

- If Flávio consolidates the right, becomes a credible challenger to Lula
- Watch for endorsements from Centrão parties (PP, Republicanos, União Brasil)
- Tarcisio de Freitas is his main rival for right-wing consolidation
- Father Jair's active campaigning could be an asset or liability depending on polarization dynamics
