# Claudia Sheinbaum

## Summary
Claudia Sheinbaum Pardo (born 24 June 1962) is a Mexican physicist, academic, and politician who served as the first female Head of Government of Mexico City from 2018 to 2023. She won the 2024 Mexican presidential election as the candidate of the MORENA party, receiving approximately 60% of the vote on June 2, 2024. She is the first woman and first person of Jewish heritage to be elected president of Mexico. Her selection as MORENA's candidate was a classic **dedazo** — outgoing President [[domains/latin-america/entities/andres-manuel-lopez-obrador]] anointed her as his preferred successor, effectively eliminating rivals like [[domains/latin-america/entities/marcelo-ebrard]] in the primary process.

## Significance for Forecasting
Sheinbaum represents the [[domains/latin-america/entities/morena]] party's continuity project — the candidate handpicked by President [[domains/latin-america/entities/andres-manuel-lopez-obrador]] (AMLO) to extend the MORENA project beyond his single term. Her candidacy and victory exemplify the [[domains/latin-america/concepts/incumbent-successor-dominant-party/_concept]] dynamic: in dominant-party systems with a popular outgoing incumbent, the chosen successor inherits the incumbent's approval coalition and is structurally favored to win. Sheinbaum maintained polling leads of 20-30 points over the opposition [[domains/latin-america/entities/xochitl-galvez]] throughout the 2023-2024 campaign period.

## Key Data for Forecasting
- **Election system**: Mexico uses a single-round plurality (first-past-the-post) presidential system. A candidate needs only a plurality, not a majority, to win.
- **Incumbent approval transfer**: AMLO's approval rating remained at approximately 60-65% throughout 2023-2024. Sheinbaum inherited this support coalition.
- **Opposition fragmentation**: The opposition coalition (PAN-PRI-PRD) nominated Xóchitl Gálvez, while the Citizens' Movement (MC) nominated Jorge Álvarez Máynez, splitting the anti-MORENA vote.
- **Party dominance metrics**: MORENA and its allies have held a congressional majority since 2018 and controlled 23 of 32 state governorships by 2024.
