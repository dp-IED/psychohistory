# National Electoral Council of Venezuela (CNE)

## Summary

The National Electoral Council (Spanish: Consejo Nacional Electoral, CNE) is the electoral authority of Venezuela, constitutionally responsible for organizing, administering, and certifying all elections in the country. Under the Maduro regime, the CNE has been captured by the executive and staffed with regime loyalists, transforming it from a technical electoral arbiter into a tool for manufacturing electoral outcomes.

The CNE's role in the 2024 presidential election was central to the regime's fraud: it announced a narrow Maduro victory (~51%) on July 29, 2024, without releasing any tally sheets or polling-station-level results. The implausibly round percentages (51.20000%, 44.20000%, 4.60000%) were widely noted as statistically fabricated. CNE rector Juan Carlos Delpino later went into hiding and told the New York Times he saw no evidence of a Maduro victory. The CNE has still not released detailed results as of early 2025.

## Significance for Forecasting

- The CNE is a canonical case of "electoral commission capture" — the first mechanism in the [[concepts/authoritarian-electoral-facade]] pattern. When an electoral commission is staffed with regime loyalists, it can fabricate results, delay or withhold tally sheets, and provide a legal veneer for electoral fraud.
- The CNE's actions in 2024 demonstrate that a captured electoral commission can falsify results at the aggregate level but cannot prevent the opposition from collecting authentic tally sheets from individual polling stations.
- For forecasting authoritarian election questions: the independence or capture of the electoral commission is a material variable. A captured CNE can produce any official result the regime desires, but the opposition may still win and document its victory through parallel tabulation.

## Timeline

- **1997**: CNE established as part of Venezuela's electoral system reforms.
- **~2015**: CNE increasingly captured by the executive branch under Maduro.
- **2017**: CNE administers the controversial Constituent Assembly election, widely boycotted and criticized.
- **2018**: CNE runs the presidential election boycotted by the opposition; Maduro "wins" with 67%.
- **2023 October**: CNE fails to facilitate or observe the opposition primary (run independently by the opposition).
- **2024 March**: CNE blocks registration of Maria Corina Machado's replacement candidate Corina Yoris; Edmundo Gonzalez registered as temporary placeholder.
- **2024 July 28**: Election day; CNE controls voting machines, tally processes, and vote certification.
- **2024 July 29**: CNE president Elvis Amoroso announces Maduro win without tally sheets; claims cyberattack (no evidence).
- **2024 August 2**: CNE releases second bulletin (still favoring Maduro), still no tally sheets.
- **2025 January**: CNE still has not released detailed polling-station-level results.
