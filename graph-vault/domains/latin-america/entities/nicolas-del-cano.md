# Nicolás del Caño

## Summary

Nicolás del Caño is an Argentine politician and the leading figure of the Frente de Izquierda y de los Trabajadores - Unidad (FIT-U). A member of the Workers' Party (Partido Obrero), he has served as a national deputy intermittently since 2017. Del Caño was the FIT-U presidential candidate in 2015 (1.8%) and 2019 (2.8%), and a key figure in maintaining the Trotskyist left's limited but persistent presence in Argentine politics.

In the 2025 legislative election, del Caño led the FIT-U campaign that won 3.90% and 3 seats — maintaining the coalition's historical floor but demonstrating its structural ceiling under Milei-era polarization.
