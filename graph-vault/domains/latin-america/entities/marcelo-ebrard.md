# Marcelo Ebrard

## Summary
Marcelo Luis Ebrard Casaubón (born 10 October 1959) is a Mexican politician who served as Secretary of Foreign Affairs (2018-2023) under President [[domains/latin-america/entities/andres-manuel-lopez-obrador]] (AMLO) and previously as Head of Government of Mexico City (2006-2012). He was a leading contender for the MORENA presidential nomination in 2023 but was effectively sidelined by AMLO's dedazo — the mechanism by which the outgoing president anointed [[domains/latin-america/entities/claudia-sheinbaum]] as his preferred successor, making Ebrard's candidacy unelectable.

## Significance for Forecasting
Ebrard's treatment in the 2023 MORENA primary is a key case study in how **dedazo** operates in dominant-party systems (see [[domains/latin-america/concepts/incumbent-successor-dominant-party/_concept]]):

- **Primary process**: Ebrard ran a competitive campaign against Sheinbaum for the MORENA nomination, emphasizing his track record as Foreign Minister (including Mexico's COVID-era diplomacy and managing US-Mexico relations during Trump).
- **Dedazo operation**: AMLO publicly signaled his preference for Sheinbaum early in the primary process, making Ebrard's campaign appear futile. AMLO used his personal popularity (~60% approval) to anchor party support behind Sheinbaum.
- **Ebrard's dilemma**: He could not attack AMLO (the party founder and popular incumbent) without alienating the MORENA base, but he could not differentiate from Sheinbaum on policy without appearing to break with AMLO's "Fourth Transformation" agenda.
- **Post-primary**: Ebrard left the Foreign Ministry and was effectively excluded from the Sheinbaum administration's inner circle, illustrating how dedazo creates a winner-take-all dynamic — the anointed successor controls the party apparatus after the transition.

## Key Data
- 2006-2012: Head of Government of Mexico City
- 2012: Sought but did not win PRD presidential nomination (AMLO won)
- 2018-2023: Secretary of Foreign Affairs under AMLO
- 2023: Lost MORENA presidential primary to Sheinbaum (39% to 39% — official MORENA primary, but Sheinbaum had AMLO's backing)
- 2024: Effectively excluded from the Sheinbaum transition; political trajectory uncertain
