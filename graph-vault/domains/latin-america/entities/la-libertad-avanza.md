# La Libertad Avanza

## Summary

La Libertad Avanza (LLA, English: "Freedom Advances") is an Argentine political coalition founded by Javier Milei. Initially a minor libertarian/conservative coalition with minimal legislative representation, it won the presidency in 2023 and became the largest bloc in the Chamber of Deputies in the 2025 legislative election.

The coalition's platform combines radical free-market economics (dollarization, central bank abolition, spending cuts) with social conservatism (anti-abortion, anti-gun control) and a pro-US/pro-Israel foreign policy. Its electoral base is young, male, and economically distressed — voters alienated by the Peronist-centrist consensus that governed Argentina for most of the 21st century.

LLA went from 10 Chamber seats (2023 election) to 64 seats (2025 election, 40.66% of the vote), demonstrating a [[populist-coattail-legislative-wave]] effect.

## Significance for Forecasting

LLA's trajectory demonstrates how a populist outsider coalition can transform from a fringe vehicle to the dominant legislative force within 2 years, given presidential power and visible governance wins. Any forecast about Argentine politics must treat LLA as the dominant party, not an anomaly, through at least the 2027 cycle.
