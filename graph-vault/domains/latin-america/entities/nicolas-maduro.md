# Nicolás Maduro

## Summary

Nicolás Maduro Moros is a Venezuelan politician who has served as president of Venezuela since 2013, succeeding Hugo Chávez. A former bus driver and union leader, Maduro rose through Chávez's United Socialist Party of Venezuela (PSUV) to become foreign minister (2006-2012) and vice president (2012-2013) before assuming the presidency upon Chávez's death.

Maduro's rule has been characterized by a severe economic and humanitarian crisis: hyperinflation, mass emigration (over 7 million Venezuelans have fled), oil production collapse, and widespread food and medicine shortages. His regime has consolidated authoritarian control over state institutions, including the judiciary, electoral authority (CNE), and military. He has imprisoned opposition figures, barred leading candidates from office, and used the TSJ (Supreme Court) to validate disputed electoral outcomes.

In the 2024 presidential election, Maduro claimed victory but the opposition produced detailed vote tallies showing [[edmundo-gonzalez|Edmundo González]] won by a wide margin. Independent observers (Carter Center, UN, OAS) rejected CNE's results. Despite this, Maduro remained in power through military loyalty and institutional control.

## Significance for Forecasting

- Maduro represents a canonical case of an authoritarian leader who maintains power not through electoral legitimacy but through institutional control (military, judiciary, electoral authority).
- His refusal to concede despite clear evidence of losing the 2024 vote demonstrates the limits of electoral pressure as a mechanism for democratic transition when the incumbent controls the security apparatus.
- The question of Maduro's tenure and the conditions under which he might leave power is an active forecasting domain.

## Timeline

- **2013 March**: Chávez dies; Maduro assumes presidency after narrow election win.
- **2018 May**: Maduro re-elected in election boycotted by opposition and widely condemned as illegitimate.
- **2024 July 28**: Presidential election; Maduro's CNE claims victory, but opposition tallies show González won.
- **2024 August 22**: Maduro-aligned TSJ "validates" CNE's disputed results.
- **2025 January 10**: Maduro sworn in for a third term despite international non-recognition.
