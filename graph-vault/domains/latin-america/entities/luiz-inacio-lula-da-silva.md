# Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva

Incumbent President of Brazil (2023-2026). Left-wing Workers' Party (PT).
Former union leader, president from 2003-2010, convicted and imprisoned 2018-2019 (conviction later annulled).
Won 2022 election defeating Jair Bolsonaro.
Seeking re-election in 2026 at age 81.

- Approval rating: ~45-50% (varies by pollster, trending slightly downward from 2023 highs)
- Key governance challenges: inflation management, Amazon deforestation policy, congressional coalition maintenance
- Lula's position on BRICS expansion and Global South diplomacy shapes Brazil's international standing
