# Clan del Golfo

Clan del Golfo (Gulf Clan), also known as the Gaitanist Self-Defense Forces of Colombia (AGC) — Colombia's largest and most powerful drug trafficking organization. Controls key cocaine smuggling routes through the Urabá region, into the Caribbean and Pacific.

## Operations

- **Cocaine trafficking**: Primary export route via Caribbean to Central America, Mexico, and the United States. Estimated 30-40% of Colombia's cocaine flow passes through Clan-controlled corridors.
- **Territorial control**: Strongholds in Antioquia, Córdoba, Chocó, and along the Panama border. More than 5,000 armed members.
- **Criminal portfolio**: Gold smuggling, illegal mining, extortion, migrant smuggling, and fuel theft in addition to narcotics.

## Relationship with Colombian State

- **Petro administration**: Initially negotiated a truce (Dec 2023) as part of "Total Peace," but the truce collapsed by mid-2024 when Clan leaders refused to surrender.
- **Post-2026 prospects**: Any incoming Colombian government will face the Clan as the dominant organized crime threat. Clan leaders have explicitly warned they will attack any government that extradites them to the US.

## Forecasting Relevance

- **US strike scenario**: Clan del Golfo is the most likely target for any potential US kinetic strike in Colombia. They have the size, threat profile, and transnational reach to theoretically draw US military attention.
- **FTO designation**: Designation of Colombian cartels as FTOs would apply to Clan del Golfo, potentially creating legal pathways for expanded US operations.
- **American hostages**: Clan has historically avoided targeting US citizens, but any shift in this calculus would be a strike trigger scenario.
