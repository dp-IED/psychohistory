# Venezuela

Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela. Neighbor and regional flashpoint for 6+ prediction market domains (oil, migration, regime change, US foreign policy, Colombia border dynamics, authoritarian resilience).

## Current Status (May 2026)

- **Governance**: Nicolás Maduro remains in power after the disputed July 2024 election. Edmundo González (opposition) won the vote count but was blocked from taking office. Post-election crisis ongoing.
- **Economy**: Hyperinflation tamed (annual CPI ~50-100%), but GDP remains at ~30% of 2012 peak. Oil production at ~850K bpd (vs 3.2M bpd peak). US sanctions reinstated Apr 2024.
- **Security**: Armed groups operating along Colombia-Venezuela border (ELN, FARC dissidents, colectivos). Maduro's military control is consolidated domestically.

## Forecasting Relevance

- **US-Colombia strike scenarios**: Venezuela border dynamics are a key trigger scenario — if Maduro-escalated cross-border incidents threaten Colombia, US strikes on targets near the border become conceivable.
- **Venezuela election markets**: Post-2024 election uncertainty drives markets on regime change, transition scenarios, oil sanctions policy.
- **Migration**: ~8M Venezuelans have left the country since 2015. Migration flows affect US border politics and Colombia's domestic stability.
- **Oil markets**: Venezuela holds the world's largest proven oil reserves. Any regime change or sanctions relief would affect global oil supply.
