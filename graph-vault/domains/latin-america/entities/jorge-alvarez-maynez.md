# Jorge Álvarez Máynez

## Summary
Jorge Álvarez Máynez (born 8 July 1985) is a Mexican politician who served as the presidential candidate for the Citizens' Movement (Movimiento Ciudadano, MC) in the 2024 election. He placed third with approximately 10% of the vote. His candidacy was significant for splitting the anti-MORENA vote.

## Significance for Forecasting
Álvarez Máynez's third-place candidacy illustrates the fragmentation dynamics of single-round plurality elections. MC's refusal to join the PAN-PRI-PRD "Fuerza y Corazón por México" coalition ensured that the anti-MORENA vote was split between a centrist-conservative coalition (Gálvez) and a centrist-left party (MC). In Mexico's single-round plurality system, this fragmentation structurally guaranteed a MORENA win regardless of the opposition's combined vote share.

The MC vote share (~10%) exceeded the margin of defeat for Gálvez (~28% vs Sheinbaum's ~60%), meaning even if MC had joined the opposition coalition, it would not have changed the outcome — Sheinbaum's victory was driven by positive support, not merely by opposition fragmentation.
