# Axel Kicillof

## Summary

Axel Kicillof is an Argentine economist and politician, Governor of Buenos Aires Province since 2019 and the leading figure of Fuerza Patria, the successor to the Peronist coalition. A heterodox economist who served as Economy Minister under President Cristina Fernández de Kirchner (2013-2015), Kicillof emerged after Milei's 2023 victory as the standard-bearer of the reorganizing Peronist movement.

In the 2025 legislative election, Fuerza Patria won 33.70% and 47 seats, maintaining the Peronist base but failing to compete with Milei's LLA surge (40.66%, 64 seats). Kicillof is widely considered a leading candidate for the 2027 presidential election, representing the Peronist successor faction.
