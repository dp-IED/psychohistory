# Myriam Bregman

## Summary

Myriam Bregman is an Argentine lawyer, Trotskyist politician, and deputy in the Chamber of Deputies representing the Workers' Socialist Party (PTS), a component of the FIT-U coalition. She was the FIT-U presidential candidate in the 2023 general election, the coalition's first female presidential candidate, and is one of the most visible national figures of the Argentine far left alongside Nicolás del Caño.

## Role in FIT-U

Bregman represents the PTS faction within FIT-U and has been a parliamentary deputy since 2017. She has a higher national profile than most FIT-U figures due to her media presence and her role as a candidate. In the 2025 legislative election, she was one of the coalition's leading candidates but the coalition won only 3 seats, down from 4 in the previous term.

## Forecasting Significance

Bregman is significant primarily as:
- A potential FIT-U presidential candidate if del Caño steps aside
- A sign of internal faction dynamics within FIT-U (PTS vs. Partido Obrero tensions)
- A benchmark for far-left ceiling visibility — even a relatively media-savvy figure like Bregman cannot break through the structural 3-5% national ceiling
