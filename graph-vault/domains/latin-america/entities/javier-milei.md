# Javier Milei

## Summary

Javier Gerardo Milei is an Argentine economist, author, and politician who has served as President of Argentina since December 2023. A self-described libertarian and anarcho-capitalist, Milei rose to prominence as a television pundit known for his confrontational style, disheveled appearance, and radical economic proposals including dollarization, abolishing the Central Bank, and drastic spending cuts. He founded the La Libertad Avanza coalition.

Milei won the presidency in November 2023 with 55.7% of the vote, defeating Peronist Economy Minister Sergio Massa. His victory was widely described as a political earthquake — the most dramatic ideological shift in Argentine history. He inherited an economy with over 200% annual inflation, a 50% poverty rate, and depleted foreign reserves.

## Significance for Forecasting

Milei represents a pure case of the anti-system populist who wins on a radical economic platform and then implements it — demonstrating that voters who elect a radical reformer may tolerate severe short-term pain (austerity, devaluation) if promised long-term structural change. His success or failure will shape expectations about the viability of libertarian/radical reform movements elsewhere, particularly in other crisis-economy democracies.

## Removal Risk Assessment

Milei was never seriously at risk of leaving office early in 2025. Key factors:
- No viable removal pathway reached its threshold (impeachment lacked 2/3 supermajority)
- Strong economic trajectory (inflation 300%→1.5% monthly) created protective political environment
- Argentina's post-1983 democratic stability prevented any extra-constitutional removal
- Opposition was fragmented across multiple Peronist successor parties
- International support (IMF $20B, US Treasury) provided a financial backstop
