# Tarcisio de Freitas

Governor of São Paulo (2023-2026). Center-right Republicanos party.
Former Minister of Infrastructure under Bolsonaro.
Leading right-wing candidate for the 2026 presidential election if Bolsonaro remains barred.
Age 51 — represents generational change from both Lula and Bolsonaro.

## Forecasting Relevance

Tarcisio must consolidate the Bolsonaro base while appealing to centrists. His success depends on:

1. **Bolsonaro endorsement**: Without it, the right-wing vote fragments. With it, Tarcisio becomes the clear opposition candidate.
2. **São Paulo governorship performance**: São Paulo is Brazil's economic engine. A strong record on infrastructure, security, and fiscal management builds national credibility.
3. **Coalition building**: Republicanos is smaller than PL (Bolsonaro's party). Tarcisio needs a broader coalition to compete with PT's established alliance.
4. **Electoral math**: Brazil's polarized electorate means Tarcisio needs to hold Bolsonaro's 49% from 2022 while adding moderate/centrist voters who rejected both Bolsonaro and Lula.
