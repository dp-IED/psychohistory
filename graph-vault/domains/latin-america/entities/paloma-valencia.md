# Paloma Valencia

Colombian senator and 2026 presidential candidate for the Uribista Centro Democrático party. Represents the traditional right-wing establishment. Lawyer and columnist.

## 2026 Presidential Campaign

- Polymarket: 0.9% to win first round, 11% to win presidency
- Struggles to consolidate right-wing vote against de la Espriella's populist appeal
- Represents Uribe's political legacy
- Part of [[domains/latin-america/concepts/fragmented-right-wing-field/_concept|fragmented right-wing field pattern]]: traditional right split between Uribista (Valencia) and populist (de la Espriella) factions, preventing consolidation
