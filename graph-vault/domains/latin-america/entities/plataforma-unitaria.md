# Plataforma Unitaria Democrática (PUD)

## Summary

The Unitary Democratic Platform (Spanish: Plataforma Unitaria Democrática, PUD) is the main opposition political coalition in Venezuela, formed in 2021 as an alliance of opposition parties — including Primero Justicia, Democratic Action, A New Era, Popular Will (whose leader Leopoldo López was in exile), and others. The PUD succeeded the previous opposition coalition and was the vehicle for the 2024 presidential campaign of [[edmundo-gonzalez|Edmundo González]].

The coalition's strategy in 2024 involved:
- Uniting behind a single consensus candidate after [[maria-corina-machado|María Corina Machado]] was barred
- Building a vote-monitoring infrastructure to collect and tabulate results independently of the CNE
- Releasing detailed parallel vote tabulations after the election to demonstrate the fraud
- Coordinating international recognition of González as the legitimate winner

The PUD's vote-tallying operation (ConVzla) was critical: it collected official tally sheets from 81% of polling centers and published results showing González won 67% of the vote — a level of independent electoral verification unprecedented in Venezuelan authoritarian elections.

## Significance for Forecasting

- The PUD's 2024 campaign provides a template for how opposition coalitions can contest elections in authoritarian contexts where the regime controls the official electoral apparatus.
- The parallel vote tabulation (ConVzla) is a forecasting-significant innovation: when opposition coalitions invest in vote-monitoring infrastructure, they can provide credible evidence of election outcomes regardless of the official results.
- The coalition's ability to unite behind a single candidate after Machado's disqualification demonstrates that structural unity can overcome candidate-name-recognition deficits.
