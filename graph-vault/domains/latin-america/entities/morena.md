# MORENA (Movimiento de Regeneración Nacional)

## Summary
MORENA (National Regeneration Movement) is the ruling political party of Mexico, founded by [[domains/latin-america/entities/andres-manuel-lopez-obrador]] in 2011 as a left-nationalist, populist movement. It won the presidency in 2018 (AMLO with 53%) and consolidated control over Congress and state governments through 2024. In 2024, its candidate [[domains/latin-america/entities/claudia-sheinbaum]] won the presidency with ~60% of the vote.

## Significance for Forecasting
MORENA represents a dominant-party realignment in Mexican politics, replacing the PRI's 71-year dominance (1929-2000) with a new dominant party. Key forecasting metrics:

- **Congressional strength**: Held majority in both Chamber of Deputies and Senate since 2018; supermajority (with allies) for constitutional amendments in 2024.
- **State-level dominance**: Controlled 23 of 32 state governorships by 2024
- **Party identification growth**: MORENA became the largest party identification in Mexico, surpassing PAN and PRI combined
- **Coalition structure**: MORENA runs in coalition with the Labor Party (PT) and Greens (PVEM), forming the "Sigamos Haciendo Historia" alliance

## Forecasting Data Points
- 2018 presidential: 53% (AMLO)
- 2021 midterms: MORENA coalition won 46% of vote, retained lower house majority
- 2024 presidential: 60% ([[domains/latin-america/entities/claudia-sheinbaum]])
