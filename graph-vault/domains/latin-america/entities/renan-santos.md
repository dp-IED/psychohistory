## Summary

Renan Santos is a Brazilian politician and candidate in the 2026 Brazilian presidential election. He is a right-wing/far-right candidate running against incumbent President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva (PT) and São Paulo Governor Tarcisio de Freitas (Republicanos). Santos represents the wing of the Brazilian right that seeks to continue the Bolsonaro political project following former President Jair Bolsonaro's 2023 electoral court conviction and 2025 criminal conviction.

## Significance for Forecasting

Santos is a minor candidate in a polarized two-candidate-dominant race. In Brazil's two-round system, the most likely top-two finishers are Lula (incumbent, ~40-45% base) and Tarcisio (center-right, leading the opposition). A third candidate like Santos finishing second would require a significant disruption to the expected Lula-Tarcisio polarization — either Lula's health incapacitation or Tarcisio collapsing under scandals. His Polymarket probability of 9.6% for second place reflects this structural assessment.
