# Raúl Castro

Raúl Modesto Castro Ruz (born June 3, 1931) — retired Cuban revolutionary and politician. Younger brother of Fidel Castro.

## Career

- **1959-2008**: Minister of the Revolutionary Armed Forces (FAR), Second Secretary of PCC
- **2008-2018**: President of the Council of State and Council of Ministers (succeeded Fidel)
- **2011-2021**: First Secretary of the Cuban Communist Party (PCC)
- **2021**: Retired from all formal leadership positions at age 89
- **Current status (2026)**: Age 95, retired, living in Cuba, no formal political role

## Legal Status (US)

- **No US criminal indictment**: Raúl Castro has never been indicted by the US Department of Justice
- **No Interpol Red Notice**: No international arrest warrant exists
- **No extradition framework**: US and Cuba have no extradition treaty
- **No bounty program**: US has not designated Raúl for any reward/capture program
- **Sovereign immunity questions**: Even if indicted, former heads of state typically have some immunity for official acts
