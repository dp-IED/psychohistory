# Latin America Domain

## Overview
The Latin America domain covers political, economic, and social developments across Mexico, Central America, South America, and the Caribbean. The region is characterized by:

- **Dominant-party and populist dynamics**: Several countries (Mexico under MORENA, Venezuela under PSUV, Nicaragua under FSLN, Bolivia under MAS) have experienced dominant-party consolidation or democratic backsliding since the late 2010s.
- **Commodity-dependent economies**: The region's economic cycles are heavily influenced by commodity prices (oil, copper, lithium, soybeans, agricultural products).
- **Structural inequality**: High Gini coefficients, informality, and weak state capacity create recurring patterns of social unrest and institutional fragility.
- **Left-right pendulum swings**: The "pink tide" (2000s-2010s) followed by a conservative reaction, followed by a new left wave since 2018 (López Obrador in Mexico, Petro in Colombia, Boric in Chile, Lula in Brazil).
- **Criminal violence and state capacity gaps**: Drug trafficking, organized crime, and weak rule of law in several countries create security crises that shape political outcomes.

## Key Countries for Forecasting (by prediction market frequency)
- **Mexico**: Largest economy, dominant-party system under MORENA, scheduled elections every 6 years (presidential), constitutional reform dynamics, USMCA trade framework
- **Brazil**: Largest population, Lula's third term, 2026 presidential election approaching, Amazon/environmental policy, BRICS dynamics
- **Argentina**: Milei's radical reform program, IMF relations, chronic inflation, 2025 midterms
- **Venezuela**: Authoritarian electoral dynamics, presidency crisis, oil sector, migration crisis
- **Chile**: Constitutional reform process, Boric administration, lithium policy
- **Colombia**: Petro administration, peace process with ELN, security situation
