# Argentina-Milei Realignment

## Definition

The Argentine political realignment catalyzed by Javier Milei's rise from fringe libertarian to president (2023-2025), restructuring a party system that had been dominated by Peronism (PJ) and anti-Peronism (JxC/UP) for two decades. The realignment has three phases: (1) Milei's breakthrough victory in 2023, (2) his legislative consolidation via the 2025 midterm wave, and (3) the emerging post-Milei succession question. This concept encodes the structural forecasting patterns from arguably the most consequential Latin American political transformation since Chávez (1998).

## The Three-Phase Framework

### Phase 1: Shock Victory (2023)
Milei won the presidency with 55.7% of the runoff vote against Sergio Massa, despite his party (LLA) holding only 8% of Chamber seats and zero governorships. This was a **protest-driven breakthrough** enabled by 140%+ inflation and Peronist infighting, not organic party-building. Key forecasting principle: a disruption candidate can win the presidency but cannot govern without legislative consolidation.

### Phase 2: Legislative Wave (2025)
The 2025 midterms saw LLA win the most seats in the Chamber (the vault's p_yes=0.78 forecast was correct), consolidating Milei's legislative position. This validated two patterns:
- **Populist coattail wave**: A first-term president with high salience can transform a fragmented legislature even without strong party organization
- **Squeeze confirmation**: The regional third-way (HNP/Primero País) was squeezed from 24 to 8 seats as polarization consolidated behind LLA and UP

### Phase 3: Post-Milei Succession (Projected)
The realignment's durability depends on whether Milei builds an enduring party or his movement collapses after his departure (expected 2027 or 2031). The Kirchner precedent (2003-2015) suggests personalist movements can persist, but the Peronist institutional infrastructure (unions, provincial networks) that sustained Kirchnerism does not exist for LLA.

### Key Calibration Signals

1. **Milei approval trajectory**: If Milei holds above 40% approval entering the 2027 cycle, LLA will likely retain structural parity with UP. If approval drops below 30%, LLA fragments and a new anti-Peronist pole emerges.

2. **UP reconstruction**: Peronism's ability to rebuild after the 2025 floor (~28-30% of seats) will determine whether the realignment is permanent or cyclical. Key indicator: whether UP retains union loyalty or the CGT shifts toward neutrality.

3. **JxC/CC fate**: The once-dominant anti-Peronist coalition has collapsed to third-party status. Its voters have migrated to LLA (right) or stayed home. A JxC/CC recovery would require LLA's collapse — making it a counter-indicator: strong JxC/CC signals weak LLA.

4. **Economic anchor**: Milei's reform program (dollarization, spending cuts, deregulation) is the structural anchor of the realignment. Success (inflation <30%, GDP growth) locks in LLA dominance; failure brings a return to Peronist orthodoxy.
