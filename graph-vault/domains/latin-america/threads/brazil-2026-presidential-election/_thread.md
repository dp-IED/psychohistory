# Brazil 2026 Presidential Election

## Key Actors

### Left / Center-Left
- [[domains/latin-america/entities/luiz-inacio-lula-da-silva|Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva]] — Incumbent president (2023-2026), left-wing PT. Seeking re-election. Polymarket: 44.5% ($5.6M vol).
- [[fernando-haddad|Fernando Haddad]] — Finance Minister, PT. Possible successor if Lula doesn't run. Polymarket: 4.2% ($4.8M vol).

### Right / Center-Right
- [[domains/latin-america/entities/flavio-bolsonaro|Flávio Bolsonaro]] — Senator (PL), eldest son of Jair Bolsonaro. Current right-wing Polymarket leader at 23.1% ($5.8M vol). Carries the Bolsonaro brand without father's ineligibility.
- [[domains/latin-america/entities/tarcisio-de-freitas|Tarcisio de Freitas]] — Governor of São Paulo, center-right Republicans. Leading center-right candidate. Polymarket: 0.4% ($11.5M vol — high volume despite low price suggests institutional hedging).
- [[domains/latin-america/entities/michelle-bolsonaro|Michelle Bolsonaro]] — Former first lady (PL). Polymarket: 3.3% ($6.4M vol).
- [[domains/latin-america/entities/eduardo-bolsonaro|Eduardo Bolsonaro]] — Federal Deputy (PL), third son. Polymarket: 0.1% ($8.8M vol).
- Ciro Gomes — Center-left PDT, perennial candidate.

### Minor / Centrão
- [[domains/latin-america/entities/tereza-cristina|Tereza Cristina]] — Senator (PP), former Agriculture Minister. Polymarket: 0.4% ($994K vol). Placeholder candidate — likely to withdraw for Centrão negotiations.
- [[domains/latin-america/entities/renan-santos|Renan Santos]] — Polymarket: 14.2% ($5.5M vol). Unclear candidacy viability.
- Romeu Zema — Governor of Minas Gerais (Novo). Polymarket: 5.5% ($2.7M vol).
- Ronaldo Caiado — Governor of Goiás (União Brasil). Polymarket: 1.2% ($2.9M vol).

## Election Dynamics
- Brazil uses two-round system: if no candidate gets 50%+ in first round (Oct 4, 2026), top two go to runoff
- Lula's base is ~40-45%, but his coalition is fraying
- The right is divided: Tarcisio (center-right) vs Bolsonaro loyalists (far-right)
- Key issues: economy (inflation, growth), crime/security, corruption narratives

## Forecasting Relevance
- If Tarcisio consolidates the right, it becomes a competitive race
- Bolsonaro's ineligibility creates an open right-primary dynamic
- Lula's age (81 in 2026) and health are latent risks
