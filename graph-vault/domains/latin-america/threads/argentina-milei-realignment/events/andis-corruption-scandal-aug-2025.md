# Karina Milei ANDIS Corruption Scandal (Aug 2025)

## Summary

In August 2025, leaked audio recordings implicated Karina Milei (Secretary General of the Presidency, President Milei's sister and chief of staff) in alleged kickbacks from pharmaceutical contracts managed through Argentina's National Disability Agency (ANDIS). The recordings suggested that Karina Milei used her position to steer contracts to allied pharmaceutical distributors in exchange for kickbacks.

President Javier Milei denied the allegations, characterizing them as a political attack by the Peronist opposition. However, his approval rating dropped from approximately 48% to 39% in the immediate aftermath — the steepest single-month decline of his presidency to that point.

## Timeline

- **2025-08 (early)**: Leaked audio recordings surface implicating Karina Milei in ANDIS contract kickbacks
- **2025-08 (mid)**: President Milei denies allegations; approval drops ~48% → ~39%
- **2025-09**: Milei approval hits all-time low of 32.1% following compound crises: Jose Espert drug-ties revelations, peso crisis (Central Bank burns $1B in reserves), and the lingering ANDIS damage
- **2025-09 (late)**: US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent announces support for Argentina, halting gradual financial implosion. Approval stabilizes.

## Significance for Forecasting

The ANDIS scandal is the second major corruption test of Milei's political survival, following the $LIBRA Cryptogate (Feb 2025). Key forecasting differences from LIBRA:

1. **Family member involvement is more damaging**: While Milei's own role in $LIBRA was promotional (he promoted a shady crypto coin), ANDIS directly implicated Karina Milei — his sister and most trusted adviser — in corrupt contract steering. Family scandals are harder to dismiss as isolated errors because they signal systemic issues with the inner circle.

2. **Approval erosion is cumulative but recoverable**: The 9-point approval drop from ANDIS was significant, but Milei's approval never fell below 32% — far above the ~20% threshold historically associated with presidential resignation. The compound crisis of Sep 2025 (ANDIS + Espert + peso) was the true stress test, and Milei survived it because international support (US Treasury) stabilized the financial dimension.

3. **No removal pathway materialized**: As with $LIBRA, impeachment remained structurally impossible (LLA had only 10 pre-election seats). No protests reached regime-threatening scale. The opposition remained fragmented (Fuerza Patria, Primero País, FIT-U unable to coordinate).

4. **Forecast application**: The ANDIS scandal is the second data point in an emerging pattern: Milei's approval undergoes scandal-driven dips but recovers, and no removal mechanism reaches feasibility threshold. This informs Bayesian priors for future Milei survival questions — each survived scandal raises the prior for survival of the next one.
