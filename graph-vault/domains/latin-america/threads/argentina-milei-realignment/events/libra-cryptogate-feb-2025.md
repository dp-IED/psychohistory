# $LIBRA Cryptogate (Feb 2025)

## Summary

In February 2025, Argentine President Javier Milei publicly promoted a cryptocurrency called $LIBRA on his X account, calling it a "private initiative dedicated to stimulating Argentina's economy." The coin surged to a market cap of $4.5 billion in hours before crashing in what market analysts described as a classic "rug pull" — insiders cashed out while retail investors absorbed losses. Milei deleted the promotion post after the crash.

The scandal triggered immediate impeachment calls from opposition parties. Charges were filed by Claudio Lozano (Popular Unity party), alleging Milei violated the Ethics in Public Office law by promoting a financial instrument in which he or his associates may have had undisclosed interests.

## Significance for Forecasting

The $LIBRA scandal was the first major test of Milei's political survival. Key forecasting lessons:

1. **Scandal damage is trajectory-dependent**: At PIT cutoff (2025-03-08), the scandal was fresh with unknown trajectory. The question was whether it would fade as a one-off error or cascade into broader corruption revelations. (In the event, the scandal faded — Milei's approval recovered — but this was deeply uncertain at cutoff.) Forecasters should discount scandal effects in inverse proportion to elapsed time.

2. **Impeachment feasibility threshold**: Argentina's impeachment requires 2/3 of both chambers. LLA held only 10 of 257 Chamber seats in Feb 2025. No opposition supermajority existed. This made impeachment politically impossible regardless of the scandal's severity — a structural constraint that forecasters should identify early.

3. **Populist scandal resilience**: Milei's response (conceding error, deleting the post, but defending the practice of promoting Argentine ventures) followed the radical-reformer-political-survival playbook: acknowledge the mistake to limit damage, don't resign, and rely on polarization to retain base support.

4. **Gold set connection**: This event was the key uncertainty suppressing the [[runs/20260521-034615-will-lla-win-the-most-seats-in-the-chamber-of-deputies-follo|LLA seat forecast]] (p_yes=0.78 vs instinctive higher). The market priced Milei's governance wins but discounted the LIBRA overhang, creating a slight divergence between vault-model and market at cutoff.
