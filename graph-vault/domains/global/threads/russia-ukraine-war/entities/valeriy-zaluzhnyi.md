# Valeriy Zaluzhnyi

## Summary
Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces of Ukraine from July 2021 to February 2024. Widely credited as the architect of Ukraine's successful 2022 defensive operations (Kyiv defense, Kharkiv counteroffensive, Kherson liberation). Fired by President Zelenskyy in February 2024 amid reported tensions over mobilization policy, military strategy, and political ambition. Subsequently appointed Ukraine's ambassador to the United Kingdom (March 2024). His dismissal removed Ukraine's most popular military commander and signaled internal friction in Ukraine's wartime leadership.

## Significance for Forecasting
Zaluzhnyi's dismissal in February 2024 was a significant data point for 2024 ceasefire probability:
1. **Leadership cohesion signal**: The firing of a popular C-in-C during wartime indicated internal political stress within Ukraine's government. Military-civilian friction raised the risk of wartime instability.
2. **Strategy shift**: Zaluzhnyi publicly acknowledged the positional stalemate (Nov 2023 Economist essay). His replacement by Colonel-General Oleksandr Syrskyi (commander of Bakhmut defense) signaled a shift toward defensive/attrition posture rather than offensive.
3. **Political ambition**: Zaluzhnyi was widely viewed as having political ambitions (approval ratings rivaling/preceding Zelesnky's). His removal and exile to an ambassador post was a preemptive political neutralization — potentially reducing Ukraine's democratic resilience but stabilizing command unity.
