# Hezbollah

## Summary

Hezbollah (Party of God) is a Lebanese Shia Islamist political party and militant group founded in 1985 with Iranian support. It is the most powerful non-state military actor in the Middle East, possessing an extensive rocket/missile arsenal and combat-proven ground forces. Hezbollah fought Israel to a stalemate in the 2006 Lebanon War and subsequently built a deterrence relationship along the Israel-Lebanon border. The group deployed thousands of fighters to support the Assad regime in the Syrian civil war (2011-present), gaining significant combat experience. Hezbollah is a central node in Iran's "Axis of Resistance" network, serving as Iran's most capable proxy.

This is a structural factor in the Israel-Lebanon withdrawal forecast: Israel has reduced incentive to withdraw while Hezbollah could act independently of a weakened Iran.

## Timeline

- **1985**: Founded by Iran's Revolutionary Guard during the Lebanese Civil War
- **2006**: July-August war with Israel; military stalemate, political victory for Hezbollah
- **2012-present**: Combat deployments to Syria supporting Assad regime
- **2023 October 8**: Hezbollah opens "support front" with Israel following Hamas's Oct 7 attack
- **2024 September 27**: Hassan Nasrallah killed in Israeli airstrike on Beirut HQ
- **2024 November 26**: Israel-Hezbollah ceasefire announced
- **2025**: Post-ceasefire period; Hezbollah weakened but retains significant military capacity
- **2025 June**: Iran-Israel Twelve-Day War; IRGC command decimated; Hezbollah proxy autonomy increases
