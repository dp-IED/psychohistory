# Xi Jinping

## Summary

Xi Jinping is the General Secretary of the Chinese Communist Party (CCP), President of the People's Republic of China, and Chairman of the Central Military Commission. In October 2022, at the 20th National Congress of the CCP, Xi secured an unprecedented third term as General Secretary, breaking with the two-term precedent established by Deng Xiaoping and followed by Jiang Zemin and Hu Jintao. His ideology "Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era" was enshrined in the Party constitution. The 20th Congress elevated allies from his home province of Zhejiang (Li Qiang, Ding Xuexiang) to top leadership positions, further consolidating his dominance over Chinese political institutions.

## Significance

Xi's consolidation of power and third term represent a structural break in Chinese governance norms, ending the informal term limits that had operated since the 1980s. His leadership has been associated with intensified state control over the economy, a national security-first approach to foreign policy, and the tightening of domestic political controls. His policy directions -- including "common prosperity," "zero-COVID," and intensified Taiwan rhetoric -- have major implications for global supply chains, technology decoupling, and cross-strait stability.
