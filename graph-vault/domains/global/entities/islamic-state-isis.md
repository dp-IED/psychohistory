# Islamic State (ISIS)

## Overview

The Islamic State (ISIS/ISIL) is a Salafi jihadist militant group and former unrecognized proto-state. It gained global prominence in 2014 when it captured large territories in Iraq and Syria, declared a caliphate under Abu Bakr al-Baghdadi, and became the world's wealthiest and most operationally capable terrorist organization. The group was territorially defeated in 2017-2019 (loss of Mosul, Raqqa, and the final Baghouz pocket in March 2019) but has since reconstituted as an insurgency and global attack network, primarily through its Afghan affiliate ISIS-Khorasan (ISIS-K / ISKP).

**Designated as a terrorist organization** by: UN Security Council, US, EU, UK, Russia, India, Turkey, Saudi Arabia, and dozens of other nations.

## Key Affiliates

### ISIS-Khorasan (ISIS-K / ISKP)
The Islamic State's Afghanistan-Pakistan branch, established in January 2015. After the Taliban's takeover of Afghanistan in August 2021, ISIS-K emerged as the primary threat to the Taliban regime and one of the most operationally active global jihadist groups. Unlike the Taliban, which is focused on territorial control of Afghanistan, ISIS-K maintains a global attack agenda and has conducted operations in:
- **Afghanistan**: Continuous attacks on Taliban targets, Hazara civilians, and international actors
- **Pakistan**: Attacks on Chinese interests, security forces
- **Iran**: January 2024 Kerman bombings (killing ~100 at Soleimani anniversary)
- **Russia**: March 2024 Crocus City Hall attack (killing 145)
- **Europe**: Multiple disrupted plots (Germany, Austria, 2024)
- **Central Asia**: Recruitment and operational networks among Central Asian diaspora

### Other Active Affiliates
- **ISIS-West Africa (ISWAP)**: Active in Nigeria, Lake Chad Basin
- **ISIS-Sinai (Wilayat Sinai)**: Egyptian Sinai insurgency
- **ISIS-Central Africa**: DRC and Mozambique operations
- **ISIS-Somalia**: East Africa operations

## Timeline of Key Events

- **2014**: Capture of Mosul (June), declaration of caliphate (June 29), beginning of US-led international coalition airstrikes (August)
- **2015**: Paris attacks (November 13, 130 killed) — deadliest in Western Europe since Madrid 2004
- **2017-2019**: Territorial defeat — loss of Mosul (July 2017), Raqqa (Oct 2017), final Baghouz pocket (March 2019)
- **2019**: Death of Abu Bakr al-Baghdadi (Oct 27, US Delta Force raid in Idlib, Syria)
- **2021-08-26**: Abbey Gate bombing, Kabul — ISIS-K kills 13 US service members and ~170 Afghan civilians during evacuation
- **2022**: ISIS-K establishes itself as the most active global ISIS branch post-Taliban takeover; intensified targeting of Taliban
- **2024-01-03**: Kerman bombings, Iran — ISIS-K claims twin bombings at Soleimani memorial, ~100 killed
- **2024-03-22**: Crocus City Hall attack, Moscow — 145 killed, largest terrorist attack in Russia since Beslan (2004)

## Forecasting Significance

The Islamic State, particularly ISIS-K, remains the most operationally capable transnational jihadist group for forecasting purposes because:
1. **Global reach**: ISIS-K has demonstrated capability to conduct complex, mass-casualty attacks outside Afghanistan (Iran Jan 2024, Russia Mar 2024)
2. **Anti-regime targeting**: ISIS-K targets both Western adversaries (Russia, Iran) and Western allies (Taliban now, potentially Europe/US)
3. **Diaspora recruitment**: Central Asian diaspora in Russia and Europe provides operational cover
4. **Rivalry with Taliban**: ISIS-K's competition with the Taliban drives it toward increasingly spectacular attacks to demonstrate relevance
5. **Propaganda value**: Attacks in adversary states provide ISIS-K recruiting material and demonstrate continued operational relevance
