# Vladimir Putin

## Structural Position
- **Role**: President of Russia (since 2000/2012); de facto leader since 1999 (PM under Yeltsin)
- **Political system**: Authoritarian consolidation — United Russia party dominance; systematic opposition suppression (Navalny death Feb 2024); media control
- **Power base**: Siloviki (security services); military leadership; oligarchic elite; nationalist public
- **International**: Sanctioned by US/EU; ICC arrest warrant issued Mar 2023 (war crimes in Ukraine); Trump engaged via Alaska Summit (Aug 2025)

## Key Traits for Forecasting
1. **Risk tolerance**: High — invaded Ukraine Feb 2022 despite overwhelming odds of Western response; escalated through multiple nuclear threats
2. **Negotiating style**: Trump-aligned transactional approach; values direct leader-level engagement; distrusts multilateral institutions
3. **Domestic constraint**: War legitimizes regime; accepting unfavorable peace risks elite discontent; frozen conflict is optimal (maintains war footing without indefinite casualties)
4. **Succession**: No clear successor designated; age 73 (Oct 2025); leadership stability is a nontrivial tail risk

## Forecasting Relevance
- Russia-Ukraine ceasefire negotiations (Putin's willingness to accept armistice terms)
- US-Russia bilateral relations (Putin-Trump dynamic post-Alaska Summit)
- Regime stability and succession (tail risk to all Russia forecasts)
- Nuclear posture (Putin's nuclear signaling in Ukraine war context)
- Sanctions relief timeline (Putin's price for accepting Ukrainian neutrality)
