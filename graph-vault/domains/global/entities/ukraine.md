# Ukraine

## Structural Position
- **Population**: ~37M (pre-war ~44M; war losses, emigration, occupied territories)
- **Economy**: War economy; sustained by Western financial aid ($60B+ US aid, €50B EU facility)
- **Government**: Presidential-parliamentary system; Volodymyr Zelenskyy (President since 2019); martial law since Feb 2022 with regular elections suspended
- **Military**: Combat-hardened; NATO-trained and equipped brigades; innovative drone warfare; asymmetric strike capability (long-range drones reaching deep into Russia)
- **Geopolitical position**: EU candidate (accession talks opened Jun 2024); NATO partnership deepened but no membership; Western security umbrella through bilateral US/EU commitments

## War with Russia — Structural Position (2026)
Ukraine has been defending against Russia's full-scale invasion since February 2022. As of mid-2026:
- **Territorial loss**: ~18% of territory under Russian occupation (Crimea since 2014, Donbas, southern Ukraine since 2022)
- **Military posture**: Defensive; manpower shortages after 4+ years of high-intensity combat; Western equipment flows continue but at reduced political velocity
- **Kursk salient**: Ukrainian forces have held ~400 sq km of Russian territory since Aug 2024 incursion — key bargaining chip
- **Civilian infrastructure**: Power grid repeatedly targeted; population enduring systematic attacks
- **Demographic crisis**: Acute manpower shortage — mobilization-age men reduced by casualties, emigration, and war exhaustion

## Key Dynamics for Forecasting
### Zelenskyy's Decision Calculus (2026)
- **Core demand**: NATO membership or equivalent security guarantee (bilateral US security pact)
- **Red line**: No formal recognition of occupied territories as Russian
- **Acceptable compromise**: Korean-style armistice — ceasefire-in-place without de jure territorial concession, framed as "war paused, not ended"
- **Domestic politics**: Ukrainian public 80%+ opposed to territorial concessions; accepting a freeze is politically toxic without ironclad security guarantees
- **Deal incentive**: War fatigue, manpower crisis, infrastructure destruction — continuing the war indefinitely is unsustainable without dramatically increased Western commitment

### Ceasefire Pathway (see [[runs/20260618-russia-ukraine-ceasefire]])
- Korea-style armistice along current lines of control is the most probable format
- Ukraine needs: security guarantees (below NATO membership) + reconstruction package + face-saving narrative
- Key variable: Can Zelenskyy sell "freeze, not surrender" to Ukrainian public and military?
- EU/NATO division: Eastern members (Poland, Baltics) oppose concessions; Western members (Germany, France, Italy) more pragmatic
