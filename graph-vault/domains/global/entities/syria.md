## Summary

Country in the Middle East, ruled by [[entities/bashar-al-assad]] until the regime's collapse on December 8, 2024. The Assad regime's fall was a structural shock to Iran's "Axis of Resistance" and a critical precondition for the January 2025 Gaza ceasefire.

## Significance for Forecasting

Syria's regime change exemplifies [[concepts/terminal-crisis-declining-dynasty]] — a long-tenured authoritarian regime that appeared stable before rapidly collapsing when multiple stress factors converged. Key lessons:
- Regime resilience can mask structural fragility — Assad survived a 13-year civil war (2011-2024) before collapsing
- External patron overextension is a vulnerability signal: Iran and Russia were focused on the Israel-Iran escalation and Ukraine respectively
- Regime collapse in one theater (Syria) had cascading effects on a different conflict (Gaza) by severing Iran's weapons/logistics corridor to Hezbollah
