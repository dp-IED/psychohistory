# ByteDance

## Summary

ByteDance Ltd. is a Chinese internet technology company founded in 2012 by Zhang Yiming. It is the parent company of TikTok (international) and Douyin (Chinese domestic version of TikTok). Its core business is AI-powered content recommendation engines. ByteDance became one of the world's most valuable private companies, valued at over $200 billion at peak.

## Significance for Forecasting

ByteDance's ownership structure (Chinese company, no US board representation, subject to Chinese law) made it the target of the most significant forced divestiture legislation in US history. The company's refusal to sell TikTok — and the US government's insistence on a sale — became a defining case study in national security-driven tech decoupling.

## Timeline

- **2012**: ByteDance founded by Zhang Yiming
- **2016**: Launched Douyin (Chinese short-video app)
- **2017**: Acquired musical.ly, launched TikTok internationally
- **2020**: Trump executive orders target TikTok; ByteDance enters Oracle-Walmart partnership negotiations (never finalized)
- **2024-04-24**: US passes Protecting Americans from Foreign Adversary Controlled Applications Act, ordering ByteDance to divest TikTok within 9 months or face ban
- **2025-01-17**: Supreme Court upholds divest-or-ban law
- **2025-01-18**: TikTok goes dark in the US
