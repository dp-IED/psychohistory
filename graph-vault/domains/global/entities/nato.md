# NATO (North Atlantic Treaty Organization)

## Structural Position
- **Members**: 32 states (Finland joined Apr 2023, Sweden joined Mar 2024)
- **Article 5**: Collective defense clause — an attack on one is an attack on all
- **Budget**: US provides ~70% of alliance defense spending; European members increasing post-2022 (2% GDP target)
- **Command structure**: SACEUR (Supreme Allied Commander Europe) is always a US general
- **Political context**: Trump administration skeptical of alliance burden-sharing; threatened NATO withdrawal during first term; second-term relationship complicated but alliance holds

## Key Dynamics for Forecasting
### NATO-Ukraine Relationship
- Ukraine is a NATO partner but not member — membership was ruled out by Biden/Trump before the war
- NATO has provided weapons, intelligence, and training but no troops or no-fly zone
- Post-war security guarantees for Ukraine are the central unresolved question — will NATO provide a bilateral US-Ukraine security pact with NATO-adjacent status?
- Membership Action Plan (MAP) is politically impossible with occupied territories and active conflict

### NATO-Russia Relations (2026)
- Russia's invasion of Ukraine was partially motivated by NATO expansion; Putin demands NATO rollback to 1997 borders
- Finland/Sweden accession doubled Russia's NATO border; Putin cannot accept this reversal
- Alaska Summit (Aug 2025) excluded NATO — Trump's bilateral approach bypasses alliance structures
- European NATO members (France, Poland, Baltics) oppose a settlement perceived as rewarding aggression
- Eastern flank members fear frozen conflict leaves them vulnerable to future Russian adventurism

## Forecasting Relevance
- Russia-Ukraine ceasefire terms (NATO membership for Ukraine vs alternative security guarantees)
- NATO cohesion under Trump (transatlantic strain from tariffs, Ukraine policy, US-Russia bilateralism)
- European defense spending trajectory (2%+ GDP targets)
- NATO-Russia confrontation management (Drills, Baltic air policing, Black Sea access)
- Sweden/NATO integration post-accession
- US security commitment to Europe
