## Summary
The Iran-Israel conflict refers to the period of direct military confrontation that began in April 2024, when each side struck the other's territory for the first time. After decades of proxy warfare (Hezbollah, Hamas, Houthis) and covert operations (cyberattacks, nuclear scientist assassinations), the conflict escalated through multiple thresholds culminating in the June 2025 Twelve-Day War — a full-scale conventional war that ended with a US-brokered ceasefire. The war left Iran's nuclear program significantly degraded and its Axis of Resistance dismantled, but the underlying conflict continues.

## Significance for Forecasting
This conflict provides the most recent canonical example of escalation-bargaining termination. Key lessons: (a) the escalation ladder had 8+ identifiable steps, each normalizing the next; (b) IAEA non-compliance findings preceded military action by 24 hours; (c) superpower direct entry triggered rapid termination rather than prolonged war.
