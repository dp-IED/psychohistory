# Pakistan (Islamic Republic of Pakistan)

## Structural Position
- **Population**: ~250M (5th largest globally)
- **Economy**: $376B GDP; IMF-supported economy with recurring balance-of-payments crises
- **Government**: Coalition government (PML-N led, Shehbaz Sharif PM since 2024)
- **Military**: 6th largest military; nuclear-armed state
- **Geopolitical position**: Strategic pivot between China (CPEC), US (counterterrorism partner), Gulf states (remittance/Saudi dependence), and Iran (Shia-majority neighbor, 900km border)

## Mediator Role — US-Iran Peace Deal (2026)
Pakistan emerged as the critical mediator for the landmark US-Iran permanent peace deal signed June 14, 2026, ending the conflict that began with the February 28 US-Iran war. Pakistan's unique mediator credentials:

1. **Trusted by Washington**: Long-standing counterterrorism partnership; Pakistan's ISI has operational channels with US intelligence
2. **Trusted by Tehran**: Shia-majority neighbor sharing a 900km border; deep trade and energy interdependence
3. **No regional baggage**: Unlike Saudi Arabia (Iran's rival), UAE (normalized with Israel), Turkey (NATO member), or Switzerland (Western-aligned)
4. **Previous mediator track record**: Facilitated US-Taliban talks (2018-2020), Saudi-Iran normalization talks (2021-2023 via Chinese mediation)
5. **Geography**: Proximate to both parties; can host face-to-face meetings with minimal protocol friction
