## Summary

Yoav Gallant served as Israel's Minister of Defense from 2022 to 2025. A former military commander and career officer in the IDF, Gallant was a key architect of Israel's military strategy in the Gaza War following the October 7, 2023 Hamas attack.

## Significance for Forecasting

1. **Named in ICC arrest warrant**: On November 21, 2024, the International Criminal Court issued arrest warrants for both Gallant and Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu for war crimes and crimes against humanity in Gaza. This was a landmark escalation of legal pressure on Israeli leadership and a key variable in ceasefire timing — the warrants increased the diplomatic cost of continued war for Israel and created an incentive to reach a settlement before further legal consequences could materialize.

2. **Defense policy architect**: Gallant oversaw the military campaign in Gaza, the escalation against Hezbollah, and the defense posture toward Iran. His positions — particularly the tension between his military realism (he publicly advocated for a ceasefire/Hostage deal by September 2024, suggesting the military had achieved its tactical objectives) and Netanyahu's political calculus — created an internal Israeli political dynamic that affected ceasefire timing.

3. **Public ceasefire advocacy**: In September 2024, Gallant stated publicly that the military phase in Gaza was concluding and that a ceasefire-hostage deal was necessary — contradicting Netanyahu's harder line and signaling internal Israeli government divisions that external mediators could exploit.
