## Summary

UN-affiliated international organization that promotes peaceful nuclear energy and monitors nuclear proliferation. Played a critical role in the Iran nuclear diplomacy timeline leading to the Twelve-Day War.

## Key Role in Iran-Israel Escalation

The IAEA's findings provided the legal and diplomatic foundation for escalation:
- **May 31, 2025**: Reported Iran had enough enriched uranium for nine nuclear warheads
- **June 12, 2025**: IAEA Board of Governors passed resolution declaring Iran non-compliant with nuclear obligations — first such finding since September 2005
- Israel cited the IAEA non-compliance finding as legal cover for Operation Rising Lion one day later (June 13)

## Significance for Forecasting

IAEA compliance findings operate as a legitimizing signal for military action against targets with nuclear programs. The sequence (IAEA finding → military action within 1 day) demonstrates that IAEA findings can be a near-term leading indicator of escalation — not just a background diplomatic factor.
