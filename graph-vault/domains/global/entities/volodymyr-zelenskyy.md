# Volodymyr Zelenskyy

## Structural Position
- **Role**: President of Ukraine (since May 2019)
- **Background**: Former comedian/actor; elected on anti-corruption platform with 73% first-round win; transitioned to wartime leader after Feb 2022 invasion
- **Political position**: Terms extended by martial law (elections suspended since Feb 2022); approval ratings surged post-invasion (90%+) but have declined to ~50-60% by 2026 due to war fatigue
- **International**: Hero status in West; addresses Western parliaments regularly; manages delicate relationship with Trump administration (more transactional than Biden)

## Key Traits for Forecasting
1. **Survival as wartime leader**: No viable replacement mechanism under martial law — would remain president unless killed, incapacitated, or voluntarily resigns
2. **Negotiating stance**: Has consistently demanded: (a) full territorial restoration (pre-2014 borders), (b) NATO membership, (c) accountability for Russian war crimes. In practice has shown flexibility on timeline (membership can be post-war) but not on red lines (no formal territorial concessions)
3. **Domestic constraint**: Ukrainian public 80%+ opposes territorial concessions; accepting a freeze requires ironclad security guarantees and face-saving narrative
4. **Trump relationship**: Tenuous — Trump has criticized US aid levels; Zelenskyy must maintain Ukrainian sovereignty while not alienating the US administration that controls aid flow

## Forecasting Relevance
- Russia-Ukraine ceasefire terms (can Zelenskyy sell armistice to Ukraine?)
- Ukraine EU accession trajectory
- Post-war Ukrainian politics and reconstruction
- US-Ukraine relations under Trump
- Zelenskyy political survival (assassination risk, elite discontent, post-war election scenarios)
