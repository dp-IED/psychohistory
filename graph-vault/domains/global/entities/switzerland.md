# Switzerland (Swiss Confederation)

## Structural Position
- Federal directorial republic; 26 cantons
- Population ~9M; GDP ~$900B
- Neutral country — not an EU, NATO, or EEA member
- Swiss political system: dual-majority initiative system (popular + cantonal majority required for constitutional amendments)
- Federal Council (7-member executive) governs by consensus; no single head of government (rotating presidency, largely ceremonial)

## Foreign Policy & Protecting Power Role
- **US protecting power in Iran (since 1980)**: Switzerland has represented US interests in Iran continuously since the 1980 break in diplomatic relations. The US Interests Section in the Swiss embassy in Tehran handles consular services, diplomatic communications, and crisis coordination.
- **Diplomatic infrastructure**: 45-year unbroken track record of secure communications between Washington and Tehran via Swiss channels. Experienced Swiss diplomats familiar with Iranian negotiation culture.
- **Track record of major US-Iran diplomacy**:
  - JCPOA negotiations (Geneva/Lausanne, 2013-2015)
  - Prisoner swap talks (Geneva, multiple rounds 2019-2023)
  - Ceasefire negotiations (Geneva, 2025-2026)
- **No alternative host matches Switzerland's combination**: neutral infrastructure, no regional baggage, established trust, secure channels

## Swiss Political System (for Forecasting)
- **Dual-majority referendums**: constitutional amendments require both a national popular majority AND a majority of cantons to pass
- **Popular initiatives**: citizens can propose constitutional amendments via 100K signatures; Federal Council and Parliament can counter with a direct counter-proposal
- **EU treaty constraints**: Swiss-EU bilateral treaties limit immigration, environmental, and economic policy — referendums that violate EU treaty obligations create legal incompatibility
- **Federal Council opposition**: when all 7 Federal Council members oppose an initiative, historical NO rate exceeds 80%
- Examples: "No to 10M" initiative (June 2026, vault p_yes=0.22, resolved NO ~55%)
