# Russia (Russian Federation)

## Structural Position
- **Population**: ~144M
- **Economy**: $2.0T GDP (war economy footing since 2022); heavily sanctioned post-2022 invasion of Ukraine
- **Government**: Authoritarian presidential system; Vladimir Putin (President since 2000/2012); single-party dominance (United Russia)
- **Military**: Largest nuclear arsenal; second-largest conventional military; combat-tested in Ukraine since 2022
- **Geopolitical position**: Permanent UNSC member; Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO); BRICS; ostracized from G8/Western institutions post-Crimea (2014) and Ukraine invasion (2022)

## War in Ukraine — Structural Position (2026)
Russia has been fighting a full-scale war in Ukraine since February 2022. As of mid-2026:
- **Territorial control**: ~18% of Ukrainian territory (including Crimea, Donbas, southern Ukraine)
- **Military posture**: Defensive-operational; slow offensive advances in Donbas; Kursk Oblast partially occupied by Ukrainian forces since August 2024
- **Casualties**: Estimated 700K+ casualties (killed + wounded) across 4+ years of combat
- **Sanctions burden**: Sustained but adapted; energy revenue reduced but not eliminated; military-industrial complex expanded
- **Diplomatic isolation**: Durable but not absolute — China, India, Iran, North Korea maintain relations; Trump administration re-engaged through Alaska Summit (Aug 2025)

## Key Dynamics for Forecasting
### Putin's Decision Calculus (2026)
- **War continuation**: War is central to regime legitimacy — defining Putin as defender against NATO encroachment
- **Peace incentive**: Sanctions relief, European gas market re-entry, reduced military expenditure
- **Constraints**: Territorial concessions are politically toxic; annexed regions (Donetsk, Luhansk, Zaporizhzhia, Kherson, Crimea) cannot be formally relinquished
- **Trump relationship**: Alaska Summit established direct bilateral channel; Putin trusts Trump's transactional approach more than Biden's multilateralism

### Ceasefire Pathway (see [[runs/20260618-russia-ukraine-ceasefire]])
The Alaska Summit framework (Aug 2025) and subsequent Saudi-mediated talks have established ceasefire contours. Key variables for a 2026 ceasefire:
- Korea-style armistice (freeze along current lines) is most likely format
- Russia's core demand: Ukrainian neutrality (no NATO membership)
- Trade-off: territorial freeze + sanctions relief vs security guarantees for Ukraine
- Timeline catalyst: US-Iran deal completion (Jun 14) freed Trump's diplomatic bandwidth for Ukraine push
