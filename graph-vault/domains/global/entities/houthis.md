# Houthis (Ansar Allah)

## Summary

The Houthis (officially Ansar Allah) are a Zaydi Shia Islamist political and armed movement that emerged in northern Yemen in the 1990s. Since seizing control of Sanaa in 2014, they have fought a Saudi/UAE-led coalition to a stalemate in Yemen's civil war. The Houthis are part of Iran's "Axis of Resistance" network, receiving Iranian weapons, training, and funding. From late 2023, they launched sustained attacks on commercial shipping in the Red Sea (the Red Sea Crisis) in support of Palestinians during the Gaza war, disrupting global trade routes and drawing US/UK retaliatory airstrikes. The Houthi Red Sea campaign (2023-2025) transformed the group from a regional insurgent actor into a global maritime security threat.

## Significance for Forecasting

- **Chokepoint disruption as non-state tactic**: The Houthis demonstrated the ability of a non-state actor to project power at strategic maritime chokepoints (Bab el-Mandeb strait), forcing rerouting around the Cape of Good Hope and causing significant economic disruption to global trade. This pattern has forecasting implications for future conflicts where non-state actors control access to strategic waterways.
- **Proxy warfare escalation variable**: As Iran's most geographically distant proxy operating independently, the Houthis are a critical variable in Iran-Israel escalation forecasting. Their ability to strike Israeli-linked shipping and launch long-range missiles at Israel creates a multi-front pressure dynamic that constrains Israel's operational freedom.
- **Red Sea security forecasting**: The trajectory of Houthi Red Sea attacks — whether they resume, escalate, or subside — is a direct input for global shipping cost forecasting, oil price predictions, and Suez Canal revenue projections.
- **Post-ceasefire reconstitution**: The Houthis' military capability and political position post-Yemen ceasefire tracks closely with Iranian regional strategy in the post-Twelve-Day War environment.

## Timeline

- **2023 October**: Houthis begin Red Sea attacks on Israel-linked shipping
- **2024 January 12**: US-led coalition launches Operation Prosperity Guardian airstrikes
- **2024-2025**: Sustained Houthi attacks continue despite strikes
- **2025 June**: Iran-Israel Twelve-Day War temporarily shifts regional attention
