# Respiratory Season 2025-26

## Overview

The 2025-26 Northern Hemisphere respiratory season (CDC Week 40, ~Oct 1
through Week 20, ~May 17) is the first full post-COVID season with a
normalized surveillance baseline. This season is the subject of the
Metaculus respiratory outlook tournament (~33 questions spanning flu,
RSV, COVID-19, hospitalizations, and intervention likelihood).

## PIT Conjuncture at Cutoff (2026-05-21)

**Season status**: Late-season — Week 34. The peak has almost certainly
passed. Remaining uncertainty is in final hospitalization totals,
late-season influenza B wave potential, and whether the season's
cumulative burden exceeds specific thresholds.

**Known as of cutoff**:
- ILINet national baseline: 1.9% (recalibrated pre-season, first
  post-COVID normalization baseline)
- Vaccine composition: NH 2025-26 vaccines based on WHO February 2025
  strain selection meeting
- Surveillance infrastructure: Fully operational FluView, NREVSS,
  NHSN, and wastewater surveillance networks
- CMS hospital reporting mandate remains intact (post-COVID
  regulatory preservation)
- RFK Jr. confirmed as HHS Secretary (February 2025) — potential
  vaccine-hesitancy signal that could depress uptake

**Unknown as of cutoff (forecast targets)**:
- Peak ILI% week and magnitude
- Total influenza hospitalizations for the season
- Whether any HHS region triggers epidemic thresholds
- Late-season influenza B activity (typically peaks Feb–Apr)
- RSV season severity relative to baseline
- Whether composite respiratory metrics exceed specific thresholds

## Key Actors

- **[[domains/health/entities/cdc.md]]**: Primary US surveillance;
  operates FluView, ILINet, NREVSS
- **[[domains/health/entities/who.md]]**: Global strain surveillance via
  GISRS; NH vaccine strain recommendations
- **[[domains/health/entities/cms.md]]**: Hospital capacity tracking
  via NHSN; mandatory reporting infrastructure
- **State health departments**: Report notifiable disease data to CDC;
  can independently declare regional epidemics
- **HHS/RFK Jr.**: Policy environment for vaccination campaigns and
  public health communication

## Seasonal Timeline Structure

### Phase 1: Pre-Season (Aug–Sep 2025)
- WHO vaccine strain selection for NH 2025-26 (February 2025)
- Southern Hemisphere season concludes; VE data from Australia/NZ
  available as leading indicator
- CDC recalibrates ILINet baseline using 2022-25 data
- Vaccine manufacturing and distribution begins

### Phase 2: Season Onset (Oct–Nov 2025)
- CDC Week 40 marks official season start
- Early-season strain characterization: subtype distribution
  (H1N1 vs H3N2 vs B), genetic drift from vaccine strains
- First ILI baseline crossings typically occur in HHS regions

### Phase 3: Peak Period (Dec 2025–Feb 2026)
- Peak ILI% typically occurs between Week 52 and Week 6
- Hospitalization rates accelerate; CMS capacity tracking
  becomes the primary severity metric
- CDC publishes interim VE estimates (Feb 2026)

### Phase 4: Late Season (Mar–May 2026)
- Influenza B wave potential (influenza B often peaks later
  than influenza A)
- End-of-season VE estimates published
- Final hospitalization and mortality totals compiled

## Forecasting Significance

- This is the **thread of record** for all 33 Metaculus respiratory
  tournament questions.
- **Quarter-level data**: See [[2025-Q4]] and [[2026-Q1]] for weekly
  FluView snapshots, hospitalization rates, and outbreak signals.
- The ILINet baseline (1.9%) is the anchor for all "above baseline"
  questions. Cross-check against regional baselines for region-
  specific questions.
- The composite respiratory metric (ILI + COVID-like illness + RSV)
  lacks a mature baseline — default to pathogen-specific metrics for
  higher-confidence forecasts.
