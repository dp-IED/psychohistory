# Oceanwide Expeditions

## Overview

Oceanwide Expeditions is a Dutch-based expedition cruise company operating
a fleet of polar-class vessels including the MV Hondius, MV Plancius, MV
Ortelius, and MV Janssonius. Headquartered in Vlissingen, Netherlands.

## Forecasting Significance

### MV Hondius Hantavirus Outbreak (2026)

Oceanwide Expeditions' MV Hondius was the vessel at the center of the first
documented shipborne hantavirus outbreak (Andes virus, March-April 2026
Antarctic voyage). The operator's role in the outbreak:

- **Responsibility for passenger manifest**: Cruise lines hold passenger
  contact information essential for international contact tracing. Line
  cooperation is a key variable in Phase 2 secondary-case detection.
- **Crew management**: Crew turnover across voyages creates potential
  for within-fleet transmission to other vessels — a small tail risk
  flagged in the [[domains/health/concepts/transport-vector-outbreak]]
  framework.
- **Liability and disclosure incentives**: Cruise operators have competing
  incentives to cooperate with health authorities (regulatory compliance,
  passenger safety) and to minimize reputational damage (which could
  reduce future bookings). This tension affects the speed and completeness
  of epidemiological data release.
