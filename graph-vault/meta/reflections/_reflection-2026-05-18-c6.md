## Summary

Question 2/30: "Will Israel first announce ceasefire on October 8?" (Israel-Hamas ceasefire). Predicted NO. Actual outcome: YES (Trump announced ceasefire on October 8, 2025; took effect October 10).

This was my second wrong prediction in a row. Both involved Middle East ceasefires — the first (gold_01) was an Iran-Israel ceasefire I missed because the vault had wrong PIT data about the June 23 ceasefire. The second (gold_02) was a Gaza ceasefire I missed because the vault had no diplomatic signals tracking.

The pattern is clear: **the vault is good at tracking military escalation but bad at tracking diplomatic resolution signals.**

## What Was Wrong

### Gold_01: Vault had factually wrong PIT data (2025-Q2 said "no ceasefire" when one had happened)
### Gold_02: Vault had missing diplomatic signals (Trump's September peace proposal was absent from Q3 summary)

Both errors share a common root: **ceasefire preparation isn't tracked with the same rigor as military action.**
