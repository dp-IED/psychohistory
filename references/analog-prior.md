# Analog prior

How a claim worker uses a matching analog card when live news disagrees. Graded on P-usca-338: the 18 Aug row followed a last-day source-split (“delay appears unlikely”) and remaining autos/dairy gaps, and missed; the matching claim was only the 19 Aug scrape. After that three-day pause, duties took effect 22 Aug 2026; last-hour “work is not finished” quotes did not restart the delay prior. Procedure for predict. Decision: ADR 0013.

A card is a **prior**, not a citation to paste after the scrape. Last-day quotes are evidence only after the Structure block has applied the card, including which **clock phase** the card names.

## When a card matches

1. Read the card before live news (`references/structure.md`).
2. The default outcome family is the card’s **base rate for this clock phase**, unless a listed **falsifier** has already fired on this forecast day. A first administrative deadline and an already-extended / post-pause clock are different priors on the same card (`references/cases/tariff-proclamation-deadline-delay.md`). On the majority-runoff card (`references/cases/majority-primary-runoff.md`), a wide first-round gap and a GOP MAGA consolidator are different priors; when the sitting president or MAGA/Tea Party lane has named one of the two, that consolidator phase outranks a non-consolidator first-round plurality.
3. These are **not** falsifiers of the matching phase: last-day source-split; “coin flip,” “long shot,” or “deal is moving along” quotes; remaining gaps on the real bargain while talks are still live; a press noun (“deal” vs “pause”) that disagrees with the cheap administrative instrument; CBP implementing the already-published next date.
4. If analog and news disagree, follow analog and say so in Structure. Follow news instead only when a falsifier has fired, and name it. A new suspend/amend instrument on this forecast day is a falsifier of post-pause take-effect, not last-hour optimism.

## When no card matches

Class is `none`. Say why. Do not invent a base rate from parametric memory. News-now then carries the claim. A Motivation that names a structural class is **not** a card; do not treat it as analog-prior until reflection files `references/cases/…`. Far analog-regime clocks with no live news still wait on a card.

## Prize

The earliest matching **claim** is still the prize. A last-day scrape that ignored a good analog is a method miss even if that scrape is correct.

A second miss on P-usca-338: after the 19 Aug three-day pause, **Resolution** stayed 19 Aug, so predict stopped and there was no scored 20–21 Aug take-effect row. When a delay instrument names a new public date, slide that problem’s resolution day later (same heading). Then the post-pause prior can be a dated claim, not only a card footnote. Do not wait for weekly discover, and do not open a duplicate problem for that pause.

A third miss on the same series: later reflect ticks told **predict** to slide, but they left **Resolution** at 19 Aug, so 20–21 Aug predict never saw the row and the 22 Aug take-effect was never a dated claim. On reflect, if a selected series named a later public answering date than **Resolution**, slide that heading later in this tick (later only). If the new date is still on or after today, subsequent predict ticks can score the next phase. If it is already past, still slide for ledger truth and keep this hatch so the next freeze is repaired while the new date is live. Do not write claims on reflect.

A fourth miss on the same series: the 19 Aug matching row was a past-tense **pause recap** (a deal delayed 338 to 22 Aug) and never stated the post-pause line. That recap is not a take-effect forecast. After Resolution slides, later predict can treat the recap as “outcome unchanged” and skip the take-effect row — which is what left this series with no dated 22 Aug claim even after the 24 Aug slide. On the tick that sees a delay instrument, today’s **Claim** is the **new clock’s** modal (already-extended take-effect unless a further suspend/amend exists today). Name the instrument in Structure. A pause recap is not the same outcome as take-effect at the new date.
