# Analog prior

How a claim worker uses a matching analog card when live news disagrees. Graded on P-usca-338: the 18 Aug row followed a last-day source-split (“delay appears unlikely”) and remaining autos/dairy gaps, and missed; the matching claim was only the 19 Aug scrape. After that three-day pause, duties took effect 22 Aug 2026; last-hour “work is not finished” quotes did not restart the delay prior. Procedure for predict. Decision: ADR 0013.

A card is a **prior**, not a citation to paste after the scrape. Last-day quotes are evidence only after the Structure block has applied the card, including which **clock phase** the card names.

## When a card matches

1. Read the card before live news (`references/structure.md`).
2. The default outcome family is the card’s **base rate for this clock phase**, unless a listed **falsifier** has already fired on this forecast day. A first administrative deadline and an already-extended / post-pause clock are different priors on the same card (`references/cases/tariff-proclamation-deadline-delay.md`).
3. These are **not** falsifiers of the matching phase: last-day source-split; “coin flip,” “long shot,” or “deal is moving along” quotes; remaining gaps on the real bargain while talks are still live; a press noun (“deal” vs “pause”) that disagrees with the cheap administrative instrument; CBP implementing the already-published next date.
4. If analog and news disagree, follow analog and say so in Structure. Follow news instead only when a falsifier has fired, and name it. A new suspend/amend instrument on this forecast day is a falsifier of post-pause take-effect, not last-hour optimism.

## When no card matches

Class is `none`. Say why. Do not invent a base rate from parametric memory. News-now then carries the claim.

## Prize

The earliest matching **claim** is still the prize. A last-day scrape that ignored a good analog is a method miss even if that scrape is correct.

A second miss on P-usca-338: after the 19 Aug three-day pause, **Resolution** stayed 19 Aug, so predict stopped and there was no scored 20–21 Aug take-effect row. When a delay instrument names a new public date, slide that problem’s resolution day later (same heading). Then the post-pause prior can be a dated claim, not only a card footnote. Do not wait for weekly discover, and do not open a duplicate problem for that pause.
