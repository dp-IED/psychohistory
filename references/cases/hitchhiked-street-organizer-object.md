- Mechanism: Two clock phases on a low-bar street score (“≥1 nationally reported demo against X on date T”). **Owned object:** the groups that booked T publish X as the hour’s object (speakers, tract, agenda URL). National desks usually file T as X. **Hitchhike:** a parliamentary group or later overlay tells people to join someone else’s already-booked T “to denounce X,” while that kit still names Y (budget, climate adaptation, anti-austerity, fall-the-government). National desks file T as Y. A climate-only or austerity-only write-up misses an X score even if the overlay party is physically present. A later owned date that *does* name X (here 3 Oct Syngenta) is a different problem, not a reason to reclassify T.
- Base rate: **Hitchhike onto an organizer-owned Y:** the modal is no nationally reported X-street on T (P-fr-pest-street 15 Sep 2026; Bloquons tout 10 Sep 2025 vs Duplomb). **Organizers’ kit names X:** the modal is that T *is* X-street at the low national-copy bar (Duplomb 29 Jun 2025). Unconditional “a booked ecology hour plus a tribune that mentions the law ⇒ pesticide-provision street” is the wrong prior. Speaker-list and roundup split in the weeks before T are the usual news, not a last-day scrape.
- Typical openings: Name the scored object X and who published T. If the organizer kit still names Y and no listed falsifier has fired, follow no-X-street over the hitchhike tribune. Do not import an owned later pesticide/labor date onto T. An intersyndicale that owns both the date and the object (`P-fr-fp-0929`) is not this hitchhike phase. An opposition niche-day repeal vote (`P-fr-pest-niche`) is not street.
- Disanalogy: The groups that booked T already name X in the kit. Representative unions or peasant unions own T and X (fonction-publique 29 Sep; Confédération 3 Oct). The score is a parliamentary vote, not street copy. Coverage on T already reports street action against X.
- Falsifiers: Organizer kit adds X (LUA / acétamipride on the Bercy object); AFP-class or equivalent national dispatch on T reports street action against X; the overlay party publishes a distinct X march that national media then cover as X. Not falsifiers: a stale tribune that hitchhikes X onto Y; overlay deputies or union guests speaking about heat, budget, or austerity at Y’s hour; any ecology street that day; an owned X date later the same autumn.
