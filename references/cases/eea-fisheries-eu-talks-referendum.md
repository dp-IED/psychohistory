# Class: EEA/fisheries-state EU-talks referendum

- Instantiations: P-is-eu-ref (29 Aug 2026 Iceland consultative vote on resuming EU accession talks, not membership: Nei 118,040 / 52.84% vs Já 105,339 / 47.16%, turnout 82.52%; only Reykjavík North/South Yes, Northwest 62.9% No / South 60.5% No: https://www.ruv.is/frettir/innlent/2026-08-30-thjodin-sagdi-nei-island-ekki-ad-snua-baki-vid-evropu-485497 ; https://www.ruv.is/english/2026-08-29-iceland-rejects-eu-accession-talks-485377 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Icelandic_European_Union_membership_negotiations_referendum); Norway EC membership 25 Sep 1972 — No 1,118,281 / 53.51% vs Yes 971,687 / 46.49%, Oslo/Akershus Yes and north/west No (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972_Norwegian_European_Communities_membership_referendum); Norway EU membership 27–28 Nov 1994 — No 1,516,803 / 52.18% vs Yes 1,389,997 / 47.82%, turnout 89.11%, same periphery map after EEA access already existed (https://www.ssb.no/valg/statistikker/euvalg ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_Norwegian_European_Union_membership_referendum); Greenland consultative EC withdrawal 23 Feb 1982 — ~52% leave, turnout 74.9%, fisheries/home-rule (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Withdrawal_of_Greenland_from_the_European_Communities); Switzerland 4 Mar 2001 “Ja zu Europa” *talks* initiative — No 1,982,549 / 76.85% vs Yes 597,217 / 23.15% (https://www.sudd.ch/event.php?id=ch012001 ; https://swissvotes.ch/vote/474.00).
- Mechanism: The polity already has EEA (or equivalent market access) without the Common Fisheries Policy. The live ballot is often a cheap first step (resume talks; second referendum promised on any treaty). Actors: capital/professional Yes vs coastal/rural fisheries and resource periphery No; a cabinet or Labour/Social-Democrat Yes does not flip that map. Incentives: No keeps CFP/króna/sovereignty while keeping the market; Yes is “only talks.” Clock: high-turnout Saturday; early counts from the capital look like Yes; rural batches take the night. Cheap-option wording is compatible with No, not a reason to start from Yes. Mid-campaign 51–53 Yes among decided, a last Maskína/Gallup split, and “the government called it so Icesave does not apply” are the usual noise around a ~52–53 No finish.
- Base rate: North Atlantic EEA/fisheries (or resource-periphery) votes on EU deepening finish No by about 52–54% when the capital is Yes and the coast is No (Norway 53.5 then 52.2; Iceland 52.8; Greenland leave ~52). A talks-only question does not invert that (Iceland 2026). A government-opposed talks initiative in a non-fisheries alpine state can be a much larger No (Switzerland 2001) — same cheap-option failure, different magnitude. Unconditional “cabinet Yes plus second-vote promise ⇒ Já” is the wrong prior.
- Typical openings: Name the geography before the latest 51/49. Treat talks≠membership, a promised second referendum, and EEA-already as compatible with No. Do not dismiss Norway/Greenland because this ballot is consultative or government-called. Last-week source-split among decided is not a Yes prior. An early capital-heavy count is the usual path, not a late surprise.
- Disanalogy: Binding membership in a non-EEA industrial applicant that actually joined (Austria/Finland/Sweden 1994). A debt-guarantee fight in the same country (Icesave 2010–11). No fisheries/resource periphery, or the CFP exemption is already locked so the coastal bloc has flipped. The government has publicly opposed Yes *and* the map is not coastal (then expect a larger No, not a 53/47).
- Falsifiers: Consecutive public polls with Yes clearly ahead outside the margin, including rural/coastal samples; certified Yes; the coastal/fisheries organizations publicly back Yes. Not falsifiers: mid-campaign Yes-lean among decided; one last poll still Já against another Nei; remaining Saturday turnout; “talks are not membership” copy; early Reykjavík Yes.
