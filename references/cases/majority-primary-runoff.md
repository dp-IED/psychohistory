# Class: majority-primary runoff after a fragmented first round

- Instantiations: P-sc-sen-r-gop (25 Aug 2026 SC GOP Senate runoff: first-round Graham ~33% vs Norman ~25% on 11 Aug; Graham won ~52.5–47.5 with Trump/Tim Scott and eliminated 3rd-place Fry consolidating: https://apnews.com/article/south-carolina-election-lindsey-graham-03899983c3702842039e99d62e16ddad ; https://www.live5news.com/2026/08/26/sen-darline-graham-wins-scgop-senate-nomination-runoff-over-norman/ ; https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2026-election/darline-graham-winner-south-carolina-gop-senate-primary-rcna593581); P-ok-sen-d-run (25 Aug 2026 OK Dem Senate runoff: June 16 Thomas 45.2 / Priest 23.9 of 168,480; Thomas ~60–40 despite being outspent: https://ballotpedia.org/United_States_Senate_election_in_Oklahoma,_2026_(June_16_Democratic_primary) ; https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2026-election/nkiyla-jasmine-thomas-wins-oklahoma-senate-primary-democrat-rcna593618 ; https://votes.decisiondeskhq.com/races/2026-08-25/oklahoma-us-senate-democratic-runoff); P-ok-gov-r-gop (25 Aug 2026 OK GOP governor runoff: June 16 Drummond and Mazzei each ~26%, Drummond first by ~1k; Mazzei 186,604 / 50.27% vs Drummond 184,584 / 49.73%: https://www.news9.com/oklahoma-elections/oklahoma-governor-runoff-results-drummond-mazzei ; https://www.oklahoman.com/story/news/politics/elections/2026/08/25/oklahoma-runoff-election-results-2026-governor-race-gentner-drummond-mike-mazzei/91342715007/ ; https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2026-election/mike-mazzei-winner-oklahoma-republican-governor-primary-trump-rcna593583); Oklahoma GOP governor 28 Aug 2018, United States — Cornett 29% then Stitt 24% in June; businessman Stitt won the runoff 54.56–45.44 (164,892–137,316): https://ballotpedia.org/Oklahoma_gubernatorial_election,_2018_(August_28_Republican_primary_runoff) ; https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/elections/election-results/2018-election-results/2018-school-runoff-election-results/20180828-seb.pdf ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Oklahoma_gubernatorial_election.
- Mechanism: Ten US states delay a second round when no one hits a majority (or a lower threshold). The first-round electorate is larger; the runoff is a two-person, lower-turnout clock (FairVote: turnout fell in 266 of 276 federal House/Senate runoffs 1994–2022, median −40%: https://fairvote.org/report/primary-runoffs-report-2022/). First-round votes mostly stick. The trailer must harvest eliminated candidates. Two phases. **Wide-gap:** a first-round leader with a double-digit (or ~8pp-plus) lead, especially near 40%+, does not need new converts; money and electability attacks on the leader are the trailer’s usual play and usually fail. **Near-tie GOP MAGA consolidator:** when June is a few points or a coin flip and a sitting president plus the MAGA/self-funder lane has already named one of the two, that endorses consolidation in a smaller runoff electorate even if a statewide AG or metro mayor led the fragmented first round. Last-week 44–44 polls are the usual noise, not a new clock.
- Base rate: **Wide-gap:** first-round leaders with ≥10pp and/or ≥40% usually hold (Texas open runoffs since 2002: ~80–86% when the first-round margin is ≥10pp: https://txelects.com/by-the-numbers-first-place-candidates-historical-runoff-performance/). A ~21pp June gap (Thomas) is far from the Senate-runoff coin-flip implied by Bullock/FairVote’s ~54% first-round-leader rate across *all* Senate runoffs (https://archive.fairvote.org/irv/bullock.htm). Come-from-behind exists (FairVote ~32% of 276 federal runoffs 1994–2022) but clusters on close first rounds. **Near-tie GOP MAGA consolidator:** the modal is the endorsed MAGA/self-funder, not the first-place traditional officeholder (Stitt 2018; Mazzei 2026). Unconditional “first-round leader wins” is the wrong prior on that phase.
- Typical openings: Name the phase. On a wide first-round gap, treat trailer cash, debate-refusal copy, and establishment endorsements as compatible with the leader holding, not as proof the runoff flips. On a GOP near-tie, follow the sitting president’s named consolidator over a last-poll tie and over a June-night plurality of a few thousand votes. Do not invent a 50/50 from one independent survey. A first-round plurality primary with no delayed majority (New Hampshire, Massachusetts) is a different class.
- Disanalogy: The first round already produced a majority (no runoff). The state uses plurality nomination. The runoff is a *general*-election jungle/top-two (Georgia/Louisiana Nov), not an intra-party delayed majority. Democrats cross over to pick the GOP nominee (Mississippi 2014 Cochran–McDaniel). The White House walks back the consolidator. The trailer is ahead in consecutive public polls or an early-vote dump.
- Falsifiers: **Wide-gap:** a public poll or early-vote dump with the trailer ahead; certified trailer win. **Near-tie MAGA consolidator:** endorsement cancelled or flipped; consecutive polls with the traditional officeholder clearly ahead; MAGA/self-funder scandal or dropout. Not falsifiers of either phase: last-day “too close to call” copy; the trailer outspending; one 44–44 survey; remaining turnout uncertainty.
