- Mechanism: Ten US states delay a second round when no one hits a majority (or a lower threshold). The first-round electorate is larger; the runoff is a two-person, lower-turnout clock (FairVote: turnout fell in 266 of 276 federal House/Senate runoffs 1994–2022, median −40%: https://fairvote.org/report/primary-runoffs-report-2022/). First-round votes mostly stick. The trailer must harvest eliminated candidates. Two phases, and **MAGA/insurgent consolidator outranks a non-consolidator plurality**. **Wide-gap:** a first-round leader with a double-digit (or ~8pp-plus) lead, especially near 40%+, does not need new converts when no MAGA/Tea Party lane has named the other person; money and electability attacks on the leader are the trailer’s usual play and usually fail (Thomas). **GOP MAGA consolidator:** when a sitting president plus the MAGA/self-funder (or Tea Party) lane has already named one of the two, that name consolidates the smaller runoff electorate. If that name is also the first-round leader, both phases agree (Graham: first-time/appointment/nepotism copy is compatible with holding). If that name is the trailer, it outranks a June plurality even at ~10pp / ~45% for the traditional officeholder (Cruz 2012; Mazzei 2026 was the near-tie version). Last-week 44–44 polls are the usual noise, not a new clock.
- Base rate: **Wide-gap without a named MAGA trailer:** first-round leaders with ≥10pp and/or ≥40% usually hold (Texas open runoffs since 2002: ~80–86% when the first-round margin is ≥10pp: https://txelects.com/by-the-numbers-first-place-candidates-historical-runoff-performance/). A ~21pp June gap (Thomas) is far from the Senate-runoff coin-flip implied by Bullock/FairVote’s ~54% first-round-leader rate across *all* Senate runoffs (https://archive.fairvote.org/irv/bullock.htm). Come-from-behind exists (FairVote ~32% of 276 federal runoffs 1994–2022) and, in GOP races, clusters on the named MAGA/Tea Party consolidator rather than on “the trailer in general.” **GOP MAGA consolidator:** the modal is the endorsed MAGA/self-funder, not the first-place traditional officeholder (Cruz 2012 over Dewhurst’s 10pp lead; Stitt 2018; Mazzei 2026). Unconditional “first-round leader wins” is the wrong prior once that lane has named someone.
- Typical openings: Name the phase, then check whether the White House/MAGA lane has named one of the two. If it has named the leader, treat first-time, appointment, and nepotism copy as compatible with holding (Graham). If it has named the trailer, follow that consolidator over a June plurality, over trailer-vs-leader cash, and over a last-poll tie — do not treat a 10pp establishment lead as wide-gap that cancels the consolidator (Cruz; Mazzei). On a wide first-round gap with no such named trailer, treat trailer cash, debate-refusal copy, and establishment endorsements as compatible with the leader holding (Thomas). Do not invent a 50/50 from one independent survey. A first-round plurality primary with no delayed majority (New Hampshire, Massachusetts) is a different class.
- Disanalogy: The first round already produced a majority (no runoff). The state uses plurality nomination. The runoff is a *general*-election jungle/top-two (Georgia/Louisiana Nov), not an intra-party delayed majority. Democrats cross over to pick the GOP nominee (Mississippi 2014 Cochran–McDaniel). The White House walks back the consolidator. The trailer is ahead in consecutive public polls or an early-vote dump. A Dem or non-MAGA wide-gap is not Cruz/Mazzei.
- Falsifiers: **Wide-gap (no named MAGA trailer):** a public poll or early-vote dump with the trailer ahead; certified trailer win. **GOP MAGA consolidator:** endorsement cancelled or flipped; consecutive polls with the unnamed traditional officeholder clearly ahead; MAGA/self-funder scandal or dropout. Not falsifiers of either phase: last-day “too close to call” copy; the trailer outspending; one 44–44 survey; remaining turnout uncertainty; first-time/appointment/nepotism attacks on a named consolidator.
