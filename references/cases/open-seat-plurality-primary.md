# Class: open-seat plurality primary with a home-state poll leader

- Instantiations: P-nh-sen-r-prim (8 Sep 2026 New Hampshire GOP Senate primary: open Shaheen seat; former Sen. John E. Sununu vs former Massachusetts Sen. Scott Brown; AP called Sununu election night; NHPR 8:20 p.m. Sununu ~70% vs Brown ~25% with 16% in; Secretary of State official: Sununu 75,789 / 68% vs Brown 29,672 / 27% of 111,018 GOP Senate votes: https://www.nhpr.org/politics/2026-09-08/pappas-sununu-win-senate-primaries-nh-newhampshire-elections-2026 ; https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2026-election/chris-pappas-john-sununu-new-hampshire-senate-primary-winner-rcna596479 ; https://rollcall.com/2026/09/08/pappas-and-sununu-set-up-marquee-senate-clash-in-new-hampshire/ ; https://indepthnh.org/2026/09/10/democrats-turn-out-40000-more-voters-than-republicans/ ; https://smartpolitics.lib.umn.edu/2026/09/09/new-hampshire-gubernatorial-and-us-senate-primaries-by-the-numbers/); Illinois GOP Senate 2 Feb 2010, United States — open Obama/Burris seat; Rep. Mark Kirk 420,373 / 56.6% vs Patrick Hughes 142,928 / 19.3% (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_United_States_Senate_election_in_Illinois); South Dakota GOP Senate 3 Jun 2014, United States — open Johnson seat; former Gov. Mike Rounds 41,377 / 55.54% vs Larry Rhoden 13,593 / 18.25% and Stace Nelson 13,179 / 17.69% (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_United_States_Senate_election_in_South_Dakota ; https://ballotpedia.org/United_States_Senate_elections_in_South_Dakota,_2014).
- Mechanism: One clock, not a delayed majority and not a sitting incumbent. The first-round electorate *is* the nomination. A home-state former statewide (senator, governor) or sitting in-state House member who already owns name ID, cash, and the party lane, with consecutive large public-poll leads, usually holds against a recent in-migrant or a fragmented rest of field. Carpetbagger copy, remaining undecided, and “Trump backlash among Republicans” are the trailer’s usual play. Sununu 2026 had UNH and Saint Anselm both ~59–20/21 in June plus a February Trump/NRSC lane; Brown’s 2010-style outsider pitch did not compress that. Kirk 2010 and Rounds 2014 are the same finish family without a carpetbagger: the home-state poll and cash leader still won ~55–57%.
- Base rate: **Home-state poll leader with a still-lopsided lead (roughly 20pp-plus, both late polls agreeing) and the party lane still owned:** the modal is that leader (Sununu 2026 certified 68–27; Kirk 2010 56.6–19.3; Rounds 2014 55.5). Unconditional “carpetbaggers sometimes win this state’s GOP primary” is the wrong prior once one home-state name already has a 30pt table. The rare side is when no home-state candidate has that lead and the field is split (New Hampshire GOP 9 Sep 2014: recent in-migrant Scott Brown 58,775 / 49.86% over Jim Rubens 22.98% and former Sen. Bob Smith 22.56% — no 30pt home-state leader: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_United_States_Senate_election_in_New_Hampshire). A late collapse to single digits (Ayotte 2010 vs Lamontagne, 53,044–51,377) is a different phase, not a 35pt June table.
- Typical openings: Name plurality (no delayed second round) and that the seat is open. If consecutive public polls still show a large home-state lead and no listed falsifier has fired, follow that poll leader over carpetbagger/outsider copy, remaining undecided, and Iran/tariff backlash among GOP. Do not import Brown 2014 (split field, no 30pt home-state leader) as the prior for Sununu 2026. A sitting incumbent vs named challenger is `references/cases/incumbent-plurality-primary.md`. A delayed majority runoff is `references/cases/majority-primary-runoff.md`.
- Disanalogy: A sitting senator is on the ballot (incumbent plurality). The state delays a majority runoff. The clock is a top-two/jungle or ranked-choice general. Consecutive late polls have already compressed to single digits or flipped. The party lane has walked back the poll leader. The home-state field is split with no lopsided leader (Brown 2014).
- Falsifiers: Consecutive public polls with the named challenger within ~8 points or ahead; certified challenger win; poll-leader dropout or party-lane walk-back that shows up in those polls. Not falsifiers: carpetbagger/outsider pitch; ~20% still undecided; Iran/Trump or tariff backlash copy while both late polls remain lopsided; cash still lopsided for the home-state name.
