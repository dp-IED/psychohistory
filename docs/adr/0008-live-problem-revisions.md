# Live problems append revision claims; do not overwrite

A live problem may receive a new dated claim on the predict tick when the predicted outcome changes. The new row has its own claim, justification, and forecast day. Earlier rows stay. Do not overwrite an old row. After resolution day, stop revising; reflection grades the whole series.

**Considered options**: (1) freeze one claim until Y; (2) overwrite the same row as news arrives; (3) append a revision row when the forecast changes. (1) hides later information. (2) erases the early forecast. (3) is the training-bed rule.
