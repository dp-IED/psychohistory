# Forecasts are scored; openings are not; playbooks are not the product

The long-term use is to help parties such as LFI, DSA, and similar organisations see where a fight can be pushed (Gramsci: long institutional/common-sense fight, and short fight when power is contestable). The system is **multitenant**: not built for one party only. That does not change what the live loop **grades**.

**Scored:** dated **claims** on **problems** (ADR 0009–0010). The world answers on **resolution day**. Shared across tenants.

**Allowed, not scored:** **openings** — from the **tenant** asking this run (where they can push, will be blocked, or the other side is weak). These are not claim rows. Do not bake one tenant’s openings into shared overlay as everyone’s facts.

**Not a product of the grade:** a **playbook** (“do this next”). Mixing that into the claim keeps advice that sounds right when the forecast was wrong.

Does not add a fourth host job. Does not say how tenant id is passed or where openings are stored yet. Does not restore graph-vault or a world-model schema.

Glossary: `CONTEXT.md` (**Opening**, **Playbook**, **Tenant**).
