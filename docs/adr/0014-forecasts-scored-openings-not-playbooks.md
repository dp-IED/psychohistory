# Forecasts are scored; openings are not; playbooks are not the product

The long-term use is to help parties such as LFI, DSA, and similar organisations see where a fight can be pushed (Gramsci: long institutional/common-sense fight, and short fight when power is contestable). That does not change what the live loop **grades**.

**Scored:** dated **claims** on **problems** (ADR 0009–0010). The world answers on **resolution day**.

**Allowed, not scored:** **openings** — where a named organisation can push, will be blocked, or faces a weak opponent. These are not claim rows.

**Not a product of the grade:** a **playbook** (“do this next”). Mixing that into the claim keeps advice that sounds right when the forecast was wrong.

Does not add a fourth host job. Does not say where openings are written yet. Does not restore graph-vault or a world-model schema.

Glossary: `CONTEXT.md` (**Opening**, **Playbook**).
