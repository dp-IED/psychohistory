# One clock: resolution day

Due and Y were two dates (wakeup vs score). They are the same fact: when the world answers. Call it resolution day. The problem owns that date. Discover sets it. Predict runs while today is on or before it. Reflection runs after it.

Each dated claim still needs a forecast day: when predict wrote that row. That is how early correctness is visible. Do not use Due for forecast day, and do not use git history as the domain clock.

Supersedes the split-clock reading of ADR 0003/0004/0009 (Due as first wakeup, Y as score).
