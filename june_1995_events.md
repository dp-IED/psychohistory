# Major World Events -- June 1995

Research compiled from Wikipedia (1995, 1995 in science, 1995 in music, 1995 in film, 1995 in sports), historical records.

---

## POLITICS & GOVERNMENT

### June 2
- **US F-16 shot down over Bosnia**: A United States Air Force F-16 piloted by Captain Scott O'Grady is shot down over Bosnia and Herzegovina while patrolling the NATO no-fly zone. O'Grady is rescued by U.S. Marines six days later (June 8).
  - *Source: Wikipedia "1995" (June 2), citing multiple news reports from June 1995*

- **Erich Priebke extradited**: Waffen-SS Hauptsturmfuhrer Erich Priebke is extradited from Argentina to Italy to face charges for war crimes committed during World War II.
  - *Source: Wikipedia "1995" (June 2)*

### June 6
- **South Africa abolishes capital punishment**: The Constitutional Court of South Africa unanimously abolishes capital punishment in the case of S v Makwanyane and Another.
  - *Source: Wikipedia "1995" (June 6); Constitutional Court of South Africa ruling*

### June 13
- **France resumes nuclear tests**: French President Jacques Chirac announces the resumption of nuclear tests in French Polynesia, sparking international condemnation.
  - *Source: Wikipedia "1995" (June 13); The New York Times, June 14, 1995*

### June 16
- **Salt Lake City awarded 2002 Winter Olympics**: The International Olympic Committee selects Salt Lake City, Utah, to host the 2002 Winter Olympics.
  - *Source: Wikipedia "1995" (June 16)*

### June 22
- **ANA Flight 857 hijacking**: Japanese police rescue 365 hostages from a hijacked All Nippon Airways Flight 857 (Boeing 747-200) at Hakodate Airport. The hijacker was armed with a knife and demanded the release of Shoko Asahara, leader of the Aum Shinrikyo cult.
  - *Source: Wikipedia "1995" (June 22); Associated Press, June 22-23, 1995*

### June 29
- **Iraq disarmament crisis**: According to UNSCOM, the unity of the U.N. Security Council begins to fray as a few countries, particularly France and Russia, become more interested in making financial deals with Iraq than in disarming the country.
  - *Source: Wikipedia "1995" (June 29)*

### June 29
- **Sampoong Department Store collapse**: In the Seocho-gu district of Seoul, South Korea, the Sampoong Department Store collapses, killing 502 people and injuring 937. It remains one of the deadliest peacetime disasters in South Korean history.
  - *Source: Wikipedia "1995" (June 29); Korean newspaper archives, July 1995*

## TECHNOLOGY & SCIENCE

### June 6
- **Norman Thagard breaks US space endurance record**: U.S. astronaut Norman Thagard breaks NASA's space endurance record of 14 days, 1 hour and 16 minutes, aboard the Russian space station Mir.
  - *Source: Wikipedia "1995" (June 6)*

### June 8
- **PHP scripting language released**: Danish/Greenlandic/Canadian programmer Rasmus Lerdorf releases the first version of the scripting language PHP (then called "Personal Home Page Tools"), which over the next 15 years becomes the server-side language on over 75% of all Web servers.
  - *Source: Wikipedia "1995 in science" (Computer science); Lerdorf, Rasmus (1995-06-08) - Newsgroup: comp.infosystems.www.authoring.cgi*

### June 23
- **Jonas Salk dies**: Dr. Jonas Salk, American medical researcher and virologist who developed the first safe and effective polio vaccine, dies at age 80.
  - *Source: Wikipedia "1995 in science" (Deaths); The New York Times, June 24, 1995*

### June 29
- **STS-71: Shuttle-Mir docking**: Space Shuttle Atlantis docks with the Russian Mir space station for the first time, a landmark event in the US-Russian space cooperation program.
  - *Source: Wikipedia "1995" (June 29); NASA mission archives*

## SOCIETY & CULTURE

### Music

#### June 1
- **Alan Wilder leaves Depeche Mode**: Alan Wilder announces his departure from the electronic band Depeche Mode, marking the end of an era for the group's classic lineup.
  - *Source: Wikipedia "1995 in music" (Events)*

#### June 13
- **Bjork releases "Post"**: Icelandic singer Bjork releases her critically acclaimed second solo album "Post," featuring hits including "Army of Me," "Hyperballad," "I Miss You," and the Betty Hutton cover "It's Oh So Quiet."
  - *Source: Wikipedia "1995 in music" (Events)*

#### June 20
- **Michael Jackson releases "HIStory"**: Michael Jackson releases his first double-album "HIStory: Past, Present and Future, Book I," which goes on to become the best-selling multiple-disc album of all time with 35 million copies (70 million units) sold worldwide.
  - *Source: Wikipedia "1995 in music" (Events)*

### Film & Entertainment

#### June 10
- **"Pocahontas" premiere in Central Park**: Disney's "Pocahontas" has the largest premiere ever for a film, screening in Central Park, New York City, with an audience of 100,000 and free admission. The film goes on to commercial success worldwide despite mixed reviews.
  - *Source: Wikipedia "1995 in film" (Events); The New York Times, June 11, 1995*

#### June 16
- **"Batman Forever" released**: The third Batman film, directed by Joel Schumacher and starring Val Kilmer as Batman, is released in theaters and scores the highest-opening weekend of all time at that point, generating $52.8 million.
  - *Source: Wikipedia "1995 in film" (Events)*

### Sports

#### May 25 - June 24
- **1995 Rugby World Cup**: Hosted by South Africa, the tournament is won by the host nation after defeating New Zealand 15-12 in the final (with an extra-time drop goal by Joel Stransky). This was the Springboks' tournament debut after World Rugby lifted their ban following the end of apartheid. The event was a major moment in post-apartheid South African reconciliation.
  - *Source: Wikipedia "1995" (May); Wikipedia "1995 in sports" (Rugby union)*

#### June 16
- **2002 Winter Olympics awarded to Salt Lake City** (see Politics)
