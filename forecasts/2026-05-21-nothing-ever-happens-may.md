## "Nothing Ever Happens" — Definition

These markets typically define "nothing happens" as NO occurrence of:
- Nuclear weapon detonation
- Assassination of a world leader (head of state/government of a P5 or G20 nation)
- Major terrorist attack (100+ casualties in a developed nation)
- Catastrophic natural disaster (10,000+ deaths)
- Unexpected declaration of war between major powers
- Sovereign default by a G20 nation

Routine events (elections, rate decisions, scheduled summits, market movements) do NOT constitute "something happening." The question is about genuinely surprising, catastrophic, or historically significant events.
